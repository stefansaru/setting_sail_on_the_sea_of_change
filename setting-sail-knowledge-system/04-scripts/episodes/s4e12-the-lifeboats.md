# Production Breakdown — S4E12: "The Lifeboats"

> Production and cinematography breakdown for [[setting-sail-the-seed-ledger]] **Season 4, Episode 12** (Act III,
> Phase C). **Hull Four, bay C and the lifeboat dock beyond its doors.** The small ships leave with the bodies they
> can hold, and **Teo's is among them** ([[the-seed-fleet]]: bodies cross by lifeboat, `WR-E3`; lifeboats can't hold
> deep stasis, so the bodies go awake, `Q-62`, `Q-63`). Kai's body cannot go: the cradle buckled in E10, and the line
> is failing. **Only the mind can leave, and packing a mind before the hull dies takes a faster clock than Kai is
> allowed. Only a Ghost gets one** (`WR-M5`, `Q-50`, [[the-cyborg-slip]]). Kai **100% organic Shadow**; glyph
> **cracked**; the "arrive" square **dims**.

## Setup & hook
Close on two proxy hands on a steel rail, straining. Capsule 10's cradle, bent by E10's bell, won't slide. The
proxy's servos whine past their limit, and the rail doesn't move a millimetre. In the corner of Kai's Lens the
count reads `49:20:11`, and on the cradle a small tag reads `C-10 · CRADLE · JAMMED`.

## Cinematography — Caravaggio, then the dark between hulls
| Element | The dock (the bodies leave) | Bay C (what stays) |
|---------|-----------------------------|--------------------|
| **Lighting** | [[chromatic-expressionism\|Caravaggio]] tenebrism: each lifeboat bay lit by one warm source overhead, a face waking under it like a painting of a resurrection. | The emergency red again, and nine empty cradles, cold. One capsule left, its amber line the only warm thing in the room. |
| **Camera** | Through the dock port, locked: the glass is the frame within the frame. | High and wide, then down to Kai at eye level, sitting. |
| **Sound** | Real breath for the first time in the season: a body's first gasps, coughing, the wet sound of lungs remembering. | The single pump on capsule 10, a little irregular. Then nothing but the count. |

## UI behavior (the Perspective Lens)
Kai's Lens only (`WR-L2`). Teo, awake in the flesh, is seen through glass, through Kai's proxy.
- **The count** in the bottom-left square, `49:20:11` → `47:51:02`, ticking. Beside it, the **"arrive"** square.
- **Capsule 10:** `C-10 · CRADLE · JAMMED`, and on its line, `LINE · FAILING`, amber, steady.
- **The boats:** small tags over each leaving craft: `BOAT 3 · C-09 · → HULL ONE`. Its second bay shows, lit and
  empty, `C-10`.
- **The pack table (the turn).** Kai asks for it, and it opens as a plain table, the most UI on screen all season:
  `PACK · 8 mo inside` / `TIER 0 · 243 d` / `TIER 1 · 4 d 01 h` / `TIER 2 · 1 h 37 m · CREDIT · SHADOWS RARELY` /
  `TIER 3 · 4 min · GHOSTS ONLY`. The first two rows grey out against the count. The last row does not.
- **The square dims:** "arrive" loses half its light and stays that way.

## Philosophical climax
Every crossing has a door shaped for one kind of person. Bodies leave by boat, slowly, at a cost in chemistry the
receiving hull never budgeted. Minds leave by beam, if they can be packed in time, and how much time you have
depends on your clock, which depends on what you are. Kai is a mind tied to a body that can't be moved, at a clock
too slow to pack in. The arithmetic is not hostile. It's a table. The only row that fits the time left belongs to
a kind of person Kai is not. That's why the option that was always theoretical becomes the only door. Teo's line
through the glass is the reel's mercy, and Kai's answer is its first lie: a small, kind deception, offered to a
friend so that he can leave. The High Clock does the same at the scale of a fleet.

## Script (draft v1)
> 9:16 vertical · ~2.5 min · cinematic photoreal · *Black Mirror / Silo* register. **KAI** = the lead (`CHR-08`);
> **TEO** (`CHR-09`) = bay-mate, awake in his own body for the first time in years; **DECIMUS** (`CHR-10`) = the bay
> safeguard. Five short lines. The table carries the rule, so nobody has to say it.

*Castes (`WR-B1`): Kai is a Shadow attending at Tier 0 by leased proxy (`WR-B6`); the body is in capsule 10, in deep
stasis on a failing line. Teo is a Shadow whose body is **woken** as it crosses into the lifeboat (no deep stasis
aboard, `Q-63`). His mind runs on the boat's small Tier 0 stack, tether only (`Q-62`). The eight other bodies of
the rota group (C-01 to C-08) are Shadows, also woken as they board. Decimus is a digital agent (`Q-71`). Clocks
(`WR-T1`, `WR-T4`): Tier 0 throughout, one clock. The reel spans about an hour and a half real, `49:20:11` →
`47:51:02` on Hull Four's count, about **T+4.2** (strike + 32 h). The pack table, recomputed: packing a mind takes
about 8 subjective months, ≈ 243 days. At Tier 0 that is 243 real days. At Tier 1, 243 ÷ 60 ≈ **4 d 01 h**,
longer than the 47–49 hours left. At Tier 2, 243 × 24 ÷ 3,600 ≈ **1 h 37 m**, possible only on rare credit for a
Shadow (`WR-B5`). At Tier 3, 243 × 86,400 ÷ 86,400 s = **243 s ≈ 4 min**, for Ghosts only aboard (`WR-M5`). Spends
(`WR-E1`): each lifeboat burns **chemistry** (fuel is chemistry, `Q-70`, and every woken body breathes and eats from
the boat's stock); the boats' Tier 0 stacks and the dock's rails draw **watts**; capsule 10's failing line is
chemistry leaking away. Kai's Tier-2 request, sent at the end, would spend both.*

**1 · THE CRADLE — CLOSE · HOOK (0:00–0:05).** Proxy hands on the bent rail, straining. Servos whine past their
limit. Nothing moves. [UI: `49:20:11` · `C-10 · CRADLE · JAMMED`.]

**2 · BAY C — WIDE · CONTEXT (0:05–1:15, shots 2–5).** Kai lets go. Around capsule 10, the other nine slide out on
their rails one by one, through the bay doors and under the stencil THE STATION, one letter still dark from E10,
toward the dock.

> **DECIMUS:** It won't release. Cut the line to carry it, and the body has about four minutes.
>
> *(Kai looks at the amber line. `LINE · FAILING`.)*

**3 · THE DOCK — through the port.** In the first lifeboat bay a capsule's frost clears, and a stranger's real eyes
open under one warm overhead light, the Caravaggio resurrection. A gasp, wet and loud. A body coughing up years of
stillness. Then the next.

**4 · TEO WAKES.** Boat 3. Teo's capsule clears. His real face, which we have never seen, is thinner and plainer than
his proxy's. He breathes for the first time in years, badly, then better. His hand finds the edge of the bay. He
turns his head and sees the bay beside him: lit, empty, tagged `C-10`.

**5 · KAI AT THE PORT.** Kai's proxy at the glass. Through it, Teo, awake, sees a maintenance rig and knows who is
in it.

**6 · THE GLASS · FRICTION (1:15–2:15, shots 6–8).** Teo's voice is muffled, real air on real glass, and he talks with
his hands as much as his mouth.

> **TEO:** There's room.
>
> **KAI** *(a beat; the easiest lie in the world):* Next one.

Teo nods. He believes it, because he has never been lied to by anyone who loved him. He puts his palm flat on the
inside of the glass. Kai's proxy palm meets it from outside: flesh and rig, a centimetre of glass apart.

**7 · THE BOATS LEAVE.** The hatch seals. Three small craft drop off the hull into the dark, one after another, lit
from inside like lanterns. [UI: `BOAT 3 · C-09 · → HULL ONE`.] They get smaller. They are very slow.

**8 · BAY C.** Kai walks back in. Nine empty cradles. One full. Kai sits on the deck beside capsule 10, back to the
cradle, as you'd sit next to someone in a hospital.

> **KAI:** Then pack me.
>
> **DECIMUS:** Not at your clock.

**9 · THE TABLE · TURN (2:15–2:30, shots 9–10).** The pack table opens in Kai's Lens, plain as a timetable. Row by
row it greys out against the count: `TIER 0 · 243 d` (grey) · `TIER 1 · 4 d 01 h` (grey) · `TIER 2 · 1 h 37 m ·
CREDIT · SHADOWS RARELY` · `TIER 3 · 4 min · GHOSTS ONLY`. Kai reads the last row for a long time. One proxy finger
taps the Tier 2 row: `REQUESTED · PENDING`.

**10 · FINAL IMAGE.** The bottom row of Kai's Lens: `47:51:02`, ticking. Beside it, the "arrive" square **dims** to
half its light and stays there. Above the doors, the stencil's dark letter. Cut to black.

*(Theme in subtext, not stated: four days at the second rung would have been plenty, for someone who had four days.
Kai has two. The boat leaves with Kai's place in it, lit and empty, and Kai's first act of protection is a lie
told through glass, the same act the High Clock commits all season, made small and loving. Teo's hand on the glass
is the last warm thing he gives anyone in S4, and he doesn't know what he gave the night before.)*
