# Production Breakdown — S2E15: "The Absolution"

> Production/cinematography breakdown for [[setting-sail-tier-ascent]] **Season 2, Episode 15** (Act III's close,
> Phase C′). To earn the merit for the cure honestly, **Adam** (`CHR-06`, 60% → **30% organic**, Tier 2 until E16,
> `Q-48`) checks himself into [[the-ministry-of-hope|Hope]] as a **voluntary Penitent**: no police, no sentence
> (`WR-S4`, `WR-S6`), an Absolution, once proven, restores merit. It mirrors Raul walking in as a voluntary Seeker in
> S1E11–13 (`Q-09`, `Q-116`): both leads choose Hope. This is the reel that spends **Hope's carceral half**
> (`FAC-03`, `MAG-16`): the [[the-absolution-protocol|Ledger of Absolution]], the indefinite sentence, the candle
> that never fills (`VIS-14`). Passing the teal Silence Cloisters he sees **one welded cell door**, and no one
> explains it: the tissue-lock plant for S3 (`Q-67`, `Q-77`, `Q-119`); [[the-encrypted-block-paradox]] itself stays
> held, and [[the-locked-block]] stays a standalone short (`Q-76`). He takes **Vance's keys** knowing since E14 that
> they are a forger's (`Q-61`): they buy his exit and his 2.4 Apex hours, and with them comes an
> [[intent-patch|Intent-Patch]] on his own squares, the **regrade** of his last flesh, paid in installments to E19
> (`Q-96`). He never takes the Validation. **Answers an S1 question:** what the rest of Hope is.

## Setup & hook
The **Sanctuary Void**: a white wall so tall the top is lost, a figure at its foot no bigger than a thumbnail. Adam,
in a plain Penitent's grey, crossing a threshold. In the lower corner of his Lens the village feed, which has been
there since E4, flickers once and goes dark.

## UI behavior (the Perspective Lens)
All UI is in Adam's Lens (`WR-L2`). Inside Hope it shifts to **Parish Metrics** (`VIS-14`), keeping the Lens grammar
(`WR-L1`).
- **On entry:** the village feed goes dark (Hope's cloister admits no outside window). Tag: `PENITENT · VOLUNTARY`.
- **Top circles (Context):** the scales (equilibrium) and the open book (exit report), its pages writing and
  un-writing.
- **Bottom of frame:** the candle, `ABSOLUTION 0%`. It moves twice in the reel: to `3%` across weeks, and to `4%` once.
  It never fills.
- **Bottom squares (Intention):** his own, `cure her`, and, returning when Vance appears at the grille, the pending
  `KEYS · VANCE` (E12), now reading `EXIT · APEX · 2.4 h`.
- **The door:** every cell door in the cloister carries a small candle tag; one door carries none.
- **The turn (`Q-96`):** as he signs, a thin gold seam draws itself under his bottom row of squares, the seam he
  magnified under Vance's in E13, tagged `REGRADE`. The first installment is taken: `Organic: 30%`. Not the alignment
  setting: nothing here declares alignment kind or cruel (`WR-L4`). The hollow glyph doesn't change (cold-complete
  waits for E16).

## Philosophical climax
Hope is the one honest road left, and it is honest all the way down: it will not let him out until he tells the
truth, and the truth is not *"to save her."* It's *"it stopped hurting"*: the first bite, E2. Hope takes that, and
moves the candle one percent, and will take every other truth too, for as long as it takes, and she has about three
hours. **The system is patient. She isn't.** So he buys the other road, from the forger, knowing; and the price comes
due at once, in flesh, painlessly, a body he isn't in being carved in a rented room. The gold seam under his squares
is the one Vance wears: the trick from E13, now written on him.

## Script (draft v1)
> 9:16 vertical · ~2.5 min · cinematic photoreal · *Black Mirror / Silo* register. **ADAM** = the S2 lead (`CHR-06`);
> **THE INTERCESSOR** = a cleric of Hope, gentle and absolute; **VANCE** = the S2 antagonist (`CHR-04`), at the visiting
> grille. **NAOMI** and **RUTH** are seen on the village feed, at the end, and don't speak. A reel of people: the
> confession is the scene.

*Castes (`WR-B1`): Adam is a cyborg, 60% organic → 30% at the turn, his body in a rented city capsule (`Q-91`), his
mind at Tier 2, in Hope's digital cloister, which runs at his clock; the Intercessor is a Ghost in a shell (`Q-87`),
here at Adam's clock; Vance is a Ghost (`Q-90`); the Penitents at the benches are Shadows and Ghosts; Naomi and Ruth
are Purists. Timing (`WR-T4`): he checks in about **35 real minutes** after Ruth's sheet (E10), about **87 days** at
his clock (35 × 3,600 = 126,000 min = 2,100 h); the candle crawls to 3% across **six weeks** of his (about **17 real
minutes**); he signs at about **52 real minutes**, about **130 days**. The first installment takes the rigs about
**2 real minutes** at the capsule (5 days of his; he feels none of it). That leaves about an hour at Tier 2 since the
sheet, as `Q-48` requires; then 2.4 Apex hours and a ~40-minute descent (`Q-93`), about four real hours in all.*

**1 · COLD OPEN — SANCTUARY VOID (9:16) · HOOK (0:00–0:05).** A white wall to the top of frame and beyond. At its foot,
tiny, ADAM steps over a threshold in Penitent's grey. [UI: the village feed in the corner flickers, and goes dark.
`PENITENT · VOLUNTARY`.] [SOUND: stone silence.]

**2 · THE CONFESSIONAL · CONTEXT (0:05–1:15, shots 2–4).** One high window. THE INTERCESSOR, in slate-grey vestments,
staff resting against the wall, sits facing him with total, unhurried kindness. [UI: Parish Metrics bloom: the
scales and the open book above; at the bottom of frame, a candle: `ABSOLUTION 0%`.]

> **THE INTERCESSOR** *(soft):* Why did you climb?
>
> **ADAM:** To save her.

[UI: on the open book, his answer writes itself in ink, and fades. The candle doesn't move.]

> **THE INTERCESSOR:** That's why you stayed. *(beat)* Why did you climb?

**3 · DISSOLVE — weeks.** The same window, the light crossing the floor again and again. Adam at a long bench among
other Penitents, all bent over glowing calculations; Adam in the confessional; at the bench; the confessional. [UI:
the candle, `3%`.]

**4 · THE CLOISTER WALL.** An Intercessor walks him back to his cell down a deep **teal** corridor, the Silence
Cloisters: cell doors at intervals, each with a small candle tag at its edge. [UI: a percentage on each.] The camera
tracks with them and does not stop. One door they pass is **welded shut**, a raised seam of old weld around its whole
frame, and it carries **no tag at all.** A faint hum behind it. Adam's eyes go to it. The Intercessor's don't.
Neither of them slows. *(Nothing more, ever, in S2.)*

**5 · THE TRUTH · FRICTION (1:15–2:15, shots 5–6).** The confessional again. Adam has been silent a long time.

> **ADAM** *(barely):* …It stopped hurting.

[UI: the ink on the book holds. The candle: `4%`.] He looks at the four percent, and does the arithmetic the viewer
is doing.

> **THE INTERCESSOR** *(warm, meaning it):* Good. *(beat)* Again tomorrow.

**6 · THE GRILLE.** The visiting grille, a lattice of white stone. VANCE on the other side, unlit, at ease. [UI: the
pending square returns to Adam's bottom row: `KEYS · VANCE`, and under it now, `EXIT · APEX · 2.4 h`.]

> **VANCE:** Hope will wait a thousand years for the rest of that sentence. *(beat)* Will your mother?

*(Recall cue, ≤ 2 s: the empty frosted row from E14, `10,000` over it. He knows what he's signing.)* Adam looks at
the dark corner of his Lens where the feed used to be.

**7 · HE SIGNS · TURN (2:15–2:30, shots 7–8).** He closes his hand on the pending square. [UI: a thin gold seam draws
itself under his bottom row, the seam from E13, and settles: `REGRADE`.] Insert, real world: a rented capsule bay, the
bay tag `RENTED` (E3); surgical rigs close over Adam's real body with the visceral *clunk* of S1E13. [UI: `Organic:
60%` → `30%`.] In the confessional, Adam doesn't flinch. Nothing hurts. [UI: the candle, at `4%`, goes out.]

**8 · FINAL IMAGE.** The exit of the Sanctuary Void opening on light; Adam, tiny, walking out of it. In the corner of
his Lens the village feed blinks back on (the hook's window, returned): Naomi on her pillow, Ruth beside her, the
sheet on the lintel. Under his squares, the gold seam. Cut to black.

*(Theme in subtext, never stated: the honest road is real and would take longer than she has; the other road is quick
and paid in flesh he no longer feels. The candle that never fills goes out instead. A cold viewer sees a man buy his
way out of a reckoning; a serial viewer sees Vance's seam, from E13, drawn on Adam, and knows it has installments
left.)*
