## Setup & hook
Two capsules in a city bay, inches apart. In one, Adam's body. In the other, a stranger's, worn grey the way Grid bodies wear. Their monitors disagree:
one reads `Tier 2`, the other `Tier 1`. While the stranger lives a minute, Adam lives an hour. They have never met.
They never will. Their hands are a forearm's length apart.

## Cinematography — together, and a lifetime apart
| Element | The tribe (Tier 2, 3,600×) | The capsule bays (real, 1:1) |
|---------|----------------------------|------------------------------|
| **Lighting** | **Turrell** ([[chromatic-expressionism]]): a borderless violet-gold field, no walls, no corners; everyone lit the same, from nowhere. | **Caravaggio** over Flavin strip-neon (E3's bay): one hard source per capsule, deep black between them. |
| **Camera** | Gimbal-smooth, slow circling; at the synced laugh, a single locked wide so all 150 move as one. | Locked off; one slow lateral pan per bay, capsule to capsule, the standalone's move. |
| **Sound** | One shared room tone that breathes in a single rhythm, like a crowd breathing together. | Pumps, a drip, nothing else. |
| **Blocking** | Bodies close, shoulders touching. The closeness is the seduction. | Bodies close, plastic touching plastic. The closeness is the lie. |

## UI behavior (the Perspective Lens)
Adam's Lens throughout (`WR-L2`). **No glyph** (absent until E9).
- **Top circle (Context) — the tribe overlay:** `TRIBE · 150`, a ring of 150 small lights pulsing **in one beat**,
  his own among them, at last on the beat. Two fingers open it into a map of the city: the 150 bodies scattered across
  every district, Ghosts as hollow dots with no body anywhere; the nearest capsule to his own is **not one of
  them**.
- **The tether line (from S1E6):** at his frame edge, as on every Shadow-bodied mind. The Ghosts around him have none.
- **Vesper's tag:** `VESPER · Ghost`. Her light in the ring pulses **off the beat**: two clocks that never match.
  When her burst ends, the tag turns to `VESPER · paused`.
- **Her capsule feed (shot 7):** a window she opens into his view. The bay's physical labels, not Lens text: **VESPER**
  and **RAUL**.

## Philosophical climax
Adam came from a village where everyone shares one *now*: the same fire, the same dark. Up here a tribe is how you get
that back, and for a moment he has it, 150 people laughing on the same breath. Then the map: their bodies are
everywhere; his lies next to a stranger. Belonging up here is a clock, not a place. Vesper is what that costs over
time: she shared a wall with the man she loved and never shared his hour, and now both their boxes are empty. Being
near was never being with.

## Script (draft v1)
> 9:16 vertical · ~2.5 min · cinematic photoreal · *Black Mirror / Silo* register. **ADAM** (`CHR-06`), about 19 in
> body; **VESPER** (`CHR-05`), a Ghost, Golden Baseline, ageless; **THE TRIBE**, about 150, unnamed. Three spoken
> lines.

*Castes (`WR-B1`): Adam is a cyborg, 90% organic, Lens on, body in his rented city capsule at 1:1 (`Q-91`), mind at
Tier 2. Vesper is a Ghost (`Q-92`), no body, no tether; she runs at Tier 2 in bursts and is paused between them
(`Q-121`). The tribe are Shadows and Ghosts. The stranger in the neighbouring capsule is a Shadow at Tier 1, his body worn grey as Grid bodies wear (`WR-B2`), not old.
Timing (`WR-T4`): about **thirteen real weeks** into the residency, Adam has just been up-clocked: about **one real
hour** at Tier 2 = **about 150 days for him** (3,600 h). His body is 19; his mind about 34 (about fifteen years at
Tier 1, `Q-48`). This reel covers about **two hours of his = two real seconds**: in the hook's bay, the stranger's hand
moves once. **Vesper's clock:** at Tier 2 while she runs; how long she has lived, and how long she was paused before
tonight, stays unsaid. **Raul's capsule** has stood empty since his body died in S1E17, about **eleven real years**.*

**1 · TWO CAPSULES · HOOK (0:00–0:05).** Real time, a city bay, amber light. ADAM's capsule, `RENTED` on its edge,
Ruth's cord on his wrist; inches away, plastic to plastic, a **STRANGER** asleep in the next, grey and worn. Their two small displays,
side by side: **`Tier 2`** ice-blue on Adam's, **`Tier 1`** amber on the stranger's. The stranger's fingers twitch.
Adam's are still.

**2 · THE TRIBE · CONTEXT (0:05–1:15, shots 2–4).** Tier 2. A Turrell field, no walls. ADAM stands among
**THE TRIBE**, close as a congregation. [UI: top circle `TRIBE · 150`, a ring of lights pulsing in one beat; his light
joins it and **locks on the beat**.] Someone says something we don't hear, and **all 150 laugh on the same breath**,
the same instant, perfectly in step. Adam laughs with them. His face opens. It is the first time since the village
that he has been inside a *we*.

**3 · THE MAP.** Later, still glowing, he idly spreads the circle with two fingers. [UI: the ring unfolds into a map of
the city at night: 150 lights scattered across every district, miles apart; a few hollow dots with no body at all.
His own light blinks in a bay on the far side of the river. The nearest light to his is grey, not tribe: the
stranger.] He looks at the map a beat too long, and closes it.

**4 · VESPER ARRIVES.** In the ring a single light **comes on off the beat**. Among the tribe a woman is suddenly
there who wasn't: VESPER, ageless, mid-step, as if she has walked in from another room that is centuries away. [UI:
`VESPER · Ghost`; her light pulses half a beat behind the rest.] The tribe's eyes slide over her politely: a stranger.
She is used to it.

**5 · THE LINE · FRICTION (1:15–2:15, shots 5–7).** She notices Adam because of the thing at the edge of him: the
**tether line**, the only one near her. She reaches toward it the way you'd touch an old scar on someone else.

> **VESPER:** Where are you?
>
> **ADAM:** Next to a stranger.

She takes that in. Something almost like a smile.

**6 · HER HABIT.** VESPER lifts her hand and, after a hesitation that says she has not done this in a long time, opens
a window into the space between them: a feed. She doesn't look at it yet.

> **VESPER:** Mine was next to—

**7 · THE FEED.** A capsule bay, real time, another district. The lateral pan, slow (the standalone's move): two
capsules, **inches apart**, lids up, **both empty**. On each slot edge, a physical label, worn: **VESPER**.
**RAUL**. She looks. Her sentence doesn't finish. On the feed, nothing moves at all.

**8 · ADAM · TURN (2:15–2:30, shots 8–9).** Adam reads the second label. A beat. His lips part, as if on a name he
knows. He turns to her, and **VESPER is still**: mid-breath, eyes on the feed, one hand raised. [UI: her light drops
out of the ring; her tag reads `VESPER · paused`.] Her burst has ended. He is standing inches from her, and she is a
lifetime away.

**9 · FINAL IMAGE.** High wide, locked: the tribe moving and laughing around them in one perfect rhythm; Adam beside
the frozen woman, like two figures in a frieze; between them, the small window of the feed: two empty capsules,
inches apart. The tribe's beat goes on. Cut to black on the beat.

*(Theme in subtext, never stated: the tribe is the closest thing to the village the Grid can sell, a shared *now*, and
it is real while it lasts. But the bodies are scattered, his lies beside a stranger, and Vesper's lay beside the man she
loved for years without one shared hour. The viewer who remembers S1E6's untagged capsule finishes the sentence: Raul's
box is empty because he died out of it, and he is alive, a Ghost in a shell, by a fire she has never seen. She paused
before anyone could tell her.)*
