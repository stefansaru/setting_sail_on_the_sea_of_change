# Production Breakdown — S2E08: "The Standardized Vices"

> Detailed production/cinematography breakdown for [[setting-sail-tier-ascent]] **Season 2, Episode 8** (Act II,
> Phase B′, the sweetest seduction). **Tier 2.** **Adam** (`CHR-06`), a **90%-organic cyborg**, tastes the Grid's
> licensed pleasures: [[standardized-vices|Standardized Vice Frameworks]] (`WLD-15`), the reel's one system. A licensed
> [[disruptive-algorithms|Disruptive Algorithm]] (`MAG-19`, the word's first time on screen) for a high, every act
> consented, covered and logged. The hollowness: **accountability has replaced meaning.**
> [[intimate-commerce]] (`WLD-16`) is a throwaway only, one tag drifting past a door, `GHOST INTIMACY · LICENSED`,
> never explained (budget fix, 2026-11-03; `Q-116`). The checking system is **not named**: the CVP first shows in
> E11 (`Q-116`). World: [[standardized-vices]] · [[disruptive-algorithms]] · [[chromatic-expressionism]] ·
> [[the-memory-substrate]].

## Setup & hook
A row of green ticks writes itself down the edge of the frame, one per line, calm as a receipt: consent, licence,
liability, logged. Then the walls of the room let go of their corners and pour into colour. Adam laughs, the way you
laugh when nothing can hurt you. Nothing does.

## UI behavior (the Perspective Lens)
Adam's Lens throughout (`WR-L2`). **No glyph** (absent until E9).
- **The licence card (shot 3):** `Disruptive Algorithm · licensed`.
- **The ledger (the reel's UI device):** a thin column of rows down the frame's right edge, compliance-green, one tick
  per act: `consent ✓` · `licence ✓` · `liability: covered ✓` · `logged ✓`. Every act gets its rows: the dose, the
  replays, the larger dose. It never refuses. It only covers.
- **The door tag (shot 2):** `GHOST INTIMACY · LICENSED`, drifting past on a closed door. Nobody looks at it.
- **Bottom squares (Intention):** dark all night. Nothing lights them. The audience may not notice until the turn.
- **The village feed:** the small window in the lower corner (from E4), carried all reel.

## Philosophical climax
In the village a vice cost something: a sore head, a word you couldn't take back, a night half-remembered. That was
how you knew it had happened to you. Up here every pleasure is licensed, consented, covered, logged and kept forever
in perfect detail, so nothing can go wrong, and nothing can go *anywhere*. Every box is green. The one row that would
say why he did it is empty. Below, on the feed, a dying woman spends his whole night lifting a cup of water, and it
is the only act in the frame that costs anyone anything.

## Script (draft v1)
> 9:16 vertical · ~2.5 min · cinematic photoreal · *Black Mirror / Silo* register. **ADAM** (`CHR-06`), about 19 in
> body; **THE HOST**, the arcade's licensing attendant; on the feed, **NAOMI** (`CHR-11`) and **RUTH** (`CHR-07`),
> silent. Two spoken lines.

*Castes (`WR-B1`): Adam is a cyborg, 90% organic, Lens on, body in his rented city capsule at 1:1 (`Q-91`), mind at
Tier 2. The Host is a Shadow (as Grid officials are, `Q-87`). Naomi and Ruth are Purists, no Lens; the feed is a
camera, not a Lens. Timing (`WR-T4`): about **one real day** at Tier 2 = **about ten years for Adam** (24 × 3,600 h =
3,600 days). His body is 19; his mind about 44. This reel covers **one night of his, about eight hours = eight real
seconds** on the feed: in the whole of it, Naomi lifts a cup from her lap to her mouth.*

**1 · THE LEDGER · HOOK (0:00–0:05).** Close on ADAM's face, at ease, lit violet. [UI: down the right edge, a column of
green ticks writes itself, calm as a receipt: `consent ✓` · `licence ✓` · `liability: covered ✓` · `logged ✓`.] The
wall behind him lets go of its corners and **pours into colour**. He laughs.

**2 · THE ARCADE · CONTEXT (0:05–1:15, shots 2–4).** Earlier the same night. A long white arcade at Tier 2, Flavin
bars, a hundred identical doors, each with its small lit plate. People pass in and out, unhurried, unashamed. A plate
drifts by on a closed door: **`GHOST INTIMACY · LICENSED`**. Nobody looks at it. Adam doesn't either. [UI: the
village feed in the lower corner, as always: NAOMI sitting up in bed under wool, a wooden cup in both hands in her lap;
RUTH beside her on a stool. Both still as a painting.]

**3 · THE HOST.** A counter of white stone. THE HOST, ageless, kind, slides a card across to him. [UI: the card:
**`Disruptive Algorithm · licensed`**; beside it the column of rows waits, empty.]

> **THE HOST** *(warm, practised):* Consent?
>
> **ADAM:** Yes.

[UI: the rows fill, one tick at a time: `consent ✓` · `licence ✓` · `liability: covered ✓` · `logged ✓`.] The Host
smiles the way a pharmacist smiles. No warning follows. There is nothing to warn about.

**4 · THE HIGH.** A small white room. Adam sits. The lines of the room **dissolve**: corners into gradients, the floor
into a field of violet that turns slowly to gold, gold to rose, without edges, without end. He drifts in it. It is the
most beautiful thing he has ever seen, and it doesn't stop being beautiful. [SOUND: one sustained chord that never
resolves.] His face is open, unguarded, very young.

**5 · AGAIN · FRICTION (1:15–2:15, shots 5–7).** The room re-forms. He reaches for it again, two fingers, and it
comes back **exactly** as it was: the same violet, the same gold, the same turn to rose at the same instant. [UI: new
rows: `run 2 ✓` · `logged ✓`.] Again. Identical. [`run 3 ✓`.] He watches it this time instead of falling into it,
the way you watch a film you know.

**6 · THE EDGE.** He goes looking for an edge. He opens the card and drags the dose up, past where the slider
glows, past where any body would have stopped. [UI: the rows don't refuse. They extend: `dose ✓` · `liability:
covered ✓` · `logged ✓`.] The colour field deepens, perfect, and holds him like water holds a stone. Nothing goes
wrong. He looks almost disappointed. [UI, lower corner: the feed. Naomi's cup has risen an inch from her lap. Her hands
shake. Ruth's hand has come up under hers.]

**7 · THE MORNING THAT ISN'T.** The room returns to white. Adam sits up, clear-eyed, rested, nothing aching, nothing
fogged, nothing to regret and nothing to half-remember. He could replay every second of the night in perfect detail.
He doesn't.

**8 · THE ROW · TURN (2:15–2:30, shots 8–9).** Close on the frame's edge. [UI: the ledger, complete: a long green
column, every act of the night ticked, covered, logged. Then, for the first time, the camera finds the **bottom row**:
his Intention squares. **Dark.** All night. Not one of them lit.] He looks at the empty squares as if noticing a
room he forgot he had.

**9 · FINAL IMAGE.** Push into the feed in the lower corner until it is the whole frame, still in his Lens: Caravaggio
lamplight, through glass. Across his entire night it has taken this long: NAOMI's cup reaches her lips, both her
shaking hands on it and RUTH's hand under both. She drinks. Nothing ticks. Cut to black.

*(Theme in subtext, never stated: every pleasure licensed, consented, covered and kept, so nothing can go wrong and
nothing can go anywhere; the ledger answers *what* and *whether*, and the row that would say *why* stays dark. The only
act in the frame with a cost is a dying woman drinking water with help. The viewer finishes the sentence.)*
