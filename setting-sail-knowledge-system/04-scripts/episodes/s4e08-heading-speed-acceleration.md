# Production Breakdown — S4E8: "Heading, Speed, Acceleration"

> Production/cinematography breakdown for [[setting-sail-the-seed-ledger]] **Season 4, Episode 8** (Act II, Phase B).
> Split frame: **the Apex** above, **the base** below. Layer three of [[the-lure-beacon]] (`MAG-31`) asks for the fleet's
> vector relative to the star: *"please send us your heading, speed and acceleration — rest assured it did not go
> supernova."* The **High Clock** (`FAC-05`) reads the reassurance as the tell, weighs three options in an afternoon that
> is one blink of **Teo**'s (`CHR-09`, [[teo]]), and orders **silence**. The base is told *that*, not *why*
> ([[the-sealed-manifest]], `WLD-24`). The order is not new: it was drafted decades ago and waited in the manifest for a
> date (`Q-30`: the High Clock suspected something). **One reveal:** the silence order, and the clock gap that makes
> it possible. Minor: fuel as chemistry (`Q-70`); the old draft stamp.

## Setup & hook
Top half of the frame: white, endless, a council of still figures. Bottom half: a dim stasis bay, Teo's proxy at a
console, and Teo's eyes, open. The split is hard, a line across the 9:16 frame. In the bottom half, Teo begins to blink.
In the top half, a whole afternoon begins.

## Cinematography
| Element | The Apex (top) | The base (bottom) |
|---------|----------------|-------------------|
| **Lighting** | **Turrell** void (`VIS-15`): seamless white, no horizon, soft light from nowhere. The council's faces are the only texture. | **Caravaggio** single source: the bay console's glow on Teo's proxy and, through his Lens, on Teo. Rows of bodies in stasis fall away into black behind him. |
| **Camera** | Slow, unhurried coverage: the council speaks in full sentences, walks, sits (`WR-T2`). Normal motion, as it feels inside. | **Locked.** One shot for the whole afternoon above: Teo's eyelid, descending, in extreme slow motion, at the speed the Apex sees it. |
| **Sound** | Quiet voices, a room tone with no machines in it. | Nothing moves. A single low tone: one tick of the bay's clock, stretched across the whole debate. |

## UI behavior (the Perspective Lens)
Top-half UI is in a council Ghost's Lens; bottom-half UI is in **Kai's** Lens and then Teo's (`WR-L2`). The decoded text is
diegetic on the bay console.
- **Apex, the options (diegetic, on the white):** three lines.
  `ANSWER` · `TURN OFF ITS LINE · FUEL · 7 HULLS` · `SILENCE · 0 W`.
- **Apex, the draw (frame edge):** `86.4 MW per seat · 0.17 s REAL`.
- **Apex, the order:** an old document drawn out of the manifest, stamped `DRAFT · VOYAGE YEAR 9`. Only one line is blank:
  the date. It fills: `VOYAGE YEAR 41`.
- **Base, the order arrives:** a single **closed square** drops into the bottom row: `SILENCE`. Under it, a smaller field,
  where a reason would be: dark.
- **Base, the decoder (Teo's Lens, the turn):** `LAYER 4 · 12%`, still running.

## Philosophical climax
The High Clock is right. The reassurance *is* the tell: whatever wrote it knew what the base thought it was watching, so
it has been watching them watch. Answering gives away the fleet; steering off its line burns fuel that is also oxygen and
water and, in the end, bodies; silence costs nothing. They choose silence in the time it takes a young man to close his
eyes. Then they choose again, and this is the season's question: they could tell the base *why*. They don't, for a reason
that is also right. Fear lasts the rest of the crossing; an unexplained *no* annoys people for a week. *We kept them safe /
we never asked them.* The order was drafted thirty-two years ago, by people who suspected something was out there and
never said so. The base gets a closed square and a blank where the reason goes. And the one person still decoding sees
that the message isn't finished.

## Script (draft v1)
> 9:16 vertical · ~2.5 min · cinematic photoreal · *Black Mirror / Silo* register. **THE FIRST COUNCILLOR** (collar
> `1:2000`) and **THE SECOND COUNCILLOR** (collar `1:1000`) = Apex Ghosts of the High Clock, unnamed; others of the council
> are seen, uncounted; **TEO** = Kai's bay-mate (`CHR-09`); **KAI** (`CHR-08`), present, silent; **THE BEACON** = text and
> voice only (`WR-R2`). About eighty spoken words.

*Castes (`WR-B1`): the councillors are Ghosts at the Apex (Tier 3; the fleet's Apex is Ghosts only, `WR-B5`); Teo and Kai
are Shadows, 100% organic, bodies in deep stasis in this bay, minds at **Tier 0** attending the bay through leased
maintenance proxies (`WR-B6`); the beacon's makers are heard, never shown (`WR-R2`). Timing (`WR-T4`): about **T+4.19**,
**day 16** after first light (E6). Layer three decodes at the base at Tier 0. The council's afternoon, about **four
subjective hours**, is **0.17 real seconds** (4 × 3,600 s ÷ 86,400): one blink at the base. Kai and Teo live it at 1×, so
for them the order arrives the moment the question does. The order's draft is dated voyage year 9; the fleet launched about
**T−36**, so today is about **40.2 years** after launch, **voyage year 41**: the draft is about **32 years old**. Light-time:
bible only, layer three left the beacon about three months ago (about 0.25 ly). Spend (`WR-E1`): the Apex session draws
**86.4 MW per seat** (the drafted 1 kW per mind at 1× × 86,400) for 0.17 s, about **14.4 MJ (4 kWh) a seat**, paid from the
High Clock's watch ration on the reactor (`WR-T5`); the rejected turn would spend **fuel on all seven hulls**, which is
chemistry, the same stock a body draws (`Q-70`); silence spends **0 W**. Teo's and Kai's proxies draw the bay's lease.*

**1 · COLD OPEN — SPLIT FRAME (9:16) · HOOK (0:00–0:05).** Top: white, still figures. Bottom: Teo's face in the console glow,
eyes open. The bay console prints a line, letter by letter. [Diegetic: *please send us your heading, speed and acceleration
relative to the star.*] Teo's eyelid starts to fall.

**2 · THE BASE · CONTEXT (0:05–1:15, shots 2–4).** Bottom half. The rest of the line prints. [Diegetic: *— rest assured it did
not go supernova.*]

> **THE BEACON** *(even, breathless; under the text):* Rest assured.

KAI's proxy at the next console, still. Teo's eyelid, a third of the way down. It stays there for the rest of the reel's
top half.

**3 · THE APEX.** Top half opens up and takes most of the frame. The council, walking, unhurried, the line hanging in the
white between them.

> **THE FIRST COUNCILLOR:** "Rest assured."
>
> **THE SECOND COUNCILLOR:** It knows what we thought we were looking at.
>
> **THE FIRST COUNCILLOR:** Then it watched us watch.

**4 · THE OPTIONS.** Three lines on the white. [UI: `ANSWER` · `TURN OFF ITS LINE · FUEL · 7 HULLS` · `SILENCE · 0 W`.] The
first line greys before anyone touches it. The second councillor rests a hand on the second line. Below it, small, the
fuel's equivalent: what a body would have breathed. She lifts the hand away.

**5 · THE BLINK · FRICTION (1:15–2:15, shots 5–8).** Bottom half, a sliver: Teo's eyelid, halfway. Top half: the afternoon
goes on. Someone sits. Someone stands at the edge of the white looking out at nothing. The light does not change, because
it never does.

**6 · WHY.** The two councillors, close.

> **THE SECOND COUNCILLOR:** And the base?
>
> **THE FIRST COUNCILLOR:** Tell them not to answer.
>
> **THE SECOND COUNCILLOR:** Tell them why?
>
> **THE FIRST COUNCILLOR:** *Why* frightens them for the rest of the crossing. *No* annoys them for a week.

A pause in which no one disagrees. [UI, frame edge: `86.4 MW per seat · 0.17 s REAL`.]

**7 · THE DRAFT.** The first councillor reaches into the white and draws out a document: the order, already written, its
edges soft with age. [UI: `DRAFT · VOYAGE YEAR 9`.] One line on it is blank. It fills: `VOYAGE YEAR 41`. Nobody remarks on
the gap between the two numbers. *(Recall cue, ≤ 2 s: E5, the council finished talking before Kai arrived.)*

**8 · THE SEAL.** The document folds into a square and drops out of the bottom of the white, through the split line.

**9 · THE BASE · TURN (2:15–2:30, shots 9–11).** Bottom half takes the frame back. Teo's eyelid completes the blink, and opens.
In Kai's Lens, and in Teo's, a **closed square** has landed in the bottom row: `SILENCE`. The small field beneath it, where
the reason would go, is dark.

> **TEO** *(soft, reading it, almost relieved):* They don't want us to be scared.

**10 · KAI.** Kai looks at the square for a long second, then back to work. [UI: Kai's `arrive` square, faint, beside the closed
one.]

**11 · FINAL IMAGE.** Teo's console. The beacon's line sits where it printed. Under it, a progress line Teo never closed,
still running: `LAYER 4 · 12%`. His eyes go to it, and stay. Cut to black.

*(Theme in subtext, never stated: the High Clock is right about the beacon and right about fear, and it gives the base the
answer without the reason; the reason was ready for thirty-two years. The base's only window into the decision is a blink
long. The one man who reads the square as kindness is the one still decoding.)*
