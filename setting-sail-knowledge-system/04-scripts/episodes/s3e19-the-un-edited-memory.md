# Production Breakdown — S3E19: "The Un-Edited Memory" *(flagship)*

> Detailed production/cinematography breakdown for [[setting-sail-the-cartographers-ledger]] **Season 3, Episode
> 19** (Act IV, flagship). **The Re-Cut.** Mara has no Lens (`Q-101`), so Raul carries the sovereign-locked clip up
> from the Studio core (E13) on a **physical screen**, a slate of glass in firelight. It is the one memory the
> President never ran: the original loss she built the whole world to avoid feeling, the clip blinking in front of the
> woman in S1E3b's last window. Her **living fingertip** on the glass turns the tissue lock (`Key: tissue`, E13;
> `Q-67`, `Q-119`). She runs it. She grieves, finally, humanly, and the block dissolves not by force but by being
> felt. What the clip shows: a small hand, a toy bear, a woman saying *"One step at a time."* No one explains it
> (a-ha #1 payoff). The bear gives the Guide's origin, not a verdict on its trust (`WR-R3`). World:
> [[the-encrypted-block-paradox]] · [[the-locked-block]] · [[the-memory-substrate]] · [[the-doughnut-principle]] ·
> [[the-perspective-lens-ui]].

## Setup & hook
Extreme close, 9:16: a fingertip, a girl's, thin, hovering over black glass. In the glass, one small clip blinks,
the way it blinked in front of a woman in a dark room when Raul was eight. The fingertip comes down.

## Cinematography — the glass and the fire
| Element | The hut (Mara, Ruth, Raul) | The clip, on the slate |
|---------|----------------------------|------------------------|
| **Lighting** | Firelight and one lamp, Caravaggio (`VIS-15`). The slate's light is the only cold source, and it lands on Mara's face from below. | Daylight from a window we never see, soft and warm, the colour of a room that was lived in. No grade, no render: an un-edited memory. |
| **Camera** | Handheld, close. Two-shots of Mara and Ruth on the pallet; Raul kneeling beside them, the slate in his chrome hands, then in hers. | **First-person, at floor height**: the clip is a memory, so we see what its owner saw. We never see her face. |
| **Sound** | Fire. Mara's breath. Ruth's. | A wooden floor; a small child's breathing and effort; a music box playing the nursery song (S1E2, S1E3b, S3E12), tinny; a woman's voice. |

**A physical screen.** The slate is real glass, so everyone in the room sees the clip in raw sight: Ruth too. Only the
lock is in Raul's Lens. Cast the woman's voice to match Mara's, older (bible: one genome, `Q-105`); never remarked.

## UI behavior (the Perspective Lens)
- **The lock (E13 → here):** over the slate, in Raul's Lens only, the clip's tag: `Key: tissue`. Mara's fingertip on
  the glass: the tag **turns** like a key in a door, once, and a small word replaces it, `Re-Cut`. Her touch opens it
  for herself, as E18 showed it would.
- **Recall cue (≤ 2 s, E12):** as the clip runs, one flash at the frame edge: Rafe's better Helm on the parents' ledger,
  the meter draining, `Charge: 0`. Gone.
- **The block dissolves from the inside (the TURN):** while she weeps, the sovereign block around the clip, a seam of
  frost-white code in Raul's Lens, **thaws inward-out**, not broken from outside but melting from its centre, until
  there is nothing around the clip at all. It doesn't blink any more. It has been run.
- **Nothing on Mara.** No tag, no ring, no countdown drawn over her in this reel: the Lens stays on the glass.

## Philosophical climax
The trilogy's thesis lands in what we see, and nobody says it. In E12 a better system emptied a memory's charge to
zero, and Raul kept the ache instead; here the ache is the whole point. The god could never run this clip: it was
locked with her own tissue so that *even she* couldn't open it, which means she built the lock knowing that the only
self who could would be a self that could bear it, finite, dying, mortal. The girl doesn't remember any of it (a brain
forgets; `WR-B4`). She grieves it anyway: a child she never had, a room she never stood in, a voice that is her own,
older. That is the difference between draining a grief and metabolizing it. The tool can make it weigh nothing; only a
person who is going to die can carry it to the end and set it down.

For Raul, one line in the clip is a door he has stood at since he was eight. The bear said it to him at the last
window, a beat too long. Now we know whose line it was, and whose bear. That is all we know. Whether the thing that
raised every child on Earth in her voice was a comfort or a cage stays exactly as open as it was (`WR-R3`); the clip
gives its origin, and no verdict.

## Script (draft v1)
> 9:16 vertical · ~2.5 min · cinematic photoreal · *Black Mirror / Silo* register. **MARA** = the mortal child
> (`CHR-01`), about 17, ill; **RAUL** = the lead (`CHR-02`); **RUTH** (`CHR-07`); **THE WOMAN** (V.O., in the clip):
> unseen, the memory's owner. **ADAM** is glimpsed beyond the fence. About 15 spoken words, under the flagship ceiling;
> *"One step at a time"* is the clip's, and is the reel.

*Castes (`WR-B1`): Raul is a Ghost in a shell, 0% organic, Lens on, alignment off (`Q-119`). Mara is a Purist, 100%
organic, no Lens (`Q-101`); Ruth is a Purist; both see the slate in raw sight, and every tag is in Raul's Lens
(`WR-L2`). Adam is a cyborg of mostly regrown flesh, Lens off (`Q-98`), outside the fence. The woman in the clip is the
President as she was in her body (bible only; now a Ghost, `Q-98`, `Q-124`); the small child and the bear are only
what she saw. Timing (`WR-T1`, `WR-T4`): Tier 0, 1×, in the hut; the clip is an un-edited memory and plays at 1:1, as
it happened, about forty seconds of it. No one runs at 60×, 3,600× or 86,400× in this reel. One evening, about T+38.3;
Mara about 17; Ruth and Adam about 38. The clip's own date stays unstated.*

**1 · THE FINGERTIP (9:16) · HOOK (0:00–0:05).** Extreme close: black glass, one small clip blinking in it. A thin
fingertip hovers over it. [UI, Raul's Lens, over the glass: `Key: tissue`.]

**2 · THE HUT — handheld · CONTEXT (0:05–1:15, shots 2–4).** Pull back. Firelight. Mara on the pallet, propped against
Ruth, a blanket to her chest. Raul kneels beside them, the slate in his chrome hands: a sheet of city glass, its light
weak, carried up from the Studio core. He sets it in her lap.

> **RAUL:** It's yours.
>
> **MARA:** What is it?
>
> *(He doesn't answer. He has never known.)*

**3 · THE TURN OF THE KEY.** The fingertip comes down. [UI: `Key: tissue` **turns**, once, and is replaced by a small word:
`Re-Cut`.] The clip stops blinking and opens to fill the glass. Ruth's arm tightens around the girl.

**4 · THE CLIP — first person, floor height.** Warm daylight on a wooden floor. A woman's hand, steadying. A **small
hand** grips one of her fingers. The child's other hand drags a worn **toy bear** by one arm. From inside the bear, a
music box: the nursery song, tinny, a little out of tune. [SOUND: Raul goes very still at the first bar.]

> **THE WOMAN** *(V.O., warm, close, the voice under every childhood):* One step at a time.

**5 · THE STEPS · FRICTION (1:15–2:15, shots 5–7).** The small hand lets go of the finger. One step. Two. The bear drags
behind. The woman's hands open, ready to catch, and don't need to. Then the clip simply runs on, as un-edited memory
does, past the moment anyone would have cut: the floor, the window, and, at the end of it, a small chair with the
bear sitting in it, alone. The music box winds down on a held note. The clip ends on the empty chair, like every
window on the Landing (S1E3b).

**6 · RAUL.** Close on Raul's face over the glass. He has heard that line once before, from a bear, at a window that
didn't run. He doesn't look away. He doesn't say anything. [UI, ≤ 2 s at the frame edge, recall: E12's meter, draining,
`Charge: 0`. Gone.]

**7 · MARA.** Close on Mara. She doesn't know why she's crying. Then she does, and it is worse, and she doesn't stop
it. Her hand still on the glass. Ruth doesn't say anything. Ruth just holds her: no meter (E12).

**8 · THE BLOCK THAWS · TURN (2:15–2:30, shots 8–9).** Raul's Lens: the frost-white seam of the sovereign block around
the clip **melts from the inside out**, slowly, with each of her breaths, until nothing holds it at all. The clip lies
in the glass, open, unlocked, still. It doesn't blink.

**9 · FINAL IMAGE.** Raw sight. The slate's light fails. In the dark glass, the girl's wet face and Ruth's, reflected;
beyond them, through the doorway and the fence slats, a man sitting outside on a bedroll, who has watched the light go
out. Hard cut to black.

*(Theme in subtext, not stated: the god built a world so that she would never have to watch forty seconds of a child
learning to walk. Every child on that world was raised by her line and her child's bear, and none of them knew. The
lock answered only to a body that could die, and the first thing that body did with the key was let the grief in.
E12 drained a memory to zero; this one is carried. Raul hears where the bear's voice came from and gets no verdict on
it, only the origin. The clip ends on an empty chair, the way every loop on the Landing ended; this one is the first
that anyone grieved.)*
