# Production Breakdown — S2E06: "The Dream Hunger"

> Detailed production/cinematography breakdown for [[setting-sail-tier-ascent]] **Season 2, Episode 6** (Act II,
> Phase B′, the first of *the gifts that subtract*). The slotted standalone [[the-dream-hunger]] (`Q-11`), recast with
> **Adam** (`CHR-06`) as lead (`Q-76`); the standalone stays a short of its own. **Tier 1**, the city, in the last
> weeks before Tier 2. Adam, a **90%-organic cyborg** whose body has lain in a rented city capsule since E3 (`Q-91`),
> has not slept in ten years of his own time: no nightmares, no lost hours. Then the hunger: the brain-cache
> countdown of [[tether-decay|tether decay]] (`MAG-22`), and the Lens's offer of the one way down,
> `SABBATICAL · 1:1 · LICENSED` (`Q-72`), which he waves off (plant → E17 reminder → E19 payoff). The Purists cite the
> Dream Hunger (`Q-15`). World: [[tether-decay]] · [[velocity-tiers]] · [[the-shadow-entity]] ·
> [[the-memory-substrate]].

## Setup & hook
A clean gold wall at Tier 1, and across it a stain of firelight blooms, soft-edged and warm, with a woman's profile
in it, and is gone like breath off glass. Adam blinks. Nobody else saw it. It was his own brain, six floors down in a
rented capsule, dreaming without him.

## Cinematography — three rooms, one of them asleep
| Element | Adam, Tier 1 (60×) | The bleeds | The capsule bay (1:1) |
|---------|--------------------|------------|------------------------|
| **Lighting** | High-key gold, shadowless (`VIS-16`), as in E3–E4. ## UI behavior (the Perspective Lens)
Adam's Lens. **No glyph** (absent until E9).
- **The care chart (from E3):** `Organic: 90%`; under it a new line, `Wakefulness: 10 yrs`.
- **The TTD meter:** a thin bar at the frame's lower edge, tagged `TTD`, no numbers. Each bleed takes a sliver off it.
  He never looks at it until shot 6; the audience watches it from shot 1.
- **The offer (shot 6):** when the bar dips into amber, one card blooms in the bottom row: `SABBATICAL · 1:1 ·
  LICENSED`. It waits. He waves it off with two fingers; it folds into the frame edge and stays there, small.
- **The village feed:** a small window in the corner, carried from E4: night in the village, two women asleep.
- **Raul's relay (shot 5):** one line of text from Raul at Tier 0, as in E4.
- **The turn is not in the Lens.** The bay is shot in the third person (`WR-L2`: the body has no Lens of its own).

## Philosophical climax
Up here no one sleeps, so no one wakes screaming, and no hour is lost to the dark. That is the gift, and it is real.
The cost is that the brain keeps the old appointment anyway, alone, in a box, and the dreams it has are of the place
he left. The Grid doesn't take his dreams from him. It takes *him* from his dreams. He is offered the way back down,
at 1:1, into a body that still aches; he declines the ache, and with it the only room where he still goes home.

## Script (draft v1)
> 9:16 vertical · ~2.5 min · cinematic photoreal · *Black Mirror / Silo* register. **ADAM** (`CHR-06`), about 19 in
> body. On the feed, **NAOMI** (`CHR-11`) and **RUTH** (`CHR-07`); **RAUL** (`CHR-02`) as a line of text only. No
> spoken dialogue; one line of relayed text.

*Castes (`WR-B1`): Adam is a cyborg, 90% organic, Lens on, body in his rented city capsule at 1:1 (`Q-91`; `WR-B6`),
mind at Tier 1. Naomi and Ruth are Purists, no Lens; every tag is in Adam's Lens, on a feed (`WR-L2`). Raul is a
Ghost in a shell at Tier 0, Lens on, heard only as text. Timing (`WR-T4`): about **nine real weeks** into the
residency (63 days) = **about 3,780 days, roughly ten years and four months, for Adam** (63 × 60), all of it awake.
His body is 19; his mind about 29. Tier 2 is about four real weeks off (about four and a half more years of his,
`Q-48`). This reel covers about **three hours of his = three real minutes**. On the feed one real second is one minute
for him: Naomi's four-second question takes **four minutes**; Raul's text arrives at once. A Sabbatical runs at 1:1:
one real night of eight hours would cost him **480 hours, twenty days**, of the city's time.*

**1 · THE WALL · HOOK (0:00–0:05).** Tier 1, gold, shadowless. ADAM at a long clean desk, mid-task, at ease. Across
the white wall beside him a **stain of firelight** blooms, smoke-soft, a woman's profile at a hearth inside it, and is
gone. [SOUND: one crack of real fire, cut off clean.] He blinks at the wall. [UI: at the lower edge, a thin bar,
`TTD`, loses a sliver.]

**2 · THE GIFT · CONTEXT (0:05–1:15, shots 2–4).** The same room; the light shifts toward evening and never reaches
it. Adam works on. People pass behind him, bright, rested, unhurried. Nobody yawns. Nobody has ever yawned here.
[UI: the care chart at the frame's edge, `Organic: 90%`; under it, `Wakefulness: 10 yrs`.] He reads the line the way
you read a mileage, and is quietly pleased.

**3 · THE FEED.** In the corner of his frame, the village feed, from the lintel post: night at Tier 0, a single banked
fire. NAOMI asleep on the bench by the door under wool; RUTH asleep sitting up against the post beside her, her head
fallen to one side. At his clock they have been asleep for days. They are the only people he has seen sleep in ten
years. He looks at them a long moment, the way you look at animals.

**4 · THE SECOND BLEED.** He turns back to the desk, and a **well-rope** runs through his hands that isn't there:
wet hemp, a bucket rising out of the dark, the creak of the pulley, all of it laid over the clean desk like light
through water. Then gone. His hands are dry. [UI: `TTD` loses another sliver.] He flexes his fingers and goes on.

**5 · THE QUESTION · FRICTION (1:15–2:15, shots 5–7).** On the feed NAOMI has woken. She is speaking toward the fire,
toward someone just out of frame. It takes her **four minutes** at his clock: the slow sub-bass drone of a voice
slowed sixty times, wordless, grief-shaped. Adam lets it run. [UI: one plain line of text arrives.]

> **RAUL** *(text, in Adam's Lens):* She asks if you still dream.

Adam reads it. He doesn't answer. On the feed, Naomi lies back down; it takes a long time.

**6 · THE OFFER.** The bleeds come faster now, overlapping: the **lintel post** with its ten notches, laid across a
colleague's face as she passes; a **child's hand** in a woman's hand; the hearth again, nearer, so warm the gold room
looks cold beside it. [UI: `TTD` slides into amber, and a card blooms in the bottom row: **SABBATICAL · 1:1 ·
LICENSED**.] Adam looks at it properly for the first time. As his eyes rest on it, a hairline of **cold blue** runs
at his collarbone, the implant-light of E1, and under the room tone the old **ache tone** of E1–E2 returns, faint, the
first time since he crossed up. His jaw sets against it from memory.

**7 · THE WAVE.** He lifts two fingers and waves the card off. It folds into the edge of the frame and stays there,
small. The ache tone stops. His face smooths. [UI: `TTD` keeps sliding, slower, into the amber.] He goes back to his
work. Behind him the evening that never comes keeps not coming.

**8 · THE BAY · TURN (2:15–2:30, shots 8–9).** Hard cut, real time, amber service light: the rented capsule, `RENTED`
on the slot's edge, ADAM's body under plastic, Ruth's cord still knotted on the right wrist (E3). Close on the face.
Under the closed lids, the **eyes are moving**, fast, side to side: a sleeper's eyes, deep in a dream. The lips part
on a word with no sound. [SOUND: a sleeper's breathing, close, the only human sound in the reel.]

**9 · FINAL IMAGE.** A tear gathers at the corner of the closed eye and runs, very slowly, to the ear. Upstairs he is
dry-eyed and working; down here, in a box, the body he rents out is dreaming of home, and he isn't in it. Hold. Cut to
black on the breathing.

*(Theme in subtext, never stated: the Grid didn't take his dreams, it took him out of them. The brain still dreams of
the village, alone, and what reaches him upstairs is only leakage, which costs him time. The offer of 1:1 is the way
back into the dream and the ache together, and he chooses the painless room. The viewer finishes the sentence: the
body is still a Purist; it is the man who left.)*
