# Production Breakdown — S3E18: "The Root Key"

> Detailed production/cinematography breakdown for [[setting-sail-the-cartographers-ledger]] **Season 3, Episode
> 18** (Act IV). **Vance forces the question.** The root key (`// M.C.`) is in the girl's living tissue, where no
> rating reaches. Her touch can turn the lock, but only for herself: the seat takes its author back only if the shard
> comes home. So the **re-merge is put to the god, not the girl** (`Q-65`), in Ruth's hut, at night, through **Raul's
> shell** (`Q-101`). **Vance** (`CHR-04`), in a leased shell, makes his case (`Q-103`, `WR-S6`). **Ruth** objects
> (`Q-100`). **Raul** counsels the god to let her stay mortal, and takes his own voice back to do it: wisdom, or his
> own bias? The god declines; the fork stays a **Branch** (`WR-M1`). World: [[the-distributed-president]] ·
> [[the-encrypted-block-paradox]] · [[the-one-in-n-rule]] · [[the-cosmic-clone-paradox]] ·
> [[intention-in-context-law]] · [[the-perspective-lens-ui]].

## Setup & hook
Raul's Lens POV, night, rain on a thatched eave. At the foot of his frame, where his own shell's speaker tag has read
`RAUL` all season, the letters flip: `// M.C.` Something else is wearing him. In the doorway, rain on its shoulders, a
leased shell with a collar tag: `VANCE · 1:10000`.

## Cinematography — one room, two rings
| Element | The hut (Ruth, the sleeping girl, the visitors) | Raul's Lens |
|---------|-------------------------------------------------|-------------|
| **Lighting** | One lamp, low. Caravaggio (`VIS-15`) on Ruth and on the hanging cloth the girl sleeps behind. Vance's shell stays on the threshold, half rain-light, half lamp. | The god-ring cold and white; the mortal ring warm and small. They are the only two colours the Lens adds. |
| **Camera** | Handheld on Ruth; **locked** on Vance (he doesn't move unless he means to); on Raul's shell, locked while the god wears it, **handheld** the instant it's his again. | Locked POV inserts. |
| **Sound** | Rain. The girl's breathing behind the cloth, the reel's clock. Vance's shell makes a faint servo sound when it shifts its weight. Raul's shell, while the god wears it, makes none. | No Lens tones. |

**Rafe** stands against the far wall in his leased shell, the whole reel, and says nothing. He brought this question in
E16. Vance has come to the same answer for a different reason. They don't look at each other.

## UI behavior (the Perspective Lens)
- **The speaker tag (the bracket):** `RAUL` → `// M.C.` at the hook; `// M.C.` → `RAUL` at the turn, and it **stays**.
- **The seat, shown by Vance:** he opens it between them in Raul's Lens, the root chamber's empty seat from E15:
  `Requires 1 : all` over it (`WR-S2`); beside it, his own collar, `1:10000`, short of it. Under the seat, the lock:
  `Key: tissue` (E13's tag) and a second line, `author only`. The key turns for the one it was cut from, and for no
  one else (the major).
- **Two rings (the choice):** high in the frame, the god's cold-complete ring, the President, everywhere; low, over
  the hanging cloth, a warm, finite ring, the girl's. While the god wears the shell, the cold ring **drifts down**
  toward the warm one: the pull home. At Raul's line it **stops**. Then it rises, slowly, back to the top of the frame,
  and the warm ring is left where it was.
- **No prompt.** The `MERGE ⟷ BRANCH` prompt is not drawn here; it belongs to E20.

## Philosophical climax
Vance has lost the seat twice: his forged `1:10000` can't reach `1 : all`, and the only other way in is a lock that
opens for its author alone. So the hunter becomes the advocate. If the key is in a girl who will die by spring, the seat
will be empty for ever; the Law will run with nobody behind it; and he says, credibly, that a world with no author is a
world no one answers for. He is right about something (`WR-S6`). Rafe, the better engineer, wants the same outcome for
the gentlest reason: she wouldn't feel a thing.

The god is in the room, in Raul's shell, listening. Ruth says the only thing the god cannot argue with: an age. Then
Raul does what no one has done all season: he takes his own shell back from the god, mid-breath, to speak. Is that
counsel, or a man pushing God out of his body to win an argument he has been having since S1E16 ("Go home, Rafe")?
The Lens tells us only that nothing writes Raul's squares: the bottom row is empty, as it has been since S1E19. Whatever
he says is his own, unrated, unvalidated, his. The god hears it, and goes, and doesn't come back to his voice.

## Script (draft v1)
> 9:16 vertical · ~2.5 min · cinematic photoreal · *Black Mirror / Silo* register. **RAUL** = the lead (`CHR-02`),
> whose shell the President wears for most of the reel; **THE PRESIDENT** speaks no line; **VANCE** (`CHR-04`) in a
> leased shell; **RUTH** (`CHR-07`); **RAFE** (`Q-22`), silent; **MARA** (`CHR-01`), asleep behind a cloth, unseen but
> for her hand. About 25 spoken words; Vance's case (`Q-103`) and Ruth's objection (`Q-100`) are ruled lines.

*Castes (`WR-B1`): Raul is a Ghost in a shell, 0% organic, Lens on, alignment off (`Q-119`); the President, a Ghost
(`Q-98`), speaks through his shell (`Q-101`) and is otherwise only a ring in his Lens. Vance is a Ghost (`Q-90`) in a
leased shell. Rafe is a Ghost (the E2 reply copy, `Q-54`) in a leased shell. Ruth and Mara are Purists with no Lens
(`WR-L2`); every tag is in Raul's Lens. Adam, a cyborg of mostly regrown flesh, Lens off (`Q-98`), is glimpsed outside
the fence. Timing (`WR-T1`, `WR-T4`): Tier 0, 1×, in the hut. The seat Vance shows is read at 1:1, as the oldest strata
only play (E2, `Q-117`), so a Ghost gains nothing by speed there; no one runs at 60×, 3,600× or 86,400× in this reel.
One night, about T+38.2; Mara about 17, `HORIZON` (E16) at about 110 days, unshown; Ruth about 38.*

**1 · RAUL'S LENS (9:16) · HOOK (0:00–0:05).** Rain on the eave. At the foot of the frame the speaker tag flips, `RAUL`
→ `// M.C.` In the doorway, rain on its shoulders: a leased shell. [UI: `VANCE · 1:10000`.]

**2 · THE HUT — handheld on Ruth · CONTEXT (0:05–1:15, shots 2–4).** Lamplight. RUTH on a stool by the hanging cloth. A
girl's breathing behind it, uneven. Against the far wall, RAFE, in his shell, arms folded, eyes down. Through the door
behind Vance, past the fence, a rolled bedroll on wet grass, a man sitting on it in the rain (E17). Vance doesn't come
in. He looks past Ruth to Raul's shell, and he knows whom he's looking at.

**3 · THE SEAT.** Vance lifts one hand and opens the seat between them. [UI, Raul's Lens: the empty seat from E15;
`Requires 1 : all`; beside it his collar, `1:10000`, short. Under the seat, the lock: `Key: tissue` · `author only`.]
He lets it hang there long enough to be read. Then he closes his hand, and it's gone.

**4 · THE RINGS.** [UI: high in the frame, the god's cold-complete ring; low, over the hanging cloth, a warm, finite
ring.] Very slowly the cold ring begins to drift down toward the warm one.

**5 · THE CASE · FRICTION (1:15–2:15, shots 5–7).** Locked on Vance.

> **VANCE:** A world with no author is a world no one answers for.

Rafe looks up, at that. He doesn't speak. The cold ring drifts lower.

**6 · THE OBJECTION.** Ruth stands, between the doorway and the cloth. She speaks to Raul's shell, to the thing wearing
it, not to Vance.

> **RUTH:** She's seventeen.

Behind the cloth, the breathing catches, and goes on. Under its edge, a hand, thin, unmarked, curled on the blanket: the
key, asleep.

**7 · THE VOICE BACK.** Close on Raul's shell, locked, perfectly still, the god's stillness. Then a tremor at the jaw.
[UI: the tag flickers, `// M.C.` → `RAUL`.] The camera on him starts to breathe, handheld. It is his voice, rougher
than the god's silence.

> **RAUL:** Let her keep it.

[UI: the cold ring **stops**, a hand's breadth above the warm one. Raul's own bottom squares, at the foot of the frame:
empty. Nothing wrote that line but him.]

**8 · THE GOD DECLINES · TURN (2:15–2:30, shots 8–9).** Hold. The cold ring hangs. Then it rises, slowly, back to the top
of the frame, and stays there, distant, complete. The warm ring stays where it was, over the cloth. [UI: the speaker
tag reads `RAUL`. It stays.] Vance looks at Raul a beat longer than he needs to, then turns and goes out into the rain,
past the man on the bedroll, without a word.

**9 · FINAL IMAGE.** Raw sight: the girl's hand under the edge of the cloth. Ruth's hand comes down over it. Hard cut to
black.

*(Theme in subtext, not stated: three people in the room want the god to come home, for an argument, for a seat, for
mercy; one woman says an age; one man takes his own mouth back from God to say four words. The Lens can show that no
system wrote them; it cannot show whether he was right. The god leaves his voice and doesn't return to it, and the fork
she made of herself stays a person. The key goes back to sleep in a girl's hand, where nothing can turn it but her.)*
