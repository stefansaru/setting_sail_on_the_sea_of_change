# Production Breakdown — S3E07: "The Vessel"

> Detailed production/cinematography breakdown for [[setting-sail-the-cartographers-ledger]] **Season 3, Episode 7**
> (Act II). **The Purist Village (`PLC-06`), Tier 0, raw [[chromatic-expressionism|Caravaggio]] firelight, no Lens.**
> **Ruth** (`CHR-07`), about 20, is asked to carry the impossible child. *Why a Purist?* Only the unrushed can carry:
> the S2 vault's **"Hurried"** pays here, from Naomi's S2E5 line ([[the-quickening]], `WR-B2`). *Why her?* Because she
> is the one who stays. The Book-of-Ruth turn. Mara is a **true clone** of the god; Ruth only carries (`Q-105`). World:
> [[the-memory-substrate]] · [[capsule-birth]] · [[the-perspective-lens-ui]].

## Setup & hook
Naomi's house, a year and more after her death. Ruth lives in it now. Over the door, the lintel: eleven notches, and
clean wood after them that nobody has cut. Raul's chrome hand sets something on the table between them: a small
vial, frosted.

## Cinematography — no Lens in the room
| Element | Ruth and the village | Raul (Ghost in a shell) |
|---------|----------------------|-------------------------|
| **Lighting** | One oil lamp, then the fire: single-source Caravaggio (`VIS-15`) on skin, wood, wool. | The lamp catches his chrome but gives it no warmth; his optics carry a faint cold point of their own. |
| **Camera** | Handheld, breathing, close; Ruth's eyeline sets the frame. **No UI on her side of any cut** (`WR-L2`). | The only UI in the reel lives in **Raul's** Lens, and only in his POV shots, which are rare and locked off. |
| **Sound** | Fire, wind, a hen, the village settling for the night; Ruth's breathing. | His servos, very quiet. |

## UI behavior (the Perspective Lens)
Ruth has no Lens (`WR-L2`); Mara never will (`Q-101`). The reel is UI-free until its last shot.
- **Raul's POV, once (shot 10):** over Ruth, one line his Lens can't finish: `Carrying: 1 · Caste: —`. The Lens has
  no caste for a god in Purist flesh. *(It fills `Purist` at E10.)*
- Nothing else: no scan, no decay tag, no squares. Raul doesn't scan her.

## Philosophical climax
The Grid can make anything except a child. Its bodies are rushed, and rushed bodies are barren; the god who built the
Grid has to come to the people who refused it and ask. Ruth answers the first question herself, in a dead woman's
words, the line Naomi spoke to her son at the threshold before he climbed (S2E5): *The Hurried don't have children.
We do.* The second question, *why me*, nobody answers aloud. The camera answers it: the lintel she still keeps, the
bed she nursed a dying woman in, the house she came back to. In the Book of Ruth the young widow says *where you go I
will go* to a woman who calls herself Mara, "bitter." Here she says nothing. She holds out her hand.

## Script (draft v1)
> 9:16 vertical · ~2.5 min · cinematic photoreal · *Black Mirror / Silo* register. **RUTH** (`CHR-07`), about 20, a
> Purist; **RAUL** (`CHR-02`), a Ghost in a shell; **ADAM** (`CHR-06`), seen, silent; **VILLAGERS**. About twenty
> spoken words.

*Castes (`WR-B1`): Ruth is a Purist, no Lens; Adam is a cyborg of mostly regrown flesh, Lens off (`Q-98`); the
villagers are Purists; Raul is a Ghost in a shell, Lens on (`Q-119`). Mara: the President is a Ghost (`Q-98`); the
embryo in the vial has no caste, and the Lens has none to give it. Timing (`WR-T4`): Tier 0 throughout, one clock. The
ask is at about T+20.75, some weeks after E6's seal; the last shot is about two weeks later, when Ruth comes back over
the ridge. Ruth and Adam are about 20. Nine months from the transfer puts the birth at about T+21.55 (E10). The
transfer is done offscreen at the city-edge clinic of S2E2 (`Q-131` (7)); carrying another's child is not a breach of
"One life, kept whole": nothing in Ruth's body is replaced.*

**1 · THE VIAL · HOOK (0:00–0:05).** Lamplight. A chrome hand sets a small frosted vial on a scrubbed wooden table and
takes the hand away. [SOUND: the click of glass on wood; fire.] No UI.

**2 · NAOMI'S HOUSE · CONTEXT (0:05–1:15, shots 2–4).** Wider: RUTH across the table, sleeves rolled, flour on one wrist;
RAUL opposite, chrome under the borrowed coat. Behind her, the bed where Naomi died, made and empty. The camera
finds the door: the **lintel**, eleven notches, and a hand's width of clean wood after the last.

**3 · THE ASK.** Ruth looks at the vial, not at him.

> **RAUL:** She wants to be born. Here.
>
> **RUTH:** Who does?

Raul's eyes go past her, out of the open door, over the dark ridge, to the **cold tower light** blinking above the
city (S2E1). Ruth follows his look. She understands. She laughs once, without humour.

**4 · THE VILLAGE, THROUGH THE DOOR.** The fire outside; villagers at the long table; ADAM at its far end, the chrome
seam at his ribs catching the light as he reaches for bread. Close by the door, an old woman glances in at the chrome
man in Ruth's house, and away.

**5 · WHY A PURIST · FRICTION (1:15–2:15, shots 5–8).** Back on Ruth.

> **RUTH:** Why not one of yours?

Raul doesn't answer. He doesn't need to.

**6 · RECALL CUE (≤ 2 s).** A lossy flash, firelight smeared: Naomi at the lintel in S2E5, facing the camera post, her
mouth shaping words the sound drops. Gone.

**7 · RUTH.** She answers herself, quietly, in the dead woman's cadence.

> **RUTH:** The Hurried don't have children. *(beat)* We do.

**8 · WHY HER.** She doesn't ask it aloud. Her eyes go round the room the way you'd count: the made bed, the shawl on
its peg, the lintel. Raul's eyes go to the same places and stop on the notches. Neither says it. Through the door,
Adam has looked up from the table. He watches her, and he doesn't come in.

**9 · THE HAND · TURN (2:15–2:30, shots 9–10).** Ruth holds out her hand, palm up, across the table. Not for the vial:
for his hand. A beat. Raul puts the chrome hand in hers. She closes her fingers over it, then picks up the vial with
the other. *(The Book-of-Ruth turn: no vow said aloud.)*

**10 · FINAL IMAGE — two weeks later, dawn.** The ridge, grey light. Ruth comes back over it alone from the direction of
the city airlock, walking slowly, one hand flat below her navel. Raul waits at the fence. As she passes him into the
village, his POV, locked off: [UI, Raul's Lens, over her: `Carrying: 1 · Caste: —`.] She walks on into the warm frame
and out of the Lens; the line hangs a moment over the empty path, unfinished. Hard cut to black.

*(Theme in subtext, never stated: the Grid's god needs the one thing the Grid can't make, and the people it calls
Glacial are the only ones who can still give it. Ruth's yes is a dead woman's sentence finished by the woman who
stayed with her. Raul never tells her why her; the room does. The empty caste field is the world's machinery failing
to classify a god in a womb.)*
