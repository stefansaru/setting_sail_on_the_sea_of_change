# Production Breakdown — S2E01: "The Marked Boy"

> Detailed production/cinematography breakdown for [[setting-sail-tier-ascent]] **Season 2, Episode 1**
> (Act I, Phase A′: leaving the harbor of the flesh). **Tier 0, the Purist Village** (`PLC-06`). The protagonist,
> **Adam** (`CHR-06`), is a **90%-organic cyborg** ([[progressive-digitalization]]), a Purist by birth and faction
> whose chest carries the chrome the KAVANA vial made in S1E20 (`Q-119`). **No Lens** (`WR-L2`): the whole reel is
> raw, un-mediated sight, the inverse of [[setting-sail-tier-descent|S1E1]]'s pristine Tier 3. Pays a-ha #7 (the
> withheld choice, seen once, lossy, `WR-R4`); plants a-ha #3 (the lintel notches). World: [[the-memory-substrate]] ·
> [[chromatic-expressionism|Caravaggio]] · [[the-quickening]].

## Setup & hook
Open inside a memory that a fevered eight-year-old kept badly: firelight smeared to streaks, a woman's face too
close, words that don't arrive, a chrome hand closing over hers, a glint of glass. It is S1E20 from the bed, and the
choice itself is the part the memory lost. Snap awake: the boy is nineteen, and the hand he presses to his ribs
still hurts.

## Cinematography — one world, one crack
The reel is shot by a single rule: everything is Caravaggio except one thing. The cold is rationed to the seam in
Adam's chest and one light on the horizon.

| Element | The village (warm) | The seam (cold) |
|---------|--------------------|-----------------|
| **Lighting** | Single-source tenebrism ([[lighting]] §4): dawn through smoke, cookfire, oil lamp. Deep warm shadow on skin, wood, wool. | Clinical **cold blue** leaking through linen from the chrome along his ribs. The only blue in the reel, besides the far tower light it pulses with. |
| **Camera** | **Handheld, breathing**, close to faces and hands; never a gimbal. | When the seam flares, the camera **locks off**: one machine-still frame inside a breathing film. |
| **Sound** | Wind, buckets, chickens, real laughter, fire. Under everything, from the first shot, a faint low **ache tone**: Adam's pain, scored. | A high, thin digital **tick**, the same interval as the tower light's blink. |
| **Memory (shot 1)** | Photoreal lossy recall (`VIS-13`): smeared shutter, crushed blacks, a dropped audio track, focus that never lands. Not an illustration; a damaged recording. | The chrome hand is the only sharp edge in it. |

## UI behavior (the Perspective Lens)
**None.** Adam has no Lens; Naomi and Ruth are Purists with none (`WR-L2`). Raul's Lens is on (since S1E20,
`Q-119`), but the reel never cuts to his POV, so not one circle or square reaches the frame. The absence *is* the
UI note: this is the last reel in which Adam sees anything un-tagged. The glyph is **absent** (`Q-12` mirror).

## Philosophical climax
Adam has everything Raul spent Season 1 climbing down to reach: a body that aches, a mother who counts years, a
girl who ties a cord on his wrist, bread passed by hand. He also has ten percent of the thing his people refused,
and it has started calling home. The reel's argument is in the last image: the cold light of the Grid falling
across the ten notches his mother cut, and across the space left for one more.

## Script (draft v1)
> 9:16 vertical · ~2.5 min · cinematic photoreal · *Black Mirror / Silo* register. **ADAM** (`CHR-06`), about 19,
> the protagonist; **NAOMI** (`CHR-11`), his mother; **RUTH** (`CHR-07`), his betrothed; **RAUL** (`CHR-02`), a
> [[the-ghost-entity|Ghost]] in a [[shells-with-ghosts-within|shell]]; **VILLAGERS**. Dialogue is near-absent; the
> reel is carried by hands, light and one sound.

*Castes (`WR-B1`): Adam is a cyborg, 90% organic, Purist-born, no Lens; Naomi, Ruth and the villagers are Purists,
with no Lens; Raul is a Ghost in a shell, Lens on, never in POV. Timing (`WR-T4`): Tier 0 throughout, one clock. On
the series clock this is about **T+19.0**: about 10.5 real years since S1E19–20 (T+8.5), so the lintel holds **ten**
notches; Adam is about 19 (about 8 at S1E18); Naomi's death (S2E20) is about six real months away (`Q-48`).*

**1 · COLD OPEN — THE LOSSY MEMORY · HOOK (0:00–0:05).** Black, then firelight smeared into streaks. A woman's face
(NAOMI, ten years younger) far too close, out of focus, her mouth shaping words the audio drops. One fragment
surfaces, warped, half a sentence: *"…still be him…"* A chrome hand closes over hers, the only sharp thing in frame.
A glint of glass, a smear across it where a label would be. A child's ragged cough. The shot breaks before anything
is chosen. [SOUND: the cough, then the ache tone, first heard here.] *(The S1E20 choice is never shown clean;
`WR-R4`. No vial is administered on screen.)*

**2 · DAWN, THE HOUSE · CONTEXT (0:05–1:15, shots 2–4).** ADAM jolts awake on a straw pallet, nineteen, hand already
flat on his left ribs. Breathes through it. As he pulls his shirt on, the camera catches what the shirt covers: a
chrome seam running from under his arm across three ribs, flesh healed around it like bark around wire. [SOUND: the
ache tone, steady.] He ducks out under the doorframe, and the camera stays a beat on the **lintel**: ten notches cut
into the wood, evenly spaced, and a hand's width of clean grain after the last.

**3 · THE WELL — handheld, Caravaggio.** Morning smoke, chickens, a long table being set. Adam carries a water yoke,
two full buckets; the weight bends him, and he keeps going. NAOMI, grey at the temples, passes under the lintel and,
without looking, runs her thumb along the notches the way some people touch a doorpost. Adam sees her do it. She
doesn't see him see. At the well, RUTH is waiting, as if she has been for a while. She takes one bucket off his yoke
without asking, and they walk back at her pace, not his.

**4 · THE EDGE OF THE FIRELIGHT.** RAUL sits on a stump at the village edge, chrome under a borrowed coat, still as
furniture. Children no longer stare at him. They stare, briefly, at Adam's side, where his shirt has pulled.

**5 · THE EVENING MEAL · FRICTION (1:15–2:15, shots 5–7).** The long table, fire, wrinkled hands. Heads bow for a
grace; their lips move together and the words are lost under the fire and the wind. *(Writers' room: the grace is
the vow, "One life, kept whole" (`Q-74`); its words first reach the screen in S2E5.)* Adam's lips move with the
others, a half-beat behind. Bread goes hand to hand down the table. When it reaches the old man beside Adam, he sets
the loaf on the board in front of Adam instead of into his hand. Nobody remarks it. Adam takes it.

**6 · INSERT — the cord.** After the meal, in the half-dark by the fire, RUTH loops a plain woven cord twice around
Adam's right wrist and knots it. A few of the women see and look away, smiling. Naomi sees, and doesn't smile, and
doesn't look away either. Ruth keeps her hand on the knot one beat longer than the knot needs.

**7 · THE FIELD AT NIGHT — wide, negative space.** Adam alone at the field's edge, the village glow behind him. Far
off on the black horizon, a single **cold tower light** blinks, slow and regular: the city. He watches it the way
you watch weather. RAUL is beside him; we didn't see him arrive.

> **RAUL** *(quiet; the S1E18 question, handed back)*: Are you lost?

Adam doesn't answer. [SOUND: very faint, under the wind, a high **tick** in time with the tower's blink.]

**8 · THE LINTEL · TURN (2:15–2:30, shots 8–9).** Adam comes home in the dark and stops under the doorframe. His
breath catches. Through the linen over his ribs, the seam **cracks cold blue**, a hard pulse, then another, in
time with the far tower. [Camera: locks off, machine-still, for the first time in the reel.] The blue strobes up
across the lintel, across the ten notches, across the clean wood after them. [SOUND: the ache tone swells and the
tick rides on top of it.]

**9 · FINAL IMAGE.** NAOMI is in the doorway, woken, her face lit blue from below, too close, exactly the framing of
the memory in shot 1, sharp this time. She doesn't reach for the seam. She puts her hand over his on the lintel.
Hold on the notches flickering cold. Hard cut to black on the tick.

*(Theme in subtext, never stated: the village is everything the Grid can't price, and the boy at its center is
already ten percent the Grid's. The cold light measures her years before anyone says what the notches count. The
memory is lossy because it lives in a brain (`WR-B4`); the next time Adam remembers anything, it won't be.)*
