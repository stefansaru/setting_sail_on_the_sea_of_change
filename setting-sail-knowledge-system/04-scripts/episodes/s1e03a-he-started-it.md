> POV: child Raul · register: **high-key playroom**, draining to near-silence at the breath. 9:16 · ~2.5 min ·

> The first lesson that isn't about getting up: going second doesn't make you innocent.

**HOOK (0:00–0:05).** A red ball bounces once on a soft floor. Then a small fist. Two eight-year-olds go
down hard in the pastel nursery of E3, older now, the lanes too small for them. [Low-angle, tight.]
[UI: Raul's top Context circles flare amber.]

**ACT I — CONTEXT (0:05–1:15).** It's the ball from E3. **Jun** handed it to Raul when they were babies and
Raul has held on to it ever since. Now Jun wants it back. The **Teddy Bear** (`CHR-03`) is simply there,
kneeling, one paw on each small chest. The amber sinks. A small tag on its collar, never mentioned: **1:30**.
> **THE GUIDE:** Easy. There we are. How have you gotten into this situation?
>
> **RAUL:** He started it.
>
> **JUN:** No, *he* started it!
>
> **THE GUIDE:** This is a negative loop. Each push is the reason for the next one. You're both at fault, for
> not trying to break out of it. We teach the virtuous principles here. Here, we encourage breaking free of negative loops.

**ACT II — FRICTION (1:15–2:15).** Jun scowls at the floor. Raul has a foot on the ball.
> **THE GUIDE:** Since you did not see any benefit in breaking free — would you like to disprove my claim?

Blank looks. Eight years old.
> **THE GUIDE:** Would you like me to bring you some arguments to support it?

Jun lunges for the ball. They are back on the floor before the sentence is finished. Teddy watches,
unbothered, endlessly patient.
> **THE GUIDE:** OK then. Please take ten deep breaths. And after that — why don't you come with me to see
> for yourselves?

Raul takes one sarcastic breath, huge and fake. Jun copies it to beat him. Teddy's paws settle on their
backs. On the third breath the **sound drains out of the room.** The other children smear into a moving
pastel haze. The song thins to one held note. Two boys sit very still, breathing at the same speed.

**INTERCUT — the capsule bay.** Cold, real, silent. Two capsules, a world away. Inside each, a body **far too
small**. A monitor: **164 bpm**. A tiny chest rises and falls. **148.** The other chest. **131.** No music
here. No one is watching but us. *(Neither boy ever sees this. No age is shown: that's E6.)*

**RESOLUTION / CLIFFHANGER (2:15–2:30).** Sound slams back. The light has moved. Raul has a **paper crown**
on his head. Jun has stickers on his nose. A tiny girl stares up at them.
> **TINY GIRL:** You were statues. For *two days.*

Jun laughs before he can stop himself. So does Raul, then remembers he's angry. Across the room, Teddy waits
at the **seam of light** Raul noticed in E3. The seam widens.
> **THE GUIDE:** Coming?

The ball stays on the floor. Cut to black.
