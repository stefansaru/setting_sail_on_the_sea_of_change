# Production Breakdown — S3E02: "The Sea Beneath"

> Detailed production/cinematography breakdown for [[setting-sail-the-cartographers-ledger]] **Season 3, Episode 2**
> (Act I, *The God's Hole*). **The Synchronization Node, then down under the Grid**, Tier 0, **T ≈ +20.5**. E1's clause
> came from somewhere older than the tiers, and old layers play back only at 1:1. Raul needs an engineer, and the only
> mind he trusts is his own: he opens the file he never opens. **Rafe** (`Q-14`, `Q-54`), the E16 reply copy, paused
> twelve years, wakes **mid-goodbye** (S2 vault spent). They go down together, and under the warm Doughnut world a grey
> one surfaces, with an old logo and one legible word: **SEA** (`WLD-01`, a wonder beat, unexplained). The turn is
> Rafe's: his squares relight as S1E15 left them, and the last one greys, `Recipient: 4.4 ly` (a-ha #6 reminder).
> **Mara: 0% organic** (the President, a Ghost, `Q-98`), unseen. World: [[the-cosmic-clone-paradox]] ·
> [[the-shadow-grid]] · [[the-perspective-lens-ui]] · [[the-doughnut-principle]].

## Setup & hook
S1E15's first frame, again: a Raul opens his eyes, gasping, and for a beat doesn't know which Raul he is. The gasp is
the render's reflex; there is no chest. He is in the same featureless room where he was last told to go home, facing
the same man. The man's ring has changed shape.

## Cinematography — warm world, grey floor
| Element | The Node and the warm Grid | The grey under it |
|---------|---------------------------|-------------------|
| **Lighting** | The Node: S1E16's neutral void. The descent: the Grid's warm, glazed **feel-good gold** (`VIS-09`) thinning as they go down, like paint worn through. | **Desaturated sea-grey and blue** with clean white metal: the legacy HELM palette (`VIS-01`), here a **fossil layer**, lit by its own dead interfaces (`VIS-02`). No gold anywhere. |
| **Camera** | Locked on the two faces in the Node, framed as S1E16's reflections; then a slow vertical **drop** through the layers (`VIS-04`, machine-smooth). | Still and wide, heavy **negative space** ([[negative-space]]): two small figures on a grey floor. |
| **Sound** | The Node's dead room tone; S1E16's selection-matrix tick, once. Going down, the Grid's choral hum thins. | Water against something far off. Wind with nothing to blow through. No hum at all. |

**The wonder beat (`CINE-05`, `VIS-06`):** the gold underfoot goes thin as old varnish, and through it, like a seabed
through clearing water, the grey world surfaces: corridors, water-stained white walls, a sail motif repeated on every
surface. One old logo, a **sail on grey water**, fills a wall. Under it a line of text, worn past reading, except one
word: **SEA**.

## UI behavior (the Perspective Lens)
Two Lenses: **Raul's** (shots 1, 3–6) and **Rafe's** (the turn). No Purists in this reel.
- **The file opens (hook):** `REPLY COPY · PAUSED · 12 yrs` → `RESUMING`.
- **Two glyphs (S1E16 recalled):** in Rafe's waking view, Raul's ring is **solid** now (S1E20), no longer the fractured
  ring he last saw steadying; his own is S1E15's different shape. Time has passed in one of them only.
- **E1's clause**, carried: `// M.C. → Tier 0 · sector: unmeasured · PENDING`, `derived from: —`. Raul hands it across;
  under Rafe's fingers its trace opens downward, a thread with no bottom in sight.
- **The descent:** no readout but depth. The ribbon of the law-feed passes overhead and is gone.
- **The turn, Rafe's Lens (a-ha #6 reminder):** his bottom **Intention squares**, dark since the pause, relight one by
  one exactly as S1E15 left them: *stay* · *build* · *love*. Then *love* **greys**, and a small field resolves under it:
  `Recipient: 4.4 ly`. Nothing else in the frame moves.

## Philosophical climax
Rafe was the one who got to go. "Go home, Rafe" was the gift S1E16 ended on: a copy allowed to be a person, sent back to
the life he made. Waking here, he learns the gift had a second half. A long beam forks; one of him went home to her,
and this one was kept, paused, in the room where he was let go. Raul kept him for twelve years and never woke him, and
now wakes him because he needs him. The reel never says which of those is the kindness. Underneath the world they find
its oldest layer, a grey sea with one word left on it, and Rafe, standing on it, finds the one fact he can't engineer:
the person his squares point at is 4.4 light-years away, with him.

## Script (draft v1)
> 9:16 vertical · ~2.5 min · cinematic photoreal · *Black Mirror / Silo* register. **RAUL** = the lead (`CHR-02`);
> **RAFE** (`Q-14`, `Q-22`) = Raul's Branch, this instance the S1E16 reply copy (`Q-54`). About 25 spoken words.

*Castes (`WR-B1`): Raul is a Ghost in a shell; his shell sits idle on its stump in the village at Tier 0 while his mind
dives (as in S2E18). Rafe is a Ghost: bodiless by beam (`WR-B7`), with no shell yet. The President (`Q-98`, a Ghost) is
present only as E1's clause. Clocks (`WR-T1`, `WR-T4`): everyone runs at **Tier 0**; the old layer was written before
the tiers and plays back only at 1:1, so real and subjective time are one clock for both divers. The reel cuts across
about three real days of reading. Rafe's pause: from S1E16 (T ≈ +8.2) to now (T ≈ +20.5), about 12.3 real years and no
subjective time; for him, "Go home, Rafe" was a second ago. Distance (`WR-T3`): `Recipient: 4.4 ly` is a Lens field,
not a link; nothing crosses it in this reel. Bible only: his other instance landed at the colony about T+12.6 and
merged there (`Q-54`); never stated.*

**1 · RAUL'S LENS — the file · HOOK (0:00–0:05).** Locked. `REPLY COPY · PAUSED · 12 yrs`. Raul's finger, after twelve
years, touches it. [UI: `RESUMING`.] Cut: a Raul opens his eyes, gasping, the render's reflex.

**2 · THE NODE — symmetrical two-shot · CONTEXT (0:05–1:15, shots 2–4).** S1E16's staging room. RAFE, star-worn, faces
RAUL, exactly where they stood. He is still mid-goodbye; he looks around for the beam that was supposed to take him.
His eyes find Raul's ring: **solid**. [UI, in Rafe's view: two glyphs, his and a solid one.] It was fractured a second
ago.

> **RAFE:** You said go home.
>
> *(Raul doesn't answer. He doesn't look away, either.)*

**3 · THE CLAUSE.** Raul opens his hand. E1's clause turns between them, grey. [UI: `// M.C. → Tier 0 · sector:
unmeasured · PENDING`, `derived from: —`.] Rafe reads it the way an engineer reads a fault: fingers in it at once. Its
trace opens under his hands, a thread going straight down, no bottom.

> **RAFE:** That's older than the tiers.
>
> **RAUL:** Then it plays at one to one.

Rafe looks at him: *you woke me for this.* Raul holds the look. Rafe goes back to the thread.

**4 · THE DROP.** A slow vertical fall through the Grid: gold layers, glazed and warm, one under the next, each thinner.
Rafe works as they sink, opening old formats like hatches; Raul follows. The ribbon of the law-feed passes overhead and
is gone. [SOUND: the choral hum thinning to nothing.]

**5 · THE GREY · FRICTION (1:15–2:15, shots 5–7).** The gold underfoot goes thin as old varnish. Through it, like a
seabed through clearing water: **grey**. Corridors of white metal, water-stained. A small sail painted on every door,
every rail. The two of them stand on it, small, in all that negative space. [SOUND: water against something far off.]

**6 · WONDER BEAT — the logo.** A wall-sized logo: a **sail on grey water**. Beneath it, a line of text worn past reading,
except one word, clean: **SEA**. Raul reads it. Nothing explains it. Rafe doesn't look up from the thread: it runs on,
down, under the floor.

**7 · RAFE, WORKING.** Close. He has found something he can fix; it calms him. His hands steady. [UI, Rafe's Lens, for
the first time: the bottom squares, dark until now, begin to relight.] *stay*. Then *build*.

**8 · THE SQUARE · TURN (2:15–2:30, shots 8–9).** Rafe's Lens, POV, the SEA logo filling the frame behind the squares.
*love* lights, and then, quietly, **greys**. Under it a field resolves: `Recipient: 4.4 ly`. His hands stop.

**9 · FINAL IMAGE.** Two-shot, wide, the grey sea-wall over them. Rafe doesn't ask. Raul doesn't tell. They stand on the
oldest floor in the world, one of them having just learned that the other half of his life went home without him.
Hard cut to black on the grey square.

*(Theme in subtext, not stated: "go home" had two halves, and Rafe is the half that stayed. Raul woke him because he
needed him, and the reel lets that be both a trust and a theft. They go looking for an author and find, under
everything, the one word the series has been sailing toward: the sea, the part you can't control. Rafe's last square
is the sea for him: real, read-only, 4.4 light-years wide.)*
