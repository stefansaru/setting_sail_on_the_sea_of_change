# Script — "The First Supper"

> A **standalone culture reel** (Act-III / [[setting-sail-tier-descent]] or S2 candidate) dramatizing
> [[flavor-scripts|digital gastronomy]] vs the physical [[the-post-labor-economy|Hospitality Economy]].
> POV: **The Patron** (a high-tier elite on a [[recreation-culture|Glacial Vacation]], unnamed) ·
> register: morphing **Caravaggio gold** (scripted) → warm, imperfect **Caravaggio** (real). 9:16 · ~2.5 min.
>
> *Castes (`Q-87`): the Patron is a Shadow, here in their own body on a licensed Sabbatical (`Q-72`, the one
> exception to `WR-B6`); the chef, and the attendant who supervises the Sabbatical, are cyborgs at Tier 0, in the flesh.*

## Setup & hook (0:00–0:05)
The Patron executes a **`Vintage Cabernet`** [[flavor-scripts|Flavor Script]] — no glass, no mouth, just
a recursive packet routed to the receptors. On cue the whole virtual room **morphs** into a rich, warm
[[chromatic-expressionism|Caravaggio gold]] to match the chemical weight of the taste. Perfect.
Repeatable. **Logged in eidetic 4K** ([[the-memory-substrate]]). The Patron has "eaten" ten thousand
flawless meals and remembers every one.

## Friction (0:05–1:15)
[[tether-decay|TTD]] forces a mandatory [[recreation-culture|Sub-Velocity Sabbatical]]: antenna off, down
to [[velocity-tiers|1:1]], boots on un-optimized dirt at a surface resort. [UI, the last tag before the antenna
drops: *SABBATICAL · LICENSED · SUPERVISED.*] An attendant at the door logs the Patron in. A **human chef** — a displaced
real-time worker of the [[the-post-labor-economy|Hospitality Baseline]] — sets down a plate of *actual
food.* It's **asymmetrical.** Steam. An imperfect, slightly bruised tomato. The Patron's Lens reflexively
tries to **parse and log it** — and *can't.* No script. No data. The taste arrives blurry, warm, already
half-forgetting itself.
> **THE PATRON (whisper, unsettled):** It's not... rendering. Where do I *save* this?

## Climax (1:15–2:15)
The first real bite lands wrong and then *right* — salt, char, a sourness no algorithm would dare ship.
A reflexive shudder of disgust (the high-tier [[cultural-taboos|Organic Leakage]] taboo, turned inward) —
then it breaks into something the Patron has no file for: **wonder.** Hoarse, the Patron reaches for the
only verb the tiers ever taught: *"Run it again."* The chef glances at the plate: *"Only had the one
tomato."* The Caravaggio gold here isn't a flavor-mirror the system painted — it's
candlelight the Patron can't control, flickering on a meal that **refuses to be recorded.**

## Ending cut (2:15–2:30)
The Patron sets down the fork, understanding the bargain at last: this supper will **rot into the blurry
warmth of a mortal cache** and be gone — and *that* is what makes it real. A held breath. Outside the
window, real weather, unscriptable. Fade on a face tasting impermanence for the first time.
