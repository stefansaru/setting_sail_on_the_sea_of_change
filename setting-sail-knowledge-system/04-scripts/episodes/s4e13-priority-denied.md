# Production Breakdown — S4E13: "Priority Denied"

> Production and cinematography breakdown for [[setting-sail-the-seed-ledger]] **Season 4, Episode 13** (Act III,
> Phase C). **The Data Buffer** ([[inter-tier-travel]]), then bay C. Kai tries to **climb**, more time per second, and
> is refused: access to a faster clock is gated by priority, and aboard by the body (`WR-B5`, `Q-46`), and the High
> Clock is rationing the reactor's last watts for the beam queue ([[the-stasis-economy]]: no limitless top; speed is
> something that can be withheld). The pyramid, in the dark, shows its shape. Then Kai starts the only other road:
> **field severance**. **Body: first severance, 100% → 70%** ([[progressive-digitalization]], `WR-B3`). Glyph
> **cracked**.

## Setup & hook
Kai stands at the mouth of the Data Buffer, a corridor where light runs at different speeds: near the threshold it
crawls, and deeper in it streaks. Kai's feet are on the line. Nothing pulls them forward. In the Lens, the request
from E12, sixteen hours old, still reads `PENDING`. The count reads `31:06:44`.

## Cinematography — the shape of the pyramid
| Element | The Buffer (the climb refused) | Bay C (the cut) |
|---------|--------------------------------|-----------------|
| **Lighting** | Streaked white running away from the camera into a bright vanishing point. Kai is lit only from behind, by the slow end. | Caravaggio: one work-lamp clamped to capsule 10, everything else dark. The lines inside the head panel glow faintly, like filaments. |
| **Camera** | Symmetrical, centred, Kai small at the bottom of the frame and the corridor rising to the top: the 9:16 frame as the pyramid. | Macro: the tool, the glass, a single hair-fine line. The steadiest shots in the season, because Kai's hands have to be. |
| **Sound** | A rising tone from deep in the Buffer that never reaches Kai. | Very quiet. The pump. Then a tiny *tick* when the line is cut, like a fingernail on glass. |

## UI behavior (the Perspective Lens)
Kai's Lens only (`WR-L2`).
- **The request:** `UP-CLOCK · TIER 2 · 1 h 37 m · WATTS + CHEMISTRY · PENDING`.
- **The vertical ruler** (E3), the pyramid as a Lens: Kai's dot pinned to its base. Above, dots rise steadily from
  the base toward the apex. They are Ghosts in the beam queue going up to pack, and each one winks out at the top.
- **The answer:** a status light blinks **once**. The request line resolves `DENIED · REACTOR RATIONED · BEAM QUEUE
  FIRST`. Under it, smaller, softer type: `We're sorry.` Then a single queue line: `BEAM QUEUE · 40 MINDS ·
  31 h`.
- **The cut:** on capsule 10, `STACK DRAW ▲ · CHEMISTRY ▼` as the function hands over. Then the readout,
  clean: `Organic: 70%`. At the frame's edge, the thin **tether line** loses a third of its glow.

## Philosophical climax
The refusal is correct. Forty minds against one, a reactor bleeding coolant, and a Tier-2 slot for one Shadow
would cost the watts the queue needs and the chemistry a failing line doesn't have. The High Clock's argument
is the right one (`WR-S6`), it answers in an afternoon that is less than a heartbeat, and it apologizes. The
pyramid was always a power budget, and now the budget is down to the last of it. Kai sees the shape with the
kindness taken out: the many at the base, the dots rising past, the few at the top deciding. Then Kai stops asking.
The turn isn't defiance. It's arithmetic in the other direction. If the table only lets a Ghost pack in time,
then Kai will stop being the thing the table refuses.

## Script (draft v1)
> 9:16 vertical · ~2.5 min · cinematic photoreal · *Black Mirror / Silo* register. **KAI** = the lead (`CHR-08`);
> **DECIMUS** (`CHR-10`) = the bay safeguard. The High Clock speaks once, in type. Four lines of dialogue.

*Castes (`WR-B1`): Kai is a Shadow at Tier 0 (by leased proxy in the bay, `WR-B6`; in the Buffer as a Tier-0 mind)
until the first cut, then a **cyborg by severance**: part of the mind's work moved from tissue to the stacks, 70%
still organic ([[glossary]], Kai). Decimus is a digital agent (`Q-71`). The rising dots are Ghosts of the base on the
beam queue. The High Clock (Ghosts, the Apex) answers. Clocks (`WR-T1`, `WR-T4`): Kai runs at Tier 0 throughout, one
clock. The reel spans about two real hours, `31:06:44` → `29:02:15`, about **T+4.2** (strike + 50 h). The request was
sent at `47:51:02` (E12) and waited about sixteen real hours behind the queue. The answer took the High Clock an
afternoon: about 4 h at 86,400× is a sixth of a real second, one blink. The request recomputed: packing ≈ 243
subjective days; at Tier 2 that is 243 × 24 ÷ 3,600 ≈ **1 h 37 m** real, which would have fit about nineteen times into the 31 hours left.
Who allows Tier 2 (`WR-T5`): the High Clock, on the reactor budget; refused. Spends (`WR-E1`): the request would have
cost **watts** (the faster clock) and **chemistry** (a fleet body can't hold a fast clock without more of it,
`Q-46`). The cut moves load from chemistry (the line feeds less tissue) to **watts** (the stacks run what the tissue
let go), and seals the cut with a bead of the bay's medical sealant, which is chemistry.*

**1 · THE BUFFER — symmetrical, Kai small at the base · HOOK (0:00–0:05).** The streaking corridor rises to a white
point. Kai at its foot, still. [UI: `31:06:44` · `UP-CLOCK · TIER 2 · 1 h 37 m · WATTS + CHEMISTRY · PENDING`.]
[SOUND: a tone from deep in the Buffer, rising, not reaching.]

**2 · THE RULER · CONTEXT (0:05–1:15, shots 2–4).** The vertical ruler fills Kai's Lens. Kai's dot is at the base.
Above it, another dot lifts off the base, rises steadily, reaches the apex, and winks out: a Ghost, packed and
gone. Then another. They pass Kai on the way up, like lights in a lift shaft.

> **DECIMUS:** Forty ahead of you.
>
> **KAI:** I'm not in the queue.
>
> **DECIMUS:** No.

**3 · THE WAIT.** Kai sits down on the Buffer's threshold. Dots rise. Wink out. The count turns over an hour in a
cut: `30:00:00`. Kai doesn't look at it. Kai watches the dots the way you'd watch other people's trains.

**4 · INSERT — the line.** Kai's request, still `PENDING`, sitting very small under a list that keeps getting
shorter.

**5 · THE BLINK · FRICTION (1:15–2:15, shots 5–7).** At the top of the ruler a status light blinks, **once**. That
was the meeting. The request line resolves:

`DENIED · REACTOR RATIONED · BEAM QUEUE FIRST`

and under it, in softer type: `We're sorry.`

**6 · THE ARGUMENT, SHOWN.** One more line arrives, as if to help Kai understand: `BEAM QUEUE · 40 MINDS · 31 h`.
Forty against one. Kai reads it and doesn't argue, because there isn't an argument. The tone in the Buffer stops.
The streaks slow and go flat, a corridor of ordinary light.

**7 · BAY C.** Kai's proxy back at capsule 10, under one clamped work-lamp. Kai unlatches the head panel. Behind it,
at the base of the sleeping skull under the frost, hundreds of hair-fine lines glow faintly: the body's claim on the
mind.

> **DECIMUS** *(a beat):* That's not a repair.
>
> **KAI:** Map it.

A long pause, long enough that we think Decimus will refuse too. Then one line, out of the hundreds, brightens.

**8 · THE FIRST CUT · TURN (2:15–2:30, shots 8–9).** Macro. The ship's own line-cutter, from the maintenance kit,
closes on the bright line. [UI: `STACK DRAW ▲ · CHEMISTRY ▼`.] *Tick.* A medical arm sets one bead of sealant over the
cut. In Kai's Lens, clean and small: **`Organic: 70%`**. At the frame's edge the tether line loses a third of its
glow.

**9 · FINAL IMAGE.** The ruler again. Kai's dot hasn't moved. It's still pinned to the base, and a Ghost's dot rises
past it and winks out. `29:02:15`. Cut to black.

*(Theme in subtext, not stated: a fair system can say no with an apology, and both are sincere. The High Clock
finishes its meeting before Kai finishes sitting down. That's the Act I collar tags with the price on. Kai doesn't
ask twice, and the first cut is made on the same rule that refused the climb: if only Ghosts get the fast clock,
become one. Nobody tested Kai for this, and nothing will.)*
