# Production Breakdown — S4E9a: "One in Three"

> Production/cinematography breakdown for [[setting-sail-the-seed-ledger]] **Season 4, interlude reel 9a** (between "You
> Can Be Our Hero" and "The Open Window"; episode numbers unchanged). The perspective of a digital safeguard, told at
> **Tier 2**. **[[decimus|Decimus]]** (`CHR-10`; Latin "tenth"; pronoun *it*; a digital agent, `Q-71`, in the Teddy
> cadence) keeps Kai and Teo's rota group of **ten** on a `1:3` rating, held on a waiver, its next rungs unfunded
> ([[the-one-in-n-rule]], `MAG-32`). One Intention square in the bay, Teo's, has stayed lit since he paused for the night,
> and Decimus can't say why; at `1:3` it has no standing to raise it. Firing day: power is rerouted to the screen and the
> bore for the window ([[the-firing-protocol]]), and the safeguard's budget reads zero. The bay board offers **DORMANT
> UNTIL WINDOW CLOSES** or **VALIDATE 1:10 NOW**. **One reveal:** Decimus, and the ladder it can't afford. Minor: the
> dormant default. A reel of a person, after E9's big turn.

## Setup & hook
A single drop of fluid hangs below a chemistry line's valve, perfectly still, a bead of light. It does not fall. A calm
voice, warm and exact, counts beside it: *"One."* The camera finds another line, another bead, also not falling: *"Two."*
The whole bay is holding its breath, and only the voice is moving.

## Cinematography
| Element | The bay at Tier 2 (Decimus's side) | Decimus's interior (the stacks) |
|---------|------------------------------------|---------------------------------|
| **Lighting** | **Caravaggio** (`VIS-15`): the bay as in E9, one working light, chemistry lines as threads, but frozen: drips hanging, a proxy's dimmed optics, dust motes fixed in the beam. | **Flavin** neon: the server stacks where Decimus runs, cold tubes, its filings as columns of light. |
| **Camera** | Many fixed angles, cut together: the bay's own sensors. The camera never moves, because Decimus doesn't have a body to move it; it changes angle instead. Calm, unhurried (`WR-T2`). | A slow climb up a column of identical filings. |
| **Sound** | Near silence: at Tier 2 the bay's hum is a single held low note. Decimus's voice over it, close, gentle, a little dry. | The stacks' fans, slowed to a throb. |

## UI behavior (the Perspective Lens)
All UI is in **Decimus's** view, drawn in the Lens grammar (`WR-L1`): top circles its wards, bottom squares its own
intentions. Teo's square is in Teo's row, read from outside. The prompts are diegetic, on the physical bay board.
- **Top circles `[◯]` (Context):** **ten**, one per ward, each with a thin **tether thread** running to a body under glass.
  Nine threads are short and still: paused minds, Teo's among them. One runs a hand's width from a capsule to the proxy
  kneeling at it: Kai, awake at capsule 10 with a line tester. **All ten threads quiet.**
- **Rating (frame edge):** `1:3 · WAIVER · 10`.
- **Teo's row (read from outside):** the closed `SILENCE` square, and beside it `help`, **lit**. A small age line under it:
  `LIT · 7 h 26 m · MIND PAUSED`.
- **Decimus's bottom squares `[▢]`:** `count` · `keep`.
- **The raise (frame edge):** `RAISE · LIT SQUARE · PAUSED WARD` → `STANDING 1:3 · NOT FORWARDED`.
- **The notice (bay board, diegetic):** `SCREEN DROPS 09:40:00 · SAFEGUARD BUDGET → 0 W AT DROP`.
- **The prompt (bay board, diegetic):** `DORMANT UNTIL WINDOW CLOSES` (pre-selected) / `VALIDATE 1:10 NOW`. Under the second:
  `10 FORKS · 0.6 MWh · RESERVE 0.6 MWh` · `1:10 · ASSUMES BAY`. Further down, greyed: `LADDER · 49 FORKS · UNFUNDED`.
- **The screen icon (bay board):** the screen's disc, drawn small, its bore beginning to open.

## Philosophical climax
The rule says you may answer for N people only after a test only 1 in N would pass. Decimus has been answering for ten on a
proof for three, and filing the same polite request about it for years. The rating didn't make it responsible; it already
was. The rating only decides whether anyone would listen. Now one of its ten has a square lit that shouldn't be, and the
system replies, correctly, that a `1:3` has no standing to say so. Then the fleet takes its watts for the window and offers it
the kind thing: sleep through the dangerous minute, wake when it's over. Dormant is safe. Dormant is what a good safeguard
would do. Its fear is being asleep on the one day it was needed, and the reel ends on the moment it has to decide whether
that fear is a loop or the truth.

## Script (draft v1)
> 9:16 vertical · ~2.5 min · cinematic photoreal · *Black Mirror / Silo* register. **DECIMUS** = the bay safeguard
> (`CHR-10`), a voice only, plain and exact, a hint of old warmth: a distant, junior descendant of the Teddy cadence. Kai,
> Teo and eight other wards are present and silent. About sixty spoken words, all Decimus's.

*Castes (`WR-B1`): Decimus is a **digital agent** (`Q-71`), a made mind that was never a person; Kai and Teo and the other eight
wards are **Shadows**, 100% organic, bodies in deep stasis in this bay; nine are paused between watches, Teo among them (his
mind stays paused through this reel, his square lit); Kai is awake in bay C, minding capsule 10 at Tier 0 through a leased proxy
(`WR-B6`). No one else appears. Timing (`WR-T4`): **firing day, about
T+4.2**, the morning after E9. The reel runs from **09:39:50 to 09:40:00 ship time**, ten real seconds, which Decimus lives at
**Tier 2** as **ten subjective hours** (10 s × 3,600). From Decimus's side, every one of Kai's seconds lasts an hour; a drip that
would fall in a quarter-second hangs for fifteen minutes. Teo's `help` square has been lit since **02:13** (E9): **7 h 26 m 50 s**
real by 09:39:50. Decimus watched most of that night at Tier 0, its unfunded overnight clock. The screen drops at **09:40:00**;
the lock is about **one real minute**, which at Tier 2 is **sixty hours**. Spend (`WR-E1`): the window reroutes the hull's watts
to the screen and the bore, and the safeguard budget falls to **0 W** at the drop; Decimus's ten hours here run on the firing
protocol's allotment until then. Its **reserve** is **0.6 MWh**: exactly ten forks at Tier 2 for one real minute (10 × 3.6 MW ×
60 s = 2,160 MJ), from the drafted 1 kW-per-mind-at-1× scale. The ladder from `1:3` to `1:10` needs **49 forks** (4 + 5 + 6 + 7
+ 8 + 9 + 10) across seven rungs, about **2.94 MWh** at the same rate even if every rung took one minute: unfunded. No chemistry.*

**1 · COLD OPEN — EXTREME CLOSE (9:16) · HOOK (0:00–0:05).** A bead of fluid under a chemistry line's valve, perfectly still.

> **DECIMUS** *(close, warm, exact):* One.

Another line, another hanging bead.

> **DECIMUS:** Two.

**2 · THE BAY AT TIER 2 · CONTEXT (0:05–1:15, shots 2–5).** Cut through the bay's fixed sensors, one angle after another: the rows
of bodies under glass, every drip hanging, dust fixed in the working light. The count goes on, unhurried, *three, four*, one
body per number. [UI: ten circles at the top of Decimus's view, each with its tether thread to a body. `1:3 · WAIVER · 10`.]

**3 · NINE AND ONE.** Nine threads short and still. The tenth runs only a hand's width, from capsule 10 to the proxy kneeling beside
it. Another fixed angle: Kai's proxy at capsule 10 with a line tester, the probe halfway to a port. It will reach the port in about an
hour of Decimus's time.

> **DECIMUS** *(reaching the end of the count):* …nine. Ten.

**4 · THE FILING.** Decimus's interior: the stacks, Flavin neon. It files its morning report. Climb the column above it: the same
filing, repeated, back and back, each with an older date, each the same words.

> **DECIMUS** *(dictating, dry, without complaint):* Ten of you. Rated for three. I've mentioned it.

[UI: the new filing stamps `UNFUNDED`, like all the others.]

**5 · THE SQUARE.** Back to the bay. Teo's proxy in its cradle, optics dark; Teo's body under glass. In Teo's row, read from
outside: the closed `SILENCE` square and, beside it, `help`, **lit**. [UI: `LIT · 7 h 26 m · MIND PAUSED`.] A paused mind's squares
go dark. This one hasn't.

**6 · NO STANDING · FRICTION (1:15–2:15, shots 6–9).** Decimus tries to raise it.

> **DECIMUS:** You're asleep. Your square isn't.

[UI: `RAISE · LIT SQUARE · PAUSED WARD` → `STANDING 1:3 · NOT FORWARDED`.] It tries again, the same words. The same answer. It
does not try a third time.

**7 · THE NOTICE.** The bay board, physical, in the one working light, prints a line no one awake will read. [Diegetic: `SCREEN DROPS
09:40:00 · SAFEGUARD BUDGET → 0 W AT DROP`.] The seconds digit on the board reads `:50`. It will read `:50` for an hour.

**8 · THE PROMPT.** Beneath it the board offers two lines. [Diegetic: `DORMANT UNTIL WINDOW CLOSES` (selected) / `VALIDATE 1:10 NOW`
· `10 FORKS · 0.6 MWh · RESERVE 0.6 MWh` · `1:10 · ASSUMES BAY`. Greyed below: `LADDER · 49 FORKS · UNFUNDED`.]

> **DECIMUS** *(reading the first line back, gently, as if to a child):* Sleep through the window. Wake when it's over.

**9 · THE HOURS.** Time passes the way it does at Tier 2: Decimus counts the ten again, and again. The hanging drips. Kai's proxy at
capsule 10, the tester's probe a little nearer the port. The lit square. The selection stays on `DORMANT`. *(Recall cue, ≤ 2 s: E9, Teo's
proxy hand on the keypad, the bay light dipping. Decimus didn't see it; we did.)*

**10 · THE DISC · TURN (2:15–2:30, shots 10–11).** The board's seconds digit turns at last: `:59`. In the corner of the board, the
screen's disc icon begins to open at its bore. [UI: ten quiet threads; one lit square.] Decimus says nothing.

**11 · FINAL IMAGE.** The selection on the board. It leaves `DORMANT`. The line greys. Hold on the grey line, the lit square reflected in
the board's glass. Cut to black.

*(Theme in subtext, never stated: responsibility came before the rating, and the rating is only the right to be heard; the system's
offer of sleep is kind, correct and exactly the wrong day for it. The viewer finishes the sentence: it is going to spend everything
it has, and the ladder says it isn't ready.)*
