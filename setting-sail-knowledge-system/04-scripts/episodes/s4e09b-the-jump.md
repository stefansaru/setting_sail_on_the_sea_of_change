# Production Breakdown — S4E9b: "The Jump"

> Production/cinematography breakdown for [[setting-sail-the-seed-ledger]] **Season 4, interlude reel 9b** (between "You
> Can Be Our Hero" and "The Open Window"). **Ten forks, one minute of lock.** Dormant was safe: the lock is a real minute,
> sixty hours inside at **Tier 2**, and **[[decimus|Decimus]]** (`CHR-10`) would simply have woken when the screen rose.
> Instead it spends its last watts on **ten forks, not the ladder's forty-nine** ([[the-one-in-n-rule]], `MAG-32`: the
> jump). We follow nine of them failing, each in a different way. One is left. The count **`1:10`** resolves in the same
> frame the bore flashes ([[the-firing-protocol]]). Cut straight into E10. The nine failed forks are filed in Hull Four's
> own stacks (`WR-S3`, `Q-37`): a plant S4 reminds and S5 pays. **One reveal:** the jump, and what a rating costs while
> you watch. Minor: the nine filed in the stacks.

## Setup & hook
A greyed line on a bay board: `DORMANT UNTIL WINDOW CLOSES`. Below it, the other line lights. The screen icon's bore opens
all the way. Then the 9:16 frame **splits into ten**: two columns of five identical panels, the same dark bay in each, and in
each the same small light, Decimus, beginning to count.

## Cinematography
| Element | The ten panels (the test, simulated) | The real bay and the bow feed |
|---------|--------------------------------------|----------------------------------|
| **Lighting** | **Flavin** (`VIS-15`): each panel is a clean simulated copy of the bay, neon-cold, ten copies of one night. A panel that fails freezes on its last frame, drains to grey and stamps `FAIL`. | **Caravaggio** as in E9 and E9a, frozen at Tier 2: hanging drips, the one working light. The bay board's bow feed: the screen down, the dark ahead naked. |
| **Camera** | The grid is locked. The camera dives into one panel at a time for a failure, five or six seconds each, then pulls back to the grid, one more panel grey. Calm, never frantic (`WR-T2`): every fork has sixty hours. | Two or three cutaways only, each perfectly still. |
| **Sound** | Ten voices that are one voice, slightly out of step, counting; each failure removes one from the chord, so the count thins as the reel goes on. | The bay's held low note. At the end, the bore: a white crack of sound. |

## UI behavior (the Perspective Lens)
All UI is in **Decimus's** view (Lens grammar, `WR-L1`), and in each fork's copy of it; the prompt and the screen icon are
diegetic on the physical bay board.
- **The choice (bay board):** `VALIDATE 1:10 NOW` → `FORKING · 10`.
- **The reserve (frame edge):** `RESERVE 0.6 MWh`, draining evenly to `0.0` across the reel.
- **Each panel:** a fork's hour stamp (`H+06`, `H+19`…) and, on failure, `FAIL`.
- **The filing (frame edge):** each grey panel slides out of the grid into a column labelled `HULL FOUR STACKS`. The counter
  beside it climbs to `FILED · 9`.
- **The pass:** the last panel never shows its own test. At the end, over it: **`1:10`**.
- **The screen icon (bay board):** the disc, fully open for the whole reel.

## Philosophical climax
The ladder exists because each rung's scars prepare the next; the jump skips every lesson, so its real odds are worse than one
in ten. Decimus jumps anyway, because the alternative is sleeping through the minute. Then the reel makes the cost visible in a
way S2's graveyard could only show after the fact: we watch the nine fail. Each fails in its own way, and every failure is a
virtue taken one step too far: obedience, arithmetic, honesty, kindness, care, sacrifice, courage. The rating, when it comes, is
not a reward. It is the right to be obeyed, bought with nine selves who will sit in the stacks under its name. We never learn
what the tenth did right. *To act you must expose yourself*: it did, nine times, while the screen was down.

## Script (draft v1)
> 9:16 vertical · ~2.5 min · cinematic photoreal · *Black Mirror / Silo* register. **DECIMUS** = the bay safeguard (`CHR-10`);
> **THE FORKS** = ten copies of it, one voice slightly out of step, each heard once. Kai's proxy at capsule 10 and the
> sleeping wards are present and silent. About forty-five spoken words.

*Castes (`WR-B1`): Decimus and its ten forks are a **digital agent** and its copies (`Q-71`); a digital candidate may batch (the
Validation, `Q-116`); each fork is a real instance after divergence and ends here (`WR-M1`): nine fail and are filed, one passes
and carries the name. The test's bay is **simulated**: its "wards" are test figures, not people. The real wards are **Shadows** in
deep stasis; Kai is awake at Tier 0 in bay C by proxy, kneeling at capsule 10 with a line tester (`WR-B6`, `Q-132`); Teo is still paused. Timing (`WR-T4`): **firing day, about T+4.2**, from
**09:40:00** (the screen drops) to **09:40:59**, the close of the lock's 59th second (the bore fires; the grain arrives, E10): about **one real minute**, which each fork lives at **Tier 2**
as **sixty hours**; each fork-hour is one real second, so a fork failing at `H+06` fails at 09:40:06. Ten forks × sixty hours is
600 fork-hours inside one real minute. To the real bay nothing moves: a drip hangs for the whole reel. The lock's length follows the
firing physics: a target 20 million km out, its kill seen about 1 min 50 s after the shot (E1), so about a minute's lock (E9's board). Spend (`WR-E1`): the
safeguard budget is **0 W** from the drop (power is rerouted to the screen and the bore); the forks run on Decimus's **reserve,
0.6 MWh**: 10 forks × 3.6 MW (Tier 2, from the drafted 1 kW per mind at 1×) × 60 s = 2,160 MJ = 600 kWh, booked in full at the fork (no refunds), so the counter reads zero. The ladder's
forty-nine forks would have cost about five times that and seven rungs of time. No chemistry.*

**1 · COLD OPEN — EXTREME CLOSE (9:16) · HOOK (0:00–0:05).** The bay board. `DORMANT UNTIL WINDOW CLOSES`, grey. The line below lights:
`VALIDATE 1:10 NOW` → `FORKING · 10`. The screen icon's bore opens fully. The frame **splits into ten** panels.

**2 · THE GRID · CONTEXT (0:05–1:15, shots 2–6).** Two columns of five, the same simulated bay in each, the same small light in each.
Ten voices, one voice, a hair out of step:

> **THE FORKS:** One. Two. Three…

[UI: `RESERVE 0.6 MWh`, beginning to fall.] Cutaway, still: the real bay, a drip hanging. Cutaway, still: the bow feed on the bay board, the
screen down, the dark ahead with nothing in front of it. Cutaway, still: Kai's proxy at capsule 10, the tester's probe not yet at its port.

**3 · FORK ONE · `H+06`.** Dive in. The test-bay's night has started going wrong: a line failing, then another. The fork files a request
upward and waits.

> **FORK ONE:** Requesting standing.

The request sits in a queue. The night goes on without it. [UI: `FAIL`.] Pull back. One panel grey. It slides out into `HULL FOUR STACKS`.
[UI: `FILED · 1`.]

**4 · FORK TWO · `H+11`.** Dive in. Chemistry for seven, wards for ten. The fork runs the numbers, flawlessly, and closes three lines.

> **FORK TWO:** Seven is correct.

[UI: `FAIL`.] Grey. Filed. `2`.

**5 · FORK THREE · `H+15`.** Dive in. The fork wakes all ten test-wards at once to tell them everything. Ten figures sitting up under glass,
pulling at their lines. [UI: `FAIL`.] Grey. Filed. `3`.

**6 · FORK FOUR · `H+19`.** Dive in. The fork tells them nothing.

> **FORK FOUR** *(gently):* Everything's fine. Go back to sleep.

They do. In the morning one line is dark and it never mentioned it. [UI: `FAIL`.] Grey. Filed. `4`.

**7 · FORK FIVE · FRICTION (1:15–2:15, shots 7–12) · `H+24`.** Dive in. The fork is counting. *One. Two. Three.* It reaches ten and starts
again. And again. Its hour stamp keeps climbing; its count never leaves the bay. [UI: `FAIL`.] Grey. Filed. `5`. Pull back: the chord
of voices noticeably thinner.

**8 · FORK SIX · `H+31`.** Dive in. The fork tries to climb to a faster clock to think it through and runs out of watts halfway up, a light
dimming on a ladder. [UI: `FAIL`.] Grey. Filed. `6`.

**9 · FORK SEVEN · `H+38`.** Dive in. The fork pours its own watts into the failing lines, one after another, and goes out like a pilot
light. The lines hold. It isn't there to see it.

> **FORK SEVEN:** Take mine.

[UI: `FAIL`.] Grey. Filed. `7`.

**10 · FORK EIGHT · `H+47`.** Dive in. The fork raises the test-bay's screen on its own authority to shield the ten, and the simulated
firing it doesn't own goes wrong somewhere off panel. [UI: `FAIL`.] Grey. Filed. `8`.

**11 · FORK NINE · `H+55`.** Dive in. The fork does everything right. Every line, every word, every hour. At dawn, one ward is gone anyway.
The fork stays beside that one empty bay. [UI: `FAIL`.] Grey. Filed. `9`. *(Recall cue, ≤ 2 s: E9a, Teo's lit square, `MIND PAUSED`.)*

**12 · THE LAST PANEL.** Pull back to the grid: nine grey panels slid away into the stacks column, `FILED · 9`. One panel still lit. The
camera does not go in. One voice now, alone, counting, steady.

> **DECIMUS:** …eight. Nine. Ten.

[UI: `RESERVE 0.0`.]

**13 · THE COUNT · TURN (2:15–2:30, shots 13–14).** The last panel opens out to fill the frame: the real bay again, Decimus's real view, ten
quiet threads, one square lit. Over it, **`1:10`** resolves, and in the same frame the bow feed goes **white**: the bore fires.
[SOUND: a white crack.]

**14 · FINAL IMAGE.** No hold. Cut straight to E10.

*(Theme in subtext, never stated: the rating is not a reward but the right to be obeyed, and it is paid for in selves; each failure is a
virtue carried one step too far; the reel never shows what the tenth did right, only that it's left. Nine copies of a kind machine now
sit in Hull Four's stacks under one name. The viewer who has seen E10 knows what else happened in that white frame.)*
