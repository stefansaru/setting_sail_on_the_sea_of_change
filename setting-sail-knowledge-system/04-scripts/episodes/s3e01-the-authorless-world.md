# Production Breakdown — S3E01: "The Authorless World"

> Detailed production/cinematography breakdown for [[setting-sail-the-cartographers-ledger]] **Season 3, Episode 1**
> (Act I, *The God's Hole*). **Tier 0, the Purist Village** (`PLC-06`), **T ≈ +20.5**: one real year to the day after
> Naomi's death (S2E20). **Raul** (`CHR-02`), a [[the-ghost-entity|Ghost]] in a [[shells-with-ghosts-within|shell]],
> Lens on, keeps watch over **Adam's** borrowed life and the cure that made it. Then the law of the world, a weather
> with no author ([[the-distributed-president]]), writes one clause that nothing measured, signed with the mark nobody
> read in S2E11: `// M.C.` (a-ha #1 reminder; re-homes `FCT-002`). The world has an author, and she is stirring.
> **Mara: 0% organic** (the President, a Ghost, `Q-98`), never seen. World: [[the-shadow-grid]] ·
> [[the-perspective-lens-ui]] · [[the-cosmic-clone-paradox]] · [[the-memory-substrate]].

## Setup & hook
A thumb drawn slowly along eleven notches cut in a lintel, the way Naomi used to touch them (S2E1). The hand is new:
pink, unweathered skin that ends at a chrome seam at the wrist, Ruth's cord knotted above it. A small tag floats over
it in someone's Lens: *Regrown · donor: NAOMI*. A year ago she gave him this hand. Today the house is quiet.

## Cinematography — two layers of one dawn
| Element | The village (raw sight) | Raul's Lens (the law-feed) |
|---------|-------------------------|----------------------------|
| **Lighting** | Caravaggio dawn ([[lighting]] §4, `VIS-15`): smoke, one cookfire, low sun through the doorway. Skin, wood, wool. | **Albers** mustard (S2E11's law-chambers, `VIS-15`), shrunk to a thin ribbon across the top of the frame: clauses rewriting themselves in even, shadowless light. |
| **Camera** | **Handheld, breathing**, close on hands and the lintel. | **Locked**, machine-smooth (`VIS-04`) whenever we are in Raul's POV. The one clause that waits is the only thing in the reel that holds still. |
| **Sound** | Wind, buckets, chickens, a child somewhere, the fire. | S2E11's **choral hum with no voice in it**, very faint, and the soft rain-ticks of the CVP. When the clause arrives, the ticks stop. |

**The cold is rationed** (as in S2E1): Raul's chrome, the small light of the old feed camera on the post across the
yard (S2E2, `Q-128` (5)), and the ribbon. Everything else is fire-coloured.

## UI behavior (the Perspective Lens)
All UI is **Raul's** (`WR-L2`): Adam's Lens has been off since S2E19 (`Q-98`); Ruth and the villagers are Purists.
- **The plant (the cure, `Q-106`):** over Adam's hand, small, two lines: *Regrown · donor: NAOMI* · *Cure: on file*.
  What regrew him took a willing, dying donor, and the recipe for it still exists. Nothing says more. (Plant → E16's
  `Treatable · donor required` → E20.)
- **The law-feed (`MAG-18`, on screen from here):** a ribbon of mustard clauses along the top edge. Each one hangs from
  a thin **sensor thread** dropping into the world, and each lands with a green `CVP ✓` (S2E11). Where the frame holds
  the village, the ribbon runs **unthreaded**: nothing down here is measured, so nothing ticks (S2E11's dark patch,
  seen from inside it).
- **The edge file (plant → E2):** at the far edge of the frame, small and never opened: `REPLY COPY · PAUSED · 12 yrs`.
- **The clause (the major):** one clause arrives **slowly**, and with no thread under it. Its source line reads
  `derived from: —`. It does not tick. Its text, one line: `// M.C. → Tier 0 · sector: unmeasured`. Beneath it, grey:
  `PENDING`. The law has never asked for anything before.
- **The signature:** Raul pinches it open. The Lens tries to resolve it and fails: `// M.C. · signer: unresolved`.
- **No glyph beat.** Raul's solid ring (S1E20) sits at the frame edge and does not move.

## Philosophical climax
For two seasons the law has been weather: fair to everything it measures and blind to the one patch of the planet
where people still die by hand (S2E11). Weather does not ask. A year after a mother died under this lintel, the
weather asks for something, and what it asks for is to be **slow**, here, in the place it cannot see. Adam asked the
same law for time once (S2E11) and got a grey line for an answer; now the law has a grey line of its own. Nobody
says *author*. The viewer sees a signature on a wish and finishes it: the rules were written by someone, and that
someone wants to come where the grieving are.

## Script (draft v1)
> 9:16 vertical · ~2.5 min · cinematic photoreal · *Black Mirror / Silo* register. **RAUL** = the S3 lead (`CHR-02`,
> `Q-21`), a Ghost in a shell; **ADAM** (`CHR-06`), about 20; **RUTH** (`CHR-07`), about 20; **VILLAGERS**. The
> President is present only as text in Raul's Lens. About 20 spoken words.

*Castes (`WR-B1`): Raul is a Ghost in a shell, Lens on since S1E20 and alignment off since S1E19 (`Q-119`). Adam is a
cyborg of mostly regrown flesh, chrome seams where the S1E20 implants stayed, Lens off since S2E19 (`Q-98`), living in
the flesh since S2E20 (`Q-128` (7)). Ruth and the villagers are Purists, no Lens (`WR-L2`). The President, author of the
clause, is a Ghost (`Q-98`), unseen. Rafe, the edge file, is a Ghost, paused. Clocks (`WR-T1`, `WR-T4`): the village runs
at Tier 0, one real clock. The law-feed is written above at up to Tier 3, where one real second is one subjective day
(1 × 86,400 s = 24 h), so every clause reaches Raul already finished; the one that waits is the anomaly. Series clock:
T ≈ +20.5, one real year after S2E20 (T ≈ +19.5, `Q-58`); Adam and Ruth about 20 (`Q-104`). Rafe's copy has been
paused since S1E16 (T ≈ +8.2): about 12.3 real years, no subjective time at all.*

**1 · CLOSE — the lintel · HOOK (0:00–0:05).** Dawn. A thumb drawn along eleven notches, slow, the way Naomi did it
(S2E1). Pull focus to the hand: new pink skin ending at a chrome seam at the wrist, Ruth's cord knotted above it. [UI,
Raul's Lens, small: *Regrown · donor: NAOMI* · *Cure: on file*.] We are across the yard, looking through him.

**2 · THE YARD — handheld · CONTEXT (0:05–1:15, shots 2–4).** Wider. ADAM under the lintel, hand still on the wood. The
house behind him is dark; nobody has lit its fire. RUTH comes across the yard with a loaf, sets it on the board by the
door where no one will take it, and stands beside him, not touching, a year to the day. Villagers pass on their way to the well; each one, without stopping, touches the
doorpost. Across the yard, on its post, the old feed camera faces the door, its small light steady.

**3 · THE EDGE OF THE FIRELIGHT.** RAUL on his stump at the village edge, chrome under a borrowed coat (S2E1), as still
as furniture. He has kept this watch for a year. A child walks past him without looking up.

**4 · RAUL'S LENS — POV, locked.** The village in raw dawn, and above it the ribbon: mustard clauses re-flowing, each on
its thin thread, each landing with a green tick, soft as rain. Over the village itself the ribbon runs bare. At the
far edge of the frame, small: `REPLY COPY · PAUSED · 12 yrs`. His gaze slides over it. [RECALL CUE, ≤ 2 s: the
Synchronization Node, two Rauls face to face, his own voice: *"Go home, Rafe."*] He doesn't open it.

**5 · THE CLAUSE · FRICTION (1:15–2:15, shots 5–7).** The rain-ticks **stop**. One clause comes down the ribbon slowly,
and nothing hangs beneath it. [UI: `derived from: —`.] It doesn't tick. It reads, one line:
`// M.C. → Tier 0 · sector: unmeasured`. Under it, grey: `PENDING`. Behind the line, out of focus, Adam's thumb reaches
the last notch.

**6 · INSERT — the signature.** Raul pinches it open. [UI: `// M.C. · signer: unresolved`.] [RECALL CUE, ≤ 2 s: S2E11,
the clause rewriting itself in the law-chambers, the same small mark at its foot, the camera passing it.] He holds the
pinch, as if more might come. It doesn't.

**7 · THE ASK.** Adam has seen the stillness change. He can't see what Raul sees.

> **ADAM:** What is it?
>
> **RAUL:** Something up there asked for something.
>
> *(Adam, who once stood under a hundred arches and asked, takes a beat.)*
>
> **ADAM:** Did anyone answer?
>
> *(Raul looks at the grey line.)*
>
> **RAUL:** No.

**8 · THE PLACE · TURN (2:15–2:30, shots 8–9).** Raul's eyes go from `sector: unmeasured` down to what it names, in raw
sight: smoke, the well, Ruth's bread on the board by the door, Adam's new hand on the old wood. Then across the yard to
the camera on its post, facing the door the clause arrived for. Its light is steady.

**9 · FINAL IMAGE — RAUL'S LENS.** The grey `PENDING` over the waking village. At the edge of the frame, for the first
time, his gaze **stops** on the file he never opens: `REPLY COPY · PAUSED · 12 yrs`. Hold. Hard cut to black on the
silence where the ticks used to be.

*(Theme in subtext, not stated: law with no lawgiver doesn't ask, so the asking is the author. It arrives at the
minute a son touches his dead mother's notches, from a thing that cannot be here, asking to be slow, in the one place
it can't see. The rule-weather has a wish; Raul, who keeps the only camera on this door, has just learned he is
not the only one watching it. The file at the edge is the help he has refused for twelve years.)*
