# Production Breakdown — S2E13: "The Chain and the Patch"

> Production/cinematography breakdown for [[setting-sail-tier-ascent]] **Season 2, Episode 13** (Act III, Phase C′).
> **Pays off the S1E12 seed (`F-1`):** *"Sure you don't want your chain made public? Just in case." / "I'll think
> about it."* She did. **Adam** (`CHR-06`, 60% organic, Tier 2, `Q-48`) audits the man whose keys are waiting in his
> squares, and finds how **Vance** (`CHR-04`) keeps a spotless record: an [[intent-patch|Intent-Patch]] (`MAG-21`)
> that makes his Intention squares broadcast a flawless signature to the [[compliance-validation-protocol|CVP]],
> which mints his clean record as a **Proven-in-Use** token (`MAG-20`), the one new word. The chain is S1E12's word
> and the CVP was seen in E11, so this reel spends **one** new system, the patch. The honest baseline that shows the
> gap is Kavana's public chain ([[the-personal-blockchain]], a-ha #10), in the friction, not the turn. *(The CVP
> checks behaviour live; it is never called the Validation, `Q-73`.)* **Answers an S1 question.**

## Setup & hook
People first. In the corner of Adam's Lens, the village feed, live and still: Naomi's lintel, the ten notches cut
one for each year since his implants (E1) and the room for one more, and propped against them now, Ruth's sheet
from E10, in her own hand: *She's dying. Come home.* A candle flame beside it leans and stays leaning. It isn't a
photograph. It's twelve minutes of her time, a month of his.

## Cinematography
| Element | The record room (Vance's chain) | Hope's medical wing (the act) |
|---------|---------------------------------|-------------------------------|
| **Lighting** | **Albers** again (`VIS-15`), cooler now: the inner archways' dark grey, the chains themselves the main light source, green and red on Adam's face. | **Caravaggio** (`VIS-15`, the "lower, desperate medical wings of Hope"): one lamp, an old man's profile, an Apex capsule beside him that should be glowing and isn't. |
| **Camera** | Slow lateral tracks along two columns of blocks, like reading two gravestones side by side. Unhurried (`WR-T2`). | Static. The Tier-0 room is nearly frozen at his clock; only the green ticks over it move. |
| **Sound** | The CVP's soft ticks (E11). Under them, one archived voice from Kavana's chain. | A slow heartbeat (S1E11's), one beat in a long while. |

## UI behavior (the Perspective Lens)
All UI is in Adam's Lens (`WR-L2`; Ruth, Naomi and the Purist patient have none).
- **Vance's latest act:** a signed block on his public chain: `APEX QUEUE · RE-RANKED · MERIT FIRST`. Vance's squares
  at the moment he signed: perfectly still, one flawless reading, `STRUCTURAL BALANCE · 100%`. `CVP ✓`. Then a seal
  stamps over his whole column: `PROVEN-IN-USE` (the one new word).
- **The baseline (a-ha #10, serial layer, in the friction):** `KAVANA · PUBLIC`, opened to the block for the same
  thing, `APEX · 2.4 h`, the hours Hope bought her in S1E11. Her squares at that moment are honest and messy: `Live`
  with amber fear flickering around it, and a reading of `99%`. The same act, two records. Hers has a tremor in it;
  his has none.
- **The seam (the major):** magnified, Vance's squares show what the flawless reading sits on: a hairline gold seam
  under the bottom row. Tag: `INTENT-PATCH`. (*Plant:* the same seam returns under Adam's own squares in E15,
  tagged `REGRADE`, and fills `SAVE` in E19: E13 → E15 → E19.)
- **The turn:** the act itself, followed down to Tier 0: Hope's queue, re-ranked; the first name it pushed back.
  Over that bedside, Vance's chain keeps ticking `CVP ✓`.

## Philosophical climax
The accountability utopia promised that a clean record means a clean act. Here the record is perfect and the act is
a dying man next to a dark capsule. And Vance's act has its argument: Hope spent 2.4 Apex hours on Kavana and returned
her 8% organic, asking *"Who is this?"*; Vance moved those hours to people who choose them, people like Adam
(`WR-S6`). What gives him away isn't a law. It's a missing tremor. **An honest intent under pressure never reads a
hundred percent.** Kavana's ninety-nine is the truest number in the reel.

## Script (draft v1)
> 9:16 vertical · ~2.5 min · cinematic photoreal · *Black Mirror / Silo* register. **ADAM** = the S2 lead
> (`CHR-06`); **VANCE** appears only as his public chain; **THE INTERCESSOR'S VOICE** is archived audio on Kavana's
> public chain (S1E12, verbatim). **RUTH**, **NAOMI** and **THE PATIENT** are seen on feeds and don't speak. Almost
> no dialogue: this reel is read, like a ledger.

*Castes (`WR-B1`): Adam is a cyborg, 60% organic, his body in a rented city capsule (`Q-91`), his mind at Tier 2;
Vance is a Ghost (`Q-90`); Kavana, seen only as her chain, is a cyborg, 8% organic, since S1E13; the Intercessor
(archived voice) is a Ghost in a shell (`Q-87`); Ruth, Naomi and the patient in Hope's medical wing are Purists.
Timing (`WR-T4`): about **12 real minutes** since Ruth's sheet (E10), which is **one month** at his Tier-2 clock
(12 × 3,600 = 43,200 min = 720 h = 30 days). The reel covers about **2 hours** of his: **2 real seconds** at the
village and at Hope, so the patient's 24-hour countdown ticks exactly twice.*

**1 · COLD OPEN — THE FEED (9:16) · HOOK (0:00–0:05).** Lens corner, filling the frame: Naomi's lintel. Ten notches
and room for one. Ruth's sheet propped against them: *She's dying. Come home.* A candle flame leans, and holds the
lean. [SOUND: nothing at all from the village; a real second is an hour here.]

**2 · THE RECORD ROOM · CONTEXT (0:05–1:15, shots 2–4).** Pull out of the corner to ADAM, in the inner arches, the
pending square from E12 still parked in his bottom row: [UI: `KEYS · VANCE`]. He doesn't take it. He opens the man
instead. [UI: Vance's public chain, a column of green.]

**3 · INSERT — the latest block.** [UI: `APEX QUEUE · RE-RANKED · MERIT FIRST`, signed `VANCE · 1:10000`.] At the
moment of signing, Vance's squares read one flawless, unmoving line: `STRUCTURAL BALANCE · 100%`. `CVP ✓`. A seal
stamps down over his whole column, soft as wax: `PROVEN-IN-USE`.

**4 · ADAM, READING.** Nothing is wrong with it. That is what bothers him. He opens, beside it, the chain he trusts:
[UI: `KAVANA · PUBLIC`].

**5 · THE BASELINE · FRICTION (1:15–2:15, shots 5–6).** He finds her block for the same act: [UI: `APEX · 2.4 h`].
Her squares at that moment: `Live`, ringed with amber fear that flickers and won't settle; the reading under it,
`99%`. From the block, archived, the voice that pressed her (S1E12):

> **THE INTERCESSOR'S VOICE** *(soft, from the archive):* Your intent reads ninety-nine percent clean, Kavana.

He lays the two blocks side by side. `APEX · 2.4 h` over each. Hers trembles. His doesn't. *(Recall cue, ≤ 2 s: the
gold `Live` square at the foot of her chain, from E12.)*

**6 · THE SEAM.** Adam magnifies Vance's squares at the moment of signing, closer, closer, until the flawless line
fills the frame, and under the bottom row a hairline of gold appears, like a crack mended with gold leaf. [UI tag,
small: `INTENT-PATCH`.] His own bottom row, in the corner of the frame, has no such seam. Yet.

**7 · THE ACT · TURN (2:15–2:30, shots 7–8).** Adam follows the re-ranked queue down, one tier after another, to the
first name it pushed back: Hope's medical wing, Tier 0. An old Purist man in lamplight, breathing slowly. A real
countdown over him, `23:59:58`. Beside the bed an Apex capsule, dark, tagged `QUEUED`. [SOUND: one heartbeat, S1E11's.]
The countdown ticks once: `23:59:57`. Over the whole room, in Adam's Lens, Vance's chain goes on ticking green:
`CVP ✓`, `CVP ✓`, `CVP ✓`.

**8 · FINAL IMAGE.** The spotless green column standing over the dark capsule. The countdown ticks once more. Cut to
black on the green.

*(Theme in subtext, never stated: the society that trusts the signal of intention can be sold a perfect signal; the
honest record is the one with fear in it, and Kavana's 1% of doubt is the only proof left. A cold viewer gets a
clean record over a rotten act; a serial viewer gets the S1E12 seed paid, the hours Adam wants taken from the next
Kavana, and a gold seam they will see again.)*
