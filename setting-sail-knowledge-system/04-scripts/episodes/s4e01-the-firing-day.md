# Production Breakdown — S4E01: "The Firing Day"

> Detailed production/cinematography breakdown for [[setting-sail-the-seed-ledger]] **Season 4, Episode 1**
> (Act I, Phase A: the serene dark). Hull Four's forward bay on a routine firing day: the
> [[the-seed-fleet|scattering screen]] drops, the lock runs a long real minute, the bore fires, and a rock about
> eighteen million kilometres ahead becomes dust ([[the-firing-protocol]]). **Kai** (`CHR-08`), a stasis
> [[the-shadow-entity|Shadow]] on maintenance rota, attends by proxy and watches it the way you watch weather. The cold
> open is the season's weapon, shown as beauty. One reveal: **to fire, the hull must open.**

## Setup & hook
Open outside the hull, dead ahead of the bow. A vast curtain of glittering dust and plasma hangs in front of the
ship, held in a magnetic grip, with a dark round bore at its centre: a disc pierced by a hole. It shivers, and lets
go. It falls away like a dropped veil, and behind it the stars snap sharp. Nothing is attacking. It is a firing
day, and this is the most ordinary thing the fleet does.

## Cinematography — weather, not war
| Element | The hull (raw glass) | Kai's Lens view |
|---------|----------------------|-----------------|
| **Lighting** | *Alien*'s cold corridors: hard top-light, steel, condensation; the forward port a black slab. | The same bay with faces on it: the Lens dresses each proxy in its owner's render. Warmer by a degree, never cosy. |
| **Camera** | Locked-off, procedural; long lenses down the bay. No handheld: nothing here is emergency. | Kai's POV through the proxy's sensor bar: a slight fish-eye at the edges, steady. |
| **Sound** | The screen's magnetic hum (a deep organ note under every scene) cuts to silence at the drop; the count; the bore's shot is **silent**, a click in the bay's speakers. | The count, closer; the hum's absence as pressure. |

**The two-view rule.** In raw glass the crew are grey jointed maintenance frames with sensor bars for faces. In Kai's
Lens the same frames wear human faces, ageless and in their thirties. Cut between the two without comment; the viewer
learns that everyone awake here is somewhere else.

## UI behavior (the Perspective Lens)
Kai's Lens only (`WR-L2`); the crew's tags are what Kai's Lens draws over them.
- **Top circles `[◯]` (Context):** `SCREEN · DOWN` goes amber at the drop; beside it `LOCK 00/60`, ticking;
  under the shot, the cost in the hull's own unit: `cost: watts`. Nothing reads danger. Nothing has ever needed to.
- **Bottom squares `[▢]` (Intention):** Kai's rota, small and dull: `check`, `log`. Kai logs the screen's coupling
  lights as they come back green.
- **The glyph, introduced:** after the shot, as the screen pours back into place, a small disc draws itself at the
  frame's edge: a ring with a dark bore, **whole**. It does not change during the window. Kai never thinks of the
  window as danger, so the glyph stays whole. It will open in E10.

## Philosophical climax
The protocol is the Doughnut built as engineering: **to act you must expose yourself.** The fleet could steer
around the rock and pay in every hull's stock, or drop its screen and pay a minute of nakedness. It pays the minute,
every time, because the minute has never cost anything. The reel shows the cost as nothing: a count, a shove, a
joke. The two crew counting the lock squabble like boys, and one of them says the oldest line in the series (a-ha #16
reminder). Nobody looks up from the squabble, and when the rock's death finally arrives as light, two minutes late,
nobody looks up from their work either. Only Kai does. The beauty is the warning, and nobody reads it.

## Script (draft v1)
> 9:16 vertical · ~2.5 min · cinematic photoreal · *Black Mirror / Silo* register. **KAI** = the lead (`CHR-08`), a
> Shadow at Tier 0, attending the forward bay by proxy; **WATCH ONE** and **WATCH TWO** = the two crew on watch, adults,
> also attending by proxy (script labels only). Dialogue is the count, almost all of it; the theme lands in subtext
> and in the [[the-perspective-lens-ui|UI]] (`WR-F4`).

*Castes (`WR-B1`): Kai is a Shadow, 100% organic (`WR-B3`), body in deep stasis in a bay below, mind on the stacks; at
Tier 0 Kai attends by leased proxy, never in person (`WR-B6`). WATCH ONE and WATCH TWO are Shadows on watch, by proxy.
No Purists, no children. The decision to fire came down from above before the reel opens and is never named here.
Timing (`WR-T4`, `WR-T6`): about **T+3.9**, weeks after the Earth beam landed on this hull; the fleet about 3.9 ly
from Earth, cruising at a tenth of light speed. Kai and the crew are at Tier 0, so one clock: the lock is a real
minute and the reel counts it in real time. The vote to fire, taken at the Apex, was an afternoon there and about a
sixth of a real second here (86,400× vs 1×). Firing physics (`WR-E2`): the lock begins with the rock about 20 million
km ahead and closing at about 30,000 km/s, so a 60-second lock brings it to about 18 million km; beam and rock close
at 1.1c, so the shot lands about 55 s after firing, and the flash of dust comes back in about 55 s more: **the
rock's death is seen about 1 min 50 s after the shot.** The hull reaches the dust about ten minutes after firing; the
screen is back up within seconds of the shot. Spends (`WR-E1`): the drop, the re-raise and the shot draw the hull's
watts; the proxies draw watts; no chemistry is spent, which is the point of firing.*

**1 · COLD OPEN — EXTERIOR, DEAD AHEAD (9:16) · HOOK (0:00–0:05).** The screen fills the frame: a disc of glittering
dust and plasma, a dark bore at its centre. [SOUND: a deep magnetic hum, felt more than heard.] It shivers. It lets
go, and falls away from the bow like a veil. Stars snap sharp. [SOUND: the hum cuts to silence.]

**2 · THE FORWARD BAY — raw glass · CONTEXT (0:05–1:15, shots 2–3).** Steel, condensation, top-light. Three grey
maintenance frames, unhurried. One kneels at a coupling panel: Kai. Two stand at the forward station: WATCH ONE and
WATCH TWO. Down the bay, a wall board: tomorrow's rota, today's firing, a mug-ring of old stencilled numbers.
[Camera: locked, long lens.]

**3 · KAI'S LENS — the same bay, dressed.** Cut to Kai's POV and the frames have faces: two ordinary crew in their
thirties, one chewing a thumbnail. [UI: top circles: `SCREEN · DOWN` (amber) · `LOCK 00/60` · `cost: watts`.
Bottom squares: `check` · `log`.] On the forward port a reticle settles on a point of nothing. Kai's proxy hand
moves down a row of coupling lights, dark, dark, dark: the screen's anchors, waiting.

> **WATCH ONE** *(to nobody, the way you announce rain):* Lock's on.

**4 · THE COUNT · FRICTION (1:15–2:15, shots 4–5).** The count is real time, one number a second, and the reel keeps
it.

> **WATCH ONE / WATCH TWO** *(together, flat, practised):* One. Two. Three…

[UI: `LOCK 03/60`, `04/60`…] Kai works through it, half listening. Around twenty the two voices drift apart; WATCH TWO
is a beat ahead, then two beats, grinning. Around forty, WATCH ONE shoves him, frame against frame, a dull clank.

> **WATCH ONE:** He started it.

Nobody looks up. In raw glass: two grey machines, one rocking on its feet. In the Lens: two grown men about to laugh.
The count has fallen apart.

> **KAI** *(not looking up, logging a light):* Forty-four.

They pick it up from Kai, sheepish, in unison again.

**5 · INSERT — the coupling lights.** Kai's proxy hand rests on the dark row. [UI: `LOCK 52/60`… `58/60`.] The bay
holds its breath without knowing it is holding anything.

**6 · THE SHOT · TURN (2:15–2:30, shots 6–7).**

> **WATCH ONE / WATCH TWO:** Sixty.

Exterior, dead ahead: a silent white line leaves the hull's centreline and is gone into the dark. [SOUND: a click in
the bay speakers. Nothing else.] The dust and plasma pour back into place, the veil rising; the disc is whole again.
The coupling lights go green under Kai's hand, one by one. [SOUND: the hum returns.] [UI: at the frame's edge, a small
ring with a dark bore draws itself: **whole**. `SCREEN · DOWN` fades.]

**7 · FINAL IMAGE.** A hard time-cut: the bay, a couple of minutes on. The crew back at their boards; the rota moving.
On the forward feed, far ahead, a point of light blooms once and fades: the rock, now dust, its flash arriving late.
Nobody looks up. Kai does. Hold on the grey frame, sensor bar lifted toward the port. Cut to black.

*(Theme in subtext, not stated: the fleet buys its safety with a minute of nakedness and calls it weather. The count
is the protocol's only ritual, and it collapses into a boys' shove and the line every Grid child is raised against;
the adults don't notice they said it. The rock's death arriving two minutes late is the season's grammar in one
image: out here, everything you see has already happened.)*
