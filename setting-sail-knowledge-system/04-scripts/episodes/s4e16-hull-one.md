> [[the-ghost-entity|Ghost]] at the clock the High Clock allows each Ghost it receives from Hull Four (`Q-118`). **Rafe**

## Setup & hook
Extreme close on a **docking light** on a lifeboat's nose, caught halfway through a blink, and it does not finish
the blink. The hold runs long enough to feel wrong. Pull back: a small craft hanging against stars, a dark hull far off
beside it, Hull One's dock ahead, and Kai's reflection in the glass, watching. Last seen at the Apex of a dying ship,
Kai wakes into a world that has almost stopped. At Tier 2 a real second is an hour, so a light that won't finish its
blink is the reel's grammar: everything that costs anything moves too slowly to see, and the cost shows up on a board.

## Cinematography — the slow arrival
| Element | Hull One's decks (Kai, Tier 2) | The lifeboat (Tier 0, seen from Tier 2) |
|---------|--------------------------------|------------------------------------------|
| **Lighting** | **Flavin** neon of the server decks (`VIS-15`): clean vertical tubes, cool white and pale green. Even, sourceless, a little clinical. | **Caravaggio** tenebrism (`VIS-15`): one hard work-light per bay, a face cut out of black. The boat's interior is the only warm thing in the reel. |
| **Camera** | Locked, slow, calm (`WR-T2`). High speed feels normal; Kai's movements are unhurried. | Effectively frozen. Across the reel the boat closes on its dock by a body's width; we never see it move, only that it has. |
| **Sound** | A low server hum; two voices, dry and close. | Nothing: a Tier-0 sound stretched 3,600× is below hearing. One deep tremor under the floor when the clamps close. |

**Three days in two and a half minutes.** The reel cuts across Kai's first three subjective days aboard. Each cut
is marked only by the boat being a little nearer and the docking light caught mid-blink again.

- **The boat:** E12's tag, still on it: *BOAT 3 · C-09 · → HULL ONE*. Through its port, two bays: one holds Teo, awake;

- **The crew list:** in front of Rafe (seen through Kai's Lens), Hull Four's roster as a column of names, most of them

- **The ramp board** (the fleet's physical slate, as in E4, cropped to its lower rows): the `HULL FOUR` column dark from
  top to bottom. In the `HULL ONE` column, thirty slots with E4's statuses, and two names added since the hit: *20 ·

- **The "arrive" square** (written E4, dimmed E12, put out by Kai's own hand at the Apex in E15) relights in Kai's bottom row, but a thin line runs from it to the docked
  boat: the arrival it points at is someone else's.
- **The array, corner of frame:** a dark hull alongside, tagged *HULL FOUR · DARK · Δv 0.000*. Nobody looks at it.

## Philosophical climax
Kai fought a whole night to reach a faster clock, and this is what it buys: from up here the rescue is a still
photograph and the bill is a moving number. Every body that comes out of a boat is a life kept, and every one is a
slot on the board going dark. Nobody says so. The "arrive" square comes back on pointed at the lifeboat, where Teo lies
awake beside the empty bay Kai waved off with "Next one" (E12). Rafe, holding a seat no test gave him and a list of
the dead no one asked him to keep, finds one name still alive and says almost nothing. Being a Ghost gives Kai time
first, and the first thing the time shows Kai is what everyone who stayed a body costs.

## Script (draft v1)
> 9:16 vertical · ~2.5 min · cinematic photoreal · *Black Mirror / Silo* register. **KAI** = the lead (`CHR-08`), a
> Ghost since E15, pronouns open; **RAFE** = Raul's Branch (`CHR-02`, [[the-centauri-clone]]), Hull Four's senior
> surviving Ghost, holding its High Clock seat by default; **RAFE'S PARTNER** (E4's woman, unnamed), seen only in a
> small feed of the base's commons; **TEO** (`CHR-09`) in the boat, frozen. Dialogue sparse: four lines.

*Castes (`WR-B1`): Kai, Rafe and Rafe's partner are Ghosts; the High Clock members on the gallery are Ghosts. Teo is a
Shadow, awake since boarding (lifeboats can't hold deep stasis, `Q-63`), his mind on the boat's small Tier 0 stack
(`Q-62`). Every tag is in Kai's Lens (`WR-L2`). Clocks (`WR-T1`, `WR-T4`): Kai at Tier 2, 3,600×, the clock Hull One
allows each received Ghost on arrival (`WR-T5`); Rafe has come down a tier from the Apex to meet them. Rafe's partner,
beamed out earlier by priority, has already gone down to the base's clock, Tier 1, 60× slower than Kai. The reel spans about three
of Kai's subjective days, which is 72 real seconds (259,200 s ÷ 3,600); Boat 3's last approach and clamp take those 72
seconds. A half-second blink lasts half an hour at Tier 2, so every cut catches the docking light mid-blink. Series
clock: about T+4.2. Bay C's three boats left Hull Four about 32 real hours after the strike (E12) and dock in turn; Boat
3 docks about 85 hours after the strike, some four real hours after Hull Four's zero and Kai's beam. Spends (`WR-E1`):
Kai's clock and Rafe's session in watts, from Hull One's reactor; each received body in chemistry; each boat's crossing
in chemistry (fuel and breath). Body 0%.*

**1 · EXTREME CLOSE (9:16) · HOOK (0:00–0:05).** A lifeboat's docking light, halfway through a blink. It stays
halfway. [SOUND: server hum, nothing else.]

**2 · PULL BACK — the observation deck · CONTEXT (0:05–1:15, shots 2–5).** The light is on the nose of a small, scarred
lifeboat hanging against the stars, a body's width short of Hull One's dock. In the glass, KAI's reflection: the render
of a face, clean, unmarked. Kai lifts a hand to the glass; it leaves no mark. [UI: *HULL ONE · TIER 2 · RECEIVED*. The
cracked disc glyph at the frame edge.] [UI, on the boat: *BOAT 3 · C-09 · → HULL ONE*.]

**3 · THE ARRAY.** On the deck's wall feed, the fleet: lit hulls at their spacing, and one alongside with no lights at
all. [UI, small, corner: *HULL FOUR · DARK · Δv 0.000*.] Kai's eyes pass over it.

**4 · CUT — the glass, the second day.** The boat a little nearer. Through its port, in a single hard work-light, two
bays. In one, TEO, awake in his own thin face, eyes open, frozen mid-breath. In the other, nothing: a bay lit for a
body, empty, tagged [UI: *C-10*]. Kai counts, a finger on the glass, the way Kai counts everything. One.

**5 · THE BOARD.** Kai turns to the ramp board on the deck wall: the `HULL FOUR` column dark from end to end. In the
`HULL ONE` column, the bottom eight slots dark. A little above them, freshly chalked: [UI: *21 · KAI · QUEUED*.] Kai
looks at their own name for a long beat.

**6 · RAFE · FRICTION (1:15–2:15, shots 6–9).** A man stands at the far end of the deck, reading. RAFE. A column of
names hangs in front of him, almost all grey. Behind him, on a raised gallery, figures stand very still in good light,
collar tags bright: [UI: *1:1000*, *1:2000*]. Rafe's collar carries a tag frame with nothing in it. Kai's eye goes to
the empty frame and away.

> **RAFE** *(not looking up):* The Station.
>
> **KAI:** Bay C. Maintenance.

*(He runs a finger down the column. One name, near the foot, turns from grey to white: KAI.)*

> **RAFE:** You were the last beam.
>
> **KAI:** *(beat)* Who else?

**7 · HIS ANSWER.** Rafe doesn't answer. He looks past Kai, through the glass, at the boat, at the one face in the
port. Beside his column of names hangs a small window he keeps open: Hull One's base commons at Tier 1, nearly frozen,
and in it the woman Kai met at the ramp's glass in E4, mid-step. His eyes go to it once, and back to the boat.

**8 · CUT — the glass, the third day.** The boat's nose meets the dock. [SOUND: one deep tremor under the floor.] The
docking light is caught mid-blink again, as it was on the first day.

**9 · THE BILL.** On the board, [UI: *RECEIVED · 8*] turns to [UI: *9*], and slot 22 goes dark. The dark stops there.
Slot 21 stays lit. Kai sees it stay lit.

**10 · THE SQUARE · TURN (2:15–2:30, shots 10–11).** In Kai's bottom row the "arrive" square, the one Kai touched out
at the Apex (E15), relights. A thin line draws from it, across the glass, to the docked boat: to Teo's face, and to the lit, empty bay
beside him. Kai looks at the square. Kai looks at the empty bay.

**11 · HOLD.** Wide, the deck from behind: Kai at the glass, Rafe at the far end with his grey column, the boat at the
dock, and in the corner of the wall feed the dark hull, keeping pace. Cut to black.

*(Theme in subtext, not stated: the faster clock Kai bled for turns rescue into stillness and cost into motion. Rafe
calls the hull by its crew's word, S1E15's, and the serial viewer joins that reel to this one (a-ha #5 payoff). The
"arrive" square comes back pointed at someone else's body: at Teo, who was told "Next one" (E12), and at the empty bay
that was Kai's. Teo's body is the slot that just went dark; Kai's slot is still lit, for now. Rafe's empty
collar and his grey column are the High Clock's whole argument in one man: no one rated him for this, and he is doing
it anyway. The dark hull at Δv 0 is correct today, and the reel lets it sit unremarked; E20 is the reminder.)*
