# Script — S1E14: "The Centauri Data Beam"

> [[setting-sail-tier-descent]] S1E14 · Act III (Phase C) · **interstellar logistics** · Raul **70% →
> 30% organic** ([[progressive-digitalization]]) · POV: Raul · register: split-frame + Albers terminals.
> 9:16 · ~2.5 min. To go to the stars, Raul has to copy himself.

**HOOK (0:00–0:05).** A laser-beam array fires a packet of light at the sky with a *crack.* Raul stands at the
launch glass in a **leased proxy** (`Q-86`); his reflection fractures in it — two of him for an instant.

**ACT I — CONTEXT (0:05–1:15).** Raul has drawn a deep-space assignment. He learns the rules of
**[[the-cosmic-clone-paradox|data-beaming]]**: his mind, context, and ledger copied to a laser array and
fired across the void — *while his original keeps running on Earth.* But the bandwidth is brutal; his
70%-organic brain can't carry it before the launch window shuts. He must accept a **Neural-Synthetic Upgrade** — replacing more of the
last grey matter with quantum processors.
> **TECH:** Your brain won't carry the beam in time. Forty percent has to go. Sign there.
> **RAUL (hesitating over the last of himself):** …And what's left of me, after?

[UI: a bottom Intention square stays empty. Then Raul fills it himself, slowly: *Go.*]

**ACT II — FRICTION (1:15–2:15).** He signs. Surgical rigs replace skull and sensory cortex; **Raul drops to 30%
organic.** Under the stress his avatar's hands flicker to **metallic wireframe.** Then the beam splits him:
the screen cleaves into **two vertical panels** — left, the **Earth Instance** resuming his routine;
right, the **beamed copy** flashing away into black. Both are *him.* Neither will ever be wholly him
again.

**RESOLUTION / CLIFFHANGER (2:15–2:30).** At the glass, the Earth-Raul lowers the proxy's chrome hands and
watches his own copy vanish toward Alpha Centauri. [UI: the fractured glyph now reads on **two** frames at once.] Cut to
black.

glitch ([[progressive-digitalization]]). Sets up E15 (the clone) and E16 (the collision). Staging (`Q-86`,
`WR-B6`): Raul's body stays in its capsule, where the rigs operate; he attends the launch glass by leased proxy at
