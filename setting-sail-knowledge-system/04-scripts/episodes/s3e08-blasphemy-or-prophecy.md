# Production Breakdown — S3E08: "Blasphemy or Prophecy"

> Detailed production/cinematography breakdown for [[setting-sail-the-cartographers-ledger]] **Season 3, Episode 8**
> (Act II). **The Purist Village (`PLC-06`), the meeting house, Tier 0; the law-chambers, Tier 2.** The tribe splits:
> is a Grid-thing in a Purist womb an abomination to be cut out, or a return some elders call prophecy (`Q-107`), God
> come to die among them? The **oldest elder** is loudest for cutting it out (plant → E20, `Q-106`). They split on
> everything but one promise: **the child is never told** (`Q-60`). Purists live off the Grid, so Full Disclosure
> can't reach the vow; it holds only as long as people do (`WR-S5`). Meanwhile **Vance** (`CHR-04`) learns that a shard
> of the **root key** is loose, inside a pregnancy. The only cold light in the hut is Raul's shell, and that is how.
> World: [[the-personal-blockchain]] · [[intention-in-context-law]] · [[the-distributed-president]].

## Setup & hook
A flat old hand slaps the meeting-house table and the cups jump. The oldest elder doesn't raise his voice. He doesn't
need to.

## Cinematography — firelight and one cold point
| Element | The meeting house (real) | The law-chambers (in-grid) |
|---------|--------------------------|----------------------------|
| **Lighting** | One central fire, Caravaggio (`VIS-15`): faces lit from below, the room's edges black. In the corner, the **only cold light**: Raul's optics, a steady blue point. | **Albers** (`VIS-15`): nested square archways receding, each a slightly different grey-gold; the law-feed scrolling down the walls. |
| **Camera** | Handheld, circling the fire as the room divides; a slow drift that keeps returning, almost by accident, to the blue point in the corner. | Locked, perfectly centred, deep perspective through the arches. |
| **Sound** | Fire, voices overlapping, a child sent out; at the promise, silence. | A high clean tone; no echo. |

**The split is blocking, not speech.** As the argument runs, people move: those for cutting it out drift to the
oldest elder's side of the fire, the others to the old woman's. By the end the fire stands between two halves of a
tribe.

## UI behavior (the Perspective Lens)
No Purist has a Lens (`WR-L2`); nothing is drawn on Ruth or the tribe.
- **Vance's Lens (in-grid):** the law-feed on the archway walls. He lifts out one small signature from the foot of a
  clause, `// M.C.`, and his Lens tags it **`ROOT KEY`**. A hair-thin line runs from it out through the arches to a
  single entry on a public chain: header `RAUL`, the entry `Carrying: 1 · Caste: —` (E7). His own collar in the
  frame's corner: `1:10000`.
- **Raul's Lens, once (the turn):** his own chain entry, `Carrying: 1 · Caste: —`, and under it a new line: `Read:
  VANCE · 1:10000`.

## Philosophical climax
The tribe argues the oldest question a people can argue: is the stranger in our midst a curse or a gift? They can't
agree, and the reel doesn't decide for them. Both sides are right about something. The oldest elder sees the Grid
reaching into the one place it never could; the old woman sees the maker come home to the only people who can still
let her die. What they *can* agree on is mercy: whatever she is, she will never be told. A vow made with no ledger,
held by nothing but the people who make it. And in the corner sits the one thing in the room that keeps a ledger, its
cold light on, recording the line they couldn't finish, published by default to anyone who looks. The tribe's secret
holds. The Grid's doesn't.

## Script (draft v1)
> 9:16 vertical · ~2.5 min · cinematic photoreal · *Black Mirror / Silo* register. **THE OLDEST ELDER** (Purist, `Q-106`,
> unnamed by design); **THE OLD WOMAN** (Purist; the S2E19 woman who murmured "Hurried"); **RUTH** (`CHR-07`), about
> four months pregnant; **ADAM** (`CHR-06`); **RAUL** (`CHR-02`), in the corner; **VANCE** (`CHR-04`), in-grid;
> **ELDERS**. About forty spoken words.

*Castes (`WR-B1`): the oldest elder, the old woman, Ruth and the elders are Purists, no Lens; Adam is a cyborg of mostly
regrown flesh, Lens off (`Q-98`); Raul is a Ghost in a shell, Lens on, his chain open by default (Full Disclosure has an
opt-out he never took, `Q-124`); Vance is a Ghost (`Q-90`), collar `1:10000` (forged, S2E14). Mara: the President is a
Ghost (`Q-98`); the fetus has no caste yet. Timing (`WR-T4`): the village runs at Tier 0, about T+20.9, Ruth about four
months along, Ruth and Adam about 20. Vance reads in the law-chambers at Tier 2 (3,600×): his whole scene, about twenty
seconds of screen, is about **six real milliseconds** (20 ÷ 3,600 ≈ 0.0056 s), cut into the village's argument at the
moment the room is loudest.*

**1 · THE HAND · HOOK (0:00–0:05).** The meeting house. An old, flat hand slaps the table; the cups jump. THE OLDEST
ELDER, seated, very calm.

> **THE OLDEST ELDER:** Cut it out.

**2 · THE ROOM · CONTEXT (0:05–1:15, shots 2–4).** Wider, circling. The whole tribe around one fire. RUTH sits at its
centre, a shawl over the swell of four months; ADAM stands behind her, not touching her. In the far corner, on a
stool, RAUL: chrome, still, and his optics the one cold point in a room of fire.

**3 · THE OTHER SIDE.** THE OLD WOMAN, across the fire.

> **THE OLD WOMAN:** Or it came home. To die where it can.

A murmur on her side: two elders, low, almost together: *"Prophecy."* From the oldest elder's side, one word back,
flat: *"Blasphemy."* People begin to move: a shuffle of feet, a bench dragged. The fire starts to divide the room.

**4 · THE QUESTION.** The oldest elder looks at Ruth's belly, then at Adam, whose shirt hides the chrome seam everyone
knows is there.

> **THE OLDEST ELDER:** Whose is it?
>
> **RUTH:** Hers.

Adam doesn't look away from the elder. The elder looks away first.

**5 · THE LAW-CHAMBERS · FRICTION (1:15–2:15, shots 5–8).** Hard cut to stillness. Albers arches receding into gold-grey;
the law-feed running down their walls. VANCE, alone, centred, his collar `1:10000`. He reaches into the feed and lifts
out a signature from the foot of a clause, as small as a crumb. [UI: `// M.C.` → tagged **`ROOT KEY`**.]

**6 · RECALL CUE (≤ 2 s).** S2E11: the same `// M.C.` sliding past at the foot of a rewritten clause, unread, on the way
to Adam's `PENDING` line. Gone.

**7 · THE TRACE.** [UI: from the tag, a hair-thin line runs out of Vance's hand, down through the arches and out of the
chamber, and ends on one entry of a public chain: header `RAUL`; the entry `Carrying: 1 · Caste: —`.] Vance reads it
twice. He tilts his head, the way a man does at a sound he didn't expect from an empty house. Then he smiles, very
slightly.

**8 · THE PROMISE.** Back in the meeting house: the room is in two halves now, the fire between them, everyone
talked out. Ruth stands. She is the only one on her feet.

> **RUTH:** Whatever she is, she's never told.

Silence. The old woman nods first. Then her side. Then, one by one, the other side, and last, after a long moment,
the oldest elder: one short nod, his eyes on the fire. Adam nods. *(No one writes it down. There is nothing here to
write it on.)*

**9 · THE COLD POINT · TURN (2:15–2:30, shots 9–10).** The camera, drifting, comes to rest on the corner. Raul's
optics. His POV, locked off: [UI: his own chain entry, `Carrying: 1 · Caste: —`, and beneath it, new: `Read: VANCE ·
1:10000`.] Raul doesn't move. He looks at the fire, at Ruth, at the people who just promised, and back at the line.

**10 · FINAL IMAGE.** The tribe dispersing into the night, two halves leaving by two doors. The fire burning down. In
the dark corner, one cold blue point that doesn't go out. Hard cut to black.

*(Theme in subtext, never stated: the tribe can't agree on what she is, only on what she must be spared, and a promise
with no ledger is the strongest thing in the room. The only machine in the room is the leak. Raul, who came to the
village to be among people who don't record, has been recording all along; his counsel's trustworthiness, the S2
vault's question, takes its first hit from his own Lens. Nobody in the reel says the word "secret".)*
