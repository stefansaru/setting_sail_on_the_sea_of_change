# Production Breakdown — S3E05: "The Decision to Become Small"

> Detailed production/cinematography breakdown for [[setting-sail-the-cartographers-ledger]] **Season 3, Episode 5**
> (Act I's turn and close, *The God's Hole*). **Tier 0, the Purist Village** (`PLC-06`), night, **T ≈ +20.5**. Rafe
> builds the missing input he promised in E4, and **Raul**, who doesn't argue, answers him the only way he answers
> anyone: he brings him up to the village and lets him look. Rafe, in a leased shell, sees mortal life for the first
> time. His perfect part comes back unopened; E1's request bounces off the one place the law can't reach. Then the
> President **decides**: her source code resolves, line by line, into a **genome helix** aimed at the village. End of
> Act I: God chooses flesh. The mechanism (what she compresses, what fits, what is forgotten, who carries it) is E6–E7's
> and stays unsaid. **Mara: 0% organic** (the President, a Ghost, `Q-98`). World: [[the-distributed-president]] ·
> [[the-memory-substrate]] · [[shells-with-ghosts-within]] · [[the-perspective-lens-ui]].

## Setup & hook
In Rafe's Lens, turning in his open palm: a grey sphere he has built, flawless, matched edge for edge to the hatched one
in her ring. A small line under it reads *fit: 100%*. He lifts his hand and lets it go, up into the law.

## Cinematography — the firelight and the feed
| Element | The village at night (raw sight) | The Lenses |
|---------|----------------------------------|------------|
| **Lighting** | Single-source **Caravaggio** (`VIS-15`): one fire, oil lamps in doorways. Skin, wool, smoke. Two cold figures at the edge: Raul's chrome and Rafe's new shell. | The law-feed's mustard ribbon (E1) along the top; Rafe's grey sphere; then the helix, which starts in the feed's **mustard** and warms as it forms, until it is the colour of the fire beneath it. |
| **Camera** | **Handheld, breathing**, close on hands and faces (S2E1's rule). | **Locked** whenever we are in a Lens (`VIS-04`). At the turn the locked frame holds the helix *and* the fire, and for the first time the two grammars share a shot. |
| **Sound** | Fire, a baby feeding, a child's sudden cry and its stopping, wind over the ridge. | The choral hum, faint; when the helix forms, the hum drops away under the fire, as if the feed had gone quiet to listen. |

**Rafe's shell.** A cheap leased machine body, new to 1:1 and slightly wrong in it: a beat late on every step, too
still when it stops. Rafe has not worn a body of any kind since the beam (S1E14).

## UI behavior (the Perspective Lens)
Raul's Lens and Rafe's (both Ghosts in shells, both with Lenses); every villager is a Purist, so nothing is tagged
on them (`WR-L2`), and nothing in the reel is in a Purist's view.
- **The part (hook):** Rafe's grey sphere, `fit: 100%`. His *build* square lit, *stay* lit, *love* grey with
  `Recipient: 4.4 ly` (E2), carried quietly at the bottom.
- **E1's clause:** `// M.C. → Tier 0 · sector: unmeasured · PENDING`, signed `mara.c` (E3).
- **Returned:** the sphere rises to the clause and comes back down to his palm, untouched. [UI: `RETURNED`.]
- **The bounce:** the clause's grey `PENDING` flips to `NOT APPLICABLE · sector: unmeasured`. The law can't execute in a
  place it cannot reach (S2E11's un-ticked patch).
- **The decision (the turn):** under the signature, the code beneath the clause scrolls into view, her code, line on
  line, and begins to **resolve**: each line collapsing into a short run of four letters, `A C G T`, and the runs
  twisting round each other, line by line, into a **double helix**. Its target field stays: `sector: unmeasured`. When
  the last line resolves, the `NOT APPLICABLE` tag goes out. She has stopped asking.
- **No glyph beat** for her. Raul's solid ring sits at the edge and doesn't move.

## Philosophical climax
E4 ended on a hole, and an engineer looking at a hole sees a part. Rafe builds it perfectly, and it is refused
unopened, not because it is badly made but because it is *made*: no part fits a pain that has to be lived through.
Raul doesn't say so (he never says so). He brings Rafe up the ridge and lets him watch a child fall and be held, a
mother feed a baby, a village keep the things the Grid can't. The law asked to come here and can't, because here is
outside the law. So the author of the world finds the only form that crosses into the unmeasured: not code, not a
shell, not a beam. A seed. The viewer, who has just watched a baby feeding by the fire, finishes the sentence.

## Script (draft v1)
> 9:16 vertical · ~2.5 min · cinematic photoreal · *Black Mirror / Silo* register. **RAUL** (`CHR-02`); **RAFE**
> (`Q-14`, `Q-22`), in a leased shell; **ADAM** (`CHR-06`); **RUTH** (`CHR-07`); **A NURSING MOTHER**, **A SMALL CHILD**,
> **AN OLD WOMAN**, villagers. About 6 spoken words.

*Castes (`WR-B1`): Raul is a Ghost in a shell, Lens on, alignment off (`Q-119`). Rafe is a Ghost in a shell: a leased
machine body, his first time in the village (the blueprint's "leased shell when he is in the village"). Adam is a
cyborg of mostly regrown flesh, Lens off (`Q-98`). Ruth, the mother, her baby, the child, the old woman and the
villagers are Purists, no Lens (`WR-L2`). The President is a Ghost (`Q-98`), present only as code. Clocks (`WR-T1`,
`WR-T4`): the village is **Tier 0**, one real clock. The helix forms above, where she runs up to Tier 3: it takes about
40 real seconds of the reel, which is about **40 of her days** (40 × 86,400 s). Series clock: T ≈ +20.5, the close of
Act I, about a real month after E1; the pregnancy and the birth (E6–E10) fall inside the next year, T +20.5 → ≈ +21.5.*

**1 · THE PART · HOOK (0:00–0:05).** Rafe's Lens, locked: in his open palm a grey sphere, flawless, turning. [UI: *fit:
100%*.] His *build* square lit. He lets it go, up into the mustard ribbon of the law.

**2 · THE RIDGE · CONTEXT (0:05–1:15, shots 2–4).** Wide, night: the city's tower light behind the ridge (S2E1), and
coming down the path, two cold figures. RAUL, steady. RAFE in his new leased shell, a beat late on every step. Raul
doesn't explain where they are going. He walks Rafe in toward the fire.

**3 · THE VILLAGE — handheld.** Firelight. A MOTHER feeds her baby in a doorway. RUTH at the fire, mending, the work in
her lap. ADAM carries water past with his new hands; he nods to Raul and looks once, hard, at the stranger who stands
exactly the way Raul stands. An OLD WOMAN watches Rafe's shell go by.

> **OLD WOMAN** *(low, not to him):* Smoke.

Rafe stops at the edge of the light, too still, the way a machine stops.

**4 · THE FALL.** A SMALL CHILD running past the fire trips and goes down hard on the packed earth. A beat; then the cry,
huge. Nobody fixes anything. Ruth puts down her mending and picks the child up, and holds it, and the crying goes on in
her arms until it doesn't. Rafe watches all of it. Raul watches Rafe.

**5 · RETURNED · FRICTION (1:15–2:15, shots 5–7).** Rafe's Lens: above the village the ribbon runs bare (E1), and at the
edge waits her clause [UI: `// M.C. → Tier 0 · sector: unmeasured · PENDING`]. His sphere reaches it, and comes back
down, untouched, into his palm. [UI: `RETURNED`.] He sends it again. It comes back again.

**6 · THE BOUNCE.** In both Lenses at once, the clause's grey tag flips. [UI: `NOT APPLICABLE · sector: unmeasured`.] The
law has asked to come here, and here is the one place the law can't go. Rafe closes his hand on his perfect part.

> **RAFE:** Then what does she want?
>
> *(Raul doesn't answer. His eyes are on the doorway: the mother, the baby at her breast.)*

**7 · THE CODE.** Under the bounced clause, beneath the signature `mara.c`, her code scrolls up into view: line on line
of it, more than the frame holds, the oldest text in the world.

**8 · THE HELIX · TURN (2:15–2:30, shots 8–9).** Raul's Lens, locked, the fire below. The code begins to **resolve**: each
line collapsing into a short run of four letters, `A C G T`, and the runs twisting round each other, line by line, into
a **double helix**. As it forms its colour warms from mustard toward the fire under it. [SOUND: the choral hum falls
away; only the fire.] [UI: target, still: `sector: unmeasured`.] The last line resolves. The `NOT APPLICABLE` tag goes
out. Nothing replaces it.

**9 · FINAL IMAGE.** One locked frame holding both worlds for the first time: the helix turning slowly in Raul's Lens,
and through it, in raw firelight, the village going to sleep. Ruth sets the quiet child down. The mother lifts the
baby to her shoulder. Rafe, in his borrowed body, stands with his fist closed on the part nobody needed. Hard cut to
black on the helix completing its first turn.

*(Theme in subtext, not stated: a better-built part is refused because it was built; what she lacks can't be fitted,
only lived. The law can't reach the one place where people are born and hurt and held, so its author stops asking to
be let in and writes herself into the only thing that can enter: a seed. Raul argued none of it; he took his other
self to the fire and let him watch. God chooses flesh, and the last thing on screen she can see is a mother lifting a
baby.)*
