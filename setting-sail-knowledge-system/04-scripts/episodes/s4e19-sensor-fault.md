# Production Breakdown — S4E19: "Sensor Fault"

> Detailed production/cinematography breakdown for [[setting-sail-the-seed-ledger]] **Season 4, Episode
> 19** (Act IV, Phase D). **Tier 3, still on Rafe's ration** (`Q-109`). Kai pulls Hull Four's last log from the
> fleet's shared array at Apex speed and reads what no one at the base could. The hit's timestamp sits directly under a
> line Kai knows: a burst logged leaving Hull Four, sent by Teo, the night before. **Two lines, an order of events, no
> "because"** (`Q-66`, `Q-111`). Kai tells no one: not the base, not Teo (`Q-35`: the act stays Teo's; the silence is
> mercy). The disc glyph is **re-drawn solid**, as a Ghost's. The Unlisted are never shown (`Q-30`, `WR-R2`). Body
> **0%**. World: [[the-firing-protocol]] · [[the-lure-beacon]] · [[the-sealed-manifest]] · [[the-seed-fleet]] ·
> [[velocity-tiers]] · [[the-perspective-lens-ui]].

## Setup & hook
Extreme close on **text falling like rain**: tens of thousands of log lines streaming down a dark pane in Flavin
green, too fast for anyone but an Apex mind. A hand reaches into the stream and it stops. Pull back: Kai alone in the
array's archive, the stream frozen around them in mid-fall, every line legible. The whole reel is reading. The danger
is in where two lines happen to sit.

## Philosophical climax
The log doesn't lie. It's the one thing on the fleet that can't. It also doesn't explain. A burst leaves Hull Four at
thirteen minutes past two in the morning; at twenty to ten, in the last second of the lock, something comes through
the window. Put those two lines next to
each other in front of a base that just lost a hull, and the base will write the missing word itself, and the word will
be Teo's name. The High Clock saw that and chose two others: *sensor fault*. Kai, reading it at the Apex, does
exactly what the High Clock did, for the same reason, and knows it. Further down, the same log holds Kai's own two
lines, a tether severed and a beam sent, six minutes apart, with no "because" between them either. *I escaped* and
*my body is dust*: the log keeps them in one frame without asking how. That's what the glyph learns to do. It closes.

## Script (draft v1)
> 9:16 vertical · ~2.5 min · cinematic photoreal · *Black Mirror / Silo* register. **KAI** (`CHR-08`), a Ghost, alone
> at the Apex on Rafe's ration; **TEO** (`CHR-09`) on the base's commons, frozen, unknowing. **No dialogue.** The log
> is the script.

*Castes (`WR-B1`): Kai is a Ghost; Teo is a Shadow, re-stilled since E17, his mind on watch at Tier 1 (`WR-B5`); the
base on the commons is mostly Ghosts on watch with a few Shadows. Every tag, Teo's square included, is in Kai's Lens
(`WR-L2`). Clocks (`WR-T1`, `WR-T4`, `WR-T5`): Tier 3, 86,400×. Rafe's second ration is six real seconds, six subjective
days (518,400 s); the reel ends at about *REAL +0:00:06*. Teo, at Tier 1, runs 1,440× slower than Kai: one second of his
laugh is 24 of Kai's minutes. The burst (02:13, E9) and the hit (09:40:59: the screen drops at 09:40:00, E9a, and the
grain arrives at the 59th second of the lock, E10) are about 7 h 28 m apart. The strike plus 81 hours is 18:40:59 on day
14,686; Kai's beam line sits on it (E15: the beam fires at `00:00:00.0`), and the severance line 6 m 12 s before (E15:
the cut at `00:06:12`; the climb and the four Apex minutes of packing, `Q-79`, fit between). Voyage day 14,683 is about 40.2 years from launch
(T−36 to T+4.2). Series clock: about T+4.2. Spends (`WR-E1`): watts, from the Hull Four seat's ration; Rafe pays
(`Q-109`). Body 0%.*

**1 · EXTREME CLOSE (9:16) · HOOK (0:00–0:05).** Green text streaming down a dark pane, too fast to read. A hand enters
the stream. The stream stops. [SOUND: a dry rustle, then nothing.]

**2 · PULL BACK — the archive · CONTEXT (0:05–1:15, shots 2–5).** KAI stands among columns of frozen green lines, the
whole log hanging in the air like rain stopped mid-fall. [UI: *TIER 3 · 6 s REAL · RATION: RAFE · HULL FOUR SEAT*.]
[UI: *HULL FOUR · LAST LOG · 41,206 LINES*.]

**3 · THE COLLAPSE.** Kai sweeps a hand. Routine folds away in sheets: heartbeat lines, coolant, roster, the bay's
counts. [UI: *ROUTINE · 41,198 HIDDEN*.] The room empties to eight lines standing in the dark, spaced like gravestones.

**4 · THE SERIAL LINE.** Kai walks the eight. One passes at the edge of frame, never centred, never stopped on. [UI:
*BEACON · ANALYSIS · EARLIEST PATTERN MATCH: PACKET · EARTH ORIGIN · IN TRANSIT*.] Kai's eyes don't stop on it either.

**5 · THE FIRST LINE.** Kai stops at a line with a name on it. [UI: *DAY 14,683 · 02:13 · TX · HULL FOUR · 1.2 MW · 0.4 s
· KEYED: C-09 · TEO*.] A long beat. Kai paused for the night at ten past eleven, with Teo still at the decoder (E9). In
the morning Kai asked him if he'd slept. *Some.* (E10.)

**6 · THE SECOND LINE · FRICTION (1:15–2:15, shots 6–9).** Directly beneath it, nothing between: [UI: *DAY 14,683 ·
09:40:59 · FWD ARRAY · SENSOR FAULT · LOCK 00:59*.] The words Kai watched rewrite themselves on the bay board (E10). The
room floods Rothko red, the Despair field of E10, and holds one beat, and drains back to green. Kai doesn't move. The two lines stand one above the other. Nothing on
either says *because*.

**7 · THE COMMONS.** Kai's Lens finds Hull One's Tier-1 commons. TEO is there, on watch, among others, mid-laugh, one
hand half-raised in the gesture we last saw through the port in E17, frozen. [UI: *TEO · SHADOW · ON WATCH*.] Under him,
his one Intention square, still lit after all this: [UI: *help*.]

**8 · THE SQUARE.** In Kai's own bottom row, faint, a square begins to form. [UI: *tell Teo*.] Kai looks at it. On the
commons Teo's laugh has not finished. Kai looks back at the two lines. The square dims, and goes out. No thread draws.

**9 · KAI'S LINES.** Kai walks on, to the last lines standing. Two of them are Kai's. [UI: *DAY 14,686 · 18:34:47 ·
C-10 · KAI · ORGANIC MASS 0% · TETHER SEVERED*.] Below it: [UI: *DAY 14,686 · 18:40:59 · KAI · BEAM · → HULL ONE*.]
Six minutes apart. Nothing between them. Kai reads them both, once.

**10 · THE CLOSE · TURN (2:15–2:30, shots 10–11).** Kai lifts a hand and the eight lines fold back into the stream; the
stream folds back into the dark, the way the manifest's archways closed in E18. Nothing is sent. [UI: *REAL +0:00:06*.
The ration tag greys.] At the frame edge, the cracked disc glyph Kai has carried since E10 re-draws: the crack fills, the
bore stays open, the disc is **solid**.

**11 · HOLD.** One frame: the solid glyph at the edge, and far below on the commons, Teo, mid-laugh,
his *help* square lit. Cut to black.

*(Theme in subtext, not stated: correlation only. The log puts the burst above the hit and never says why, and a base
would supply the why and the name. The High Clock wrote "sensor fault" to stop that, and now Kai does the same, and the
*tell Teo* square going out is mercy and the High Clock's own sin at once. Kai's own two lines, a tether severed and a
beam sent, show the same grammar turned on Kai: "I escaped" and "my body is dust," with nothing joining them. Kai
holds both, and the glyph re-draws solid. The serial viewer catches the Earth packet in passing (a-ha #4): the lure was
written from something that came from home. The cold viewer gets two lines and a friend who doesn't know. a-ha #16, "he
started it," is restated by the order of the lines alone.)*
