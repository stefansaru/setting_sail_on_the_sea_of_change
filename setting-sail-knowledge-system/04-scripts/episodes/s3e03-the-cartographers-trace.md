# Production Breakdown — S3E03: "The Cartographer's Trace"

> Detailed production/cinematography breakdown for [[setting-sail-the-cartographers-ledger]] **Season 3, Episode 3**
> (Act I, *The God's Hole*). **The grey layer under the Grid**, Tier 0, **T ≈ +20.5**. Following E1's thread down,
> **Raul** (`CHR-02`) and **Rafe** find the breadcrumbs a person left in the world's oldest floor, re-homed from the
> legacy traces (`FCT-001`, `FCT-003`, `FCT-005`): a foghorn that is a **recorded voice**; a photo the Lens **cannot
> tag**; an account, `mara.c`, with **no score**. Both divers know the face: the woman behind the last window, seen at
> eight (S1E3b, a-ha #1 reminder). The god has a **name** and a **grief**. The bear and the line stay unexplained until
> E19. **Mara: 0% organic** (the President, a Ghost, `Q-98`), seen only as she was. World: [[the-shadow-grid]] ·
> [[the-perspective-lens-ui]] · [[the-personal-blockchain]] · [[the-memory-substrate]].

## Setup & hook
Fog on grey light. Somewhere below, unseen, a foghorn sounds: long, low, patient. Rafe catches it in his hands as a
waveform and slows it, and slows it again, and inside the horn there is a **breath**. A woman's, holding one note.

## Cinematography — the grey, and one warm thing
| Element | The grey layer | The photo |
|---------|----------------|-----------|
| **Lighting** | Sea-grey and blue, clean white metal (`VIS-01` as fossil), lit by dead interfaces; fog lying in the corridors like low water. | The only **warm** object in the reel: a print in a frame, its colour a little faded, lit by nothing but itself, as if it had kept a room's light. |
| **Camera** | Machine-smooth tracking (`VIS-04`) down the corridors; wide, heavy negative space. | **Handheld** for the first time in two reels, when Raul picks it up: the camera breathes with him. |
| **Sound** | The foghorn, far below, at long intervals. Water. The divers' steps. | Silence. When the horn sounds again at the end, we hear the breath in it without help. |

**The room.** A workroom at the bottom of a corridor: a long desk, and on every wall **hand-drawn maps**, coastlines and
soundings in fine ink, the work of someone who charted things. Every object in it is tagged. One isn't.

## UI behavior (the Perspective Lens)
Two Lenses, Raul's and Rafe's; no Purists in this reel.
- **The waveform (`FCT-001`):** in Rafe's Lens the foghorn opens as a long grey wave. He stretches it; under the horn's
  edge a second, smaller wave appears, human-shaped: breath. No label. The Lens doesn't name it either.
- **The room, tagged:** Raul's Lens does what it always does, small white tags on everything: `desk`, `chair`, `glass`,
  `map`, `map`, `map`.
- **The photo (`FCT-003`, the one object the Grid cannot tag):** the tag box opens over the frame and **stays empty**, its
  cursor blinking. Raul's Lens tries again. Empty.
- **The accounts (`FCT-005`):** a terminal on the desk lists the layer's users, oldest at the top, each with a name and a
  score in a column. The oldest row: `mara.c`. Its score field: **blank**. Not zero; nothing. (The column stays unnamed;
  E14 calls back to this blank.)
- **The resolve (the turn):** E1's clause, carried in Raul's Lens, its signature still `// M.C. · signer: unresolved`.
  The field clears and fills: `signer: mara.c`.

## Philosophical climax
In S1E3b an eight-year-old asked who started the loop in the last window, and the Guide answered a beat too late with
the line it always uses. Now both men who were that boy stand in a room of maps and look at the same face from a
second side: not a loop in a window but a woman in a photograph, laughing at something below the frame. The Grid can
tag every object in creation except her. A god, it turns out, has initials, an account nobody scored, a voice left in
a horn, and a picture she kept. The reel doesn't say what she lost. It shows where the photograph was cut.

## Script (draft v1)
> 9:16 vertical · ~2.5 min · cinematic photoreal · *Black Mirror / Silo* register. **RAUL** (`CHR-02`); **RAFE**
> (`Q-14`, `Q-22`). The woman in the photo is **Mara** (`CHR-01`), never in motion, never speaking. About 10 spoken
> words.

*Castes (`WR-B1`): Raul is a Ghost in a shell, his shell idle in the village at Tier 0 while he dives; Rafe is a Ghost
with no shell. Mara, in the photo, had a body then; as the President she is a Ghost (`Q-98`), unseen. Clocks (`WR-T1`,
`WR-T4`): Tier 0 throughout; the old layer plays only at 1:1, so one clock. The reel cuts across about a week of real
reading after E2. Memory (`WR-B4`): both men saw the last window in-grid at eight, before the fork (S1E14), so both
recall it perfectly and identically. T ≈ +20.5.*

**1 · FOG — the horn · HOOK (0:00–0:05).** Grey light, fog lying in a corridor like low water. Far below, unseen: a
**foghorn**, long and low. RAFE's hand closes on the sound. [UI, Rafe's Lens: the horn as a long grey wave.] He pulls it
wider.

**2 · THE BREATH · CONTEXT (0:05–1:15, shots 2–4).** He slows it, and slows it again. Under the horn's edge a second,
smaller wave rises and falls: breath. [SOUND: inside the horn, a woman's breath, holding one note.] He lets go of it.
RAUL has heard it too. Neither says anything. The thread from E1 runs on down the corridor, and they follow it.

**3 · THE CORRIDOR.** Machine-smooth track: white metal, water stains, a small sail on every door. A week of this, in
cuts: the same two figures, smaller each time, further down.

**4 · THE ROOM.** The thread ends at a door with the sail on it. Inside: a long desk; on every wall, maps, hand-drawn,
coastlines and soundings in fine ink. [UI, Raul's Lens: `desk` · `chair` · `glass` · `map` · `map` · `map`.]

**5 · THE PHOTO · FRICTION (1:15–2:15, shots 5–7).** On the desk, face-down, a frame. Raul turns it over. [UI: the tag
box opens over it and **stays empty**, cursor blinking. Again. Empty.] [Camera: goes handheld.] The only warm colour in
the reel: a woman, laughing, her eyes on something **below the frame's edge**, where the print has been cut.

**6 · RECALL CUE (≤ 2 s).** S1E3b, the last window: a woman alone in a dark room, a single clip blinking in front of her,
unplayed. The same face, not laughing.

**7 · TWO WHO WERE EIGHT.** Raul looks up. Rafe is already looking at the photo, and then at him. The one memory both of
them own, from before there were two of them.

> **RAFE:** The last window.
>
> **RAUL:** We were eight.

**8 · THE NAME · TURN (2:15–2:30, shots 8–9).** Rafe wakes the terminal on the desk: the layer's accounts, oldest at the
top, a name and a score on each row. He scrolls to the top. [UI: `mara.c`, and in the score column, **nothing**.] In
Raul's Lens, E1's clause is still open at the edge. Its signature field clears, and fills: [UI: `signer: mara.c`.]

> **RAUL** *(very quietly, the first time anyone has said it):* Mara.

**9 · FINAL IMAGE.** The photo in his hand: the woman laughing at something cut away. Far below, the foghorn sounds
again, and this time we hear the breath in it without anyone slowing it down. Hard cut to black.

*(Theme in subtext, not stated: the Grid can tag every object in the world but the one its maker kept. The god turns
out to be a person who drew maps, left her voice in a foghorn, and never let anyone score her. Her grief is
shown only as an edge: a photograph cut below her eyes, at whatever she was laughing at. Two forks of one boy
recognize her at once, because a fork keeps the past it was made from; that is all they share now.)*
