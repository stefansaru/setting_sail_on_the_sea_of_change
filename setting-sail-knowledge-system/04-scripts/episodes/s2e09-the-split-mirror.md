# Production Breakdown — S2E09: "The Split Mirror"

> Detailed production/cinematography breakdown for [[setting-sail-tier-ascent]] **Season 2, Episode 9** (Act II,
> Phase B′, the deepest subtraction yet). The slotted standalone [[the-split-mirror]] (`Q-11`), recast with **Adam**
> (`CHR-06`) as lead (`Q-76`); the standalone stays Raul's origin short. **Tier 2.** The [[parallel-pedagogy|Merging]]
> (`WLD-18`) dramatized at last, as an **adult convert-Merging**: since the up-clock Adam has run as forked threads,
> by consent (`WR-M1`, `WR-M2`: an adult may fork; the forks end here, in a Merge). To advance, they fuse into one
> ledger: cognition gained, the singular mortal "I" lost. *The Split Mirror:* the mortal self and the grid-self can't
> both survive the fusion, and only one of them is awake to vote. He chooses fusion. The Grid issues him the **hollow
> "Camel's Doughnut"** glyph (`Q-12` mirror: absent → **issued hollow**). Organic **90% → 60%** is scheduled here and
> lands in E10. World: [[parallel-pedagogy]] · [[the-cosmic-clone-paradox]] · [[the-memory-substrate]] ·
> [[progressive-digitalization]] · [[chromatic-expressionism]].

## Setup & hook
The frame splits into three bands inside a hard Albers grid. Three faces, all Adam. Two are awake, in two different
lights, living two different lives. The third is asleep behind plastic, and doesn't move at all. They have never met.

## UI behavior (the Perspective Lens)
Adam's Lens, seen whole across all three bands; the body band is his body feed (S1E6's device), not a Lens of its own
(`WR-L2`).
- **Thread tags:** a small `α` top band, `β` middle band; the bottom band reads only `1:1`.
- **The countdown (across all three):** **`Graduation Protocol · Automated Merging · T−60 s`** (the standalone's
  notice, reused: the Grid gives a late graduate the children's paperwork).
- **The care chart, bottom band (from E3):** a new line, pending: **`Neural-Synthetic Upgrade · Organic 90% → 60%`**
  (S1E14's procedure: the fused mind needs bandwidth the brain can't carry).
- **The hold (shot 6):** at `T−10 s` the count stops and a prompt opens: **`Consent required (adult)`**.
- **The glyph (the turn):** after fusion, the squares and circles settle into the **hollow outline ring**, issued.
  Its name is never shown.
- **The recall (the turn):** E4's scan of Naomi, the wireframe and its tag, **`Cellular decay — advanced`**, recalled
  at full resolution inside the ring.

## Philosophical climax
Every Grid child meets the Merging once, as a child, before they can know what it costs. Adam meets it as a grown man,
with his eyes open, and that is the horror: he gets to choose, and the only part of him that would choose otherwise is
asleep in a box at 1:1, dreaming it's home. The two selves who are awake decide for the one who isn't. What he gains is
real: a mind that holds a city and a hundred and fifty names at once. What he gives is the one thing a vow could keep
whole, and the proof is small: when he reaches for his mother afterward, the ledger hands him the Grid's picture of her,
perfect and tagged, and not the firelit one the dream was holding.

## Script (draft v1)
> 9:16 vertical · ~2.5 min · cinematic photoreal · *Black Mirror / Silo* register. **ADAM-α** and **ADAM-β**, two
> forked threads of Adam (`CHR-06`); **ADAM**, his body, asleep; then **ADAM**, fused. No other cast. Three spoken
> half-lines, in chorus.

*Castes (`WR-B1`): Adam is a cyborg, 90% organic, body in his rented city capsule at 1:1 (`Q-91`). α and β are his
forks, adult, by consent, at Tier 2: real people since divergence (`WR-M1`), and their outcome is **Merge**, here. No
minor is forked (`WR-M2`). Timing (`WR-T4`): about **two real days** at Tier 2 = **about 7,200 days, nearly twenty
years, lived by each thread in parallel** (48 × 3,600 h). His body is 19; each thread's mind about 54. The countdown is
**sixty seconds of his = one sixtieth of a real second**: the body band is a still frame for the whole minute. The
fusion itself takes **one real second, an hour of his** (`WLD-18`), shown as the push-in. The upgrade runs on his body
in real time after this reel and is complete by E10, about **23 real hours** (about **nine and a half years** of his)
later.*

**1 · THREE BANDS · HOOK (0:00–0:05).** The 9:16 frame splits into **three equal horizontal bands** inside a rigid Albers
grid. Three faces, all Adam. Top: awake, cold blue. Middle: awake, laughing, gold. Bottom: eyes closed behind plastic,
a woven cord on the wrist. [UI: `α` · `β` · `1:1`.]

**2 · ADAM-α · CONTEXT (0:05–1:15, shots 2–4).** Top band. ADAM-α stands inside walls of moving data: the city's roads,
loads and flows, and he is holding all of it at once, re-routing a district with a turn of the hand, unhurried. Twenty
years of this. He is brilliant, and calm, and alone.

**3 · ADAM-β.** Middle band. The tribe's field from E7, the same faces, the same beat: ADAM-β among them, laughing on
the shared breath. A shoulder against his. The ring of lights in his top circle, his own on the beat. (The viewer may
realize that this is the Adam they followed through E7 and E8. Nothing says so.)

**4 · THE BODY.** Bottom band. Real time, held still: the rented capsule, amber light, ADAM's body asleep. The eyes
under the lids caught mid-movement, the dreaming of E6, frozen. Laid faintly over the face, like light through water:
**firelight on a lintel post**, and the shadows of notches. [UI: the care chart: `Organic: 90%`.]

**5 · THE NOTICE · FRICTION (1:15–2:15, shots 5–7).** All three bands at once. [UI: **`Graduation Protocol · Automated
Merging · T−60 s`**. In the bottom band, a new pending line: **`Neural-Synthetic Upgrade · Organic 90% → 60%`**.] α
looks up from his data. β looks up from his tribe. They look up at the same instant, with the same face. The count runs
on screen. The voices layer into one chorus:

> **ADAM-α** *(top):* I can hold every road in this city at once…
>
> **ADAM-β** *(middle):* …a hundred and fifty people know my name…
>
> **ADAM** *(bottom: his own voice, younger, far off, as if from inside the dream):* …and I'm home.

**6 · THE HOLD.** At **`T−10 s`** the count **stops**. [UI: **`Consent required (adult)`**.] In the top band α's hand
lifts toward the prompt. In the middle band β's hand lifts, the same hand, the same instant. In the bottom band the
body's hand, with Ruth's cord on its wrist, lies where it lies; it cannot lift; it is asleep. The two waking hands
close on the prompt together. The count resumes.

**7 · T−3.** The firelight on the sleeping face in the bottom band flickers once, the dream still going.

**8 · THE FUSION · TURN (2:15–2:30, shots 8–9).** Zero. The horizontal dividers **vibrate and dissolve** into
bleeding fields of amber and violet; the bottom band's firelight goes out mid-flicker; the frame lines collapse inward.
A fast **push-in** drives the three faces into one point. [SOUND: a high data tone; then silence.] Snap to a single,
high-contrast 9:16 portrait: **ADAM opens his eyes.** Calm, whole, enormous, every one of forty years at once behind
them. [UI: his circles and squares settle into one shape at the frame: the **hollow outline of a ring**, issued.]

**9 · FINAL IMAGE.** He closes his eyes, the way anyone does to see someone they love. What comes is not the firelit
face of the dream. It is E4's scan, perfect, clean, turning slowly: NAOMI's wireframe, every line of her exact, and over
it in red-yellow, **`Cellular decay — advanced`**, centred in the hollow of the ring. He opens his eyes. He doesn't look
troubled. That is the cut.

*(Theme in subtext, never stated: the Merging gives cognition, not balance, and for a convert it is a choice, made by
the two selves who are awake for the one who is asleep and dreaming of home. The fused man remembers everything his
threads lived; what the brain was holding in the dark never reaches the ledger. He reaches for his mother and the
system hands him her diagnosis. The ring is issued empty.)*
