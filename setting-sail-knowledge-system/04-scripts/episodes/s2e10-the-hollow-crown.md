# Production Breakdown — S2E10: "The Hollow Crown"

> Detailed production/cinematography breakdown for [[setting-sail-tier-ascent]] **Season 2, Episode 10** — the
> **mid-season climax** (end of Act II, the peak of Phase B′). **Top of Tier 2.** Post-Merging, **Adam** (`CHR-06`)
> is brilliant, fast, beloved: a **perfect Grid life**, and the horror is that it is perfect and means nothing. The
> exact inverse of [[s1e10-the-cracking-of-the-sky|S1E10]], where Raul's harbor **broke**: here Adam's harbor is
> flawlessly **built**. The issued glyph should fill, and **stays hollow**; he notices for the first time. **→ 60%
> organic** (the Merging's upgrade, scheduled in E9). Then a real-time message on the village feed: **Ruth**
> (`CHR-07`, `Q-97`), with no Lens and no Grid, holds a handwritten sheet up to the feed's camera: *"She's dying. Come
> home."* At his clock her ten seconds last ten hours (`WR-T4`). The sweetness curdles. World:
> [[velocity-tiers]] · [[the-macro-doughnut]] · [[parallel-pedagogy]] · [[progressive-digitalization]] ·
> [[chromatic-expressionism]].

## Setup & hook
Two rings in one frame. Above Adam's head, the ring of his tribe, 150 lights flaring at once for him like a crown. At
the edge of the frame, his own ring, the one the Grid issued him: hollow. Everything he climbed for is in the first.
Nothing is in the second.

## Cinematography — the harbor built, and a sheet of paper
| Element | The top of Tier 2 (3,600×) | The village (1:1) |
|---------|-----------------------------|-------------------|
| **Lighting** | High-key gold at its most perfect (`VIS-09` feel-good, glazed): a terrace over a flawless city; a sky with **no seam** anywhere (S1E3's seam of light, S1E10's crack: here there is nothing to crack). | **Caravaggio**: one lamp, a pencil, paper, a bed in shadow. Raw sight, no UI (`WR-L2`). |
| **Camera** | Gimbal-smooth, slow, celebratory crane moves; everything effortless (`WR-T2`). In the turn, one **locked** frame held while the world around it passes ten hours. | Handheld, breathing, close (the E1 camera, back for the first time since Adam left). |
| **Sound** | The tribe's single shared breath; a warm chord. | A pencil on paper; a bad breath from the bed; a door; wind. |
| **Words** | None spoken. | One sheet of paper. |

## UI behavior (the Perspective Lens)
Adam's Lens. Ruth has none, and the village shots are raw (`WR-L2`).
- **The crown (hook):** the tribe overlay from E7, `TRIBE · 150`, its ring of lights flaring above him in one pulse.
- **Top circles (Context):** every one lit warm gold, full. Nothing is missing from his context.
- **The care chart:** `Organic: 60%`, quiet, clamped at the frame edge like S1E10's diagnostic widget.
- **The glyph (`Q-12` mirror, issued hollow in E9):** the circles' light runs down toward the ring as if to fill it,
  sweeps once around the outline, and **slides off**. Hollow. Twice. The third time he watches it happen.
- **The beat:** for one pulse his own light in the tribe ring falls **off the beat** (Vesper's, E7), then rejoins.
- **The village feed:** the small window in the lower corner (from E4); a real-time stamp on its edge, the feed's
  own, counts seconds: `00:00:01` … `00:00:10`.
- **The last square (final image):** the bottom row, where nothing he wrote himself has ever lit, lights with his own words:
  **`cure her`** (carried into E11).

## Philosophical climax
S1's mid-season broke Raul's comfort with a storm. S2's mid-season does something worse to Adam: nothing breaks. He has
every gift the climb promised (no pain, no hunger, no fear, no sleep, a mind that holds a city, 150 people who love
him) and the ring that should mark a whole life won't take a drop. Then the only thing in the frame that weighs
anything arrives: a girl's handwriting, held up to a camera for ten seconds by arms that get tired. He spends ten hours
of his perfect life looking at it. And the first thing he writes for himself since he came up is not *home*.

## Script (draft v1)
> 9:16 vertical · ~2.5 min · cinematic photoreal · *Black Mirror / Silo* register. **ADAM** (`CHR-06`), about 19 in
> body; **THE TRIBE**, about 150, unnamed; in the village, **RUTH** (`CHR-07`) and, in bed, **NAOMI** (`CHR-11`). No
> spoken lines: the only words in the reel are written by hand.

*Castes (`WR-B1`): Adam is a cyborg, now **60% organic**, Lens on, body in his rented city capsule at 1:1 (`Q-91`),
mind at Tier 2, one fused mind since E9. The tribe are Shadows and Ghosts. Ruth and Naomi are Purists, no Lens.
Timing (`WR-T4`): about **71 real hours** at Tier 2 = **about 29 years for Adam** (71 × 3,600 h ≈ 10,650 days), about
**23 real hours, nine and a half years**, since the Merging. His body is 19; his mind about 63. This is about **four
real hours before Naomi's death** (`Q-48`, `Q-93`): about one more real hour at Tier 2 (about **150 days** of his,
E11–E15), 2.4 at the Apex, about 40 minutes down. **Ruth's sheet:** she holds it up for **ten real seconds = ten hours
at his clock** (10 × 3,600 s). The reel covers about **eleven hours of his = eleven real seconds**; the writing of the
sheet, before it, takes Ruth a few real minutes, about a week of his, unseen by him.*

**1 · TWO RINGS · HOOK (0:00–0:05).** ADAM on a high terrace at the top of Tier 2, serene, lit gold. [UI: above his
head the tribe's ring of 150 lights **flares** in one pulse, for him, like a crown. At the frame's edge, his own ring:
the hollow outline, issued in E9. Empty.]

**2 · THE PERFECT LIFE · CONTEXT (0:05–1:15, shots 2–4).** A flowing sequence, effortless. Below the terrace a
knot forms in the city's flows; Adam sees it, and with a turn of the hand it untangles, a district re-routed before
anyone else has noticed. THE TRIBE turns toward him and lifts its cups in **one movement**, 150 arms on one breath.
Someone's hand on his shoulder. He walks among them, laughing on the beat. [UI: every top circle lit, warm, full. The
care chart, small: `Organic: 60%`.] Nothing hurts. Nothing is hungry. Nothing is afraid. Nobody has slept.

**3 · THE SKY.** He looks up. A perfect sky, gold to blue, without one cloud, one bird, one seam, and it stays
exactly so, unchanging to the last shade, for as long as he looks.

**4 · THE RING · FRICTION (1:15–2:15, shots 4–6).** [UI: the full circles' light gathers and runs down the frame
toward the ring, sweeps once around its outline, and **slides off**, like water off glass. Hollow.] It happens again.
The third time, Adam **watches** it happen, for the first time. He lifts a finger to the ring at the frame's edge and
touches it. His fingertip passes straight through the hole. Behind him the tribe laughs on the beat; [UI: for one
pulse his own light in the ring falls **off the beat**, then rejoins.] He doesn't laugh.

**5 · THE VILLAGE — real time, raw, no UI.** Handheld, close, the breathing camera of E1. One lamp. RUTH at a table, a
pencil stub, a sheet of paper, writing slowly, carefully, pressing hard. Behind her, in shadow, NAOMI in bed, her
breath catching. Ruth stands, crosses the yard to the post, looks up at the small camera fixed there, and raises the
sheet in **both hands**.

**6 · THE SHEET.** Adam's frame. [UI: in the lower corner, the feed: a white rectangle where the lintel was.] He sees
it. He opens the feed with two fingers until it fills his frame: the sheet, close, in Ruth's hand, pencil pressed into
the paper:

> *She's dying. Come home.*

**7 · TEN HOURS · TURN (2:15–2:30, shots 7–8).** **One locked frame**: Adam on the terrace, the sheet filling the feed
before him. Around him, everything moves: the tribe's evening drifts past at speed; people come close and go; cups are
set down; the gold light swings slowly across the terrace into evening, into night-gold, into evening again. [UI: the
feed's stamp counts, one real second at a time: `00:00:01` … `00:00:06` … ] The sheet never moves. Neither does he.
[`00:00:09` … `00:00:10`.] The sheet **lowers**. Behind it, RUTH's face, tired, looking straight into the lens,
not knowing whether anyone was there to see.

**8 · FINAL IMAGE.** Adam alone on the terrace, the feed shrinking back to its corner. At the frame's edge, the ring,
still hollow. And in the bottom row, where no word of his own has ever lit, one square lights, slowly, as he writes it himself:
**`cure her`**. Cut to black.

*(Theme in subtext, never stated: the inverse of S1E10. Nothing breaks; that is the catastrophe. A life with every
gift, and the ring that should mark it whole won't fill. The only thing in the reel with weight is paper held by tired
arms for ten seconds, which he watches for ten hours. "Come home" reaches him, and the first word he writes for himself
since he came up points up, not down. The viewer finishes the sentence.)*
