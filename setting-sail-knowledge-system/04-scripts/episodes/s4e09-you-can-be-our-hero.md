# Production Breakdown — S4E9: "You Can Be Our Hero"

> Production/cinematography breakdown for [[setting-sail-the-seed-ledger]] **Season 4, Episode 9** (Act II, Phase B).
> **Hull Four, a stasis bay after lights-out.** On the bay board, tomorrow's firing, routine as weather: debris ahead, the
> fuel arithmetic says *fire* ([[the-firing-protocol]], `MAG-30`), a lock is scheduled (`Q-118`: the debris setup lives
> here). **Kai** (`CHR-08`) pauses for the night. **Teo** (`CHR-09`, [[teo]]) keeps decoding. Layer four of
> [[the-lure-beacon]]: **"you can be our hero."** He reads the hull's heading, speed and acceleration off the firing
> schedule, keys them into the array's transmitter with his proxy's hands and **sends a burst**, disregarding the
> silence order, certain he is the only one brave enough to help (`Q-35`). Nobody notices. The array logs it. One
> Intention square lights **"help"** and stays lit; no amber thread draws (a-ha #8, the thread's last return). **One
> reveal:** the burst. The fleet has given itself away.

## Setup & hook
A dark bay. Rows of bodies in stasis, each lit only by the thin glow of its chemistry line. Close on Teo's face, lit from
below by a progress line in his Lens: `LAYER 4 · 97%`. 98. 99. His lips part before the words arrive.

## UI behavior (the Perspective Lens)
All UI is in **Teo's** Lens (`WR-L2`); the bay board and the array log are diegetic.
- **Bottom squares `[▢]`:** E8's closed `SILENCE` square, still there. At the turn, a second square lights beside it:
  `help`. It does not go out.
- **The decoder:** `LAYER 4 · 97%` → `LAYER 4`.
- **Over Kai's proxy (Teo's Lens):** a small tag as Kai's mind pauses for the night: `PAUSED`.
- **The detector:** a slow pan along the edge of Teo's Lens where, in every earlier season, an amber thread has drawn
  between two people. **Nothing draws.**
- **The bay board (diegetic):** `HULL FOUR · FIRE · 09:40` · `DEBRIS · 20,000,000 km` · `DEFLECT · 7 HULLS · FUEL ≈ 2
  BODIES` · `FIRE · 0.9 MWh · LOCK ~1 min` · and the hull's vector for the lock: `HEADING 214.6° / −3.1°` · `SPEED
  0.100 c` · `ACCEL 0.000 g`.
- **The array log (diegetic, bay board corner):** routine lines scrolling. One of them: `TX · HULL FOUR · 1.2 MW · 0.4 s ·
  02:13`. It scrolls up and out of frame.

## Philosophical climax
Teo does the most Virtuous thing anyone does all season. A voice in every language asked for help; the people who could
have helped said *no* and wouldn't say why; the last line, the one only the patient would find, said he could be the one.
He is sincere, kind, and alone, and the act he chooses is the one the lure was written for. The detector that has warned
four seasons of people about their loops stays silent, because there is no loop here, only an end, and the detector reads
loops, not ends. Two squares sit side by side in his row: the order he was given without a reason and the word he chose.
*The message was a lie / the person who answered was good.* The bay board that scheduled tomorrow's firing gave him the
numbers. Nobody sees him do it. The log sees.

## Script (draft v1)
> 9:16 vertical · ~2.5 min · cinematic photoreal · *Black Mirror / Silo* register. **TEO** = Kai's bay-mate (`CHR-09`),
> here in his maintenance proxy; **KAI** (`CHR-08`), in a proxy, until Kai pauses; **THE BEACON** = a voice only
> (`WR-R2`). About thirty spoken words.

*Castes (`WR-B1`): Teo and Kai are Shadows, 100% organic, bodies in deep stasis in this bay, minds at **Tier 0**, attending
by leased maintenance proxy (`WR-B6`); the other bodies in the bay are Shadows, paused; the beacon's makers are heard, never
shown (`WR-R2`). Timing (`WR-T4`, `WR-T6`): **day 23** after first light, **about T+4.2**, the night before the strike
([[the-seed-fleet]] table). Lights-out about 23:00 ship time; Kai pauses about 23:10; the burst leaves at **02:13**; the
firing is scheduled for **09:40**, about **7.5 real hours** later. One clock in frame: Tier 0, 1×. Nobody above reads the
log: the High Clock stands short watches, and 7.5 real hours at the Apex would be about **74 subjective years** (7.5 h ×
3,600 days), so the council is paused between watches (`Q-66`: it reads the burst only after the hit). The firing's
physics ([[the-firing-protocol]]): at 0.1 c the hull closes 20 million km in about **11 minutes** (2 × 10⁷ km ÷ 30,000
km/s ≈ 667 s), and the shot lands about **55 s** after firing, its flash back 55 s later (E1), so a lock of about a minute leaves margin. Acceleration
reads zero: the fleet is coasting; deceleration begins about T+8 (E20). Light-lag (bible only, [[the-lure-beacon]]): the
burst reaches a beacon about **0.25 ly** out about **three months** later (about T+4.45); no reply could reach the fleet
before about **T+4.7**. Tomorrow's hit is not its answer (`Q-66`, `Q-111`). Spend (`WR-E1`): the burst draws **1.2 MW for
0.4 s**, about **0.48 MJ**, from the array's transmitter on the bay's allotment, and the lights dip for it; the board's
arithmetic weighs **fuel on seven hulls, about two bodies' worth of chemistry** (`Q-70`), against **0.9 MWh** of laser
watts.*

**1 · COLD OPEN — EXTREME CLOSE (9:16) · HOOK (0:00–0:05).** Teo's proxy's face in the dark; through it, in his Lens, Teo's
own face lit from below. [UI: `LAYER 4 · 97%`.] 98.

**2 · THE BAY · CONTEXT (0:05–1:15, shots 2–5).** Wide: the stasis bay after lights-out, rows of bodies under frosted glass,
chemistry lines as faint green threads. One working light over the bay board. Two maintenance proxies sit on the step below
it: Teo, Kai.

**3 · THE BOARD.** Insert on the bay board, as routine as a weather report. [Diegetic: `HULL FOUR · FIRE · 09:40` ·
`DEBRIS · 20,000,000 km` · `DEFLECT · 7 HULLS · FUEL ≈ 2 BODIES` · `FIRE · 0.9 MWh · LOCK ~1 min`.] Under it, small, the
hull's vector for the lock. [Diegetic: `HEADING 214.6° / −3.1°` · `SPEED 0.100 c` · `ACCEL 0.000 g`.] Nobody is reading it.

**4 · KAI.** Kai's proxy stands, stiff, and looks at Teo, who hasn't moved.

> **KAI:** Leave it till morning.
>
> **TEO:** Nearly there.
>
> **KAI:** Teo.
>
> **TEO** *(not looking up, warm):* Go on. I'm right behind you.

Kai's proxy settles into its cradle. In Teo's Lens a small tag over it: `PAUSED`. Its optics dim. Teo is alone.

**5 · THE CLOSED SQUARE.** Teo's Lens, the bottom row: E8's closed `SILENCE` square, its reason field still dark. He looks at
it the way you look at a door you were told not to open, then back up. [UI: `LAYER 4 · 99%`.]

**6 · LAYER FOUR · FRICTION (1:15–2:15, shots 6–9).** The progress line completes. The beacon's voice, close, alone, in his
language and then in ours. It is the quietest it has been.

> **THE BEACON:** You can be our hero.

Teo doesn't move. Then he laughs, once, under his breath, at himself, the E6 laugh, and his eyes are wet.

**7 · THE NUMBERS.** He stands. He looks at the board. The vector is right there, scheduled for the morning's lock. His proxy's
hand goes to the array's maintenance keypad beside the board. Insert: machine fingers on real keys, slow, careful, copying
three lines from the board.

**8 · THE SECOND SQUARE.** In Teo's bottom row, beside the closed `SILENCE` square, a new square lights: `help`. *(Recall cue,
≤ 2 s: the deck at E7, the stranger he translated for, "They're just like us.")* The camera pans slowly along the edge of his
Lens, where an amber thread would draw. **Nothing draws.**

> **TEO** *(soft, to no one):* Okay.

**9 · THE BURST.** He presses send. A relay closes. For **0.4 seconds** the bay's light dips; every chemistry line flickers once.
[SOUND: a dull *thunk* in the hull.] The dark comes back. The wide of the bay: unchanged. Kai's proxy in its cradle. The bodies
under glass.

**10 · THE LOG · TURN (2:15–2:30, shots 10–11).** The bay board's corner, where the array log scrolls routine lines all night.
A new one prints among them. [Diegetic: `TX · HULL FOUR · 1.2 MW · 0.4 s · 02:13`.] Another routine line prints under it. And
another. It scrolls up, and out of frame.

**11 · FINAL IMAGE.** Teo sits back down on the step beside Kai's dark proxy, and his own proxy goes still as he pauses for the
night. In the frame the two machines sit side by side in the one working light. The `help` square is still lit. Hold. Above
them, the board: `HULL FOUR · FIRE · 09:40`. Cut to black.

*(Theme in subtext, never stated: the kindest act in the season and the most dangerous are the same act; it took the firing
schedule to give him the numbers; the detector that has guarded four seasons of loops has nothing to say about a good man
doing a good thing. The cold viewer knows he just gave the fleet away. The serial viewer knows no one will read that line
until it's too late, and that the person beside him is the one who eventually will.)*
