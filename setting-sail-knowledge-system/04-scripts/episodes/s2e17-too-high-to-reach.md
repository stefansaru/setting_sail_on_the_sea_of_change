# Production Breakdown — S2E17: "Too High to Reach"

> Detailed production/cinematography breakdown for [[setting-sail-tier-ascent]] **Season 2, Episode
> 17** (Act IV, Phase D′). **Tier 3 vs Tier 0.** Adam has the cure, but the dilation that made it still cost real
> hours: 24 Apex years were **2.4 real hours**, and Naomi is in her final mortal hour. The way down is about
> **40 real minutes** (`Q-93`), and every tier down takes a gift back. The inverse of S1E11's twenty-four-hour
> countdown: this time the countdown is *his* doing. Body **~10% organic** ([[progressive-digitalization]]); glyph
> **cold-complete** ([[the-macro-doughnut]]). World: [[velocity-tiers]] · [[inter-tier-travel]] ·
> [[the-perspective-lens-ui]].

## Setup & hook
Extreme close on **the finished cure**: a small bright lattice turning in violet air, every facet exact, its light
sliding across a chrome iris. Pull back: Adam at the Apex, calm, and in the corner of his Lens the village feed,
where a candle flame hasn't moved. The reel starts still and ends falling: when he signs the first rung of the way
down, the violet begins to drain toward Tier 2's amber and the frame begins, slowly, to drop. (E16 opened on the
hanging drop and stayed in the violet; E17 is the first reel of the descent, `Q-128`.)

## Cinematography — the brake before the brake
| Element | The Apex (Adam) | The village (on the feed) |
|---------|-----------------|---------------------------|
| **Lighting** | S1E12's Sublime lab, emptying: the compute wall dims panel by panel as the work is filed. Cold violet takes the room back; then, from the first signed rung (~2:00), the violet **drains toward Tier 2's amber**, from the top of the frame down. | The lamp and the candle, frozen mid-flicker. Warm, and still as a photograph, until the last seconds, when the flame leans a hair. |
| **Camera** | Locked. Calm. High speed feels normal (`WR-T2`): no rush, no sweat, even now. From the first signed rung, a **slow vertical fall**: the whole frame sinks, steady as a lift, to the cut. | One slow push-in on the feed across the whole reel, so slow it's barely felt. |
| **Sound** | Silence, then the soft clerical *tick* of forms being signed; under the fall, a low tone that slowly drops in pitch. | Nothing. At this clock her world makes no sound he can hear. |

**The dread is arithmetic.** He is calm because the tier makes him calm, and the audience isn't. The reel plays
the gap between the serenity on his face and the numbers on his Lens.

## UI behavior (the Perspective Lens)
- **Two clocks (blueprint):** his own subjective clock, running smoothly; beside it the real one, *REAL
  +2:24:00*, its last digits **barely moving**: only the thousandths ever change.
- **The descent clock:** *DESCENT · ~40 MIN REAL* stamped across the feed, over Naomi's bed, where S1E11 stamped
  its twenty-four-hour countdown over Kavana.
- **The licence (E6 → E17 reminder):** he spends the last of his Apex credit on the offer he waved off in E6:
  *SABBATICAL · 1:1 · LICENSED*. The credit counter empties to zero.
- **A gift per tier (the major):** the descent arrives as a compliance-green **consent ledger** (E8's "consent &
  liability" form, turned around), one line per rung, each needing his signature:
  *3 → 2 · RELEASE APEX* / *2 → 1 · RELEASE MERGED SPEED* / *1 → 0 · RESTORE PAIN*.
- **The turn (a-ha #3):** over Naomi on the frozen feed, unasked, his Lens returns the reading Raul gave her in
  S1E19: *11 yrs · elapsed*.

## Philosophical climax
The climb's logic only ever ran upward: more time per hour, more mind per second. Coming down, the same logic
reads as a bill. Every gift he took on the way up is a line he now has to sign away, and the Grid presents it
the way it presents everything, politely and green, as if pain were a setting being restored. The worst line is
the one the Lens volunteers: eleven years, spent. Raul read them off her as a deficit; she answered *"Eleven
good ones."* Adam was the boy those years were spent on, and from up here he has watched the last of them as a
still photograph. Eleven years is more real time than Raul's flesh ever had (S1E6), and her son spent the end of
them where he couldn't reach her.

## Script (draft v1)
> 9:16 vertical · ~2.5 min · cinematic photoreal · *Black Mirror / Silo* register. **ADAM** = the lead
> (`CHR-06`), alone at the Apex; **NAOMI** (`CHR-11`) and **RUTH** (`CHR-07`) on the village feed. **No
> dialogue:** a man doing paperwork while his mother dies. The theme lives in the UI and the flame.

*Castes (`WR-B1`): Adam is a cyborg at ~10% organic, his body in his rented city capsule (`Q-91`), his mind at the Apex.
Naomi and Ruth are Purists with no Lens, seen through the feed; every tag is Adam's (`WR-L2`). Clocks (`WR-T1`,
`WR-T4`): Tier 3, 86,400×. The reel spans a few of his minutes, under two-thousandths of a real second (150 s ÷ 86,400 ≈
1.7 ms). On the real clock he has been up 2 h 24 min (his 24 years) and Naomi has under an hour. The descent will take
about 40 real minutes; spent at the Apex, the same 40 minutes would be about six and a half more of his years (2,400 s
= 2,400 days). Series clock: about T+19.5. Raul's S1E19 reading was at about T+8.5, so `11 yrs · elapsed` is exact.*

**1 · EXTREME CLOSE (9:16) · HOOK (0:00–0:05).** The finished cure, a small bright lattice, turning in violet air;
its light slides across a chrome iris. [SOUND: nothing.]

**2 · PULL BACK — the Apex lab · CONTEXT (0:05–1:15, shots 2–4).** The iris is ADAM's. The cure turns before him. In
the corner of his Lens, small, the village feed: a candle flame beside Naomi's bed, motionless, as if cast in glass.
At the frame's edge, the cold ring (E16). He looks, at last, at the second clock. [UI: his subjective clock runs smoothly; beside it, *REAL +2:24:00*, only the
thousandths stirring.]

**3 · THE PRICE.** The Lens draws the way home over the feed, across Naomi's bed: *DESCENT · ~40 MIN REAL*. The stamp
sits where S1E11 stamped Kavana's twenty-four hours.

**4 · THE LICENCE.** He opens his Apex credit: a sliver left of what Vance's keys bought. At the frame's lower edge the `TTD` bar is a red sliver. He spends it on the old offer, the card
that has sat folded at the frame's edge since E6. [UI: *SABBATICAL · 1:1 · LICENSED*, accepted this time. The credit counter rolls to zero.]

**5 · THE LAB EMPTIES · FRICTION (1:15–2:15, shots 5–7).** The compute wall goes dark panel by panel as the work is
filed into the cure. The violet comes back. He stands. Calm: the tier won't let him be anything else.

**6 · RECALL CUE (≈1:35–1:50).** The feed, closer. Above the door, the lintel: **eleven notches**, the last one pale
and new. Propped beside them, Ruth's sheet from E10, *"She's dying. Come home."*

**7 · THE LEDGER (≈1:50–2:15).** The descent arrives as a form, compliance-green, one line per rung. [UI: *3 → 2 ·
RELEASE APEX*.] He signs with a glance; it ticks green, [UI: *AWARE · PROVEN*], and **the descent begins**: the violet starts to drain out of
the top of the frame toward Tier 2's amber, and the locked frame, for the first time all reel, **moves: a slow
vertical fall**, steady as a lift. [SOUND: a low tone, dropping.] Falling, the next line: [UI: *2 → 1 · RELEASE MERGED
SPEED*.] Signs; green. [UI: *1 → 0 · RESTORE PAIN*.] The glance holds. Seventy years, and this is the first form he
has hesitated over. He signs. The stamp takes a beat longer to come, as if checking: [UI: *AWARE · PROVEN*]. Green.

**8 · THE READING · TURN (2:15–2:30, shots 8–9).** Still falling, the amber climbing his face. He looks down at his
mother on the frozen feed, the way he did from Tier 1 in E4. Unasked, his Lens reads her, in the small clean type of
Raul's old scan: *11 yrs · elapsed*.

**9 · HOLD.** The frame keeps sinking. On the feed the candle flame, still for the whole reel, leans a hair. Hard cut
to black.

*(Theme in subtext, not stated: the same number, three arrows. Raul's *eleven years* was a deficit; Naomi's
*eleven good ones* was a life; Adam's Lens files it as *elapsed*, an account closed. The consent ledger makes
the season's bargain literal: every gift was a subtraction, and the Grid wants it countersigned on the way out.
The reel opens on the Apex's perfect object and ends falling, the violet draining to amber: the first rung down is
the first motion, and her flame's first lean is the first sign his clock is coming back toward hers. E16 stays the
still violet reel; this one is the start of the fall, `Q-128`.)*
