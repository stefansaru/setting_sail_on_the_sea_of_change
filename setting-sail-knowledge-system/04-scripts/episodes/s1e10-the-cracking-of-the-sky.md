**HOOK (0:00–0:05).** The flawless neon city, mid-perfect-day (callback E4). Then the **sky tears** — a
jagged seam rips across the heavens and scrolling columns of **raw grey server code** pour through the
gap. The Utopia Score stutters and dies. ([[the-shadow-grid-cooling|The solar storm]] from E9 has hit the
mirrors.)

**ACT I — CONTEXT (0:05–1:15).** Citizens freeze, screaming, as their pristine reality glitches to
wireframe. Raul looks up at the wound in the sky — and feels the surge come **down his
[[the-shadow-entity|tether]].** Far away, in a capsule, his sleeping biological body convulses; the power
spike is **cooking his nervous system.**

**ACT II — FRICTION (1:15–2:15).** Agony Raul has never known floods both worlds. Automated medical pods
seize his real body; on-Grid, his avatar **buckles** as limbs go dark. The system **amputates** the
ruined organic parts — spine, lungs — and bolts in cybernetics to save him. And the glyph: his **hollow
Camel's Doughnut FRACTURES** — the serene inherited balance splitting down the middle as a raw, weeping
**"Pain/Chaos" Context circle** opens at the top of his frame *for the very first time.* A cold
**diagnostic widget** (battery, hydraulics) clamps onto the frame edge and won't leave.
> **RAUL (through it, by reflex):** It's a perfect— It's a perf— *(the values cracking, [[modern-lions]])*

**RESOLUTION / CLIFFHANGER (2:15–2:30).** The storm passes. The sky re-skins to gold — but Raul **sees the
seam** now, and won't unsee it. He flexes a new chrome hand where flesh used to be. [UI: the glyph stays
**fractured**; the Pain circle pulses, permanent.] *He is 70% organic, and awake.* Cut to black.
