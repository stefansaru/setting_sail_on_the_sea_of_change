# Production Breakdown — S2E03: "The Painless World"

> Detailed production/cinematography breakdown for [[setting-sail-tier-ascent]] **Season 2, Episode 3**
> (Act I, Phase A′). **Tier 1**, the city ([[velocity-tiers]]). **Adam** (`CHR-06`), a **90%-organic cyborg**,
> accepts a "temporary" residency for ongoing care; his body goes into a **rented city capsule** (`Q-91`), and every
> discomfort he was raised to endure is lifted. Inverts [[internalizing-the-exterior|S1E4]]: where the child Raul
> learned to want the city, the grown Purist simply stops feeling the weight of anything. Reminders of a-ha #9
> (S1E20's vial made his chrome, `Q-49`, `Q-119`) and a-ha #10 (Kavana published) via one credit:
> **KAVANA · PUBLIC**. World: [[capsule-birth]] · [[the-shadow-entity]] · [[the-ageless-default]].

## Setup & hook
A real capsule bay, rows of sleeping bodies under amber service light. One more slides in: Adam's, still wearing
the cord Ruth tied. The slot's display reads: **RENTED**. It is the first time his flesh has waited while he lived
somewhere else. Upstairs, on the city's clock, there is no hunger, no cold, no tiredness, no fear. It feels like
grace. We watch a man who carried water stop noticing that nothing weighs anything.

## Cinematography — the weight goes out of the frame
| Element | The capsule bay (real, 1:1) | The city (Tier 1, 60×) |
|---------|------------------------------|------------------------|
| **Lighting** | Low amber service light, Flavin strip-neon along the racks ([[chromatic-expressionism]]); skin under plastic, the chrome seam's dull glow. | High-key golden "perfect day" (`VIS-09`, `VIS-16`): shadowless, glazed. Rain that catches light and doesn't chill. |
| **Camera** | Locked off. Nothing in the bay moves fast enough for the camera to bother. | Gimbal-smooth; slow push-ins; **no handheld anywhere at Tier 1** (the breathing camera of E1 is gone). |
| **Sound** | Pumps, a drip, the soft click of a rack locking. | Clean, warm, low. The **ache tone** is absent, and so is everything it used to sit under: no wind, no effort, no breath sounds when he lifts. |
| **Performance** | — | Adam's face **smooths** over the reel: the brow he held against the pain in E1 is gone by the turn. Play it as ease, not as loss. |

## UI behavior (the Perspective Lens)
Adam's Lens, now always on at Tier 1. **No glyph** (absent until E9).
- **Top circles:** *Safe* (from E2) holds; nothing new.
- **The care chart (shot 3):** a small panel at the frame's edge, the clinic's notes on his implants. One line reads
  **Organic: 90%** (the S1E13 readout's form). Under it, the compound his implants run on, credited to one open chain:
  **KAVANA · PUBLIC**. He doesn't read it. We do. *(a-ha #9 reminder: the vial's formula became his chrome; a-ha #10
  reminder: she made her chain public after all. Never explained here.)*
- **Bottom squares (shots 5–6):** for the first time they **light**, with small icons, not words: a cup, a blanket, a
  bed-shape, an open door. Wants he never had to name, offered before he has them. Each one, once he looks at it,
  fills and is answered. *(Standardized comfort, unnamed; the vice frameworks are E8's to spend.)*

## Philosophical climax
The Purists didn't endure hunger, cold and fear because they liked them. They endured them because that was what a
life weighed, and a life that weighs something is one you can carry. Tier 1 takes the weight off so gently that the
man doesn't notice his hands no longer know what holding is. The turn is not a loss he feels. It is a loss he
doesn't feel, and the cord on a wrist six floors down that still does.

## Script (draft v1)
> 9:16 vertical · ~2.5 min · cinematic photoreal · *Black Mirror / Silo* register. **ADAM** (`CHR-06`), about 19 in
> body; **SYSTEM**, the flat voice (`WLD-11` vault: never named). No other cast. One spoken line.

*Castes (`WR-B1`): Adam is a cyborg, 90% organic, Purist-born, Lens on; from shot 1 his body rests in a rented city
capsule while his mind runs at Tier 1 (`Q-91`; `WR-B6`: he can't simply walk home). SYSTEM is a voice, not a person. The people
in the plaza are Shadows (`Q-87`); the sleepers in the bay are their bodies. Timing (`WR-T4`): the residency starts about T+19.25, the start of his roughly three real months at Tier 1 (`Q-48`).
This reel spans its **first real week = about 420 days, roughly fourteen months, for Adam** (7 × 60). In the bay,
the hook and the turn are about **seven real days apart**; on the city's clock, a year and two months. His body stays
19; his mind turns about 20.*

**1 · THE CAPSULE BAY · HOOK (0:00–0:05).** Amber service light; racks of capsules, each a sleeping body under
plastic. A rack slides; ADAM's body is drawn into its slot, eyes closed, the chrome seam a dull glow through the
gown. Ruth's woven cord is still knotted on his right wrist. The rack locks. The bay's own small display, on the
slot's edge, prints one word: **RENTED**. *(A physical label, not Lens UI: Adam's Lens is upstairs with him, and the
bay is shot in the third person, `WR-L2`.)*

**2 · THE CITY · CONTEXT (0:05–1:15, shots 2–4).** Tier 1. A plaza under a perfect gold sky, people moving at ease.
Adam stands in the middle of it in clean clothes, upright, both hands loose at his sides. Nothing aches. He keeps
waiting for it to.

> **SYSTEM** *(flat, kind)*: Residency approved. Temporary. For ongoing care.

**3 · INSERT — THE CARE CHART.** [UI: a small panel at the frame's edge. **Organic: 90%**. Below it, a line for the
compound his implants run on, and its credit: **KAVANA · PUBLIC**.] Adam's eyes pass over it the way you pass over
the small print on a bottle. Nobody in this reel says the name.

**4 · THE DAYS — a quiet montage, gimbal-smooth.** A long table set with bread; Adam sits, and isn't hungry, and
doesn't eat, and doesn't notice he hasn't. Rain falls on the plaza; it runs off his face and doesn't chill him. He
walks to the very edge of a high terrace, toes at the drop, and looks down without a flicker of vertigo. Light
changes over the city; he is never tired.

**5 · THE BUCKET · FRICTION (1:15–2:15, shots 5–7).** A plaza fountain; beside it, a decorative wooden yoke and two
buckets, a quaint thing for the render. Adam's body knows it before he does: he squats, shoulders the yoke, braces
for the weight that bent him in E1, and stands up **too fast**, because there isn't any. The buckets swing, full,
weightless. He stands there holding nothing heavy. [SOUND: no breath, no creak, nothing.]

**6 · CLOSE — THE SQUARES.** [UI: the bottom Intention squares light for the first time, one after another, with
small icons: a cup, a blanket, a bed-shape, an open door. Each, as his eyes touch it, fills, and somewhere at the
edge of frame its answer is already arriving: a warm cup set down; a door sliding open on a quiet room.] Wants he
never had, offered before he could have them. His face, which held a brace against pain for ten years, has nothing
left to brace against. It smooths.

**7 · THE SAME PLAZA, LATER.** A year and more has passed for him: the plaza's trees have turned and greened again. Adam sets the yoke down
without thinking of it. He looks well. He looks like everyone else.

**8 · THE WRIST · TURN (2:15–2:30, shots 8–9).** A small habitual gesture: Adam's left hand goes to his right wrist,
to turn the cord the way he used to. The wrist is **bare**: the render never carried it. He looks at it. A beat. He
smiles, a little, the way you smile at a habit, and lets the hand fall. He doesn't mind. That is the whole turn.

**9 · FINAL IMAGE — THE BAY.** Hard cut to the capsule bay, real time, amber light: the same rack, the same body,
seven days on. The plastic, a drip, the dull seam. On the real wrist, Ruth's cord, knot and all, exactly where she
tied it. Hold on it, still, in a room where nothing moves. Cut to black.

*(Theme in subtext, never stated: a man raised to carry weight is given a world with none, and the first thing he
loses is the knowledge that he lost something. The cord stays with the flesh; the flesh is rented; the man upstairs
doesn't feel for it twice.)*
