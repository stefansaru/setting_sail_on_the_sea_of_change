# Script — S1E7: "The Cyborg Slip"

> [[setting-sail-tier-descent]] S1E7 · Act II · **Tier 0→1** · Raul **100% organic** (witness) ·
> **JUN** = Raul's close friend (cyborg) · POV: Raul (present via a **leased
> surface proxy**, [[the-shadow-entity]]) · register: **Flavin**, hard directional light. 9:16 · ~2.5 min. The first
> death Raul watches — and it's gentle.

**HOOK (0:00–0:05).** A workshop on the real surface, Tier 0. Raul's friend **Jun** — body more chrome
than flesh after a lifetime of repairs — pauses mid-sentence, mid-task, and goes very still. [SOUND: a tool
still spinning down.]

**ACT I — CONTEXT (0:05–1:15).** Raul, visiting, doesn't understand at first. Jun has been replacing
worn organic parts for as long as he can remember — a hand here, a lung there. They both knew this day was coming: the day the
**last living cell** finally dies. ([[the-cyborg-slip]].)
> **RAUL:** Jun? Jun, hey —
> **JUN (calm, listening to something inside):** Shh. I think… I think it's now.

**ACT II — FRICTION (1:15–2:15).** Jun feels a **slight, passing itch** on a synthetic wrist — scratches
it absently — and *smiles.* No drama. No grand termination. Just a threshold, crossed. A new
**[[the-ghost-entity|Ghost]]** profile logs itself onto the network. Raul stares, shaken, at a friend who
just died without dying — who looks *freed.* Raul's own tether-line (from E6) hums at the edge of frame,
suddenly heavy.
> **JUN (lighter now, untethered):** Oh. *Oh.* Raul — it doesn't hurt. It's just… quieter.

**RESOLUTION / CLIFFHANGER (2:15–2:30).** Jun's chrome body sits empty, cooling — a [[shells-with-ghosts-within|shell]]
— while somewhere on the Grid, Jun is newly weightless. Raul lays his **proxy's** cold hand on Jun's
colder chest, then presses it to where his *own* heartbeat should be — and feels only chrome; his warm,
mortal body is a world away, asleep in a capsule. [UI: hollow glyph trembles — *once.*] Cut to black.

*Canon: friend's slip (not Raul's — Raul's is E17); the "slight itch" → Ghost ([[the-cyborg-slip]]/
[[the-ghost-entity]]); foreshadows E17 directly. Raul 100% organic. Castes (`Q-87`): Jun is a cyborg who lives in
the flesh at Tier 0, so his body wore faster than a capsule body (`Q-75`); Raul is a Shadow attending by leased
proxy (`WR-B6`). Clocks: Jun's repairs span a few real months, which is years on Raul's Tier-1 clock; "as long as
he can remember" is Raul's memory, not Jun's body.*
