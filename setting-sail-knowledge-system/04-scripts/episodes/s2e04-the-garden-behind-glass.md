# Production Breakdown — S2E04: "The Garden Behind Glass"

> Detailed production/cinematography breakdown for [[setting-sail-tier-ascent]] **Season 2, Episode 4**
> (Act I, Phase A′). **Tier 1, looking down** at the Purist Village (`PLC-06`) through the village feed. **Adam**
> (`CHR-06`), a **90%-organic cyborg**, body in his rented capsule (`Q-91`), sees home the Grid's way. His Lens tags
> his mother **`Cellular decay — advanced`**: he is now the chrome scanner Raul was in
> [[setting-sail-tier-descent|S1E19]], pointed the wrong way. The scan it takes is the one he designs her cure from
> in S2E16 (a-ha #11's first touch, unspoken). World: [[the-perspective-lens-ui]] · [[the-ageless-default]] ·
> [[asymmetric-perspective]] · [[velocity-tiers]].

## Setup & hook
A tag snaps onto a warm, lined face, red-yellow, clinical: **Cellular decay — advanced**. Pull back and the face is
behind glass, in a window in the corner of a perfect golden room, and it is his mother. A real month has passed
below. Four and a half years have passed for him. He has started to watch the village the way the Grid watches
everything: as a set of readings.

## UI behavior (the Perspective Lens)
Adam's Lens. **No glyph** (absent until E9).
- **Diagnostic tags crawl the village (shot 3),** the S1E19 set in his Lens now: over an elder's knees *Untreated
  Osteoarthritis*; over a child coughing *Life Expectancy: Limited*; over the roofs and the well, nothing but
  thin amber outlines, the Lens measuring what it can't tag.
- **The scan (shot 4):** a thin line sweeps Naomi head to foot on the feed, unasked, and a small **wireframe of her
  body** settles as a thumbnail at the frame's edge. No word on it. *(Bible: the scan he will design the cure from,
  S2E16; never said here.)* Then the tag lands: **Cellular decay — advanced** (the S2E4 form of S1E19's *Advanced
  Cellular Decay*).
- **Raul's relay (shot 6):** one plain text line from Raul, arriving in Adam's Lens.
- **The turn:** the tag **stays pinned** where Naomi was, even after a door closes over her.

## Philosophical climax
In S1E19 Raul scanned a Purist mother, read her as a failing machine, then switched the Lens off and saw her. Here
her own son runs the same scan from above, and the Lens doesn't need switching on: it never goes off. He offers what
anyone on the Grid would call mercy, a bed and a cure. She answers in a sentence that lasts five minutes at his clock. The fracture isn't
her refusal. It's that the tag on her outlasts the sight of her, and he leaves it there.

## Script (draft v1)
> 9:16 vertical · ~2.5 min · cinematic photoreal · *Black Mirror / Silo* register. **ADAM** (`CHR-06`); on the
> feed, **NAOMI** (`CHR-11`), **RUTH** (`CHR-07`) and **RAUL** (`CHR-02`), Ghost in a shell; **VILLAGERS**. One
> spoken line and one line of text; every voice from the feed arrives as a wordless drone.

*Castes (`WR-B1`): Adam is a cyborg, 90% organic, Lens on, body in his rented city capsule at 1:1 (`Q-91`), mind at
Tier 1. Naomi, Ruth and the villagers are Purists with no Lens: every tag is in Adam's Lens, on a feed (`WR-L2`).
Raul is a Ghost in a shell at Tier 0, Lens on, never in POV. Timing (`WR-T4`): about **four real weeks** into the
residency (about T+19.1) = **about 1,680 days, roughly four and a half years, for Adam** (28 × 60). His body is 19,
his mind about 23. On the feed, one real second = one minute for him: Naomi's **five-second** answer lasts **five
minutes** at his clock; the door's closing, about **three real seconds**, takes three.*

**1 · THE TAG · HOOK (0:00–0:05).** Extreme close, slightly soft, through glass: warm lined skin, an eye, grey hair
lit by fire. [UI: a red-yellow tag snaps on, clinical, precise: **Cellular decay — advanced**.]

**2 · THE ROOM · CONTEXT (0:05–1:15, shots 2–4).** Pull back. The face is inside a window in the corner of Adam's
frame; Adam sits in a golden, shadowless room at Tier 1, at ease, watching the feed the way you check weather. It is
NAOMI at the lintel post. A month for her; years for him. He watches every day.

**3 · THE VILLAGE, THE GRID'S WAY.** The feed's fixed wide: the yard, the long table, the well, the fire's smoke
standing like glass. [UI: tags crawl it, unhurried. An elder's knees, *Untreated Osteoarthritis*. A coughing child,
*Life Expectancy: Limited*. Thin amber outlines over patched roofs and the well, the Lens tracing a place it can only
measure.] From up here the village is small. The houses are poor. Every one of them is failing something.

**4 · THE SCAN.** [UI: a thin line sweeps NAOMI head to foot, unasked. A small wireframe of her body settles at the
frame's edge, beside the care chart, and stays there.] Adam glances at it and doesn't close it.

**5 · THE OFFER · FRICTION (1:15–2:15, shots 5–7).** Adam leans in, speaks low, to a man a world below.

> **ADAM:** Raul. Tell her there's a bed up here. Two. They'd fix it.

On the feed, RAUL, by the fire, receives it instantly, lifts his head, and crosses the yard toward Naomi. At Adam's
clock it takes Raul a long time to cross. Raul stops before her and speaks; the drone of his voice, slow, wordless.
RUTH is at the long table behind them, a bowl in her hands, stopped.

**6 · HER ANSWER — locked on the feed.** NAOMI looks past Raul, straight up into the camera on her post, at her son.
She answers. It takes her five seconds. It takes Adam five minutes, and he watches all of them: her lips crawling
through a sentence he can't hear, a drone like something underwater. He doesn't look away. He doesn't move. [UI: one
plain line of text arrives from Raul.]

> **RAUL** *(text, in Adam's Lens)*: She says she has a bed.

**7 · RUTH.** On the feed, RUTH sets the bowl down, crosses to the lintel, and sits on the step at Naomi's feet,
her back against the post, under the camera, under the notches. It takes her a long time, at his clock, to sit. She
doesn't look up at the lens. She doesn't need to.

**8 · THE DOOR · TURN (2:15–2:30, shots 8–9).** NAOMI turns and goes inside. Ruth rises and follows her. The door
swings shut, slow as a tide. [UI: the tag **stays** where Naomi stood, pinned in the air, then settles onto the wood:
**Cellular decay — advanced**, glowing red-yellow over a closed door and ten notches, the Lens still reading her
through the planks.]

**9 · FINAL IMAGE.** Adam looks at the tag on the door. His hand lifts, as if to dismiss it, and stops, and comes
down. He leaves it. With a small gesture he shrinks the feed into the corner of his frame, beside her wireframe,
and the golden room fills the rest. Hold on the golden room, and the little lit door in its corner. Cut to black.

*(Theme in subtext, never stated: home seen from above is small, poor and failing, and the son who once watched her
thumb the notches now reads her decay off a door. Her refusal is one sentence; the fracture is that he keeps the tag.
Ruth's choice is a sit on a step, and it says everything she will hold up on paper in E10.)*
