# Production Breakdown — S4E03: "The Base of the Pyramid"

> Detailed production/cinematography breakdown for [[setting-sail-the-seed-ledger]] **Season 4, Episode 3**
> (Act I, Phase A). The Tier 1 commons, off watch. The fleet's population pyramid ([[the-stasis-economy]]) as it is
> lived from the bottom: the slow many are **mostly Ghosts on watch**, and Kai's [[frequency-tribes|tribe]] is one of
> the base's few bands with bodies (`Q-68`). The few above are a rumour. Then one of the tribe is **promoted upward**
> and vanishes mid-sentence into a faster clock. One reveal: **the pyramid, and why Kai sits at its base.**

## Setup & hook
Open on a woman laughing, mid-story, a cup halfway to her mouth. Her next words speed up into a bird's chirp, her
outline thins like breath on glass, and she is gone. The cup stays in the air for one frame, then the render lets it
settle on the table. Nobody screams. Somebody sighs. It is good news.

## Cinematography — the slow table

| **Lighting** | A rendered long hall, tall windows onto a calm grey-blue sea at dusk that exists nowhere. Soft, even, a little too kind; the light never moves. | A thin vertical scale at the frame's left edge, the only hard line in the room. |

## Philosophical climax
At the base, the pyramid is not a hierarchy anyone resents. It is the shape of the weather. The many are slow because
watts are finite; the few are fast because someone must fly the ship; promotion is a lottery everyone is pleased to
see somebody win. The cruelty is only visible from where Kai sits. A Ghost at the base can be lifted any day: there
is nothing holding it down. Kai is held at the bottom by the very thing the whole voyage is for, the body in the bay
below. The tribe envies Kai the body. Kai can't rise because of it. Both are true at the same table, and nobody says
either one aloud.

## Script (draft v1)
> 9:16 vertical · ~2.5 min · cinematic photoreal · *Black Mirror / Silo* register. **KAI** = the lead (`CHR-08`), in
> render, off watch; **THE PROMOTED** = a young woman of Kai's tribe, a Ghost; **THE TRIBE-MATE** = an older man of the
> tribe, a Ghost (script labels only). Dialogue sparse and social; the theme lands in subtext and in the
> [[the-perspective-lens-ui|UI]] (`WR-F4`).

*Castes (`WR-B1`): Kai is a Shadow, 100% organic (`WR-B3`), body in deep stasis in capsule 10, mind in the commons at Tier
1, the top of what fleet stasis holds (`WR-B5`); the commons is simulated, so no proxy is needed. THE PROMOTED and THE
TRIBE-MATE are Ghosts, no body anywhere. The rest of the tribe at the table are Ghosts; Kai's rota of ten are its
band of Shadows (`Q-68`). No Purists, no children. Timing (`WR-T4`): about **T+4.0**. The commons runs at Tier 1
(60×), so the reel's 2.5 subjective minutes are about 2.5 real seconds. THE PROMOTED climbs to Tier 2 (3,600×), sixty
times Kai's clock: the last half-second of her sentence, phase-matched upward through the Data Buffer, plays to the
table as about a hundredth of a second, a chirp. By the time Kai has finished the cup in front of Kai, about ten
seconds at Tier 1, she has lived about ten minutes. A month of hers will pass in about twelve hours of Kai's. Spends
(`WR-E1`): every clock is watts; her promotion is a larger slice of them, which is why there are fewer of her kind
above. Kai's body below draws its chemistry whether Kai is promoted or not.*

**1 · COLD OPEN — CLOSE (9:16) · HOOK (0:00–0:05).** THE PROMOTED, laughing, cup halfway up, mid-story:

> **THE PROMOTED:** —and first thing, when we're down, I'm going to stand out in the—

Her words speed into a chirp. [UI: `↑ TIER 2`, half a second.] Her outline thins and is gone. The cup hangs one frame,
then settles. [SOUND: the room dips.]

**2 · THE TABLE — earlier, handheld · CONTEXT (0:05–1:15, shots 2–3).** Rewind ten seconds of the commons and play it
loose: a long table by tall windows, a dusk sea that doesn't move right. A dozen of Kai's tribe, talking over each
other. Kai at the end, awake and in a face for the first time: the sleeper from capsule 10, eyes open, ordinary, a little
tired. [UI: top: `TRIBE · 150`, one beat. Left edge: the ruler; the camera finds Kai's tethered marker low in the
crowded band.]

> **THE TRIBE-MATE:** Anyone here ever seen one? Up top?
>
> **THE PROMOTED** *(delighted to know):* My shift lead has. Says they're lovely. Says they finish your sentences.

Laughter. Rack to the ruler: the thin band above, the few points at the top, unmoving.

**3 · THE CHIRP, again.** We arrive back at the hook from the other side: her story, her laugh, *"I'm going to stand
out in the—"*, the chirp, gone. One light lifts out of the tribe ring and climbs the ruler. [UI: `TRIBE · 149`.]
[Camera: it **locks off**, and stays locked for the rest of the reel.]

**4 · THE EMPTY PLACE · FRICTION (1:15–2:15, shots 4–5).** A beat. Someone lifts the cup she left and looks at it.
Someone raises his own, a small toast to nobody. The talk starts again, a little lower.

> **KAI** *(to the cup):* Rain.
>
> **THE TRIBE-MATE:** Hm?
>
> **KAI:** She was going to say rain.

The tribe-mate looks at Kai the way the tribe looks at the lucky.

> **THE TRIBE-MATE:** You'll be up there next.
>
> **KAI:** Can't. I've got a body.
>
> **THE TRIBE-MATE** *(not unkind; meaning it):* Lucky.

He turns back to the others. [Camera: locked; Kai alone at the end of the frame, the grey sea behind.]

**5 · INSERT — the ruler.** Kai looks at the ruler at the frame's edge. Under the table, Kai's finger touches Kai's own
marker, idly, the way you test a loose tooth.

**6 · THE CEILING · TURN (2:15–2:30, shots 6–7).** Kai drags the marker up. It rises a hair out of the crowded band,
stops as if it had met glass, and settles back. [SOUND: a tick; a softer tick.] [UI: beside it, a blink:
`CEILING · BODY · T0–1`.] Kai tries once more. It settles back to exactly where it was.

**7 · FINAL IMAGE.** Locked wide: the table talking, the sea that doesn't move, Kai still. [UI: `TRIBE · 149`, pulsing
in one beat, with one dark gap in the ring.] Kai takes the finger off the ruler. Cut to black.

*(Theme in subtext, not stated: promotion is a kindness the base gives itself, and the body is the thing that makes Kai
ineligible for it. "Lucky" and the ceiling are the same fact with two arrows. "They finish your sentences" is a joke at
this table; in two reels it will be the High Clock's manners.)*
