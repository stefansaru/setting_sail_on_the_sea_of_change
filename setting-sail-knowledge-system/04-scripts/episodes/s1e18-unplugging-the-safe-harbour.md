# Script — S1E18: "Unplugging the Safe Harbour"

> [[setting-sail-tier-descent]] S1E18 · Act IV (Phase D) · **Tier 0 surface** · Raul **0% organic** (a
> [[the-ghost-entity|Ghost]] in a [[shells-with-ghosts-within|shell]]) · POV: Raul · register: clinical
> digital → warm **Caravaggio** chiaroscuro. 9:16 · ~2.5 min. He steps off the Grid.

**HOOK (0:00–0:05).** A heavy airlock cycles. Raul — now a Ghost wearing a cyborg shell, fresh from his
death in E17 — **steps out at real-time (1:1)** and the constant UI hum drops to **silence.** Raw,
un-simulated daylight hits the lens for the first time.

**ACT I — CONTEXT (0:05–1:15).** He steps onto the actual Earth: dirt, wind, weeds through cracked
concrete — no render, no metric. His Lens still tries to tag it all, overlaying clean diagnostic frames on
a world that won't hold still for them. Then, over a ridge: smoke from cookfires. A **village.** The
**Natural Purists** (`FAC-02`), who refused every upgrade he accepted.

**ACT II — FRICTION (1:15–2:15).** The cinematography itself **converts** here — the clinical digital grade
giving way to deep, warm **chiaroscuro** ([[lighting]] §4, [[chromatic-expressionism|Caravaggio]]):
firelight on real wrinkled faces, organic shadow, no neon anywhere. A purist child stops and *stares* at
the chrome stranger — not with fear, with **curiosity.**
> **CHILD:** Are you lost?
Raul has no answer. He, who can no longer forget anything, looks at people who will forget *everything* and
die, and feels something the Grid never priced.

**RESOLUTION / CLIFFHANGER (2:15–2:30).** Raul lowers his hand from a half-raised diagnostic gesture and,
deliberately, doesn't scan the child (Adam, about 8, `Q-81`). At his side, his chrome fingers turn three times
around nothing, a quarter-turn each: a nursery habit, back from S1E3a. [UI: the fractured glyph holds,
quiet.] He walks toward the warm light. Cut to black. *(Into E19.)*

*Canon: 0% organic Ghost in a shell (post-E17); clinical→Caravaggio register shift; Purists introduced
([[setting-sail-tier-descent]] / [[the-medical-sanctuary|cf Kavana]]). Sets up E19's flagship. The shell is the
proxy he died in at E17, now a Ghost's shell (`Q-69`). Castes (`Q-87`): Raul is a Ghost in a shell; the child (Adam)
and the villagers are Purists, with no Lens (`WR-L2`); every tag is Raul's.*

*The three turns ([[nine-nine-nine]], `Q-133` (4)): the payoff of the S1E3a cube, gesture only, never named. It is the
first no Raul chooses himself; E19's Lens-off is the second. Shell optics are full-field, so no Narrow Eye here
([[the-narrow-eye]]).*
