# Production Breakdown — S3E16: "The Horizon Switches On"

> Detailed production/cinematography breakdown for [[setting-sail-the-cartographers-ledger]] **Season 3, Episode
> 16** (Act IV opens; Module 9, the Horizon, `MAG-09`). The mortal **Mara** (`CHR-01`), about 17, falls ill (`Q-59`),
> and for the first time in her existence a countdown hangs over her: an honest one, in **Raul's** Lens (`WR-L2`; she
> has none, `Q-101`). Under it the cure's price, again: *Treatable · donor required* (`WR-B8`; reminder of S3E1's
> loose cure and S2E18's rule). **Rafe** (`Q-22`) brings the better system to the god, not the girl (`Q-65`): pull the
> shard home and the illness ends with the body. The god doesn't come. Then Raul's Lens reads Rafe's own record, one
> line: *Slip: none* (a-ha #14 payoff: S1E15's *Body: none* → S2E18's third glyph → here). World:
> [[the-memory-substrate]] · [[the-cyborg-slip]] · [[the-cosmic-clone-paradox]] · [[the-perspective-lens-ui]] ·
> [[progressive-digitalization]].

## Setup & hook
Firelight, the village at dusk, early autumn. Mara laughing at something across the fire, a clay cup in her hand. In
Raul's Lens, over her, where nothing has ever been drawn, a number arrives: a countdown, set in the old grey type of
the founding stratum. The cup slips out of her fingers.

## Cinematography — two clocks in one frame
| Element | The village (Mara, Ruth, the elders) | Raul's Lens (Raul, Rafe) |
|---------|--------------------------------------|--------------------------|
| **Lighting** | Warm single-source Caravaggio (`VIS-15`): fire, one oil lamp in the hut, faces in half dark. | The same fire, but the readouts are cold: the countdown in grey, the Lens text in thin white. |
| **Camera** | Handheld, breathing, close on hands (the cup, Ruth's cloth on the girl's forehead). | Locked POV inserts, gimbal-still, whenever we look *through* Raul. |
| **Sound** | Fire, cicadas thinning for the season, the girl's breath getting shorter. | No tick. The countdown is silent; that is what makes it unbearable. |

**The grey type.** The countdown is not in the Lens's own face. It is in the grey of the old logo the divers found in
S3E2: the Horizon module, surfacing out of the oldest stratum into the one Lens in the village. Never remarked.

## UI behavior (the Perspective Lens)
- **The countdown (the major):** over Mara, in the founding stratum's grey: `HORIZON · 182 d`. It does not tick on
  screen; it simply reads one number lower each time we return to it. It is honest: it adds no time (`MAG-09`).
- **The price, under it, one line:** `Treatable · donor required` (`WR-B8`). Nothing on screen says who could be one.
- **A plant at the door (→ E20):** the Lens sweeps the elders as it did the Purists in S1E19, and the oldest elder,
  the one who in E8 wanted her cut out, draws the old red-yellow pulse: *Advanced Cellular Decay · Life Expectancy:
  Limited*. It scrolls past unremarked.
- **Rafe's better system:** between Rafe's hands, the pack bar from E6, its one stubborn field still reading
  `Memory: fragment`. He runs it **backward**: the bar empties toward home. The shard returned; the body stopped.
- **Rafe's Lens, once (→ E18, E20):** a dim line at his frame edge, `BEAM WINDOW · COLONY`, unremarked. Only in his
  Lens (`Q-131` (3)).
- **The speaker tag:** at the foot of Raul's frame, his own shell's tag reads `RAUL`. When Rafe speaks past him to the
  god, it **doesn't flip** (contrast E9). The god doesn't come.
- **Slip: none (a-ha #14):** as Rafe turns away, the Lens reads his record the way it reads anyone's: one line,
  `Slip: none`. He left his body by beam and never felt the itch.

## Philosophical climax
The Horizon is the last HELM module and the simplest: it tells you the truth about how long, and adds nothing. A god
who held every joy and could not hold the end has the end now, in grey, over a girl who barely knows what she was.
The tribe's vow holds (`Q-60`): an elder starts to say something at the door, and Ruth's look stops it. So the only
honest thing in the hut is a number nobody but Raul can see, and when she asks him, he gives it to her in a word she
can live inside.

Rafe's offer is kind, and it is engineering. Pull the shard home and she *feels nothing*: which is exactly the half
of the Doughnut the god came down to get. He brings it to the god, not the girl, as he should (`Q-65`), and the god
doesn't answer. Then the Lens reads him, and the turn is a single line of his own history: of every Raul, he is the
one who never died. The man offering a painless way out has never been through the door he's offering to close.
No one says so.

## Script (draft v1)
> 9:16 vertical · ~2.5 min · cinematic photoreal · *Black Mirror / Silo* register. **RAUL** = the lead (`CHR-02`), a
> Ghost in a shell, Lens on; **MARA** = the mortal child (`CHR-01`), about 17, called Naomi in the village (never named
> in this reel); **RUTH** (`CHR-07`); **RAFE** (`Q-22`) in a leased shell; **THE OLDEST ELDER** and elders at the door.
> Dialogue sparse: about 20 spoken words. The theme stays in the countdown and one line of record (`WR-F4`).

*Castes (`WR-B1`): Raul is a Ghost in a shell, 0% organic, Lens on since S1E20 and alignment off since S1E19
(`Q-119`). Rafe is a Ghost, the paused reply copy woken in E2 (`Q-54`), in a leased shell; Lens on. Mara is a Purist,
100% organic, no Lens (`Q-101`); Ruth and the elders are Purists with no Lens, so every tag is in Raul's Lens (`WR-L2`).
The President is a Ghost (`Q-98`), still running and distributed; she does not appear. Timing (`WR-T1`, `WR-T4`): Tier
0, 1×, for everyone on screen; no one runs at 60× (Tier 1), 3,600× (Tier 2) or 86,400× (the Apex) in this reel, so real
and subjective time are one clock. The reel spans one evening, about T+38 real years; Mara about 17, Ruth and Adam
about 38. `182 d` puts her death at about T+38.5 (E20). The two Ghosts could run faster and choose not to.*

**1 · FIRELIGHT (9:16) · HOOK (0:00–0:05).** Mara laughing across the fire, cup in hand. Hard cut into Raul's Lens:
over her, in grey, a number arrives where nothing has ever been. [UI: `HORIZON · 182 d`.] The cup slips. It doesn't
break. She looks at her hand as if it belonged to someone else.

**2 · THE HUT — handheld · CONTEXT (0:05–1:15, shots 2–4).** Lamplight. Mara on the pallet, fever-bright. Ruth wrings a
cloth, lays it on her forehead. Raul stands in the doorway; behind him, the elders. [UI, in Raul's Lens, small under
the countdown: `Treatable · donor required`.]

**3 · THE DOOR.** One elder leans in, lips parting: about to say something to the girl. Ruth looks up. Just looks. The
elder closes her mouth and steps back into the dark. [UI: the Lens sweeps the faces at the door; over the oldest elder,
the old red-yellow pulse, *Advanced Cellular Decay · Life Expectancy: Limited*, scrolling past unremarked.]

**4 · THE QUESTION.** Mara finds Raul's eyes over Ruth's shoulder. She knows he sees things the rest of them don't.

> **MARA:** You can see it. *(beat)* How long?
>
> *(Raul looks at the grey number. A long time.)*
>
> **RAUL:** Spring.

Mara nods, slowly, as if spring were a place. Ruth's hand stops on the cloth, then goes on.

**5 · OUTSIDE — two Ghosts at the fence · FRICTION (1:15–2:15, shots 5–7).** Night. RAFE waits by the woodpile in his
leased shell. Between his hands, in Raul's Lens, the pack bar from E6, its last field still `Memory: fragment`. He runs
it backward. The bar drains toward home. [UI, one beat of Rafe's own POV: at the frame edge, dim, `BEAM WINDOW ·
COLONY`. He doesn't look at it.]

> **RAFE:** Bring the shard home and it ends with the body. *(beat)* She wouldn't feel a thing.

**6 · PAST HIM.** Raul says nothing. Rafe steps closer, and speaks not to Raul but *to the shell*, to whoever else might
be listening in it.

> **RAFE:** Not you. Her.

[UI: at the foot of Raul's frame, his shell's speaker tag: `RAUL`. It holds. It does not flip.] Wind. Nobody comes. Rafe
lets the bar fade.

**7 · THE RECORD.** Rafe turns away toward the dark. As he goes, Raul's Lens reads him the way it reads anyone: one line
under his shoulder, small, white. [UI: `Slip: none`.] Once, he looked away from the third glyph (S2E18). This time he
doesn't.

**8 · ONE FRAME, TWO LINES · TURN (2:15–2:30, shots 8–9).** Raul's POV, held: through the hut's doorway, Ruth's hand on
the girl's forehead and the grey number above her, `HORIZON · 181 d` now, the day turned over; and, in the same frame,
nearer, walking out of it, the man with `Slip: none` under his shoulder. One will die. One never has.

**9 · FINAL IMAGE.** Back in raw sight, no UI: the clay cup on its side by the fire, unbroken. Hard cut to black.

*(Theme in subtext, not stated: the countdown is the first honest thing the god has ever been given, and Raul answers
it with a season instead of a number. Rafe offers the one thing she came down to lose, the not-feeling, and the record
that undoes his argument is not anything he says but the one thing he never did: die. "Slip: none" is also every
earlier Raul's question answered: the copy who left by beam is the only Raul who never felt the itch.)*
