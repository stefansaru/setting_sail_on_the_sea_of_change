# Production Breakdown — S4E17: "The Spare Margin"

> Detailed production/cinematography breakdown for [[setting-sail-the-seed-ledger]] **Season 4, Episode
> 17** (Act IV, Phase D). **Tier 2 watching Tier 3 and Tier 1.** The High Clock spends **the spare margin**, the
> chemistry the ramp was going to grow bodies with, on keeping Hull Four's refugees alive and re-stilling them. The
> planned colonist count is cut, and the cut takes the slot just queued for Kai (`Q-51`). Rafe votes for it, against
> his partner (a-ha #6 payoff). Below, the base is told only that the ramp is "paused." Kai, fast enough now to attend,
> hears both versions in one afternoon. Body **0%**; glyph **cracked**. World: [[the-stasis-economy]] ·
> [[the-sealed-manifest]] · [[velocity-tiers]] · [[the-seed-fleet]] · [[the-perspective-lens-ui]].

## Setup & hook
Extreme close on a **status lamp** on the ramp board, amber, beside a chalked name; the lamp below it is dark. Pull back:
Hull One's synthesis bay, its column of thirty slots with the bottom nine dark (E16). The lamp in the hook is slot 21,
Kai's. The reel is one vote seen three ways: a lamp in the bay, a ten-minute session upstairs, and an hour-long sentence
downstairs that says almost nothing. It ends where it began, on lamp 21.

## Philosophical climax
Nothing in the vote is cruel. Nine people arrived in bodies with nowhere to put them, and the High Clock keeps them
alive with the only chemistry there is, the chemistry meant for people who don't have bodies yet. Every life kept is a
colonist not grown. The man who votes for it votes against the woman who named him, and does it in the same flat
second as everyone else. Downstairs the base is given a word that is true and tells them nothing. Kai is the only one
who sees all three at once: the lamp, the hand that turned it off, and the sentence written over it. Kai's slot is
one of the lamps, and Teo's waiting body is one of the reasons. Kai has no quarrel with either, and that is what makes
it hard to hold.

## Script (draft v1)
> 9:16 vertical · ~2.5 min · cinematic photoreal · *Black Mirror / Silo* register. **KAI** (`CHR-08`), a Ghost at Tier
> 2; **RAFE** (`CHR-02`) on the High Clock's floor; **THE HIGH CLOCK** (`FAC-05`), figures in violet light; **TEO**
> (`CHR-09`) in the re-stilling bay; **RAFE'S PARTNER** (unnamed; E4, E16) on the base's commons. One spoken word
> upstairs; one notice downstairs, read as text. Kai says nothing.

*Castes (`WR-B1`): Kai, Rafe, his partner and the council are Ghosts; Teo is a Shadow, awake since the lifeboat, his
body waiting to be re-stilled (`Q-63`); the base on the commons is mostly Ghosts on watch with a few Shadows (`Q-68`).
Every tag is Kai's (`WR-L2`). Clocks (`WR-T1`, `WR-T4`): Kai at Tier 2 (3,600×) on Hull One's allowance. The council's
afternoon, about four subjective hours at Tier 3, is about a sixth of a real second (14,400 s ÷ 86,400), which Kai
lives as **ten minutes** (0.167 s × 3,600). The base's one-minute notice at Tier 1 is one real second, which Kai lives as
**an hour**. The whole reel is about four of Kai's hours: four real seconds, in which a cradle lid at Tier 0 closes
once. Series clock: about T+4.2, hours after E16's docking. Spends (`WR-E1`): the reallocation in chemistry (nine bodies
kept, nine one-off re-stills costing about two slots' worth between them); the session in watts. Body 0%.*

**1 · EXTREME CLOSE (9:16) · HOOK (0:00–0:05).** An amber status lamp beside a chalked name on a slate. The lamp below
it is dark. [SOUND: a coolant tick.]

**2 · PULL BACK — the synthesis bay · CONTEXT (0:05–1:15, shots 2–5).** Hull One's synthesis bay: capsules in two rows,
amber pools in the dark (E4's room, another hull). Kai stands among them as a render on the bay's feed, beside the
ramp board, where the `HULL ONE` column's bottom nine lamps are dark. The lit one in the hook is slot 21. [UI: *21 ·
KAI · QUEUED*.] [UI, top: *CHEMISTRY · SPARE MARGIN*, a bar.]

**3 · THE COLD BAY.** Through an interior port, the re-stilling bay: a single lamp over a cradle, and a queue of bodies
waiting their turn, awake. One of them is TEO, sitting up, mid-gesture, a hand half-raised toward the bay camera as if
he's seen someone he knows. At Kai's clock he doesn't move. [UI: *TEO · SHADOW · RE-STILL · QUEUED*.] Kai looks at the
raised hand for a long time.

**4 · THE NOTCH.** The cradle lid closes over the body ahead of Teo, so slowly it seems to set like glass. [UI: the
margin bar loses a notch.] On the board, lamp 21 dims by a shade and holds.

**5 · THE BOARD.** [UI: *20 · QUEUED*. *21 · KAI · QUEUED*.] Still lit.

**6 · UPSTAIRS · FRICTION (1:15–2:15, shots 6–9).** Kai opens the council's session. It isn't closed: nobody below Tier
2 could attend it anyway. Violet light, no walls. THE HIGH CLOCK stand in it at 24× Kai's speed, a quick bright
flicker, their murmur pitched high. Kai's Lens transcribes. [UI: *REALLOCATE SPARE MARGIN → HULL FOUR BODIES · RAMP
30 → 19*.]

**7 · THE MARKS.** One by one, beside the collar tags, marks appear. [UI: *1:2000 · FOR*. *1:1000 · FOR*. *1:1000 ·
AGAINST*. *1:1000 · FOR*.] Among them RAFE, very still for someone moving that fast. Beside his empty collar frame a
mark appears. [UI: *FOR*.] He doesn't look at anyone when it does.

> **A MEMBER OF THE HIGH CLOCK** *(pitched up, gone almost before it's said):* Carried.

**8 · THE BOARD AGAIN.** [UI: *20 · WITHDRAWN*. *21 · KAI · WITHDRAWN*.] The violet floor is already empty; the council
has gone on to its next afternoon.

**9 · DOWNSTAIRS.** Kai's Lens finds Hull One's commons, Tier 1: a warm, ordinary afternoon, nearly frozen. Near its
centre, RAFE'S PARTNER, the woman from the ramp's glass (E4), listening to a notice. To Kai it is a long low drone. The
Lens transcribes it a word every few of Kai's minutes. [UI: *The ramp is paused.*] Then, much later: [UI: *All margins
nominal.*] Her face, sixty times slower than Kai's, begins to relax.

**10 · TWO CIRCLES · TURN (2:15–2:30, shots 10–11).** Kai's top row splits into two Context circles above the one
margin bar: [UI: *WITHDRAWN*] and [UI: *PAUSED*].

**11 · HOLD.** Back in the bay. Through the port, Teo's hand, still raised. Lamp 21 goes dark, the way the others did.
Cut to black.

*(Theme in subtext, not stated: one budget, three clocks, and what's true depends on which clock you stand on. At the
Apex it's a vote; at the base it's a pause; in the bay it's a lamp going out. Rafe votes to keep strangers alive against
the woman who gave him his name, and she is told something kind at a speed that lets her believe it. Kai's slot pays
for Teo's waiting body, and Kai doesn't yet know what Teo sent. By the end of the reel "we kept them safe" and "we never
asked them" are both true, and nobody says either.)*
