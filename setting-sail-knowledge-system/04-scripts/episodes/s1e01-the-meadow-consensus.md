## Setting & production context
Tier 3 simulation preserve for premium cognitive development. The parents' physical bodies rest in
clinical capsule bays on Earth (`FCT-009`); their minds wander the simulated paradise. The child
already lives inside the capsule — safe, warm, learning (`FCT-010`).

**Who pays for the Apex (`Q-85`, `WR-T5`):** the preserve is a **birth grant**. Every new pair of parents gets a
short Tier-3 stay for the capsule placement. Short in real hours, long on the inside: never said on screen. The
child's own mind wakes in the Tier-2 nursery (E2). Writers' room: the gap between the two clocks is a-ha #13,
paid in S3 when Raul finds his parents' ledger (`Q-108`).

**Castes (`Q-87`):** the man and the woman are Shadows. The child is a Shadow from placement.

## Visual composition
- **Wide (the meadow walk):** cinematic wide-angle; barefoot couple in an endless pristine meadow;
  warm golden morning light on dew; symmetrical framing pushing the couple low, vast open headroom of
  ancient trees and clearing skies — peace, breathing room, security.
- **Close (the realization):** intimate medium close-up — the couple face to face against blurred
  greenery; warmth and quiet wonder. Photoreal intimacy via long lens + shallow focus + warm natural
  light (storyboard's "watercolor" = sketch note only).

## Script

Morning light warmed the quiet meadow. Dew sparkled on the grass and shifted with the breeze. A man
and a woman walked barefoot, slow and silent, listening to birds chirping in the distance.

They stopped walking.

The woman turned to face him. Her eyes searched his, calm but full of wonder.

"So, we don't have to do anything else?" she asked.

He nodded. "No. It's all automatic now. We made the choice. The rest is taken care of."

She looked out over the meadow. The tall grass swayed gently. A few birds flew low, their wings
brushing the morning air.

"And our baby… it's born?" she asked softly.

"Yes," he said. "Inside the capsule. Safe, warm, learning."

The wind shifted slightly, carrying the scent of wildflowers. A bird landed nearby, tilted its head,
then flew off again.

She stepped closer and rested her head on his shoulder. He wrapped his arms around her. They stood
like that for a moment, then continued to walk, side by side, through the meadow.

She looked ahead. "When can we meet them?"

He thought for a second. "We'll meet them when they're ready. Not as newborns. As learners."

She nodded. "I don't feel any different now that I am a mom. Do you feel any different?"

"We're the same," he said. "It just feels different than before. But some things never change. Like holding hands."

She nodded and reached for his hand. Their fingers wove together. They walked on, close together.
Behind them, sunlight shimmered through the leaves.

He squeezed her hand. She squeezed back.
