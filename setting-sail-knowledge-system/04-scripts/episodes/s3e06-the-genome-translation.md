# Production Breakdown — S3E06: "The Genome Translation"

> Detailed production/cinematography breakdown for [[setting-sail-the-cartographers-ledger]] **Season 3, Episode 6**
> (**Act II opens**: *Writing Herself Into Flesh*). **The founding stratum, Tier 0.** The President, a
> [[the-ghost-entity|Ghost]] (`Q-98`), has resolved to be born (E5). Her kept genome, the last copy on Earth, is the
> lossiest save-file there is ([[the-memory-substrate]]): only a shard of her fits, and the child it makes will
> **forget she is God**. The turn is **Rafe's** (`Q-117`): the god asks the one mind who has lived as the fork that
> left whether a fork should be made, and he says yes and packs the shard himself. Then the rival-engineer tries to
> build it better, to make the memory fit, and can't. World: [[the-cosmic-clone-paradox]] · [[capsule-birth]] ·
> [[the-distributed-president]] · [[the-perspective-lens-ui]].

## Setup & hook
E5's last frame, carried on: source code resolved into a slow grey **genome helix**, turning in the founding stratum's
light. A hand comes into frame and opens the helix like a book. It is Rafe's. Above the helix, at the edge of his Lens,
the law-feed that has never once addressed a person writes one line, to him.

## Cinematography — the oldest room
| Element | The founding stratum (in-grid) | The lab feed (real) |
|---------|-------------------------------|---------------------|
| **Lighting** | The fossil grey under the Doughnut world (E2): flat, sourceless, desaturated; the helix the only thing with an inner glow, cold white. The Sea logo, faint, on the far wall. | Flavin-cold service light (`VIS-15`); frost on glass; one amber status lamp. |
| **Camera** | Locked, symmetrical, machine-smooth (`VIS-04`); the only movement is the helix turning and Rafe's hands. Raul is held at the frame edge, watching. | A fixed feed angle inside Rafe's Lens, small, at the frame corner, until the end. |
| **Sound** | Near silence; the stratum has no room tone, only a low hum that plays at 1:1 like an old recording. Rafe's breath (a Ghost's habit). | A rig's servo whine; the tick of frost. |

**Time grammar.** The stratum's light never changes, so passing weeks are shown outside it: Raul's empty shell, parked
at the village fence while he is down here, in three inserts: leaves on its shoulders, rain, then frost.

## UI behavior (the Perspective Lens)
Rafe's Lens and Raul's; both are Ghosts with Lenses on. **No glyph** in the reel.
- **The question:** at the top edge, the law-feed scrolls as always; one line stops scrolling and writes itself, signed
  `// M.C.`: *Should a fork be made?* It is in both Lenses at once.
- **Rafe's squares** carry over from E2: *stay · build · love*, the last one greyed, `Recipient: 4.4 ly`. While he
  works, *build* is lit.
- **The pack bar (never labelled):** three rows under the helix. *Genome* fills and ticks. *Shard* fills and ticks.
  The third reads `Memory: fragment` and **will not fill**. Each rebuild resets the bar to empty and it climbs again;
  the third row always stops at the same word.
- **The lab feed:** a small window, real time: an automated surgical rig, a frosted cell holder, a vial.
- **The turn:** Rafe seals the bar with the third row unfilled. *build* dims. In the feed, the holder's last cell is
  drawn, the holder is **empty**, and the vial frosts over.

## Philosophical climax
Rafe is the only mind on Earth who has been the copy that went away and became someone. The god asks him, and he
doesn't hesitate: a fork is a person; he is one; you don't need to know what she'll be. Then the engineer in him, the
one who believes a better-built system fixes things, refuses the price he just agreed to. He rebuilds the pack for six
weeks to make the memory fit, so the child would *know* (so the fork could come home whole). Every build ends at the
same word. The forgetting isn't a flaw in his work. It's the medium. He seals it, and the thing he couldn't build is
the thing that will make her mortal.

## Script (draft v1)
> 9:16 vertical · ~2.5 min · cinematic photoreal · *Black Mirror / Silo* register. **RAFE** (`Q-14`, `Q-22`), Raul's
> Branch, the rival-engineer; **RAUL** (`CHR-02`), the S3 lead; **THE PRESIDENT** (`CHR-01`), here only a signed line
> in the law-feed, never a voice. Three spoken lines.

*Castes (`WR-B1`): Rafe is a Ghost (the E16 reply copy, woken E2, `Q-54`), in-grid, no shell needed down here. Raul is a
Ghost; his leased shell stands empty at the village fence while he is in-grid; Lens on since S1E20, alignment off
(`Q-119`). The President is a Ghost (`Q-98`), distributed across every tier at once; the shard is of an **adult** god,
the signed exception to `WR-M2` (`Q-65`), and it will end in a Branch (`WR-M1`, E18–E20). The automated rig is a
machine, no caste. Timing (`WR-T4`): the founding stratum plays only at 1:1 (`Q-117`), so Rafe and Raul run at Tier 0,
one clock, real = lived: the reel spans **about six real weeks** at about T+20.5, from the question to the seal. A
whole mind takes most of a year to pack at its own pace (`WR-M5`); a shard is a fraction. For the President's Apex
threads the same six weeks are about **9,900 years** (42 days × 86,400 ≈ 3.6 million days), a fact the reel never
shows. Ruth and Adam, offscreen, are about 20.*

**1 · THE HELIX · HOOK (0:00–0:05).** Grey light. A genome helix turns slowly at the centre of the frame, cold white
from inside (E5's final image). A hand reaches in and opens it like a book. [SOUND: a hum at 1:1, like tape.]

**2 · THE FOUNDING STRATUM · CONTEXT (0:05–1:15, shots 2–4).** Wider. RAFE stands at the helix, sleeves pushed up, the
same face as Raul's, rendered with more years on it. RAUL at the frame edge, a step back. Far behind them, faint on a
grey wall, the old Sea logo (E2). [UI: the law-feed scrolling at the top edge. One line **stops**, and writes itself
in both Lenses, signed: *Should a fork be made?* `// M.C.`]

**3 · TWO FACES.** Rafe reads it. Raul reads it, then looks at Rafe, not at the line. Rafe's bottom squares: *stay ·
build · love*, the last greyed, `Recipient: 4.4 ly`.

> **RAFE:** Yes.

**4 · RAUL.**

> **RAUL** *(quiet):* You don't know what she'll be.
>
> **RAFE:** Neither did I.

[UI: *build* lights.] Rafe puts both hands into the helix.

**5 · THE PACK · FRICTION (1:15–2:15, shots 5–8).** [UI: three rows under the helix. *Genome* fills: tick. *Shard*
fills: tick. `Memory: fragment`: the bar climbs a hair and stops.] In the corner of his Lens, the **lab feed**: an
automated rig in Flavin light, a frosted cell holder, one empty vial waiting.

**6 · THE REBUILD.** Rafe pulls the helix apart and lays it out again, tighter, the engineer's patience: strands
re-ordered, compressed, re-seated. [UI: the bar resets to empty, climbs. *Genome* tick. *Shard* tick. `Memory:
fragment`.] Insert: **the village fence, real**, Raul's empty shell standing in fallen leaves.

**7 · AGAIN.** He tries a third way: something stripped out to make room. The helix dims where he cuts. [UI: the bar
climbs; the third row stops at the same word, in the same place.] Insert: the shell in the rain. Raul hasn't moved
from the edge of the stratum. He doesn't help, and he doesn't tell Rafe to stop.

**8 · RECALL CUE (≤ 2 s).** S1E16: the Synchronization Node, `MERGE ⟷ BRANCH`, Raul's mouth on *"Go home, Rafe."* Gone.

**9 · THE SEAL · TURN (2:15–2:30, shots 9–10).** Insert: the shell at the fence, frost on its shoulders. Rafe takes his
hands out of the helix. He looks at the third row a long time. Then he seals it as it is. [UI: `Memory: fragment`,
unfilled, locked. *build* dims.] In the lab feed, the rig draws the last cell from the holder, and the holder's slot
stands **empty**.

**10 · FINAL IMAGE.** The feed fills the frame: the vial, frost spreading across its glass until nothing inside it can be
seen. Hold. Silence. Hard cut to black.

*(Theme in subtext, never stated: the man who refused to be merged back into Raul is the one who signs a fork into the
world, and the engineer who believes in better systems spends six weeks proving that this one has no better version.
The word he can't get past isn't a bug in his build; it's the price of being born. The empty holder makes the lab's
copy the only one on Earth without anyone saying so.)*
