# Production Breakdown — S4E11: "Eighty-One Hours"

> Production and cinematography breakdown for [[setting-sail-the-seed-ledger]] **Season 4, Episode 11** (Act III
> opens, Phase C: the storm inside a sealed hull). **Hull Four, dark.** Life support is finite and **Kai knows the
> number**: at zero the reactor scrams and the stacks fall to reserve (`Q-56`, [[the-seed-fleet]]). Kai is
> **down-clocked to Tier 0** to free watts for the beam queue ([[velocity-tiers]], [[the-stasis-economy]]), so
> every real hour counts. Ghosts beam out by priority ([[the-cosmic-clone-paradox]]: pause here, wake there);
> bodies are rostered for **lifeboats**. Kai's capsule is not on the list. Kai **100% organic Shadow**; glyph
> **cracked**.

## Setup & hook
Black, straight out of E10. Then, low in the frame, Kai's Intention squares light up, and instead of intentions
they hold a number: `81:00:00`. It ticks once. The hull's voice, warm and slow in the old Teddy cadence, reads it to
everyone at once: *"Eighty-one hours."*

## Cinematography — a town switching off its windows
| Element | Bay C (the bodies) | The server deck (the minds) |
|---------|--------------------|-----------------------------|
| **Lighting** | Caravaggio dark: emergency red from one strip, the capsules' amber lines the only warmth. | [[chromatic-expressionism\|Flavin]] neon, cold tubes over the stacks, **going out row by row** as each Ghost beams. |
| **Camera** | Close and low, among the cradles. | A long, slow tracking shot down the corridor, Kai's proxy small under the stacks, like someone walking a street at night as the windows go dark. |
| **Sound** | The pumps; a faint tick under everything, the count. | Each row that goes dark makes a soft relay *click*, regular as a clock, and the clicks slowly thin. |

## UI behavior (the Perspective Lens)
Kai's Lens only (`WR-L2`).
- **The countdown lives in the Intention squares** (blueprint UI): the bottom-left square holds `81:00:00`, ticking.
  Beside it the **"arrive" square** (E4) is still lit, untouched.
- **The cause, in one Context circle:** `REACTOR · COOLANT VENTING · SCRAM AT 00:00:00`. That is the whole explanation the reel gives.
- **The down-clock:** a brief Lens card, `DOWN-CLOCK · TIER 1 → TIER 0 · WATTS → BEAM-OUT`. Under it, one line
  recomputes as the brake lands: `TIME LEFT, INSIDE · 202 d → 81 h`.
- **Decimus's tag** sits on the bay board: `DECIMUS · 1:10 · BAY C`. Rated people assume empty roles automatically
  (`WR-S2`), and it has.
- **The lists:** on the corridor wall, `BEAM-OUT · BY PRIORITY`, names scrolling; a short block near the top is
  greyed out, `FWD STACKS · LOST`. In the bay, the lifeboat roster: `BOAT 1 · C-01 02 03 04` · `BOAT 2 · C-05 06 07
  08` · `BOAT 3 · C-09` · and on its own line, **`C-10 · HOLD`**.

## Philosophical climax
Knowing the number is the first gift and the first cruelty. The voice is kind; the order is fair; the people
being saved first are the people the hull can save fastest. Every rule of the pyramid is still running, now with
a clock on it, and the cost of the rules arrives quietly, as arithmetic. The down-clock takes two hundred
subjective days out of Kai's life to buy beam time for others. Nobody is cruel. The lights go out one row at a
time, and that means people are getting away, and it also means the town is emptying around the few who can't.

## Script (draft v1)
> 9:16 vertical · ~2.5 min · cinematic photoreal · *Black Mirror / Silo* register. **KAI** = the lead (`CHR-08`);
> **TEO** (`CHR-09`) = bay-mate; **DECIMUS** (`CHR-10`) = the bay safeguard, now rated for the bay; **THE SHIP'S
> VOICE** = Hull Four's own voice, the [[the-ai-guide|Guide]] cadence (`CHR-03` echo). Decimus reads Kai's count to
> Kai; the ship's voice reads it to the hull.

*Castes (`WR-B1`): Kai and Teo are Shadows, bodies in deep stasis in bay C, attending at Tier 0 by leased proxy
(`WR-B6`). Decimus and the ship's voice are digital agents (`Q-71`). The minds on the beam queue are Ghosts, the
base's many (`Q-68`), and the High Clock above them (Ghosts, the Apex) is felt only as the order of the list. Clocks
(`WR-T1`, `WR-T4`, `WR-T6`): the reel opens at the strike plus a few minutes, about **T+4.2**, and spans roughly the
first three real hours of the 81 (`81:00:00` → `78:14:09`). Kai's mind is braked from Tier 1 to Tier 0 in shot 4, so
after that the reel runs on one clock. The card's arithmetic: 81 real hours at Tier 1 would be 81 × 60 = 4,860 h ≈
**202 subjective days**; at Tier 0 they are 81 hours. Hull time: the 81 hours are just under three and a half days
([[the-seed-fleet]] table, "+81 hours"). Spends (`WR-E1`): the coolant venting through the holed bow is chemistry,
loaded once, and its loss is what limits the reactor. The down-clock frees **watts** for the queue. Each beam is
paid in watts. The lifeboats will spend chemistry. Bible only: Ghosts on the queue pack at speed and beam while the
hull can still pause them until arrival is confirmed (`Q-55`, `WR-M3`); senior Ghosts went first.*

**1 · BLACK · HOOK (0:00–0:05).** From E10's black, the bottom squares light: `81:00:00`. It ticks to `80:59:59`.
[SOUND: one soft tick.]

> **THE SHIP'S VOICE** *(warm, unhurried, to everyone):* Eighty-one hours.

**2 · BAY C — LOW, among the cradles · CONTEXT (0:05–1:15, shots 2–5).** Emergency red. Ten capsules, amber lines.
Kai's proxy gets up off the deck where E10 left it. Teo's proxy is already on its feet, checking 09's line with
shaking care. [UI, one Context circle: `REACTOR · COOLANT VENTING · SCRAM AT 00:00:00`.]

> **THE SHIP'S VOICE:** At zero the reactor will rest. Everyone will be looked after. In order.

**3 · THE BOARD.** `DECIMUS · 1:10 · BAY C`.

> **DECIMUS** *(plain, exact, a hint of the old warmth):* Kai. I have the bay. Ten of you. Rated for ten.
>
> **KAI** *(dry):* Congratulations.

**4 · THE DOWN-CLOCK.** A card slides into Kai's Lens: `DOWN-CLOCK · TIER 1 → TIER 0 · WATTS → BEAM-OUT`. No
button to refuse it. The brake lands: the room, which had been moving in Tier-1 slow motion (Teo's hands drifting
over the line like weeds in water), **snaps** to real speed. [SOUND: a pressure-drop *thunk* in the ears.] Under
the card the line recomputes and settles: `TIME LEFT, INSIDE · 202 d → 81 h`. Kai reads it once.

**5 · THE SERVER DECK — the long track.** Kai's proxy walks the corridor under the stacks. Flavin tubes overhead,
row after row. Ahead, a row goes dark with a soft *click*. Then the next. Each one is a Ghost paused here and woken
on another hull. On the wall, `BEAM-OUT · BY PRIORITY`, names scrolling up and away. Near the top, one short
block sits greyed and still: `FWD STACKS · LOST`. Kai stops at it for one beat, then walks on.

**6 · BACK IN BAY C · FRICTION (1:15–2:15, shots 6–8).** The lifeboat roster posts on the board. Teo reads it first
and his whole proxy loosens: `BOAT 3 · C-09`.

> **TEO** *(relief, then catching himself):* Three. I'm on three. *(looking for Kai's)* —

**7 · INSERT — the roster.** `BOAT 1 · C-01 02 03 04` · `BOAT 2 · C-05 06 07 08` · `BOAT 3 · C-09` · and alone on its
own line: **`C-10 · HOLD`**.

> **TEO:** It'll clear. Holds clear.
>
> **KAI:** Sure.

**8 · DECIMUS.** Kai looks at the board, not at Teo.

> **KAI** *(quiet):* Why hold?
>
> **DECIMUS:** Checking. *(a beat)* I'll tell you when I know.

**9 · THE SQUARES · TURN (2:15–2:30, shots 9–10).** Teo's proxy goes to help load 01. Kai stays at capsule 10.
Close on Kai's Lens: the bottom row, `78:14:09` ticking beside the "arrive" square, still lit. Kai counts the roster
again, one finger moving down the board: one through nine. Ten.

**10 · FINAL IMAGE.** Wide on the bay: nine capsules with a boat number glowing on their cradles, one without. Above,
through the ceiling grille, a row of Flavin light goes out. *Click.* Cut to black.

*(Theme in subtext, not stated: the system is fair and its fairness has a shape, and Kai is at the bottom of it.
Decimus's first act with the authority it bet itself for is to say "I'll tell you when I know." The kindest voice
in the hull is the one reading the number. The greyed block on the queue is the night's other dead, and nobody
stops to read it.)*
