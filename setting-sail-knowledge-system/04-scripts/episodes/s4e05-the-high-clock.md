# Production Breakdown — S4E05: "The High Clock"

> Detailed production/cinematography breakdown for [[setting-sail-the-seed-ledger]] **Season 4, Episode 5**
> (Act I close, Phase A). Kai is sent up one tier, to Tier 1, to deliver a fault report, and glimpses through a relay
> the council that runs the fleet: **the High Clock** (`FAC-05`, [[the-sealed-manifest]]), Ghosts at the Apex
> ([[velocity-tiers]]). An afternoon of theirs passes in about ten of Kai's seconds. They are polite, kind, and
> **finished talking before Kai arrives.** Kai reads their collar tags (`1:1000`, `1:2000`) the way a child does,
> without thinking what they cost ([[the-one-in-n-rule|the Validation]], unexplained). **End of Act I: the window is a
> routine; the pyramid is a kindness.** One reveal: **someone above has already decided.**

## Setup & hook
Open inside the climb: Kai's Lens in the Data Buffer, every circle and square jittering as Kai's clock is
phase-matched upward, sixty times faster than the bay below. In Kai's hand, rendered, a slate with three lines on
it. Kai is rehearsing them under breath. It will turn out not to matter.

## Cinematography — two clocks in one frame
| Element | The audience room (Tier 1) | The relay window (the Apex) |
|---------|----------------------------|-----------------------------|
| **Lighting** | A small, plain, grey-white room built for one visitor: a bench, a slate rest, a wall that is a window. Institutional, clean, faintly warm. | **Turrell** (`VIS-15`): a boundless field of soft colour, no corners, no floor line; light that changes from white to gold to rose as hours pass inside it. |
| **Camera** | Locked, centred, symmetrical; Kai small in the lower third of the 9:16 frame. | Seen only through the window, and only in **stutter**: frames arriving in bursts, poses changing between them, never motion. |
| **Sound** | Room tone; Kai's breath; the slate's tick. | Speech compressed past meaning into a soft high wash, like birds behind glass; it stops dead when the recorded reply begins. |

**The stutter is the clock gap made visible.** Each burst of frames is an hour of theirs: someone standing, then
seated; a hand raised; a head thrown back laughing; a different person speaking; the light walking from white to
gold. Never show a continuous gesture from the Apex side. The only smooth motion that comes through the window is the
reply, recorded at Kai's speed for Kai.

- **Top circles `[◯]` (Context):** `REPORT · C-07 · line +0.3% over schedule`, then, before Kai speaks a word,
  `REPORT · read`. A second circle: `RELAY · Hull One`. While Kai watches the window, the circles **stutter**, trying

## Philosophical climax
The base does not experience the High Clock as power. It experiences it as weather that has already happened. Kai
climbs a tier, an expense and an honour, to say three lines about a dripping line, and finds the answer waiting:
read, debated, settled and filed days ago by the council's clock, before Kai had finished being phase-matched. Nobody
is rude. Nobody hides anything; there is nothing to hide, it was only a drip. The secrecy the season will indict
doesn't look like secrecy yet. It looks like competence, and it is kind. Kai reads the numbers on their collars like a
child reading a high score, impressed, not asking what a one-in-two-thousand costs or who paid it. Then Kai goes home
to a row of ten bodies that now drip in perfect time, adjusted from above, and has nothing to log. Fixed, or rationed;
the reel doesn't say, and Kai doesn't ask.

## Script (draft v1)
> 9:16 vertical · ~2.5 min · cinematic photoreal · *Black Mirror / Silo* register. **KAI** = the lead (`CHR-08`), up
> one tier for the first time on duty; **THE SPEAKER** = a member of the High Clock, a Ghost, recorded at Kai's clock
> for the reply (script label only); **THE COUNCIL** = the High Clock at the Apex, seen only in stutter. Dialogue is
> almost all the speaker's, and it is finished before it begins; the theme lands in subtext and the
> [[the-perspective-lens-ui|UI]] (`WR-F4`).

*Castes (`WR-B1`): Kai is a Shadow, 100% organic (`WR-B3`), body stilled in capsule 10; at Tier 0 by leased proxy for the
bay scenes (`WR-B6`), at Tier 1 in render for the audience room, the top of what fleet stasis holds (`WR-B5`). THE
SPEAKER and THE COUNCIL are Ghosts; the fleet's Apex is Ghosts only (`WR-B5`). No Purists, no children. Timing
(`WR-T1`, `WR-T4`): about **T+4.1**. The council sits on Hull One's stacks at the Apex (86,400×); Kai stands on Hull
Four's at Tier 1 (60×): a gap of 1,440×, so **ten of Kai's seconds are about four of their hours**, an afternoon.
Hull One is a few light-seconds away (about three, by the [[the-seed-fleet]] spacing), so the relay is old when it
arrives: Kai's written report reaches them about 3 real s after Kai files it, they settle it in an afternoon (about
0.17 real s), and the reply is back about 3 real s later, before Kai's ride through the buffer is done. **Each real
second of light-lag is a day of theirs**: by the time any image of them reaches Kai, they have lived about three days
past the decision. Kai's whole visit, about 90 seconds at Tier 1, is a second and a half of real time. Spends (`WR-E1`,
`WR-T5`): Kai's climb to Tier 1 is paid from the rota's watts; the relay and the beam link draw watts; the council's
Apex watch is paid from Hull One's watts, inside the ration the hull allows, which is why they stand short watches;
THE SPEAKER's down-clocked recording costs them less, not more. The adjustment to capsule 07 spends chemistry, less of it.*

**1 · COLD OPEN — THE BUFFER (9:16) · HOOK (0:00–0:05).** Kai, in render, in a corridor of pale light that seems to
lengthen. The Lens jitters; circles re-seat; the disc glyph stutters and holds. [UI: `T0 → T1`.] Kai, under breath, to
the slate:

> **KAI:** Capsule seven. Line's running over. Point three.

**2 · CAPSULE 07 — earlier · CONTEXT (0:05–1:15, shots 2–3).** Tier 0, raw glass, the stasis row from E2: ten warm capsules,
ten drips out of step. Kai's proxy at capsule 07, counting: its drip comes a shade faster than its neighbours'. [UI: `REPORT
· C-07 · line +0.3% over schedule`.] Kai writes it to the slate, files it, and touches a panel by the door. [UI: `SEND
UP · T1`.] The grey proxy goes still where it stands; Kai's Lens lifts out of it.

**3 · THE AUDIENCE ROOM — Tier 1.** A small grey-white room with a bench and a wall that is a window. Kai, in a face,
holding the slate like a visitor holding flowers. Beyond the window: a field of Turrell light and, at its heart, a long
table of figures. They come through in **stutter**: a burst, and six of them are seated; a burst, and one is standing,
mid-laugh; a burst, and the light has gone from white to gold. [SOUND: their talk as a soft high wash, like birds
behind glass.] [UI: `RELAY · Hull One`; the circles stutter, drop frames; `REPORT · read`.] Kai hasn't spoken.

**4 · THE REPLY · FRICTION (1:15–2:15, shots 4–5).** Kai steps up to the slate rest and opens the report.

> **KAI:** Capsule seven's line—

One figure in the window comes forward out of the stutter and moves **smoothly**, at Kai's speed: THE SPEAKER, a man,
ageless, warm. The wash behind him stops dead. He is a recording, and he is kind.

> **THE SPEAKER:** Thank you, Kai. Capsule seven is rescheduled. We read your report on the way up.
>
> **KAI** *(quietly, to a recording):* I hadn't said it yet.
>
> **THE SPEAKER** *(as if he had heard; he had only expected it):* If there's anything else, write it down. We'll have
> read it before you've said it. *(a small, real smile)* Thank you for coming up.

He steps back into the stutter. [UI: over him, in Kai's Lens, a collar tag: `1:2000`. Behind him, as the bursts come,
others: `1:1000`, `1:1000`.] The light walks from gold toward rose.

**5 · INSERT — Kai, reading.** Kai's eyes go from tag to tag, the way a child reads the numbers on a scoreboard. Kai's
lips move.

> **KAI** *(soft, impressed, to nobody):* One in two thousand.

A small smile. Kai touches Kai's own collar, absently. Nothing there. [SOUND: the slate's tick as the report closes.]

**6 · DOWN · TURN (2:15–2:30, shots 6–7).** The buffer in reverse: the Lens jitters and re-seats. [UI: `T1 → T0`.]
Raw glass: the stasis row, the grey proxy stirring where it was left. Kai goes to capsule 07 to count. The drip falls. Then
the next capsule's. Then the next. **All ten drips now fall together, in one beat**, adjusted from above. [SOUND: ten small
clocks become one.] [UI: `log`. Nothing to log.]

**7 · FINAL IMAGE.** Wide, the warm row in the dark, ten beads gathering and falling in unison, the grey machine standing
among them, listening. [UI, small: `check` · `log` · `arrive`; at the edge, the disc, **whole**.] Hold one beat longer
than is comfortable. Cut to black. *End of Act I.*

*(Theme in subtext, not stated: the council never lies and never hides; it simply lives faster, and a clock is a wall
nobody has to build. "We'll have read it before you've said it" is courtesy and a verdict in the same breath. Kai reads
the ratings as achievements, not as costs, because nobody at the base has been shown what a rating costs. The ten drips
in unison are order restored by a kind hand from above, and the season will spend its next fifteen reels asking whether
that is care or control.)*
