# Script — "The Dream Hunger"

> A **standalone Act-III candidate reel** for [[setting-sail-tier-descent]] (slot `Q-11`), dramatizing
> [[tether-decay|the TTD countdown]]. Raul **`CHR-02` (~30% organic, still tethered — pre-E17)** · POV:
> Raul · register: **Turrell** violet → **Rothko Despair** → **Caravaggio** capsule. 9:16 · ~2.5 min.
> A near-miss that foreshadows his E17 death.

## Setup & hook (0:00–0:05)
A massive pulsing overlay dominates Raul's [[the-perspective-lens-ui|Lens]]: **`TTD: 00:11 (REAL-TIME)`**.
He stands in a flawless minimalist **[[chromatic-expressionism|Turrell]] violet data-vault**, mid-
conversation with his [[frequency-tribes|tribe]] — but his vision **glitches**: surreal organic
images (old-world trees, flowing water, human faces) bleed onto the clean geometry like distorted
**watercolor stains.** *A biological dream forcing its way into a server.*

## Friction (0:05–1:15 / Act I→II)
His terminal: the critical data update needs **10 more subjective hours.** His TTD: 11 real seconds at
Tier 2 (3,600×) = **11 subjective hours** before his physical brain turns to mush in the pod.
> **SYSTEM (clinical, chilled):** Tether decay accelerating. Your brain is dreaming without you. Down-clock
> now, or it lets you go.
>
> **RAUL (not looking up):** Ten hours. I have eleven.
>
> **SYSTEM:** You had.

Raul refuses to lose the tether and fall into a Ghost — *he still needs his body to reach the Purist
village.* So he does the dangerous thing: he **overclocks** to sprint the workload, his TTD timer
shredding away in fractions of a millisecond.

## Climax (1:15–2:15)
The vault curdles into an oppressive **Rothko "Despair"** charcoal field as his brain **starves.** His
avatar drops to its knees, clutching its head; real-world **capsule alarms** bleed into the audio. With
**2 subjective minutes** left, the download hits **100%** — and Raul slams the console, executing an
**emergency desynchronization.**

## Ending cut (2:15–2:30)
The silent digital vault **vanishes.** Hard cut to a raw, high-contrast **Caravaggio chiaroscuro**
close-up of Raul's *actual physical face* inside a dark metallic stasis capsule. His eyes **snap open**
behind the glass; he gasps, drenched in biological sweat, chest heaving in real-time. Then his eyes close
again, the alarms quiet, and the capsule monitor glows soft green:
> `[ Tether Stabilized · REM State Initiated · Sleep Cycle Active ]`
Fade out on his face as he drifts into a deep, dark, **biological human dream.**
