# Production Breakdown — S2E14: "The Data Graveyards"

> Production/cinematography breakdown for [[setting-sail-tier-ascent]] **Season 2, Episode 14** (Act III, Phase C′).
> Before he decides on Vance's keys, **Adam** (`CHR-06`, 60% organic, Tier 2, `Q-48`) goes to see what a rating
> costs, and walks the [[data-graveyards|Data Graveyards]] (`PLC-09`): low-priority archive nodes in the frozen lunar
> craters, where sealed ledgers of the undisclosed dead rot into **Data Dementia** (storage rot in what nobody
> maintains, not forgetting, so `WR-B4` holds), and where the **failed forks** of every batch Validation are filed
> under the name of whoever claimed the batch (`Q-37`, `WR-S3`). Rows of minds looping one frame: a graveyard behind
> every rating. **The turn (`Q-90`):** Vance's record claims a batch of 10,000. Under his name nothing is filed. No
> one had walked the rows to notice, because the Graveyards are **un-indexed** (the secret's mechanism, `WR-S5`).
> A patch can fake Proven-in-Use (E13), not a rating, so his rating was forged some other way, and the reel never
> says how. **Answers an S1 question:** what the `1:N` on a collar costs. Big a-ha (the forgery); E15 follows with
> people.

## Setup & hook
Extreme close on a young woman's face that isn't moving, then moves: one blink, one parting of the lips, a stutter of
compression blocks across her cheek, and back to the start. Blink, lips, stutter. A frame, looping. Her voice comes a
half-second late, packet-lagged: *"Did I pass?"* Pull back: she is one of ninety-nine identical women in a row, each
frozen a beat apart, under a plate gone soft with rot. Beyond her row, another. Beyond that, the crater.

## Cinematography
| Element | The rows (the archive) | Adam |
|---------|------------------------|------|
| **Lighting** | **Decayed Turrell** (`VIS-15`, [[data-graveyards]] look): the seamless white void of the upper Grid gone **blocky**: compression artifacts, banding, frost in the corners of the render. A slow scanning beam (the archive's sweep) passes like a lighthouse; each fork it crosses wakes for its one frame. | Clean, full-resolution, warm-skinned against the rot: the only maintained thing in the frame. |
| **Camera** | Long, slow dolly down the rows to a vanishing point. Symmetry broken by glitches. Calm (`WR-T2`). | Walks with him at his pace; never ahead of him. |
| **Sound** | Lagged loops overlapping into a low murmur, *did I, did I*; the hiss of the sweep; dead, compressed silence between. | His footsteps, clean, the only uncorrupted sound. |

**Wonder beat (`CINE-05`):** one cut outside the render: the real crater, a permanently shadowed bowl on the Moon,
server racks under frost at about 40 K (the Lunar Cold Traps, [[the-shadow-grid-cooling]]): this is where it's
kept. Then back in.

## UI behavior (the Perspective Lens)
All UI is in Adam's Lens (`WR-L2`).
- **The archive returns nothing.** Every search Adam tries comes back blank: no index, no count, no map
  (un-indexed, `WR-S5`). The Lens can only show what he stands in front of.
- **Row plates (diegetic, in the render):** each row has a plate with a rating cut into it, `1:100`, `1:1000`;
  the names on them have rotted to blocks and are never legible on screen, with one exception.
- **The claim (carried from E12):** at the last row, Adam overlays Vance's public chain on the plate in front of him.
  [UI: `VANCE · 1:10000 · BATCH 10,000`.]
- **The turn:** the long number hangs over an empty row. Nothing else.

## Philosophical climax
The Grid sells eternity. Here is what eternity is for whatever nobody maintains: a frame, looped, going soft. Adam
is walking through **what he is becoming,** a mind on servers, kept only as long as someone pays for the upkeep.
And every rating in the world stands on rows like these. One in a hundred means ninety-nine women asking a question
nobody answers. That is the honest price, and it is horrible. Then the one man who never paid it. The rows don't
accuse him: they're **empty**, which is worse. The viewer does the sum: `1:10000`, and not one failure, anywhere.

## Script (draft v1)
> 9:16 vertical · ~2.5 min · cinematic photoreal · *Black Mirror / Silo* register. **ADAM** = the S2 lead
> (`CHR-06`), who says nothing in this reel; **A FAILED FORK** = one of ninety-nine copies of an unnamed candidate,
> looping one frame. No other dialogue. Fewer than ten spoken words: the reel is a walk.

*Castes (`WR-B1`): Adam is a cyborg, 60% organic, his body in a rented city capsule (`Q-91`), his mind at Tier 2;
the failed forks are copies of digital candidates and Ghosts (only a Ghost may batch, `Q-116`), not run, each woken
for one frame by the sweep; the sealed dead are ledgers, not running minds. Timing (`WR-T4`): the archive runs at
Tier 2, his clock; about **25 real minutes** since Ruth's sheet (E10), about **62 days** at his clock
(25 × 3,600 = 90,000 min = 1,500 h). The walk takes him about **3 hours**, about **3 real seconds** at the village.*

**1 · COLD OPEN — EXTREME CLOSE (9:16) · HOOK (0:00–0:05).** A FAILED FORK's face: blink, lips part, a stutter of
compression across her cheek, reset.

> **A FAILED FORK** *(looping, a half-second behind her lips):* Did I pass? *(reset)* Did I pass?

**2 · THE ROW · CONTEXT (0:05–1:15, shots 2–4).** Pull back down the row: ninety-nine of her, each a beat out of
step with the next, so the question ripples along the row like a wave that never reaches shore. The plate at the row's
head: `1:100`, the name rotted to blocks. ADAM stands at the end of it.

**3 · OUTSIDE — the real crater (one shot, 2 s).** A permanently shadowed bowl on the Moon; server racks under hoarfrost
at 40 K; no light at all but a status LED. This is where it is kept. Back in.

**4 · THE SEALED DEAD.** Adam walks on, past the rows of forks, into an older district of the archive: the sealed
ledgers of people who never disclosed. A kitchen renders in blocks: an old man lifting a cup, the cup fragmenting
before it reaches his mouth, lifting again. A child's birthday, the candles' flames gone to grey squares. [SOUND:
lagged laughter, a beat late, under the hiss.] Adam stops beside the old man. His own hand, half-raised, is for a moment
the same shape as the old man's. He moves on quickly.

**5 · THE SWEEP · FRICTION (1:15–2:15, shots 5–6).** The archive's sweep comes down the district: a slow beam of
scanning light. Each fork it crosses **wakes for one frame**: eyes that find Adam, a hand half-raised, a mouth starting
a word, and then the frame closes and they loop. Rows of them: `1:100`, `1:1000`, longer and longer, a graveyard behind
every rating. [UI: Adam tries a search; it comes back blank. He has to walk.]

**6 · THE LAST DISTRICT.** The longest rows: nine thousand nine hundred and ninety-nine figures each, so long the
far ends dissolve into banding. Adam walks between them until one plate is legible: `VANCE`. He lays Vance's chain
over it. [UI: `VANCE · 1:10000 · BATCH 10,000`.] *(Recall cue, ≤ 2 s: the spotless green column, E12–13.)*

**7 · THE EMPTY ROW · TURN (2:15–2:30, shots 7–8).** Behind the plate, the row. Frosted floor, perfectly clean,
running to the vanishing point. **No one in it.** The sweep comes down the district and passes over it: on either side,
row after row stirs awake for a frame; in his row, nothing stirs, because there is nothing to wake. [UI: over the
empty row, the long number, `10,000`.]

**8 · FINAL IMAGE.** Adam at the head of the empty row, small, the murmuring rows on either side. Behind him, down the
long aisle, the first fork from the hook loops her frame. [SOUND, lagged, far off: *"Did I pass?"*] Cut to black.

*(Theme in subtext, never stated: the Grid's eternity is storage rot for whatever no one maintains, and Adam is
looking at his own future; every rating is a graveyard, which is the honest price; and the man whose rating stands on
no graveyard at all never paid it. The viewer finishes the sentence: the keys in Adam's squares are a forger's.)*
