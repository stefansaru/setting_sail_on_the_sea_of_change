# Production Breakdown — S4E6: "The Supernova"

> Production/cinematography breakdown for [[setting-sail-the-seed-ledger]] **Season 4, Episode 6** (Act II, Phase B:
> the window opens). The fleet's shared [[the-seed-fleet|sensor array]] catches a burst with the spectral shape of a
> nova, from the one star the catalogue has long expected to die. The catalogue flags it *natural*. The base of the
> pyramid ([[the-stasis-economy]]) is woken off-roster to watch the sky do something once in a lifetime, and **Teo**
> (`CHR-09`, [[teo]]), Kai's bay-mate, is the first to cry. It is not the sky ([[the-lure-beacon]], `MAG-31`), and
> nobody in the reel knows that. **Kai** (`CHR-08`, [[kai]]) watches from inside the crowd. Beam links and lifeboats are
> shown in passing as the hulls share the feed: Act III's plant. **One reveal:** the "supernova" itself, the lure in its
> disguise. The cold viewer gets wonder; the serial viewer gets a tooth in the light curve that no star should have.

## Setup & hook
Black. A pin of white appears in the dark, dead centre of a 9:16 frame. It does not twinkle. It *grows*: from a point
to a coin of light in five seconds, and the black around it goes faintly blue. Pull back: the pin sits on the forward
forward port, and the frame around it is Kai's Lens, every Context circle at the top of the frame turning, one by
one, to look at it.

## Cinematography
| Element | The real forward bay | The bay's render (where the base gathers) |
|---------|----------------------|-------------------------------------------|
| **Lighting** | **Caravaggio** single source (`VIS-15`): the forward port is the only light. Two leased proxies on watch stand in it, machine faces lit white on one side, nothing on the other. | **Rothko "Sublime"** field built in-camera: the render's walls take the feed's colour, deep blue going to bone white as the light grows. Faces lit from one direction, all the same direction. |
| **Camera** | Locked, symmetrical, cold. The proxies never move. | Slow drift through the crowd at shoulder height, calm (`WR-T2`), always finding the light over someone's shoulder. Ends on Teo's face. |
| **Sound** | Bay hum; the proxies' cooling fans; nothing else. | A crowd falling quiet the way a room does for fireworks; one breath caught; then Teo's laugh at himself, wet. |

**Wonder beat (`CINE-05`):** one exterior: the formation seen from far astern, seven hulls strung across the dark at
safe spacing, each forward screen a dim disc. As the array shares the feed, a thin beam link lights between hull and hull,
seconds apart, down the line. Below one hull's belly, the lifeboat cradles: small craft in a row, their tiny ports
catching the new light. Nobody in the reel looks at them.

## UI behavior (the Perspective Lens)
All UI is in **Kai's** Lens (`WR-L2`); the forward port and its catalogue tag are diegetic, on the real bay's port, visible
to anyone.
- **Bottom squares `[▢]`:** `arrive` (from E4), faintly lit, as it has been all season.
- **Disc glyph:** whole, small, lower corner. For one frame the growing light sits inside its dark bore.
- **The catalogue (diegetic, forward port):** a tag beside the light: `NATURAL · EXPECTED`.
- **Tether lines:** in Kai's Lens the few Shadows in the crowd (Kai, Teo, a handful of others) carry a thin tether line
  at the edge of their outline. Most of the crowd carries none: Ghosts on watch (`Q-68`). Unremarked.
- **The turn:** every Context circle fills with the same white point. Many circles, one reality.

## Philosophical climax
The base is the slow, kind, under-informed bottom of a pyramid that was built to keep it safe, and tonight it has been
given something: an unrostered wake, paid in watts, so everyone can see. It is the fleet at its best. A once-in-a-
lifetime light, a crowd that shares one *now*, a young man who cries at a star dying on schedule and laughs at himself
for it. Nothing in the frame is wrong except the thing no one can see: the light is a lie, and the people best placed to
be moved by it are the ones standing here. The reel shows the attack surface as beauty. *Every Context circle fills with
the same light* is a picture of consensus, and of a single point of failure.

## Script (draft v1)
> 9:16 vertical · ~2.5 min · cinematic photoreal · *Black Mirror / Silo* register. **KAI** = the S4 lead (`CHR-08`);
> **TEO** = Kai's bay-mate (`CHR-09`); **A GHOST ON WATCH** = one of the base's many, unnamed; **WATCH ONE** = one of the two
> E1 proxies in the real bay, heard once. Fewer than sixty spoken words. Kai's pronouns stay open: no pronoun is used.

*Castes (`WR-B1`): Kai and Teo are Shadows, 100% organic, bodies in deep stasis in the bays below, minds on the Grid;
the crowd is mostly Ghosts on watch with a few Shadows (`Q-68`); the two crew on watch attend the real bay through leased
proxies (Shadows, `WR-B6`); the High Clock is unseen. The beacon's makers are never shown (`WR-R2`); in this reel they
are not even suspected. Timing (`WR-T4`, `WR-T6`): about **T+4.15**, some three weeks before the strike (E10, about
T+4.2), on the table in [[the-seed-fleet]]. The base is down-clocked to **Tier 0** for the wake so the render keeps the
feed's real *now*: one clock for everyone in frame; the reel covers about **two real hours**, compressed. Off screen, any
Ghost at the Apex would live **60 subjective days in each real minute** of it (1 min × 86,400 = 60 days). Light-time: the
base believes the light is the dying star's, older than cities; bible only, it left a beacon about **0.25 ly** out about
**three months** ago ([[the-lure-beacon]]: within about 0.27 ly). Between hulls the shared feed lags by seconds of light.
Spend (`WR-E1`): the unrostered wake costs watts, about **0.3 MW** for Hull Four's few hundred minds at Tier 0 (drafted at
about 1 kW per mind at 1×; see the invented list); the array and beam links draw watts to share the feed. No chemistry is
spent: the bodies stay in stasis.*

**1 · COLD OPEN — EXTREME CLOSE (9:16) · HOOK (0:00–0:05).** Black. A pin of white, dead centre. It grows to a coin in five
seconds; the black around it goes faintly blue. [SOUND: nothing, then a single held breath from somewhere off frame.]

**2 · THE REAL FORWARD BAY · CONTEXT (0:05–1:15, shots 2–5).** Pull back: the light sits on the forward port. The real
bay is nearly empty: two leased proxies on watch stand before the port, grey jointed frames lit on one side. [Diegetic, on the
port: `NATURAL · EXPECTED`.]

> **WATCH ONE** *(flat, through a proxy's speaker):* Catalogue says natural.

**3 · THE RENDER.** Cut on the word to the same bay as the stacks draw it: the same port, the same light, and **hundreds of
people**, shoulder to shoulder, faces turned one way. In Kai's Lens a frame-edge notice, gone in a second: `UNROSTERED WAKE
· ALL HANDS · +0.3 MW`. KAI stands mid-crowd, counting heads out of habit, lips moving. TEO beside Kai, very still.

**4 · WONDER BEAT — EXTERIOR (one shot, 4 s).** Far astern of the formation: seven hulls across the dark, each forward
screen a dim disc. A beam link lights hull to hull, seconds apart, as the feed is shared down the line. Under one hull,
the lifeboat cradles, small craft in a row, ports catching the light. Back in.

**5 · THE CROWD.** Drift through it at shoulder height. A Ghost on watch lifts a hand to the port,
measuring the coin between finger and thumb. People who are paused between watches most of the crossing, awake together
for once. [UI: Kai's `arrive` square, faintly lit, as always.]

**6 · TEO · FRICTION (1:15–2:15, shots 6–8).** The light reaches its brightest. Teo's face: wet. He laughs at himself, wipes
it, and it comes again.

> **TEO:** Sorry. *(laughs)* It's just — that light's older than all of us.
>
> **A GHOST ON WATCH** *(not unkind):* Older than cities.
>
> **KAI** *(still counting, quiet):* Once in a lifetime.
>
> **TEO:** Once in a thousand.

**7 · KAI'S LENS.** Kai looks from Teo back to the port. The disc glyph sits small in the corner, whole; for one frame the
light is centred in its bore. [SOUND: the crowd breathing in time without meaning to.]

**8 · THE PROXIES.** Cut to the real bay for two seconds: the two machine bodies, motionless, the light on their optics. In the
render, a few hundred people are crying or smiling. In the real bay there is no one to cry. *(Recall cue, ≤ 2 s: E1's
crew counting the lock, the same two proxies, the same port.)*

**9 · EVERY CIRCLE · TURN (2:15–2:30, shots 9–10).** In Kai's Lens the Context circles turn, one after another, to the light,
until **every one shows the same white point.** The frame holds them like a row of identical eyes.

**10 · FINAL IMAGE.** Slow push past the crowd into the port, past the light itself, to the corner where the array draws
its light curve in a thin line: rising, peaking, settling, as a star's should. And on the falling edge, small, a **tooth**:
a notch, then the same notch, then the same notch again, evenly spaced. [SOUND: Teo's laugh, far off, wet.] The tag beside
it still reads `NATURAL`. Cut to black.

*(Theme in subtext, never stated: the fleet's kindness is real (an unrostered wake so the slow can see) and its base is
exactly the audience a lie would be written for. Every circle showing one light is consensus and a single point of
failure at once. The cold viewer gets a star; the viewer who catches the tooth knows stars don't repeat themselves.)*
