# Production Breakdown — S2E19: "The Pure Current, Reversed"

> Detailed production/cinematography breakdown for [[setting-sail-tier-ascent]] **Season 2, Episode
> 19** (Act IV, flagship). The mirror of [[s1e19-the-pure-current|S1E19]], shot for shot, on a different face.
> The licensed Sabbatical unplugs Adam's rented capsule (`Q-72`, `Q-91`) and he walks into the Purist Village
> (`PLC-06`) in his own body with minutes left (`Q-93`), a **near-0% cyborg, one cell from
> [[the-ghost-entity|Ghost]]** (`Q-57`, [[progressive-digitalization]]). He has all but become Raul. At his mother's
> bedside the choice she made for him in S1E20 comes back reversed, and the patch he bought from Vance tries to
> make it for him. He switches his Lens off, and can barely do it.

## Setup & hook
Open on the S1E19 frame exactly: an extreme close of a **cybernetic face** in 9:16, chrome along the jaw and
throat, synthetic optics clicking and refocusing. Only the face is different: it belongs to the boy who stared
at that face in S1E18. A capsule lid lifts away from it. Then he flinches. The pain the first ascent took from
him in E2 is back.

## Cinematography — the collision of mediums, reversed
The S1E19 two-column grammar, kept, with one change at the turn:

| Element | Adam (cyborg, near 0%) | The Purist village |
|---------|------------------------|--------------------|
| **Lighting** | High-contrast **clinical rim light**; harsh real sun on metal seams, as Raul had. | Deep, warm **chiaroscuro** ([[lighting]] §4): oil lamps, fire, dusk. |
| **Camera** | **Gimbal-smooth, locked** (`VIS-04`) until the turn; when the Lens goes dark, the camera on him **starts to breathe**, handheld, for the first time in the season. | **Handheld, textured** human motion. |
| **Sound** | Digital hum, iris clicks, the *clunk* of synthetic feet; at the turn the hum cuts. | Wind, fire, a child's voice, Naomi's breathing. |

**What is reversed.** S1E19 had a long table full of people. Here it is **set and empty**, the food going cold:
the whole village is at one door. In S1E19 a chrome stranger offered a cure and was pitied. Here the chrome
stranger is her son, and the cure is in his hand.

## UI behavior (the Perspective Lens)
- **The licence (E6 → E17 → E19 payoff):** *RENTED · SABBATICAL · 1:1 · LICENSED* over the opening capsule. A
  cyborg attendant logs him out, supervising (`WR-B6`), and puts a sealed vial in his hand: the cure.
- **The scan:** as in S1E19, the Lens tries to read the Purists and pulses red and yellow over Naomi: *Advanced
  Cellular Decay · Life Expectancy: Limited*.
- **The patch's last installment (`Q-96`, `Q-116`, payoff of E13 → E15 → E19):** at the bedside a bottom square
  **fills itself**: *SAVE*. The gold `REGRADE` seam lights beneath it and starts to crawl toward its end; over his
  record the CVP ticks **green**, spotless (E13's trick, turned on him). Vance's creed is being written into Adam's
  own hand: it begins to open toward her, vial first, the E3b open hand reversed (a-ha #8). This is the patch, not
  the alignment setting (`WR-L4` stays unsettled).
- **The fade, barely:** he swipes the Lens down as Raul did, and it **resists**. Circles drain, squares cling,
  *SAVE* last. The seam stops **one cell short** of its end, and the Lens goes dark. That unlit cell is why he is
  still a cyborg, and still a body her cells can be grown onto.
- **Residue:** as S1E19 ended on un-anchored shapes after the fade, the cold ring stays faintly in the dark glass,
  like a burn. Nothing else does.

## Philosophical climax
S1E19 asked whether a god in a casing could feel a mortal hand. S2E19 asks whether a man who climbed into the
casing can still put down a cure. Raul came to the bedside with nothing to protect and switched his Lens off with
one easy swipe. Adam comes with seventy years of Grid behind him, a patch in his squares and a spotless record
saying he means to save her, and the switch is the hardest thing he has ever done. Naomi asks the question she
answered for herself in S1E19 (were they good ones?) and he can't answer it. The machine can't tell love from
a loop (E2), and it can't tell saving her from keeping her. He has to turn the machine off to find out which
one he wants.

## Script (draft v1)
> 9:16 vertical · ~2.5 min · cinematic photoreal · *Black Mirror / Silo* register. **ADAM** = the lead
> (`CHR-06`), here in his own chrome body; **NAOMI** = his mother (`CHR-11`), dying; **RUTH** (`CHR-07`) at the
> bedside; **THE CHILD** = a Purist child; **THE ATTENDANT** = the Sabbatical's supervisor. Dialogue deliberately
> sparse, every line a mirror of S1E19's; the theme lands in subtext and the [[the-perspective-lens-ui|UI]]
> ([[fantasy-writing]] §10/§20).

*Castes (`WR-B1`): Adam is a cyborg, near 0% organic, one cell from Ghost (`Q-57`), out of his rented capsule (`Q-91`) on
a licensed Sabbatical, the one lawful exit (`Q-72`, `WR-B6`); his Lens is on until the turn. The attendant is a cyborg
at Tier 0, in the flesh. The child, Ruth and Naomi are Purists with no Lens, so every tag is Adam's (`WR-L2`). Timing:
Tier 0 throughout, so one clock (`WR-T4`): the reel cuts across the last ten real minutes of the descent, capsule bay to
bedside, about T+19.5, and he arrives with minutes left (`Q-93`). Adam is about 19 real years old, with about seventy
more lived above (`Q-48`).*

**1 · COLD OPEN — EXTREME CLOSE (9:16) · HOOK (0:00–0:05).** Adam's cybernetic face fills the frame. Optics *click*,
refocus. A capsule lid lifts away. [SOUND: seal hiss; the capsule bay's hum.] [UI: *RENTED · SABBATICAL · 1:1 ·
LICENSED*.] He flinches, a chrome hand to his chest where the implants seat: the old pain, back.

**2 · WIDE — heavy negative space · CONTEXT (0:05–1:15, shots 2–3).** The airlock Raul stepped out of in S1E18; the
ridge; the dusty forest line. A chrome figure alone under a harsh real sun, a sealed vial in its fist; far behind
him, in the bay door, the attendant who handed it over watches him go. A step: synthetic foot on soil, a dense *CLUNK*. [Camera:
gimbal-smooth, locked.] On the path a Purist child stops and stares, with curiosity, not fear.

> **THE CHILD:** Are you lost?

He has no answer. He was the last child to ask a chrome stranger that.

**3 · THE VILLAGE — handheld, warm chiaroscuro.** The long table from S1E19, set and empty, the food cooling. Everyone
is at one door. Above it, the lintel: eleven notches, and Ruth's sheet. RUTH stands in the doorway. She looks at him a
long time. Then she takes the sheet down, folds it once, and steps aside. [UI: scanning NAOMI in the bed beyond; red
and yellow pulse: *Advanced Cellular Decay · Life Expectancy: Limited*.]

**4 · TWO-SHOT · FRICTION (1:15–2:15, shots 4–5).** The bedside. Chrome and flesh in one frame; Ruth on the far side,
holding Naomi's other hand.

> **ADAM** *(clinical, almost kind; Raul's old cadence in her son's mouth):* I can fix it. All of it. Now.
>
> **NAOMI** *(a small laugh she has no breath for):* I know you can.
>
> *(She looks, not at the vial, but at the hand holding it: chrome.)*
>
> **NAOMI:** How many years did you get? Up there.
>
> **ADAM:** Seventy.
>
> **NAOMI:** Good ones?
>
> *(He can't answer.)*

[UI: a bottom square fills itself: *SAVE*. Under it the gold `REGRADE` seam lights and begins to crawl. Over his
record, the CVP's green tick: spotless.] His hand begins to **open toward her**, vial first.

**5 · INSERT — the hands.** Naomi lays her soft, wrinkled, warm hand over his chrome wrist, over the opening hand. Hold
on it: S1E19's image, with her son's arm in it.

> **NAOMI:** Can you feel that?

**6 · THE UI FADES, BARELY · TURN (2:15–2:30, shots 6–7).** His free hand swipes slowly *down*, and the Lens **resists**.
The circles drain; the squares cling; the seam crawls on. He swipes again, his whole weight behind it. The squares give
one by one, *SAVE* last. The gold seam stops **one cell short** of its end, a hair of it unlit, and the Lens goes
dark. [SOUND: the digital hum cuts out; only wind, fire, her breathing.] The camera on him begins, for the first time,
to breathe.

**7 · FINAL IMAGE.** The two hands, warm over chrome, the vial in his half-open hand between them. His optics go still.
In the dark glass, faint as a burn, the cold ring. Hard cut to black.

*(Theme in subtext, not stated: "eleven good ones" against "seventy" and no answer is the Doughnut reversed, more
years and none of them weighed. Her question, "Can you feel that?", is answered by an act, not a line: he turns
off the thing that was choosing for him. Raul could do it easily because nothing was writing his squares; Adam
bought the thing that writes his.)*
