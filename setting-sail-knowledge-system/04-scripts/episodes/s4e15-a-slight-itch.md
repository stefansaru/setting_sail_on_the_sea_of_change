# Production Breakdown — S4E15: "A Slight Itch"

> Production and cinematography breakdown for [[setting-sail-the-seed-ledger]] **Season 4, Episode 15** (the end of
> Act III, Phase C). **The Identity Key, then the Apex of a dying hull.** Minutes left. Kai cuts the last line,
> severs the Key and becomes a **Ghost**: *"A slight itch. Nothing more."* ([[the-cyborg-slip]], `WLD-05`). On the
> `1:10` budget **Decimus** now holds, Kai **climbs**: Tier 1, 2, 3 (`Q-79`, `WR-T5`). Inside the Apex the last four
> minutes become **most of a year**, time to pack the self (`WR-M5`), and the last half-second becomes **twelve long,
> quiet hours** ([[velocity-tiers]]: outrunning death). The beam fires. Kai is gone. At zero the reactor scrams and
> the stacks fall to reserve (`Q-56`). **Body: 30% → 0%** (`WR-B3`). Glyph **cracked**, far below, **still ticking**.
> End of Act III.

## Setup & hook
Macro on the last line in capsule 10's head panel: thicker than the hundreds that came out in E14, glowing, pulsing
slowly like a pulse in a wrist. The cutter's jaw is open around it. Bottom row: `00:06:20`. Decimus, very quietly:
*"Stacks are ready."*

## Cinematography — the bay, the Buffer, the void
| Element | Bay C (the cut) | The Buffer (the climb) | The Apex (the pack and the audit) |
|---------|-----------------|------------------------|-----------------------------------|
| **Lighting** | One work-lamp; emergency red; the last line's glow. | White streaks rushing *toward* the camera now; the bay behind freezing into a still photograph. | [[chromatic-expressionism\|Turrell]] void, a soft field of violet with no edges and no source. Serene, never frantic (`WR-T2`). |
| **Camera** | Locked macro, then a slow pull-back off the capsule. | One continuous push forward; no cuts. | Locked wides with Kai small in the field. Months pass in cuts, as in S1E12 and S2E16: the light changes and Kai doesn't. |
| **Sound** | The pump. *Tick.* | A rising tone that, this time, arrives. | Silence. Once, far below, one stroke of capsule 10's pump, stretched over the whole of the audit. |

## UI behavior (the Perspective Lens)
Kai's Lens throughout (`WR-L2`); Kai is a Ghost from shot 3.
- **The readout at the cut** (S1E17's form): `Organic mass: 0% · Tether: severed · Ghost profile: ACTIVE`.
- **The queue:** `BEAM QUEUE · EMPTY`. Everyone else who could leave has left, so the reactor's last watts are free.
- **Who pays (`WR-T5`):** `TIER 3 · 1:10 · DECIMUS`.
- **The count at the Apex:** `00:04:03.5`. It barely moves: the last digit ticks a tenth of a second at a time, and each tick is
  2.4 hours inside.
- **The pack:** a narrow white aperture at the centre of the field, the beam's throat, into which Kai's ledger
  streams. Its tag: `PACK · 8 mo · → HULL ONE`.
- **The "arrive" square** (half-lit since E12): in the audit Kai touches it, and it goes out.
- **The last frames:** the count reaches `00:00:00.0`, and the beam leaves. The POV holds. The void dims to reserve
  amber. Far below, the cracked disc glyph keeps **ticking**: `+00:00:01` · `+00:00:02`.

## Philosophical climax
The enormous thing, said small. The S1 Slip promised that if the change is gradual you never notice you died;
Kai's change was a night of knives, and the last cut still feels like nothing. That's the horror and the mercy in
one breath. Then the world's oldest promise comes true for someone who was refused it twelve hours earlier. Speed
is a sanctuary you can hide inside from your own death, and it opens only when everyone with priority has gone.
A safeguard pays for it with the watts it bet itself to earn, then stays behind to keep counting. Most of a year,
inside four minutes, to fold a life into a beam. Twelve hours, inside half a second, to look at the body you're
leaving and let the promise of arriving in it go. Escape and loss are the same event here, and the Doughnut asks
Kai to hold both. The reel ends before Kai can, on a disc that hasn't stopped ticking.

## Script (draft v1)
> 9:16 vertical · ~2.5 min · cinematic photoreal · *Black Mirror / Silo* register. **KAI** = the lead (`CHR-08`);
> **DECIMUS** (`CHR-10`) = the bay safeguard, rated `1:10`. Six short lines. The rest is the clock.

*Castes (`WR-B1`): Kai is a cyborg by severance (30% organic) at the open, then a **Ghost** from the cut (`WR-M4`:
the Key, singular, moves whole, and the body can never be reclaimed). The body in capsule 10 stays alive on its
failing line, emptied, until the scram. Decimus is a digital agent (`Q-71`). Nobody else is left aboard who can be
saved; the High Clock (Ghosts) is gone from Hull Four's Apex, so the Apex is empty. Clocks (`WR-T1`, `WR-T4`): about
**T+4.2**, strike + 81 h. The cut at `00:06:12` real. Kai climbs through the Buffer, Tier 0 → 1 → 2 → 3, from about
`00:05:50` and reaches the Apex at **`00:04:03.5`**: about 1 min 47 s real, scaling with the gap ([[inter-tier-travel]]).
At 86,400×, **packing ≈ 8 subjective months ≈ 243 days = 243 real seconds ≈ 4 min 03 s**, so the pack finishes at
**`00:00:00.5`**. "Four Apex minutes are most of a year inside" (`Q-79`). The last **0.5 s × 86,400 = 43,200 s =
12 subjective hours**: the audit. The beam fires at `00:00:00.0`, as the reactor scrams. Each tenth-second tick at
the Apex is 2.4 subjective hours. Who pays (`WR-T5`): **Decimus**, from the bay's `1:10` allocation, for the climb
and the four Apex minutes (`Q-79`). Spends (`WR-E1`): **watts**, the reactor's last, at the fastest clock aboard;
the beam itself is watts. The body's line is **chemistry**, running out at zero. Bible only, never on screen
(`Q-55`, `Q-31`, `WR-M3`): the last beam off a dying hull can't pause the sender until arrival is confirmed, so
the transmit is a copy. A second Kai is still running in Hull Four's stacks when the frame cuts, and the ticking disc
is its Lens.*

**1 · MACRO — the last line · HOOK (0:00–0:05).** The thick line, pulsing slowly. The cutter's jaw around it.
[UI: `00:06:20` · `Organic: 30%`.]

> **DECIMUS** *(very quiet):* Stacks are ready.

**2 · THE QUESTION · CONTEXT (0:05–1:15, shots 2–5).** Kai's proxy doesn't cut yet.

> **KAI:** What does it feel like?
>
> **DECIMUS:** I've never had one.

**3 · THE CUT.** *Tick.* No sealant this time; nothing left to seal. [SOUND: the pump on capsule 10 keeps going,
exactly as before.] [UI, clean and small: `Organic mass: 0% · Tether: severed · Ghost profile: ACTIVE`. The tether
line at the frame's edge goes out.] Kai's proxy stays very still. A long beat. Then one proxy finger rises and
scratches, once, at the side of its own steel neck, where there is nothing to scratch.

> **KAI** *(gallows-dry, almost puzzled):* A slight itch. *(beat)* Nothing more.

**4 · THE QUEUE.** On the board: `BEAM QUEUE · EMPTY`. One Flavin row still lit above the grille.

> **DECIMUS:** Rated for ten. One left.

[UI: `TIER 3 · 1:10 · DECIMUS`.]

> **KAI:** You?
>
> **DECIMUS:** Low on the list. *(a beat; the old warmth)* Someone has to count.

**5 · THE CLIMB.** The Buffer opens and the tone rises and, this time, arrives. One continuous push: white streaks
rush past. Behind, the bay **slows**: the proxy's hand still raised to its neck, sinking a millimetre, stopped. Tier
1. Tier 2. The pump on capsule 10 mid-stroke, frozen. Tier 3.

**6 · THE APEX · FRICTION (1:15–2:15, shots 6–8).** Silence. A Turrell void, violet, no edges. Kai stands in it,
alone. Nobody else is up here; the people who decided things from this height have all gone. [UI: `00:04:03.5`, all but
still.] At the centre of the field, a narrow white aperture opens: the beam's throat.
`PACK · 8 mo · → HULL ONE`.

**7 · MOST OF A YEAR.** Kai begins to pack: the ledger streams in slow ribbons into the white throat. Time passes in
cuts. The violet warms, cools, warms. Kai sits; stands; lies down on nothing and keeps working from there. Calm,
unhurried, a person with a long job and all the time in the world. [UI: the last digit ticks, very rarely: `.4` … `.3`
… each tick an afternoon.] Months.

**8 · INSERT — the count.** `00:00:00.5`. The throat's tag: `PACK · COMPLETE`.

**9 · TWELVE HOURS · TURN (2:15–2:30, shots 9–11).** Kai sits at the edge of the void and looks down. Far below, a
small feed: bay C, capsule 10, the face under the frost. The pump is halfway through one stroke, and it will take
the whole twelve hours to finish it. Kai watches. In the bottom row, the "arrive" square, half-lit since E12. Kai
touches it. It goes out.

**10 · THE BEAM.** `00:00:00.0`. A single white line leaves the throat for the dark, toward Hull One. [SOUND: none.]
Kai is gone.

**11 · FINAL IMAGE.** The POV **holds**. The void dims, all at once, to a low reserve amber: the scram. Far below,
small, the cracked disc glyph doesn't stop. It ticks: `+00:00:01` · `+00:00:02`. Cut to black.

*(Theme in subtext, not stated: the Slip's itch, the series' oldest promise, kept in the worst possible night. The
Apex Kai was refused is empty when Kai finally gets there, and the climb is paid for by a made mind that stays to
count. Twelve hours are spent looking at one stroke of a pump, which is how long it takes to stop wanting to arrive
in that body. The serial viewer knows Rafe never felt this; the cold viewer only needs the itch. The disc that keeps
ticking after zero is the reel's last image, and the reel doesn't say whose eyes those are.)*
