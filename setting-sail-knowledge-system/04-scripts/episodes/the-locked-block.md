# Script — "The Locked Block"

> A **standalone correctional reel** (slotted at S2E15, `Q-77`) dramatizing the
> [[the-encrypted-block-paradox|Encrypted Block Paradox]] — the one cell the all-seeing AI can neither
> read nor open. POV: **The Intercessor** (an Intercessor of [[the-ministry-of-hope|Hope]]) facing **The Smuggler**
> (a gaunt [[the-clock-brokers|Clock Broker]], unnamed) · register: **[[the-ministry-of-hope|
> Sanctuary Void]]** chiaroscuro (`VIS-14`) → teal **Silence Cloister** → **Caravaggio** on dying flesh.
> 9:16 · ~2.5 min.
>
> *Castes (`Q-87`): the Intercessor is a Ghost in a shell; the Smuggler is a Shadow, which is why his lock sits in
> tissue (`Q-67`). Who paid his Apex: nobody; he stole the velocity credits (`WR-T5`).*

## Setup & hook (0:00–0:05)
Aftermath of a [[velocity-braking|Velocity-Brake]]: a frozen capsule, antenna arrays dark, an Intercessor's
electromagnetic staff still humming against the casing. They cut the Smuggler from **Tier 3 to 1:1** in a
microsecond — under a tenth of a subjective second, no buffer, no warning. Now he sits in the towering white **Sanctuary
Void**, tiny under the light: emaciated, tremoring, [[tether-decay|TTD]] timer bleeding out. A
[[the-clock-brokers|Clock Broker]] who stole velocity credits to breathe and minted
[[intent-patch|Intent-Patches]] to hide it.

## Friction (0:05–1:15)
The **Intercessor** lays it out, gentle and absolute. The Smuggler's last exploit was *brilliant* — he
tore a [[chromatic-tears|Chromatic Tear]] through a vault wall, a bypass the [[the-distributed-president|Distributed
President]] never saw coming and still can't close. Open the ledger, prove it was necessity, and it's
**[[compliance-validation-protocol|Proven-in-Use]]**: the
[[the-absolution-protocol|Absolution]] gate swings, a global **Policy Update** ships, and he walks free
with his merit restored. The system doesn't want to punish him — it wants the **lesson.**
> **THE INTERCESSOR (soft):** Open that one block and you walk out today. We only want to know how you
> did it.

## Climax (1:15–2:15)
The Smuggler — who has had everything taken — exercises the **one sovereign right left to a living mind**:
he **permanently encrypts the block.** ([[the-personal-blockchain|His keys, his alone.]]) The Intercessor
goes still, because they both know the trap now: the AI **cannot pull a lesson from disconnected human
tissue** without destroying it — which its own safety core forbids. A [[the-ghost-entity|Ghost]] would be
force-unencrypted and wiped; *flesh cannot.* So the blindspot stays open, the exit can never compute —
and the door **welds shut.**
> **THE SMUGGLER (cracked, almost smiling):** You've read everything else. This one's mine.
>
> **THE INTERCESSOR (very quiet):** Then this door stays shut.
>
> **THE SMUGGLER:** I know.

## Ending cut (2:15–2:30)
A deep **teal Silence Cloister.** The Smuggler folded small in the dark, sovereign and unbreakable and
**never leaving** — a black box the machine can neither understand nor free. The Intercessor's hand rests
useless on the door; outside, the [[tether-decay|TTD]] timer ticks toward a death that will seal the
lesson forever. Fade on omniscience meeting the one wall it cannot pass: **a man who said no.**
