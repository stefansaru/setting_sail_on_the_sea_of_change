# Script — S1E6: "Shadows in the Neon"

> [[setting-sail-tier-descent]] S1E6 · Act II · **Tier 1** · Raul **100% organic** (adult Shadow) ·
> POV: Raul · glyph: **hollow Camel's Doughnut** (post-[[parallel-pedagogy|Merging]]) · register: Flavin
> neon → tether-line motif. 9:16 · ~2.5 min. Raul learns what he is.

**HOOK (0:00–0:05).** Neon-wet adult workplace; Raul, now grown, freezes as a thin **glowing geometric
line** snaps taut at the very edge of his vision — pulsing, alive, tracking *something far away.* [UI:
hollow outline Macro-Doughnut, serene; a new readout: *Host vitals — nominal.*]

**ACT I — CONTEXT (0:05–1:15).** Nobody else reacts. Raul follows the line through his Lens, menu by menu, to a
setting he has never opened: he is a **[[the-shadow-entity|Shadow]]**. His mind runs the Grid while his **living
body sleeps in a capsule on Earth**, the tether-line its lifeline. He has *always* had a body out there. He just
never looked.

**ACT II — FRICTION (1:15–2:15).** Raul pulls up the feed: a stranger's face in a stasis pod — slack,
breathing, *his.* Vertigo. He reaches toward the screen; the tether-line tightens warningly.
> **RAUL:** That's… me? Thirty-four years, I'm asleep down there?
> **COLLEAGUE (shrug):** Nobody opens that. You're up *here*. The meat's just an antenna. Don't overthink it.
But he can't stop thinking it — the [[the-ghost-entity|Ghosts]] passing by have no such line, and they
look at him with **pity.**

**RESOLUTION / CLIFFHANGER (2:15–2:30).** Raul lays a hand over his own chest — feeling for a heartbeat
that isn't here, that's a world away. On the feed, under the grown stranger's face, one small line he nearly
misses. [UI: *Host age: 14 weeks.*] He has lived thirty-four years. Hold on the number. Cut to black.

*Canon: Shadow reveal ([[the-shadow-entity]]); [[the-quickening]], unexplained until the world earns it; brain-as-antenna ([[the-memory-substrate]]); hollow glyph
established for the adult. 100% organic. Subtext: the question, not a lecture.*
