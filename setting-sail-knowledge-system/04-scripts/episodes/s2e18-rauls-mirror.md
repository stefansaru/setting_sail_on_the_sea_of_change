# Production Breakdown — S2E18: "Raul's Mirror"

> Detailed production/cinematography breakdown for [[setting-sail-tier-ascent]] **Season 2, Episode
> 18** (Act IV, Phase D′). **The descent corridor**, at its Tier-1 landing. **Raul** (`CHR-02`), a
> [[the-ghost-entity|Ghost]] whose [[shells-with-ghosts-within|shell]] waits in the village, climbs one rung to meet
> **Adam** coming down: the two leads of the series meet as mirror images. The cure's one hard limit reaches the
> screen as a single line, *NO DONOR → CHROME* (`Q-95`), and nothing more. Body **~10% → near-0% organic**
> ([[progressive-digitalization]]); glyph **cold-complete** beside Raul's solid one ([[the-macro-doughnut]]). World:
> [[velocity-tiers]] · [[inter-tier-travel]] · [[the-perspective-lens-ui]].

## Setup & hook
A tall shaft of light, 9:16 made architecture: the descent corridor. Everything in it drifts **down**: motes of
data, the light itself, Adam on a slow stair. One figure is coming **up** against it. Raul.

## Cinematography — mirror composition
| Element | Adam (coming down) | Raul (coming up) |
|---------|--------------------|------------------|
| **Lighting** | Tier-1 gold (the hospitality warmth of S1E5), the Apex violet draining out of him from above. | The same gold, but lit from below: the warm Caravaggio of the village he climbed from. |
| **Camera** | Locked, centred, symmetrical: the two men framed as reflections across the landing (`CINE-11`, [[scifi-cinematography]] §8). | The same frame, flipped. When the POV crosses into Raul's Lens at the end, the symmetry breaks. |
| **Sound** | The corridor's low downward hum. | His steps, upward, slightly out of time with it. |

**The render and the truth.** In-grid Adam still wears the young Purist face of his avatar. Once, under stress, it
fails: his hand glitches to **metallic wireframe** for a frame (S1E14's tell, `MAG-15`), and the viewer sees what
the capsule actually holds.

## UI behavior (the Perspective Lens)
- **The patch, third installment (`Q-96`):** in the hook, the gold `REGRADE` seam ticks once more, without a
  readout. He is near 0% now.
- **The spec (the major):** the cure, opened between them, turning and intricate. At its foot one line, **held a
  beat**: *NO DONOR → CHROME*. Nothing on screen says what a donor is, or who could be one (`Q-95`; the a-ha #11
  reminder stays a gap).
- **The tag (Validation mirror):** when Adam asks Raul for guidance, his Lens answers first, small, over Raul:
  *UNRATED*. Who gave this man the right to guide? (`WR-S2`.)
- **Two glyphs:** Adam's cold-complete ring turning one way, Raul's solid Doughnut (S1E20) the other. For one beat
  they align and read as a single shape; then they turn apart.
- **Raul's Lens (the last shot, a-ha #14):** on since S1E20, alignment off, so his bottom squares are **empty**
  (`Q-119`). At the very edge, far off, a **third glyph**: dim, a different shape (S1E15–16's), neither drifting nor
  pulsing. A still frame of a glyph: a paused copy, not a live link (`Q-54`, `WR-T3`). He doesn't look at it.

## Philosophical climax
Raul descended to feel one mortal thing and got it: a warm hand on a wrist he couldn't feel. Adam ascended to
feel everything and has it, and can feel nothing that matters. Each is what the other was afraid of becoming.
The spec puts the season's question in four characters: the cure would keep her alive by making her what her
son became. Adam wants to be told what to do, and Raul, whom Adam's mother begged for the same answer over
Adam's own sickbed (S1E20), refuses again. Or seems to: his one long sentence is a verdict dressed as a mirror, and
the Lens has already told Adam this man was never rated to give one. Whether Raul's counsel can be trusted
stays open (the `CHR-03` echo).

## Script (draft v1)
> 9:16 vertical · ~2.5 min · cinematic photoreal · *Black Mirror / Silo* register. **ADAM** = the lead (`CHR-06`);
> **RAUL** = the S1 lead and S2 guide (`CHR-02`). Dialogue sparse; one line is a **signed exception to `WR-F4`**
> (`Q-94`) and is kept verbatim. Everything else is the spec, a tag, and two rings.

*Castes (`WR-B1`): Adam is a cyborg, ~10% organic entering and near 0% after the third installment, his body in his
rented city capsule (`Q-91`), his mind braking down the corridor. Raul is a Ghost in a shell: the shell stands idle in
the village at Tier 0 while his mind meets Adam on the landing as an avatar. The third glyph is Rafe, a Ghost: the paused
reply copy on Earth (`Q-54`, bible only, never named here); no message crosses any distance, so `WR-T3` holds. Naomi
(Purist) appears only on the feed. Clocks (`WR-T1`, `WR-T4`): Tier 1, 60×. The reel's 2.5 subjective minutes are about
2.5 real seconds; Adam is about 20 real minutes from her door. Raul climbed from Tier 0 to meet him, where words are
cheap: at 1:1 this conversation would have cost two and a half of Naomi's last minutes.*

**1 · THE SHAFT — vertical WIDE · HOOK (0:00–0:05).** Everything falls: light, motes, a slow stair. ADAM descends it.
[UI: the gold `REGRADE` seam ticks.] Far below, one figure climbs **up**.

**2 · THE LANDING — symmetrical two-shot · CONTEXT (0:05–1:15, shots 2–4).** RAUL arrives, the climb in his shoulders.
They face each other across the landing, framed as reflections. Neither speaks first. Then:

> **RAUL:** Show me.

**3 · THE SPEC.** Adam opens the cure between them: a turning lattice, beautiful, twenty-four years deep. Raul's eyes
go down it to the foot, where one line waits. [UI, held a beat: *NO DONOR → CHROME*.]

**4 · THE GLITCH.** Raul's eyes leave the spec and go to Adam's hand. For one frame the hand **glitches to metallic
wireframe**: chrome under the render. Adam looks at it the way Kavana looked at hers (S1E13).

> **ADAM:** She'd live.
>
> *(Raul says nothing.)*

**5 · THE ASK · FRICTION (1:15–2:15, shots 5–7).** Close on Adam.

> **ADAM:** Tell me what to do.

[UI, in Adam's Lens, before Raul can answer, small over him: *UNRATED*.]

**6 · THE LINE.** Close on Raul. He takes his time.

> **RAUL:** I gave up everything to feel one mortal thing. You gave up the one mortal thing to feel everything.
>
> **ADAM:** That's not an answer.
>
> **RAUL:** No. *(beat)* It's yours.

**7 · RECALL CUE (≈1:45–2:00).** The village feed in the corner of Adam's Lens. At this clock it **moves**, at a
sixtieth of its speed: Naomi's chest rising, slow as a tide. The world has started to catch up.

**8 · THE RINGS · TURN (2:15–2:30, shots 8–9).** Between them, the two glyphs turn in opposite directions: the cold
ring, the solid Doughnut. For one beat they **align**, one shape. Then they turn apart. Adam steps off the landing and
goes on down.

**9 · RAUL'S LENS — POV.** The symmetry breaks: we are in Raul's Lens, empty squares along the bottom, watching Adam
shrink down the shaft. At the very edge of the frame, far off, a third glyph: dim, a different shape, perfectly
still. Raul doesn't look at it. Hard cut to black.

*(Theme in subtext, not stated: the mirror is exact and useless. Each man holds the half the other threw away,
and neither can hand it over. The spec's one line asks the season's question; Raul's one line answers a different
one. The paused glyph at the edge is a third Raul who never had to choose a body at all.)*
