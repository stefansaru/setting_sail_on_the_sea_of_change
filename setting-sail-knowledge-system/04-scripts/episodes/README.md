### Conventions
- **Naming:** `s1e##-slug.md` (sorts in episode order; `s2e##` for future seasons). Unslotted
  **standalone** reels keep a descriptive wiki-linkable slug.
- **Status:** ✅ full script · ✍️ partial · 📋 blueprint only.
- **Pre-render gate:** every script audited via [`../../03-engine/prompts/audit-script.md`](../../03-engine/prompts/audit-script.md)
  ([[risk-analysis]] Canon Reconciliation). Last audit: the 2026-11-03 S1 fix-and-audit round (no report file; results
  went to the orchestrator). Before that, the 2026-11-02 re-audit, `audit-reports/AuditReport_reaudit-all-seasons_d1_20261102` (all 22 scripts and the reels, every `WR` gate, P1–P9 at season level, line-by-line DIAL-08). Pacing: the 4-beat template, [[distribution-strategy]] §3.
- **Revelation cadence:** the season's information-rationing (one major reveal per reel, Reserve Vault,
  no spoilers) is governed by [[revelation-cadence]] and audited as the REVEAL layer / perspective P9.
  Latest pass: `audit-reports/AuditReport_s1-revelation-cadence_20260605` — **PASS** (one OWNER item: E12).
