# Production Breakdown — S4E7: "Help"

> Production/cinematography breakdown for [[setting-sail-the-seed-ledger]] **Season 4, Episode 7** (Act II, Phase B).
> On the **analysis deck**, closer reading of the "supernova" finds structure under the noise: the tooth the light curve
> carried in E6 repeats, and it reads the same on all seven hulls from every bearing. Not a star: **an artificial
> satellite beaming in all directions** ([[the-lure-beacon]], `MAG-31`). Layer one decodes: **"help."** Layer two:
> **"we're just like you, please help us,"** in every Earth language at once. The base is undone; **Teo** (`CHR-09`,
> [[teo]]) hears it in his own language and translates it for the person beside him. **Kai** (`CHR-08`, [[kai]]) counts.
> The High Clock, at Apex speed, is not undone. **One reveal:** it's a beacon. Minor: a-ha #4's reminder, the red
> interference band Rafe's Lens showed in S1E15, now crossing every mouth.

## Setup & hook
Extreme close on a waveform drawn in light: a jagged ridge, noise. A hand of light pulls it apart like a curtain, and
under the noise there is a shape: the same notch, the same notch, the same notch. The notches settle into four letters.
A voice with no breath in it, close, says one word: *"help."*

## Cinematography
| Element | The analysis deck (render, Tier 1) | The Apex (one insert) |
|---------|------------------------------------|-----------------------|
| **Lighting** | **Flavin** (`VIS-15`): server-deck neon, cold blue tubes along the deck's ribs, the decoded signal hanging in the air as lines of white. As the languages arrive, each mouth throws its own small warm light, and the deck warms by degrees. | **Turrell** void: seamless white, no edges, no shadow. |
| **Camera** | Locked-off wides of the deck, the crowd filling in from the edges; then handheld, close, on faces as the chorus starts. | One static frame. Nothing moves except a hand. |
| **Sound** | The deck's hum; the voice, dry and close; then the chorus: thousands of languages speaking the same sentence, not quite in sync, a wave breaking along a beach. Under all of it, a faint crackle each time the red band crosses a mouth. | Silence, entire. |

## UI behavior (the Perspective Lens)
All UI is in **Kai's** Lens (`WR-L2`). The waveform, the hull comparison and the decoded text are diegetic, on the deck.
- **Top circles `[◯]` (Context):** as layer two arrives, the circles **become mouths**, each speaking one language, more
  circles crowding in than the row can hold. Each is crossed by the same **red interference band**, stuttering left to
  right: the corruption warning from Rafe's Lens in S1E15 (a-ha #4 reminder; unremarked).
- **Bottom squares `[▢]`:** `arrive`, faint, unchanged.
- **A counter (frame edge):** `LANGUAGES · ALL`.
- **Disc glyph:** whole, small, lower corner.

## Philosophical climax
The fleet was raised Virtuous ([[the-virtuous-thread]] S4 E7): break negative loops, help where you can, lead only with
proof. *"We're just like you, please help us"* is built for exactly that mind, which is why it works. It is the dumbest
possible sentence, and it lands on hundreds of people as the most important thing they have ever heard. Teo doesn't
weep at the words; he weeps because they came to him in his own language, the one nobody aboard speaks to him in. The base is moved because it is
good. The council at the top is not moved, and the reel doesn't say whether that's wisdom or the lack of something.

## Script (draft v1)
> 9:16 vertical · ~2.5 min · cinematic photoreal · *Black Mirror / Silo* register. **KAI** = the S4 lead (`CHR-08`);
> **TEO** = Kai's bay-mate (`CHR-09`); **THE ANALYST** and **THE SECOND ANALYST** = Ghosts on watch at the analysis
> deck, unnamed; **THE BEACON** = a voice only, never a body (`WR-R2`). About seventy spoken words, most of them the
> beacon's.

*Castes (`WR-B1`): Kai and Teo are Shadows, 100% organic, bodies in deep stasis, minds on the deck's render at **Tier 1**
(fleet stasis holds Tier 0–1, `WR-B5`); the two analysts are Ghosts on watch; the crowd is mostly Ghosts with a few Shadows
(`Q-68`); the one council figure in the insert is an Apex Ghost of the High Clock, collar `1:2000` (E5's tags). The
beacon's makers are heard, never shown (`WR-R2`, `Q-30`). Timing (`WR-T4`): about **T+4.16**. Day 3 after first light
(E6), the analysts flag the repeating tooth; on **day 4** layer one decodes after about **nine subjective days** of Tier-1
watches (216 h ÷ 60 = **3.6 real hours**); on **day 6** layer two. The reel cuts between the two days. At Tier 1 a real
minute is a subjective hour. The Apex insert takes **under a real second**: one real second there is a subjective day,
so the base's whole hour on the deck would be about **ten Apex years** (3,600 days), more than any council watch runs;
the council stands short watches on the reactor's ration (`WR-T5`). Light-time: bible only, the signal left a beacon about
0.25 ly out about three months earlier; every hull's copy matches within seconds. Spend (`WR-E1`): the deck runs four
analyst minds at Tier 1, about **240 kW** (4 × 60 kW, the drafted 1 kW-per-mind-at-1× scale); the array's watts for the
hull-by-hull comparison. No chemistry.*

**1 · COLD OPEN — EXTREME CLOSE (9:16) · HOOK (0:00–0:05).** A waveform of light, all noise. A hand pulls it apart; under it,
the same notch repeating. The notches settle into four letters.

> **THE BEACON** *(close, even, no breath):* help.

**2 · THE ANALYSIS DECK · CONTEXT (0:05–1:15, shots 2–5).** Wide: the deck's render, neon ribs, a few figures at work. Day 3,
stamped small on the deck wall. THE ANALYST rotates the E6 light curve; the tooth repeats down its falling edge.

> **THE ANALYST:** Stars don't repeat themselves.

**3 · SEVEN HULLS.** THE SECOND ANALYST lays seven copies of the signal side by side, one per hull, each taken from a different
bearing. They are identical, notch for notch. She stacks them; they become one line.

> **THE SECOND ANALYST:** Same on every hull. Every bearing. *(beat)* It isn't pointed at us. It's pointed at everyone.
>
> **THE ANALYST:** It's a beacon.

**4 · DAY 4.** The deck has filled from the edges: base crew, shoulder to shoulder, as at E6. KAI and TEO among them. The four
letters hang in the air. *help.* Nobody speaks. Teo's lips move with it, silently. [UI: Kai's circles, plain; `arrive`,
faint.]

**5 · DAY 6.** Hard cut. The deck again, fuller. A second structure unfolds under the first. [SOUND: one voice, then two,
then a sound like a stadium inhaling.]

**6 · THE CHORUS · FRICTION (1:15–2:15, shots 6–9).** Layer two arrives in **every Earth language at once**. In Kai's Lens the
Context circles become **mouths**, each speaking, more crowding in than the row can hold, each crossed left to right by the
same stuttering **red band**. [UI: `LANGUAGES · ALL`.]

> **THE BEACON** *(thousands of voices, nearly in step):* We're just like you. Please help us.

**7 · TEO.** Handheld, close. One mouth in the chorus is his. He hears it before he finds it. His face goes. He turns to the
person beside him, a Ghost on watch he doesn't know, and translates, because that's what he does.

> **TEO** *(in Greek, then English, crying):* Είμαστε σαν εσάς. *(beat)* They're like us. They're just like us.

**8 · THE BASE.** Faces through the crowd, one after another, each hearing their own: undone, every one. A man holds his own
mouth. Two strangers hold hands. *(Recall cue, ≤ 2 s: S1E15, the red corruption warning stuttering across Rafe's waking Lens.
Unlabelled.)*

**9 · KAI.** Not crying. Counting. Lips moving, eyes moving from mouth to mouth in the top row, losing the count, starting
again. The red band crosses every one.

**10 · THE APEX · TURN (2:15–2:30, shots 10–11).** Hard cut to white. The Turrell void, one council Ghost, collar `1:2000`,
standing. The same sentence hangs before them in a single column, all the languages stacked as one block of text. They read
it from top to bottom in the time it takes us to see them. A hand closes it. [SOUND: nothing at all.]

**11 · FINAL IMAGE.** Back to the deck, mid-chorus, as if no time had passed, because none had: Teo still turned to the stranger,
still saying *just like us*. In Kai's Lens the mouths go on speaking, every one cut by the same red line. Cut to black.

*(Theme in subtext, never stated: the sentence works because the fleet is good; the people it moves most are the people it was
built for. The red band is radiation to a cold viewer and a fingerprint to a serial one. At the top, the same words take no
time at all, and the reel lets that stillness be either wisdom or a missing organ.)*
