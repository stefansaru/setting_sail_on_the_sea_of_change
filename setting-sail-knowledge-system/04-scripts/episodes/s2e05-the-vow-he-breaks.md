# Production Breakdown — S2E05: "The Vow He Breaks"

> Detailed production/cinematography breakdown for [[setting-sail-tier-ascent]] **Season 2, Episode 5**
> (**End of Act I**, Phase A′). **The [[inter-tier-travel|Data Buffer]], Tier 1 → toward Tier 0 → Tier 1.** **Adam**
> (`CHR-06`), a **90%-organic cyborg**, body in his rented capsule (`Q-91`), is being braked home at the end of his
> temporary residency; his implants fail as his clock nears real time, and he accepts a **permanent up-clock**,
> breaking the Purist vow **"One life, kept whole"** (`Q-74`), as Naomi once broke it for him. Naomi, at her own
> threshold: *"The Hurried don't have children. We do."* (plants S3E7, `WR-B2`; [[the-quickening]] stays
> unexplained). Mirrors [[setting-sail-tier-descent|S1E5]]'s forced down-clock and inverts its
> [[chromatic-expressionism|Despair field]]. World: [[velocity-tiers]] · [[progressive-digitalization]].

## Setup & hook
A clean chime and the world shears into streaks: S1E5's hook, played backwards. Adam's temporary residency is over
and the buffer is braking him down to 1:1, toward his own body, the walk home, the lintel. For six real weeks the
village has been a frieze. Now, as his clock falls, it begins to *move*: smoke rises at its own speed, his mother's
voice climbs out of the drone into words. And with the real world, the real body comes back, and the pain, and the
cold blue light. The Grid offers to make it stop, for good.

## UI behavior (the Perspective Lens)
Adam's Lens. **No glyph** (absent until E9).
- **The clock (shots 2–5):** a small numeral at the top edge counting his rate down: **60×**, **30×**, **8×**,
  **2×**, approaching **1:1**. *(Both clocks on screen.)*
- **The care chart:** *Organic: 90%* holds; the compound line under it flickers amber as the rate falls.
- **The offer (shot 6):** a Context circle, amber, at the top of the frame: **UP-CLOCK · PERMANENT**. Below, a
  matching Intention square, **empty**.
- **The turn:** the square **fills** as Adam nods. *(Deliberately undecided whether his nod filled it or the Lens read
  his nod and filled it: a-ha #8's question, planted again, never answered here; `WR-L4` stays open.)* Then the clock
  numeral climbs back to **60×** and locks, and the inverted Despair field blooms gold behind him.

## Philosophical climax
In S1E5 the system took Raul's work and the Despair field bloomed black-and-crimson behind his trained smile. Here
nothing is taken. Adam is offered the end of pain, permanently, and the field that blooms is gold: euphoric,
glazed, the color the Grid uses for good. The audience reads it as a door closing. The vow his people die to keep is
four words long; he says it out loud, alone, in the instant he breaks it, the way his mother must have once, over a
vial, for him.

## Script (draft v1)
> 9:16 vertical · ~2.5 min · cinematic photoreal · *Black Mirror / Silo* register. **ADAM** (`CHR-06`); on the feed,
> **NAOMI** (`CHR-11`) and **RUTH** (`CHR-07`); **SYSTEM**, the flat voice. Two spoken lines that matter: Naomi's,
> and the vow.

*Castes (`WR-B1`): Adam is a cyborg, 90% organic, Lens on, body in his rented city capsule (`Q-91`); Naomi and Ruth
are Purists with no Lens, seen on the feed (`WR-L2`). SYSTEM is a voice. Timing (`WR-T4`): about **six real weeks**
into the residency (about T+19.1) = **about 2,520 days, nearly seven years, for Adam** (42 × 60); his body is 19, his
mind about 26. The buffer brakes him from 60× toward 1:1 over about **twenty real seconds** (several minutes for him,
shrinking toward real as he slows); at about 1.5×, Naomi's **four-second** line lasts about **six** seconds for him,
nearly her own pace, the only time in Act I he hears her. After the turn he is at Tier 1 **for good**: the rest of
his roughly three real months there (about 15 years in all, `Q-48`) begins.*

**1 · THE CHIME · HOOK (0:00–0:05).** A clean chime. Adam's face freezes mid-breath while the golden city around
him **shears** into streaks of light and is gone. [SOUND: the chime; room tone dropping a step.]

> **SYSTEM** *(flat, kind)*: Residency complete. Returning to real time.

**2 · THE BUFFER · CONTEXT (0:05–1:15, shots 2–4).** The **Turrell void**: pale, borderless, Adam alone at its center,
calm. [UI: the clock numeral at the top edge: **60×** … **40×** …] In the corner of his frame, the village feed.

**3 · THE FEED, WAKING.** At 60× the village was a frieze. Now, as his rate falls, it **moves**: the fire's smoke,
standing like glass at his clock, starts to drift; a hen, one foot raised, finishes its step. NAOMI is at the
lintel, at the threshold of her house, facing the camera on its post: she was told he is coming home today. RUTH
stands beside her in her good shawl, one hand on the post. *(Neither can see him. They are looking at a pinhead
light.)*

**4 · ADAM.** He watches them speed up toward him. Something in his face that the painless years smoothed out comes
back, just slightly: he looks nineteen. [UI: **12×** … **8×** …] [SOUND: the drone of the village rising in pitch,
almost voices.]

**5 · THE PAIN · FRICTION (1:15–2:15, shots 5–7).** [UI: **4×**.] Under the room tone, faint, then not faint: the
**ache tone**, back from E1. Adam's hand finds his ribs. [UI: the compound line on the care chart flickers amber.]
[UI: **2×**.] The ache becomes the E2 pain, the kneeling kind; his breath catches exactly as it did in the field.
Hard cut: **the capsule bay**, real time, third person. His body on the rack, the chrome seam **cracking cold blue**
through the gown, an amber light blinking on the slot, the cord on his wrist lit blue in pulses. Cut back: Adam bent
double in the void.

**6 · THE OFFER.** [UI: a Context circle blooms, amber, at the top of his frame: **UP-CLOCK · PERMANENT**. Beneath
it, a single Intention square, **empty**.] The clock holds at about **1.5×**, waiting. The pain doesn't wait.

**7 · NAOMI — the feed, nearly real.** For the first time since E1, his mother moves at almost her own pace. Her voice
comes up out of the drone, a little low, a little slow, but words. She looks straight into the camera, at him, as
if she knows exactly what the light on the post is about to watch.

> **NAOMI:** The Hurried don't have children. *(beat)* We do.

Ruth doesn't look at the camera. She looks at Naomi. Her hand tightens on the post.

**8 · THE VOW · TURN (2:15–2:30, shots 8–9).** Adam, bent over the pain, says the grace from E1's table, the words we
couldn't hear at the fire, out loud, alone, very quietly:

> **ADAM:** One life, kept whole.

And he nods. [UI: the square **fills**. Whether he filled it or the Lens read the nod and filled it for him, the
frame doesn't say.] The pain **stops**, mid-breath, like a door shutting on a storm. [UI: **8×** … **60×**, and the
numeral locks.] Behind him, the Turrell void floods, practically, from the floor up, with a deep, glowing **Rothko
gold**: warm, euphoric, unbearable. [SOUND: the chord swells.]

**9 · FINAL IMAGE.** In the corner of the gold, the feed **slows**: Naomi decelerating back into a frieze with her
mouth open on a next word we will never hear, Ruth's hand on the post, the hen stopped again mid-step. Adam's face,
center frame, eased and lit gold, at peace. The surface, and her time, slipping out from under him. Hard cut to black
on her open mouth.

*(Theme in subtext, never stated: the vow is said aloud exactly once in Act I, by the man breaking it. Naomi doesn't
plead; she names what her people still have and his are about to lose, and he can't yet hear it as an argument. The
gold field is the Despair field of S1E5 with the sign flipped: the system's color for good, over the end of Act I. The
pain he escapes here is the first gift he will have to give back on the way down, `Q-93`.)*
