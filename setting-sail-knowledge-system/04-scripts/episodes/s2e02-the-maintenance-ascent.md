# Production Breakdown — S2E02: "The Maintenance Ascent"

> Detailed production/cinematography breakdown for [[setting-sail-tier-ascent]] **Season 2, Episode 2**
> (Act I, Phase A′). **Tier 0 → Tier 1.** **Adam** (`CHR-06`), a **90%-organic cyborg**
> ([[progressive-digitalization]]), makes his **first ascent** through the [[inter-tier-travel|Data Buffer]] for
> implant maintenance, and his **first Lens** ([[the-perspective-lens-ui]]) is installed. Inverts
> [[setting-sail-tier-descent|S1E2]]'s infant waking (the same first circle, *Safe*) and S1E18's airlock (he walks
> *in*). Places a-ha #8's S2 mirror: the amber thread of [[the-pattern-detector]], tagged **LOOP FORMING**, drawn
> over love ([[the-virtuous-thread]] S2E2).

## Setup & hook
The seam that pulsed at the end of E1 doesn't stop. Adam on his knees in the field at dawn, cold blue pouring
through his shirt, unable to finish a breath. The village can set a bone and sit with a fever; it can't touch this.
Only the Grid can service what the Grid made. So the Purist boy walks to the city, lies down in a machine, and goes
up; and for the first time since he was eight, nothing hurts.

| **The buffer** | — | One beat of the S1E5 grammar reversed: Adam frozen while the clinic around him smears into streaks, then *stillness* ([[inter-tier-travel]]). |

## UI behavior (the Perspective Lens)
**Before the install, nothing:** Adam's POV is raw sight (`WR-L2`); no cut to Raul's POV.
- **First circle (shot 5):** a single **Context circle** blooms over the raw clinic ceiling: *Safe*, the same first
  circle a Grid infant sees (S1E2). The bottom **Intention squares** appear empty and stay empty all reel.
- **No glyph.** The ring at the frame's heart is **absent**; the Grid doesn't issue him one until the Merging (E9).
- **The village feed (shots 7–8):** a window in the central view onto the camera Raul fixed to the post across the yard, facing her lintel.
  At Tier 1 it runs **sixty times slow**.
- **The thread (turn):** Adam's stress spikes; the **stress trigger** wakes the detector over his tether (`WR-L3`),
  and an **amber thread** draws itself from his chest down the feed to Naomi. Small tag: **LOOP FORMING**. It warns
  of a loop; it can't tell what kind. No square fills (a-ha #8 stays a plant here; S2E19 pays it).

## Philosophical climax
The first gift is relief, and it is real: we should feel it in our own chest when the ache tone stops. Then the
machine, with nothing but good intentions and a stress reading, looks at a son missing his mother and draws it as a
fault. Love and a negative loop give off the same signal. The Lens is not lying. It just can't tell, and it is now
the way he sees.

## Script (draft v1)
> 9:16 vertical · ~2.5 min · cinematic photoreal · *Black Mirror / Silo* register. **ADAM** (`CHR-06`), about 19;
> **NAOMI** (`CHR-11`); **RUTH** (`CHR-07`); **RAUL** (`CHR-02`), Ghost in a shell; **THE CLINIC TECH**, who services
> Adam's implants. About 10 spoken words.

*Castes (`WR-B1`): Adam is a cyborg, 90% organic, Purist-born; no Lens until shot 5, then a Lens. Naomi and Ruth are
Purists, no Lens. Raul is a Ghost in a shell, Lens on, never in POV. The clinic tech is a cyborg at Tier 0 (as
S1E14's tech, `Q-87`). Adam's body rests in the clinic's service capsule while his mind is up; the rented city
capsule comes in E3 (`Q-91`). Timing (`WR-T4`): shots 1–5 at Tier 0, one clock, the morning after E1 (about T+19.25; the airlock is a short walk over the ridge, as in S1E18).
From shot 6, two clocks at Tier 1 (60×): the service takes **one real hour = 60 hours for Adam** (two and a half
days without pain); on the feed, **one real second = one minute** for him.*

**1 · THE FIELD AT DAWN · HOOK (0:00–0:05).** Close: ADAM on his knees in wet grass, both hands on his ribs, cold
blue pouring through the linen in a pulse that won't stop. He can't finish a breath. NAOMI's hands come into frame
and have nothing to do. [SOUND: the ache tone, loud; the digital tick, fast.]

**2 · THE LINTEL · CONTEXT (0:05–1:15, shots 2–5).** RAUL, on a stool, fixes a small camera to the post across the yard,
facing her door and its ten notches. A pinhead light comes on. NAOMI watches him do it the way she'd watch someone nail a
thing to her door.

> **NAOMI:** Will he see me?
>
> **RAUL:** *(beat)* Slowly.

**3 · THE LEAVING — handheld, warm going cool.** Adam and Raul walk out past the last house. RUTH stands at the
fence. She doesn't wave. Her fingers find her own bare wrist, the place a cord would go. The camera holds on her
until the two men are small.

**4 · THE AIRLOCK — wide.** The forest line gives way to cracked concrete, then a heavy door. S1E18 ran this the
other way: Raul stepped *out* into raw light. Adam steps *in*, and the warm leaves the frame behind him. [SOUND: the
airlock cycles; the wind is cut off.]

**5 · THE CLINIC — fixed overhead, sourceless white.** A capsule, open. THE CLINIC TECH, chrome to the elbow, guides
Adam down into it and lays two fingers on his seam.

> **THE CLINIC TECH:** Tell me when it stops.

Adam's POV: the white ceiling, raw. Then, for the first time in his life, something is *drawn* on it. [UI: a single
clean Context circle blooms at the top of the frame: *Safe*. The bottom squares appear, empty.] He blinks at it the
way an infant would. *(S1E2's first circle, given to a nineteen-year-old.)*

**6 · THE CROSSING · FRICTION (1:15–2:15, shots 6–8).** The buffer: Adam frozen mid-breath while the clinic smears
into streaks of light around him, then a held, airless stillness. **Tier 1.** And the **ache tone stops.** Not
fades: stops. Silence so clean it rings. Adam's hand goes to his ribs and finds nothing to press against. He laughs,
once, and the laugh breaks, and he cries, and keeps his hand there as if the pain might come back if he moves it.
[Camera: gimbal-smooth, slow; the golden-white render, shadowless.] He doesn't say "it stopped." He doesn't have to.

**7 · THE FEED.** [UI: a window opens in the central view: the village feed, her door, the notches.] NAOMI
stands under the lintel, looking straight into the camera, at him. She is lifting her hand toward the notches, and
at his clock the lift takes **a full minute**. Her mouth moves; what reaches him is a slow sub-bass groan, a voice
pulled out of shape by the clock. Behind her a chicken hangs in mid-step. The fire's smoke stands like carved stone.

**8 · CLOSE — ADAM.** He reaches toward the window. His hand goes through it. She keeps lifting her hand, an inch a
breath. His breathing quickens; his jaw sets, the way a man's does when he's trying not to need something. [SOUND: a
faint rising tone, the tether's stress marker, under the silence.]

**9 · THE THREAD · TURN (2:15–2:30, shots 9–10).** [UI: a thin **amber thread** draws itself, unhurried, from the
center of Adam's chest, down into the feed window, across the frozen yard, to NAOMI. Small tag beside it: **LOOP
FORMING**.] Adam looks at the tag. He has never seen the word *loop* used about a person. He doesn't know the creed it
comes from (`WR-S1`: Purists aren't raised in it). He looks back at his mother's hand, still rising.

**10 · FINAL IMAGE.** The frame: Naomi's hand, a quarter of the way to the notches; the amber thread running into
her like a fault line; the tag. Adam doesn't close it. He doesn't know how. Hard cut to black on the amber.

*(Theme in subtext, never stated: the first gift is relief, honestly given, and the second is a way of seeing that
files a son's love under "loop". The thread is the detector working exactly as designed (`WR-L3`); whether that is
care or a cage stays open (`WR-L4`). Her voice doesn't survive the clock, which is why, ten reels from now, Ruth will
write.)*
