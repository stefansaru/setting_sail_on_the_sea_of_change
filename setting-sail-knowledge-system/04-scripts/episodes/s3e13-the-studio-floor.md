# Production Breakdown — S3E13: "The Studio Floor"

> Production/cinematography breakdown for [[setting-sail-the-cartographers-ledger]] **Season 3, Episode 13** (Act III,
> the keystone). **T ≈ +30**; **Mara** (`CHR-01`) is about **nine**. The divers reach HELM's **Studio** module
> (`MAG-04`): memory as editable film, where you can drain a memory's charge or cut the lesson out. In the **Studio core**
> they find the President's **single un-edited memory, the original loss, never run** (`FCT-004`): the root. It is
> **sovereign-locked** ([[the-encrypted-block-paradox]], `MAG-25`): the lock tag reads `Key: tissue`, and even she can't
> open it (`Q-67`, `Q-119`). One recall cue (≤ 2 s): **S2E15's welded cell door**, not the standalone reel's line
> (`Q-119`). Serial layer, never said: a child in the village has her tissue. Mirror: at her first burial the child will
> not look. World: [[the-personal-blockchain]] · [[the-distributed-president]] · [[the-memory-substrate]] ·
> [[the-locked-block]].

## Setup & hook
The child's clock first. A Purist burial in raw daylight: an old man of the tribe, wrapped in linen, lowered on ropes into
the earth. Every face around the grave watches him go down. One doesn't. A nine-year-old girl holds Ruth's hand with her
eyes shut so tight her whole face is shut.

## Cinematography
| Element | The burial (real, 1:1) | The Studio core (Grid fossil, read at 1:1) |
|---------|------------------------|--------------------------------------------|
| **Lighting** | Flat, white noon: no chiaroscuro to hide in. Fresh-cut earth, very dark. | A cutting room without end: film strips hung in rows like washing, each lit from behind (Flavin tube-light, `VIS-15`). At the end of the rows, true dark, and in it one small blinking light. |
| **Camera** | Handheld, close on the child's shut face and the ropes paying out. | Slow push down the rows; the floor in every shot. For the clip, locked; it never moves, so neither do we. |
| **Sound** | Wind; ropes creaking; a shovel; no keening (Purists bury quietly). | Film ticking through gates; the rustle of cut frames underfoot; at the end, nothing at all, then a soft, patient tap. |
| **Blocking** | The child at the grave's edge, eyes shut; Ruth beside her. | Fossil editors at benches; Raul and Rafe wading through the cut. |

## UI behavior (the Perspective Lens)
All UI is in **Raul's** Lens (`WR-L2`); nothing in the village shots.
- **Top circle (Context):** the clip's header, the signature the world has been reading on the law-feed since E1:
  `// M.C.`.
- **The lock tag**, small, beside it: `Key: tissue`. It does not change no matter what is tried.
- **Bottom squares (Intention):** none filled. Raul reaches toward the clip and stops before his hand gets there; no
  square lights.
- No counter, no log of attempts. The President's trying is shown as an image, not a number.

## Philosophical climax
The Studio sold everyone the power to edit what hurt: to turn the dial down, to snip the frame where the lesson was. Its
floor is ankle-deep in the cut. At the end of it all is the one memory its author never touched, because she could not
bear to, and then could not at all: she locked it with a key cut from her own body, and the body is gone. A god knocks on
her own door, patiently, for ever, and the door was made to answer only to flesh. In the village a girl won't open her eyes
at her first grave, and then puts one fingertip into the earth. Nobody says whose flesh it is.

## Script (draft v1)
> 9:16 vertical · ~2.5 min · cinematic photoreal · *Black Mirror / Silo* register. **RAUL** (`CHR-02`), a Ghost in a shell;
> his avatar in the stratum. **RAFE** (`Q-22`), Ghost. **MARA** (`CHR-01`), called Naomi in the village, about nine.
> **RUTH** (`CHR-07`), about 29. **THE TRIBE**, at the grave. One spoken line.

*Castes (`WR-B1`): Raul is a Ghost in a shell, Lens on, alignment off (`Q-119`); Rafe is a Ghost (a leased shell in the
village); Mara, Ruth, the tribe and the dead man are Purists, no Lens. The fossil editors are archive renders. The President
is a Ghost (`Q-98`), present here only as her signature on the law-feed. Timing (`WR-T4`, `WR-T1`): T ≈ +30, about four real
years after E12. Everyone on screen runs Tier 0, 1×, real equal to subjective; the strata play only at 1:1. The divers have
read for about 9.5 real years since E2. No one here runs Tier 1 (60×), Tier 2 (3,600×) or Tier 3 (86,400×); the President's
law-feed runs at whatever clock she likes, but the root layer plays only at 1:1, so even her knock comes at our speed. The
lock was set while she still had a body, before the fleet's launch (about T−36, bible only, `Q-119`): some 66 real years
before this reel.*

**1 · THE GRAVE · HOOK (0:00–0:05).** Real time, white noon, the village's burial ground. An old man of the tribe in linen,
lowered on two ropes into fresh dark earth. Everyone watches him go. MARA, about nine, holds RUTH's hand, eyes shut tight.
[SOUND: ropes creaking; wind.]

> **RUTH** *(very softly, not insisting)*: You can look.

Mara doesn't.

**2 · THE CUTTING ROOM · CONTEXT (0:05–1:15, shots 2–4).** The Grid, the Studio stratum. Film strips hang in endless rows,
each a memory, lit from behind. At benches between them, **fossil editors** turn small dials, and the color in a strip drains
to grey: the charge, gone. One lifts a blade and snips out a single frame, and the strip runs smoothly without it. The cut
frame falls. **The floor is deep in them:** a drift of tiny frames, ankle-high, to the walls. RAUL and RAFE wade through,
the frames rustling at their shins.

**3 · THE END OF THE ROWS.** The strips thin out. Past the last row, true dark, and in it a single clip on its own, never cut,
never dimmed, blinking slowly, like a thing waiting for a hand. [UI, Raul's Lens: top circle, the clip's header, `// M.C.`;
beside it, small, the lock tag `Key: tissue`.] Raul knows the signature. Everyone does.

**4 · RAFE'S TOOLS.** Rafe goes to it the way he goes to every fault. He tries the Studio's dial: it turns and nothing drains.
The blade: it passes through the clip like light through glass. He opens his own clean work around it, tries again. [UI: `Key:
tissue`. Unchanged.]

**5 · THE KNOCK · FRICTION (1:15–2:15, shots 5–6).** Rafe stops. From above, out of the dark, a single thin line of light
comes down: the law-feed, the same line that carries the planet's law, signed `// M.C.` along its length. It touches the
clip's lock. Slides off. Draws back. Comes down again. Touches. Slides off. Patient, unhurried, exactly the same each time, as
if it has been doing this since before either of them was born. [SOUND: a soft tap, and the silence after it.] Rafe looks up
the line to where it comes from. Then at Raul.

*(Recall cue, ≤ 2 s: S2E15's teal Silence Cloister corridor, the welded door, a raised seam of old weld around its frame, no
tag, a faint hum behind it.)*

The line comes down again. Taps. Slides off.

**6 · RAUL'S HAND.** Raul lifts his hand toward the clip, slowly. It stops a hand's width short. He doesn't try. He lowers it.
The frames at his shins shift as he steps back. [UI: the bottom squares stay empty.]

**7 · THE FILLED GRAVE · TURN (2:15–2:30, shots 7–8).** The village, late afternoon. The grave is filled; the tribe has gone.
Mara has come back alone. She stands at the mound with her eyes open now, looking at the earth, not at anything under it. She
kneels.

**8 · FINAL IMAGE.** Insert, very close: her fingertip, a child's fingertip, pressing once into the fresh soil. Only the
fingertip. Hold on it. Hard cut to black.

*(Theme in subtext, never stated: the Studio let everyone edit what hurt, and its floor is the proof of how much was cut. The
one memory left whole is the one its author couldn't face, locked by her own flesh so even she can't reach it; the god's
knock is the most powerless image in the season. A cold viewer sees a girl who won't watch a burial and then touches the
grave. A serial viewer reads `Key: tissue`, and looks at the fingertip.)*
