# Production Breakdown — S4E20: "Visual Range"

> Detailed production/cinematography breakdown for [[setting-sail-the-seed-ledger]] **Season 4, Episode
> 20** (Act IV, Phase D; season finale). **Tier 2, about four real years after E19.** Kai has stood short watches
> since E19 on Rafe's ration (`Q-109`): about **one real day at Tier 2** across the four years, **some ten subjective
> years**, Rafe's own first decade aboard (S1E15) repeated (`Q-122`). On the array's forward feed the destination
> planet resolves from a point to a disc, **seen through the bore of the screen**: a world inside a doughnut. The base is
> told the ramp resumes. Kai, who could wake into flesh now only by taking someone's slot, watches the people who will.
> Rafe's collar carries a rating (`Q-64`). Last image: far astern, **Hull Four is still transmitting** (`Q-56`
> reminder). No landing. Body **0%**; glyph **solid**. World: [[the-seed-fleet]] · [[the-stasis-economy]] ·
> [[the-firing-protocol]] · [[velocity-tiers]] · [[the-perspective-lens-ui]].

## Setup & hook
Extreme close on **a point of light inside a dark ring**: the forward feed, looking out through the bore of the
screen. It could be any star. Pull back and the ring is the screen's inner edge, the curtain of dust and plasma held
ahead of the bow with its gap for the gun, and the gap is the only clear view forward the fleet has. The reel is the
last year of the voyage told as three of Kai's watches, months apart; the cut between watches is real time, which Kai
doesn't live. Each time Kai comes back, the point is bigger, until it's a disc.

## Philosophical climax
The season was a bargain about exposure: to fire you drop the screen, to protect you seal the manifest, to get away you
give up the body. The planet arrives through the one gap in the shield, the gap that exists so the fleet can shoot,
and that is how you see where you're going: through the part that leaves you open. Kai has had ten years to hold what
E19 closed: the people who will wake into ten years of flesh were never told the number, the boy who answered the
beacon was never told the order of two lines, and Kai has kept both. The base is told the ramp resumes, and it does, for
other people. Kai could take a body only by taking one of theirs, and doesn't. Rafe has a rating now. Behind them all,
the hull they came from is still talking, from where it shouldn't be, on power it shouldn't have, and nobody in the
bay looks back.

## Script (draft v1)
> 9:16 vertical · ~2.5 min · cinematic photoreal · *Black Mirror / Silo* register. **KAI** (`CHR-08`), a Ghost on
> short watches at Tier 2; **RAFE** (`CHR-02`), rated now, on the same watch for the last one; the base at the forward
> bay's glass and on the commons. One exchange.

*Castes (`WR-B1`): Kai and Rafe are Ghosts; the base is mostly Ghosts on watch with a few Shadows (`Q-68`). Every tag
is Kai's (`WR-L2`). Clocks (`WR-T1`, `WR-T4`, `WR-T6`): about T+8.2, some four real years after E19 (`Q-122`). Kai's
watches are about one subjective day each (24 real seconds at 3,600×), about 2.5 a real day; between watches Kai is
paused. Watch 3,598 brings the total to 86,352 real seconds, **23 h 59 m**, which at Tier 2 is 3,598 subjective days,
**about 9.9 years**: one real day for one decade, as Rafe's first months aboard were (S1E15, T+3.9 to +4.2). The reel's
three watches span about the last real year (2,690 → 3,598 is 908 watches, about 370 days). The fleet is about 0.1
light-years out (about 4.3 from Earth); braking at about 0.05 g starts within the week and takes about two years to
landfall, about T+10. Hull Four's reactor scrammed about four real years ago; its stacks fell to reserve (`Q-56`).
Spends (`WR-E1`): Kai's watches in watts, paid by Rafe (`Q-109`); the ramp's resumption in chemistry; the coming brake
burn in fuel, which is chemistry (`Q-70`). Body 0%.*

**1 · EXTREME CLOSE (9:16) · HOOK (0:00–0:05).** A point of light inside a dark ring. It could be any star. [UI:
*DESTINATION · UNRESOLVED*.] [SOUND: the bay's hum.]

**2 · PULL BACK — the forward bay · CONTEXT (0:05–1:15, shots 2–5).** The ring is the screen seen from inside the bow:
a black curtain with a clear gap at its centre, and the forward feed looking out through the gap. KAI stands at the
feed, alone, in the bay's low light. [UI: *WATCH 2,690*.] At the frame edge, Kai's disc glyph, solid since E19.

**3 · CUT — WATCH 3,150.** Same frame. The point is a thin blue-white crescent now, small, unmistakably not a star.
[UI: *RESOLVING*.] A few of the base stand at the glass behind Kai, looking where Kai looks.

**4 · CUT — WATCH 3,598.** Same frame. Inside the bore, a disc: cloud, the edge of an ocean, a terminator line. [UI:
*RESOLVED · DISC*.] The screen's black ring frames it exactly; the glyph at Kai's frame edge is the same shape. [UI,
small, the bay's board: *BRAKE BURN · 6 DAYS*.]

**5 · THE COUNTER.** Kai's ration counter, as Kai checks it the way Kai checks everything. [UI: *WATCHES · TIER 2 ·
RATION: RAFE · 23 h 59 m REAL · ≈ 9.9 yrs*.]

**6 · RAFE · FRICTION (1:15–2:15, shots 6–9).** RAFE comes to stand beside Kai at the feed. Kai's eye goes to his collar
the way it did in E16. The frame that was empty now reads [UI: *1:1000*]. Neither of them says anything about it.

**7 · THE NOTICE.** Behind them, from the commons below, the base is told something in a long slowed drone. Kai's Lens
transcribes it. [UI: *The ramp resumes.*] The people at the glass turn to each other. Someone laughs. On the bay's ramp
board the `HULL ONE` lamps come back amber, one after another, 1 to 19. Slot 21 stays dark. Kai watches the people at the glass,
the ones who will wake into flesh at landfall, and doesn't look at the board.

**8 · THE EXCHANGE.**

> **RAFE:** Took me ten years to stop counting.
>
> **KAI** *(eyes on the disc):* I haven't.

**9 · THE BORE.** Close on the feed: the planet inside the black ring of the screen, a world inside a doughnut. Kai's
solid glyph and the real ring overlap for a beat in the frame, one shape.

**10 · ASTERN · TURN (2:15–2:30, shots 10–11).** A small feed in the corner of the bay wall, the one nobody watches:
looking back along the fleet's wake. The camera, for the first time in the reel, drifts toward it, away from the bow.
Far behind the last lit hull, past where the catalogue's marks thin out, one faint pulse, steady. [UI: *HULL FOUR ·
ASTERN · TRANSMITTING*.] [SOUND: a low tone at each pulse.] Nobody in the bay turns around.

**11 · HOLD.** The pulse. Once more. Once more. Cut to black.

*(Theme in subtext, not stated: the destination is seen through the gun's gap in the shield, so arrival comes through
the part of you that's exposed, which is the season's Doughnut held by a fleet. Kai has lived Rafe's decade on Rafe's
watts and still counts; Rafe now wears a rating for the seat he held by default, and its cost isn't shown. The ramp resumes for
the people at the glass and never for slot 21, and Kai watches them instead of the board. The last image is the bill
nobody has opened: in E16 the dead hull kept pace, as it should have; the fleet hasn't braked yet, so a dead hull can't
have fallen behind, and four years is longer than any reserve. Nobody remarks on it. That is Part 2.)*
