# Script — S1E17: "Tether Sickness"

> Full draft script for [[setting-sail-tier-descent]] **Season 1, Episode 17** (Act IV / Phase C→D).
> 9:16 vertical · ~2.5 min · cinematic photoreal · *Black Mirror / Silo* register. **RAUL** (`CHR-02`)
> — entering at **30% organic** cyborg, exiting at **0% organic** [[the-ghost-entity|Ghost]]
> ([[progressive-digitalization]]). **THE GUIDE** = the AI archetype (`CHR-03`, the grown voice of the
> Ep 2–3 Teddy Bear). This is the episode the [[the-memory-substrate]] ruling was built for: reaching
> for the real world forces the desync, Raul **forgets for the first time**, dies, and snaps to
> **perfect, eternal recall.**

## Production & visual
- **Tier:** 0, at the edge of the Grid (a transit hub / stasis-bay corridor).
- **Staging (`Q-86`, `WR-B6`):** Raul is at the port in a **leased proxy**, a chrome-and-carbon surface body,
  standing at the **edge of his tether's range** from the capsule where his real body lies. That distance is
  why he dies. His Lens lays his own face over the proxy's in the glass.
- **Castes (`Q-87`):** Raul, cyborg (30%) by proxy → Ghost; the Guide is a digital agent (`Q-71`).
- **Register — "Melancholy" → snap to clinical (`VIS-15`):** built practically — an overwhelming
  **carmine-red emergency wash** saturating walls/air/Raul, a thick **matte-black** floor-skirting
  baseline ([[chromatic-expressionism]] §3, [[lighting]]). At the turn it **cuts** to cold, perfect
  clarity.
- **UI:** the [[the-perspective-lens-ui|Perspective Lens]] **flickers and scrambles** as the
  brain-antenna desyncs, then **locks stable** when he becomes a Ghost ([[vertical-grammar]]).
- **Intercut:** Raul's real body in a stasis capsule (the 1:1 [[the-shadow-entity|biological host]]).

## Script

**1 · COLD OPEN — TIGHT (9:16) · HOOK (0:00–0:05).** A chrome-and-carbon hand — the proxy's — presses flat
against a sealed observation port. Beyond the glass: the raw, un-simulated **outside** — wind, dust, real sun.
Carmine emergency light throbs. In the glass, RAUL's own face lies over the proxy's: mostly synthetic now; only his
**eyes** still read warm. [UI: top circles steady; one bottom Intention square pulses — *reach · feel something
real*.]

**2 · WIDE — vertical, the void below · CONTEXT (0:05–1:15).** Raul leans the proxy's whole weight outward,
*pushing* past the last metre his tether allows. [UI: a tether-line at the frame edge **stretches, thins,
reddens**.]

> **THE GUIDE** *(calm, with an old warmth):* Raul. You're too far from your body. Come back.
>
> **RAUL** *(not turning):* That's real wind. I've never felt it. Not once.

**3 · THE DESYNC — the frame fractures · FRICTION (1:15–2:15).** He pushes too far. The UI **scrambles**; the carmine wash
strobes; the image tears into hand-held judder. And then — something he has never felt in his life on
the Grid: [VISUAL: a warm, fuzzy flash — the **kindergarten**, the Teddy Bear's clapping hands, golden
light — but it's going *soft at the edges*, slipping, like water through fingers.]

> **RAUL** *(reaching for it):* …What did it say? The bear. What did it— *(dawning wonder through the panic)* I'm forgetting.

A memory **slips away** for the first time in his existence. It is terrifying. It is the most human
thing that has ever happened to him.

**4 · INTERCUT — the capsule.** In the stasis bay, the real body's monitors fail; organic heart gives out. [SOUND: a single biological heartbeat, slowing.]

> **THE GUIDE** *(no longer a warning — almost tender):* Oh, Raul.

**5 · THE SLIGHT ITCH.** A beat of complete stillness. A tiny, passing **itch** at the back of his
synthetic neck — where the last organic cell used to be. He scratches it, absently, the way you'd
brush off nothing. [SOUND: the heartbeat **stops** — flatlines into a clean digital tone.] As the tether
severs, one frame of the bay: the **neighbouring capsule**, inches from his, untagged (`Q-88`).

**6 · THE SNAP-BACK (the curse) · TURN (2:15–2:30, shots 6–8).** The carmine chaos **vanishes**. The frame resolves into perfect,
cold, eidetic clarity — *sharper than real*, depthless 4K. The UI stops flickering and **locks**.
[UI DIAGNOSTIC, clean and quiet: *Organic mass: 0% · Tether: severed · **Ghost profile: ACTIVE***.]

**7 · THE REALIZATION.** Raul reaches back, instinctively, for the warm fuzzy memory that was slipping
— and it is **there.** Flawless. [VISUAL: the kindergarten replays in clinical, exact 4K — the Teddy
Bear's clap, the precise gold of the light, frame by perfect frame.] He can keep it forever now. He can
never lose it. He can never lose *anything* again.

> **THE GUIDE** *(inside the memory, the Bear's voice from E3, exact):* One step at a time.
>
> **RAUL** *(quiet — the sentence landing):* …I'll never forget that.

A line that should be tender. It is a life sentence.

**8 · FINAL IMAGE.** The observation port — the raw, warm, forgetting world he reached for, still
behind glass — and his own **perfect chrome reflection** laid over it: eidetic, eternal, and now
incapable of forgetting the exact moment he lost the ability to forget. The Guide says nothing. Hard
cut to black on the clean digital tone.

## Notes / continuity
- **Body-clock:** about 9 real years old (T+8.5); enters 30% organic, exits **0% → Ghost** (`progressive-digitalization`). Sets up Ep
  18 (unplug / step onto the surface) and Ep 19 (the Purist village in a [[shells-with-ghosts-within|shell]]).
- **Proxy → shell:** the leased proxy he dies in is the shell he walks out in at E18. The machine doesn't change;
  its word does (`Q-69`: proxy for Shadows, shell for Ghosts).
- **The neighbouring capsule (`Q-88`, a-ha #15):** the reminder of E6's untagged capsule, one frame, never named.
  Payoff S2E7 (`Q-92`).
- **Memory ruling (`WLD-14`):** the desync **drops him to lossy biological memory** (the forgetting);
  the death **severs the tether**, leaving him pure-digital → **permanent eidetic recall**. The one
  time he forgets is the moment he dies to forgetting forever.
- **Callback:** the Guide (`CHR-03`) is the Ep 2–3 Teddy Bear's voice — the guide that taught him to
  crawl now sees him out. "One step at a time."
- **Audit hooks:** `STORY-07` (ending = theme), `SCENE-05` (subtext), `CINE-16` (emotional truth),
  `VIS-15` (Melancholy → clinical).
