# Episode Blueprint — "The Chronological Sanctuary"

> A **standalone reel** (slotted to S2E16, recast as Adam when S2 is scripted, `Q-76`) for [[setting-sail-tier-descent]],
> built to make the [[velocity-tiers|subjective clock]] viscerally clear: **a real-world death in 0.5
> seconds vs. 12 subjective hours of perfect calm inside.** Demonstrates the Grid as *a sanctuary you
> hide inside from your own death.* 9:16 vertical · ~2.5 min · cinematic photoreal. Protagonist is a
> side character (an orbital mining worker), unnamed.
>
> *Timing per `Q-89`: hook 0:00–0:05 · context to 1:15 · friction to 2:15 · turn to 2:30.*
> *Castes (`Q-87`): the worker is a Shadow, body in a stasis capsule aboard an Earth-system station, so the capsule
> can hold any tier (`WR-B5`); he becomes a Ghost at the end. No dialogue.*

## Hook (0:00–0:05) — Tier 0 chaos
Open at **Tier 0 (real-time)** in a deep-space orbital mining station. Alarms blaring; red emergency
light floods the industrial set — a practical **[[chromatic-expressionism|Rothko "Despair"]]
composition** (charcoal-black monoliths, bleeding crimson). A hull breach: a jagged shard of structural
shrapnel breaks loose and flies straight at a worker's **stasis capsule.** In real time it will pierce the
capsule and kill the biological host in exactly **0.5 seconds.**

## Context (0:05–1:15) — the Tier-3 haven
Cut *inside* the worker's mind, matching their active **Tier-3** connection: an off-shift rest at the Apex.
[UI, small: *TIER 3 · OFF-SHIFT · hazard bonus, paid by the station.*] (`WR-T5`: the station pays.)
- **The contrast:** the sirens snap to **absolute, dead silence.** The frame becomes a
  **[[chromatic-expressionism|James Turrell]] infinite void** of deep, calming violet.
- **The pacing:** the character sits in a virtual chair, completely calm — a slow, steady, peaceful
  breath. To them, **time is normal** ([[velocity-tiers|the subjective clock]]).
- **The math:** 1 real second = 24 Tier-3 hours, so the 0.5 s to death becomes **12 subjective hours of
  tranquil stillness.**

On a floating virtual monitor, the real-world accident plays in hyper-extreme slow motion — the shrapnel
**frozen mid-air, metres from the glass.**

## Friction (1:15–2:15) — the action
The character has half a subjective day to calmly meet their own death:
1. casually **audit their public ledger** ([[the-personal-blockchain]]);
2. file a clean record with **[[compliance-validation-protocol|the CVP]]** so their life-data reads
   compliant (a check, not a patch: no Intent-Patch, `Q-73`);
3. begin an orderly, un-hurried **separation of their [[the-ghost-entity|Identity Key]]** — severing the
   biological tether and transitioning into a **Ghost.**

```
[ REAL-WORLD: 0.5 s ]  shrapnel → capsule; destruction imminent
            │  (Tier-3 dilation ×86,400)
            ▼
[ SUBJECTIVE: 12 h ]   silence · a calm breath · audit ledger · route data
```

## Turn (2:15–2:30) — the cliffhanger ending
The character finishes routing their data, **stands**, takes one last look at the frozen image of their
biological face, **smiles, utterly un-rushed — and blinks.** Outside, the 0.5 s expire; the capsule is
**crushed into scrap.** Inside, the interface simply registers:
> *[ Tether Disconnected · Identity Key Secure · Profile Updated to Ghost ]*

## Notes / canon hooks
- **Mechanic showcase:** the [[velocity-tiers|subjective clock]] (serene, not frantic), the
  Identity Key→Ghost transition ([[the-memory-substrate]] / [[the-ghost-entity]]), and a CVP check.
- **Who pays (`WR-T5`):** the station's hazard bonus, off-shift hours at the Apex. It rhymes with S1E1's birth
  grant (`Q-85`): the Grid hands out the Apex at both ends of a life.
- **Visual arc:** Rothko **Despair** (Tier 0) → Turrell **void** (Tier 3) — a clean two-register reel.
- **Slotting:** S2E16 (`Q-11`), recast as Adam when S2 is scripted; this version stays a standalone short (`Q-76`).
- **Audit hooks:** `STORY-07`, `CINE-16`, `VIS-15` (Despair→Turrell), `CINE-13` (frozen-time staging).
