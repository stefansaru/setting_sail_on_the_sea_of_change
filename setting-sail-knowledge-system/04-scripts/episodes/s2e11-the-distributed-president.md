# Production Breakdown — S2E11: "The Distributed President"

> Production/cinematography breakdown for [[setting-sail-tier-ascent]] **Season 2, Episode 11** (Act III, the first
> reel of Phase C′, *the Machine Behind the Curtain*). **Adam** (`CHR-06`), **60% organic**, his glyph still the
> hollow issued ring, climbs into the **law-chambers** looking for someone to give him Apex time for his mother's
> cure, and finds the thing that runs the world: the [[the-distributed-president|Distributed President]] (`WLD-11`),
> law derived live from the planet's measured state, every act below it checked green by the
> [[compliance-validation-protocol|CVP]] (`MAG-20`). A wonder beat that curdles. Its **ultimate nature stays held
> for S3** (`WR-R5`); its trace, `// M.C.`, crosses the frame once, unread (a-ha #1, Mara's S2 touch).
> **Answers an S1 question:** whose "planetary climate ruling" down-clocked Raul in S1E5.

## Setup & hook
Open on a sentence of law **rewriting itself** in mid-air: mustard-gold letters loosening, re-weighting, re-flowing
into a new clause like a tide turning over pebbles. Through the moving letters, out of focus, a young man's face.
The camera racks: **Adam**, tiny, center-punched under the first of a hundred nested archways.

## Cinematography
| Element | The law-chambers (the President operating) | The village feed (Naomi, Ruth) |
|---------|---------------------------------------------|---------------------------------|
| **Lighting** | **Albers** (`VIS-15`, [[chromatic-expressionism]] §4): telescoping square archways stepping mustard → olive → dark grey toward a back terminal no one stands at. Even, shadowless, calculated light. | **Caravaggio**: one oil lamp, timber, wool. The feed is small and nearly still, because a real second is an hour of his. |
| **Camera** | Locked symmetry; slow push down the axis of the arches; center-punch Adam ([[cinematography-techniques]] §8). Calm and unhurried at 3,600× (`WR-T2`). | A fixed feed camera; the only motion is human and glacial. |
| **Sound** | A choral hum with no voice in it; soft ticks like rain on glass (the CVP's checks). | Fire, a breath that takes hours to finish, nothing else. |

**Wonder beat (`CINE-05`):** through the archways' floor of smoked glass, the whole Earth Grid below — city tiers,
capsule bays, sensor threads rising from oceans and soil — and over every act down there a tiny green tick, millions
of them, falling upward like rain.

## UI behavior (the Perspective Lens)
All UI is in Adam's Lens (`WR-L2`: Naomi and Ruth have none; the feed is a camera, not a Lens).
- **Top circles `[◯]` (Context):** one circle labelled `PLANETARY STATUS`, swirling with sensor data (heat, sea level,
  soil) that pours into the arches and comes out as law.
- **Bottom squares `[▢]` (Intention):** his own square, `cure her`. When he speaks his request, a new line appears
  beside it, `APEX · 2.4 h`, and stays grey: `PENDING`. A green tick lands on his own squares too: he is being
  checked like everyone else.
- **The CVP:** the only name it has on screen, a tag on the green rain: `CVP ✓` (`Q-73`; never "the Validation").
- **The trace (a-ha #1):** at the foot of a rewritten clause, smaller than everything else, a signature:
  `// M.C.` The camera passes it on the way to his `PENDING` line. Nobody reads it, and nothing in the reel points
  at it (`WR-R5`; read from S3E1).
- **The glyph:** the issued ring sits hollow at the frame edge, as it has since E10. It doesn't react.
- **The village feed:** a small window, lower corner, carried over from E10.

## Philosophical climax
Adam came up to find **someone to ask.** What he finds is weather: law with no lawgiver, fair to everything it can
measure, and **blind to what it can't.** The whole planet ticks green, and one small patch of it ticks nothing at
all: the village, where people live and die without a chain. Is that freedom or abandonment? The reel doesn't
answer (`WR-L4`'s ambiguity, at the scale of a world). The audience sees what he doesn't yet say: **no rule up here
is going to save her, so he will have to buy it.**

## Script (draft v1)
> 9:16 vertical · ~2.5 min · cinematic photoreal · *Black Mirror / Silo* register. **ADAM** = the S2 lead
> (`CHR-06`); **SYSTEM** = the flat ruling voice of S1E5, now with a name to rule by; **NAOMI** and **RUTH** are seen
> only on the village feed, and don't speak. Dialogue is deliberately sparse: the theme lands in the image and the
> UI, never a speech.

*Castes (`WR-B1`): Adam is a cyborg, 60% organic, his body in a rented city capsule (`Q-91`), his mind at Tier 2;
Naomi and Ruth are Purists (Tier 0, no Lens); the worker on the desk below is a Shadow; SYSTEM is a voice, not a
person (as in S1E5). Timing (`WR-T4`): about **3 real minutes** after Ruth's sheet (E10), which is about **7.5 days**
at his clock (3 × 3,600 = 10,800 min). The reel covers about **4 hours of his**, about **4 real seconds** in the
village: in the whole reel Naomi takes one breath, and Ruth's hand crosses the six inches to her brow.*

**1 · COLD OPEN — EXTREME CLOSE (9:16) · HOOK (0:00–0:05).** A clause of law in mustard light, its letters
loosening and re-flowing into new words. [SOUND: a choral hum with no voice in it.] Rack focus through the letters:
ADAM's face, small, looking up.

**2 · THE ARCHES — vertical push · CONTEXT (0:05–1:15, shots 2–4).** Wide: Adam alone under a hundred nested
archways, mustard → olive → dark grey, receding to a back terminal with no one at it. [UI: top circle
`PLANETARY STATUS` swirls with sensor threads that flow up into the arches.] He walks the axis. In the lower corner,
the village feed: Naomi asleep under wool, Ruth beside her, both as still as a painting.

> **ADAM** *(to the arches, as if someone might be listening):* Apex time. For my mother.
>
> **SYSTEM** *(flat, courteous):* Request logged.

[UI: beside his square `cure her`, a grey line: `APEX · 2.4 h · PENDING`. A tiny green tick lands on his own
squares and fades.]

**3 · INSERT — the clause.** A section of law rewrites as he passes; the camera drifts over its last line, where a
small signature sits at the foot: `// M.C.` The camera does not stop. Neither does he; his eyes are on `PENDING`.

**4 · WONDER BEAT — through the glass floor.** Adam looks down. Below the smoked glass: the Earth Grid, tier on tier,
capsule bays and city blocks and sensor threads rising from the oceans, and over every act a tiny green tick, millions
of them, rising like rain. [UI: the rain carries a tag, `CVP ✓`, too many to count.] [SOUND: soft ticks, rain on
glass.] Awe. He kneels to see better.

**5 · A RULING LANDS · FRICTION (1:15–2:15, shots 5–6).** A sensor thread far below reddens: a heat anomaly. The arches
above him reflow at once; a new clause writes itself in seconds.

> **SYSTEM:** By ruling of the Distributed President, sector nine is reassigned to Tier 1.

Through the glass: a city block's lights step down a shade. At one desk a woman's face freezes mid-breath while the
room around her smears, then settles, and a warm-orange lounge blooms where her office was. *(The S1E5 image,
happening to a stranger. No one here did it; no one could undo it.)* The green ticks fall on her too.

**6 · ADAM, WAITING.** He stands. His request is still grey. [UI: `APEX · 2.4 h · PENDING`.] Around it, the green rain
keeps falling, steady and fair and indifferent. He opens the planetary view wider with two fingers, looking for the
one place he cares about.

**7 · THE MAP · TURN (2:15–2:30, shots 7–8).** The whole planet, every act ticking green, a world of tiny lights; and
there, on a dark coast, one small patch where **nothing ticks at all.** No data, no law, no rain. [UI: the planetary
view returns no tag there. Nothing.] He looks from the dark patch to the feed in the corner: in that dark, Ruth's hand
has finally reached Naomi's brow. Nothing logs it.

**8 · FINAL IMAGE.** Adam, center-punched under the nested arches, the dark patch reflected small in his eyes; above
him the law keeps rewriting itself (the hook's image, returned). [UI: `PENDING`.] Cut to black on the rain of green.

*(Theme in subtext, never stated: law with no lawgiver is weather, fair to everything it measures and blind to what
it can't; the one patch of the world that ticks nothing is where his mother lives, and the viewer finishes the
sentence: nobody up here will save her, so he will have to buy it, which is E12's door.)*
