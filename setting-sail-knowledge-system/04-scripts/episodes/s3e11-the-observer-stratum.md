## Setup & hook
Act III leads with the child's clock, not the dive's (seasons audit §6). A Purist toddler, standing for the first time,
doesn't step. She looks down at her own feet the way you'd watch someone else's, a long, level look no baby gives.
Ruth's hands wait an arm's length away. Behind them, at the fence, two machine bodies sit with their eyes open and
nobody home: Raul and Rafe are diving.

## UI behavior (the Perspective Lens)
All UI is in **Raul's** Lens (`WR-L2`): Mara and Ruth are Purists and see nothing; Rafe's Lens is not shown this reel.
- **In the village:** no UI on Mara or Ruth; the opening shot is raw sight. In the last shot, as Raul's shell wakes, his
  Lens comes back with it, and at the top of the frame the law-feed, stopped mid-line at the birth (E10), is scrolling
  again. Continuity, not a reveal: the President keeps running (Two Maras), and the god's quiet was a pause.
- **In the stratum (top circle, Context):** the ledger card, raised from the silt under the dock. Two names in soft focus,
  never legible (the E1 parents stay unnamed, glossary); one line sharp: `Tier 3 · birth grant · Lived: 192 yrs`.
- **The card's last line:** `Visits: —`. The empty field is the a-ha: the gold door he walked through at eight (S1E3b)
  opened on their tier, and their ledger logs no visit (`Q-119`).
- **Boats:** not UI. They are the stratum itself, the Observer module's old render. One noses against the dock at Raul's
  feet and waits; no one boards it.
- **Bottom squares (Intention):** quiet throughout. Nothing fills them.

## Philosophical climax
The Observer module taught you to watch your thoughts drift past without boarding them, and its failure was the people
who never stood up again. Raul's parents are filed with them. While he grew eight years in the nursery, they lived a
hundred and ninety-two in a meadow he could see from a door, and in all that time no one walked through it. *We'll meet
them when they're ready* was true, and they were never ready. Watching was the whole of their love. At the end of the
dock Raul sits with the watchers, and a boat waits at his feet, and he gets up. In the village the toddler stops
studying her feet and walks. Neither of them is told anything.

## Script (draft v1)
> 9:16 vertical · ~2.5 min · cinematic photoreal · *Black Mirror / Silo* register. **RAUL** (`CHR-02`), S3 lead, a Ghost
> in a shell; in the stratum, his Golden Baseline avatar. **RAFE** (`Q-22`), the rival-engineer, his Branch; in the
> stratum, the same face, a few years harder. **MARA** (`CHR-01`), called Naomi in the village, about one. **RUTH**
> (`CHR-07`), about 22. **THE FATHER**, a voice from S1E1 (recall cue only). One spoken line, cut off.

*Castes (`WR-B1`): Raul is a Ghost in a shell, Lens on, alignment off (`Q-119`); his shell sits inert at the fence while he
reads. Rafe is a Ghost; his leased shell sits beside Raul's. Mara (Purist from E10) and Ruth are Purists, no Lens. The
fossil watchers are archive renders of old HELM users, not running minds. The parents are Shadows (S1E1), present only as
their ledger. Timing (`WR-T4`, `WR-T1`): T ≈ +22.5, about a year after the birth (T ≈ +21.5). Everyone here runs Tier 0,
1×, real and subjective time the same: the oldest strata play back only at 1:1, so the divers have been reading for
about two real years since Rafe woke (E2). No one in this reel runs Tier 1 (60×). The ledger: Raul's first eight years ran
at Tier 2 (3,600×), about 19.5 real hours (8 × 8,766 h ÷ 3,600 ≈ 19.5 h); the same 19.5 real hours at Tier 3 (86,400×)
is about 192 years for the parents in the preserve (19.5 h × 86,400 ÷ 8,766 ≈ 192), the birth grant (`Q-85`).
192 = 8 × 24, since Tier 3 runs 24 times Tier 2.*

**1 · THE VILLAGE · HOOK (0:00–0:05).** Real time, morning. MARA, about one, stands on packed earth for the first time,
holding nothing. RUTH crouches an arm's length off, hands open. The child doesn't step. She looks down at her own feet, a
long, level, unblinking look, like someone reading. [SOUND: a hen; Ruth's held breath.] Deep in the frame, soft, at the
fence: two machine bodies sitting upright, eyes open, perfectly still.

**2 · THE FLOODED CHANNEL · CONTEXT (0:05–1:15, shots 2–4).** The Grid's oldest layer but one. A drowned dock on a
pewter channel, sky without a source. On the black water, boats drift, small and slow, each carrying one warm light and,
in the light, a flicker: a hand reaching, a mouth about to speak, a door half open. Thoughts, passing. Along the dock's
edge sit the **fossil watchers**, dozens, legs over the water, eyes on the boats, dust on their shoulders. None of them
has moved in a very long time. RAUL and RAFE walk the planks between them at an ordinary pace. [SOUND: water on pilings; a
murmur rising and fading as each boat passes.]

**3 · THE SILT.** Under the dock, in the shallows, the water is thick with pale cards, settled like silt: ledgers, filed
where they fit. Raul kneels. His hand passes over the water and one card rises to meet it.

**4 · THE LEDGER.** [UI: the card settles into Raul's top circle. Two names, soft, out of focus. One line sharp:
`Tier 3 · birth grant · Lived: 192 yrs`.] Raul doesn't move.

*(Recall cue, ≤ 2 s: S1E1's meadow, bare feet in wet grass, a man's voice, warm and sure:)* **THE FATHER (V.O.):** We'll
meet them when they're ready.

Back on the dock. Raul's face, perfectly composed, the way a Ghost's face stays composed.

**5 · RAFE · FRICTION (1:15–2:15, shots 5–6).** Rafe reads over his shoulder. He knows the names too; they are his.

> **RAFE** *(quiet)*: Did they ever—

[UI: a last line resolves under the first: `Visits: —`.] He doesn't finish. Raul draws the card open with two fingers: the
ledger unrolls into a long, bright strip of years, meadow and sky, meadow and sky, almost two centuries of the same
afternoon. He runs a finger down it, near the end, to where a small boy once stood at a gold door. The meadow there is like
the meadow everywhere else.

**6 · AMONG THE WATCHERS.** Raul sits down at the edge of the dock between two fossil watchers, legs over the water, as
they sit. Rafe stays standing behind him. The boats pass. One drifts in close and noses against the piling at Raul's feet:
its light is warm, and in it nothing we can name. It waits. He watches it. The fossil beside him watches nothing. Hold.

**7 · HE STANDS · TURN (2:15–2:30, shots 7–8).** Raul gets up. He turns his back on the water and walks. Behind him the
boat no one boarded slides off the piling and drifts away down the channel, its one light getting smaller. Rafe looks at
it a beat longer than Raul did, then follows.

**8 · FINAL IMAGE.** Hard cut: the village, the same morning. At the fence, Raul's shell blinks once, and is someone again.
In front of it Mara lifts her eyes off her feet, up to Ruth's face, and steps: one, two, a lurch, into Ruth's hands. Ruth
laughs out loud. Raul's shell, behind them, doesn't move. [UI, Raul's Lens, at the very top of the frame: the law-feed,
stopped mid-line since the birth (E10), is running again. Nobody remarks it.] Cut to black on the laugh.

*(Theme in subtext, never stated: the parents watched their child for a hundred and ninety-two years and never once came
through the door. Their ledger is filed with the people who never got up off the dock. Raul sits with them for one shot,
then stands. The child, who watched her own feet like a god watches a world, looks up and walks. A cold viewer sees a man
find out his parents never came. A serial viewer does the 8 × 24, and remembers the gold light we were not allowed to
follow.)*
