# Production Breakdown — S2E12: "The Merit Key"

> Production/cinematography breakdown for [[setting-sail-tier-ascent]] **Season 2, Episode 12** (Act III, Phase C′).
> At the threshold under the Apex, **Adam** (`CHR-06`, 60% organic, still at Tier 2, `Q-48`) meets **Vance**
> (`CHR-04`): a merit-holder and a [[the-ghost-entity|Ghost]] (`Q-90`), with no body anywhere, who holds a **merit
> key** ([[intention-in-context-law]], `MAG-18`) and wears the rating that unlocked it, `1:10000`. The reel's one
> system is **the merit key as what a rating unlocks**, and with it **the Validation** named
> ([[the-one-in-n-rule]], `MAG-32`, `WR-S2`): the honest road to the cure's Apex hours is to take one; Vance's keys
> are the other road, priced in the last of Adam's flesh. Vance's argument has to stand (`WR-S6`). The reel ends on
> Vance's chain alone; the comparison with Kavana's (a-ha #10) is saved for E13 (`Q-128`).

## Setup & hook
A collar tag fills the 9:16 frame: `1:10000`, engraved fine as jewellery on a white collar. Pull back: a man in the
flawless Grid default, late twenties, unlined, standing in the last archway before the light, watching someone with
real curiosity.

## Cinematography
| Element | Vance | Adam |
|---------|-------|------|
| **Lighting** | Lit from behind by the **Apex threshold**: a seamless Turrell glow (`VIS-15`) at the corridor's end, so he is rimmed and slightly unreadable. | The Albers corridor's stepped mustard and olive, front-lit, exposed. |
| **Camera** | Never moves when he speaks; the frame holds him like a portrait. | Handheld-adjacent micro-drift, the last organic thing in his framing (he is 60% flesh). |
| **Sound** | No footsteps (no body anywhere); his voice close and dry, as if inside the Lens. | Breath, his TTD bar from E6, low in the amber, cloth. |

**Set:** the top of the law-chambers, where the nested Albers arches end at a gate of white light, the Apex,
which Adam can see and can't enter. The gate is the reel's object: everything is staged toward it.

## UI behavior (the Perspective Lens)
All UI is in Adam's Lens (`WR-L2`). Vance is a Ghost; we never see through his eyes.
- **At the gate:** `APEX · 2.4 h · MERIT KEY REQUIRED`. Below it, the honest road, one line:
  `VALIDATION · 1:100 · ONE RUN`. (`WR-S2`: he has a body, so he gets one run, adults only; `Q-116`.)
- **Recall, no budget cost:** his `TTD` bar (E6), deep in amber, at the frame edge; his diagnostic reads `Organic: 60%`.
  Vance's eyes go to both before he names a price (`Q-15`: he sells to those already decaying; he never makes them
  decay).
- **The key:** a signature chain unspools from Vance's open hand into Adam's bottom row and parks as a pending square:
  `KEYS · VANCE`, beside his own square, `cure her`.
- **The chain (the turn):** Vance's public chain, `VANCE · 1:10000`, every block spotless green, years of them,
  not one failure, ticking on. Alone in the frame: no other chain beside it (the side-by-side with Kavana's is E13's,
  `Q-128`).

## Philosophical climax
Vance is not lying about the world: Adam's people **do** bury everyone they love, and Vance's don't. The Validation
is real and fair and costs one run at one in a hundred, and Naomi is the stake. Vance offers the rest of Adam's flesh
for the cure; mortality, to him, is a bug, and he could be right. The answer isn't spoken. It sits in one column of
light: a record with no failures in it, years long, that Adam scrolls and scrolls without finding a single red block.
The viewer may already be asking why a man rated `1:10000` has never once failed.

## Script (draft v1)
> 9:16 vertical · ~2.5 min · cinematic photoreal · *Black Mirror / Silo* register. **ADAM** = the S2 lead
> (`CHR-06`); **VANCE** = the S2 antagonist (`CHR-04`), a Ghost and a merit-holder. Dialogue sparse; every line
> is a move in a negotiation.

*Castes (`WR-B1`): Adam is a cyborg, 60% organic, his body in a rented city capsule (`Q-91`), his mind at Tier 2;
Vance is a Ghost (`Q-90`), no body anywhere, here as himself at Adam's clock. Timing (`WR-T4`): about **7 real minutes** after Ruth's sheet (E10), about **17.5 days**
at his clock (7 × 3,600 = 25,200 min). Vance, a Ghost, has slowed to meet him; both run at Tier 2 for the scene.*

**1 · COLD OPEN — EXTREME CLOSE (9:16) · HOOK (0:00–0:05).** `1:10000` on a white collar. [SOUND: a dry, close
silence.] Pull back: VANCE in the last archway, the Apex light behind him.

**2 · THE GATE · CONTEXT (0:05–1:15, shots 2–4).** Reverse: ADAM at the gate of white light, his palm on nothing; it
doesn't give. [UI: `APEX · 2.4 h · MERIT KEY REQUIRED`, and below it, `VALIDATION · 1:100 · ONE RUN`.] He reads the
second line twice.

**3 · TWO-SHOT — the corridor.** Vance walks down to him. No footsteps.

> **VANCE** *(genuinely interested):* Nobody climbs. You climbed.
>
> **ADAM:** Two and a half hours. For her.

**4 · INSERT — Adam's Lens.** Vance's gaze, in a reflection, goes to the `TTD` bar at the frame edge, then to
`Organic: 60%`. A merchant pricing stock.

**5 · THE OFFER · FRICTION (1:15–2:15, shots 5–6).** Vance nods at the second line on the gate.

> **VANCE:** One run. One in a hundred. *(beat)* You'd bet her on that?

He opens his hand. A signature chain unspools from it, fine as hair, and runs into Adam's bottom row.
[UI: a pending square, `KEYS · VANCE`, beside `cure her`.]

> **ADAM:** For what?
>
> **VANCE:** What's left of the meat.

Adam's thumb goes to his right wrist to turn a cord that isn't there: the render never carried it (E3). The thumb
stays on the bare place. Vance watches the thumb.

> **VANCE** *(not unkind; it's his faith):* Your people bury everyone they love. We don't. *(beat)* Mortality is a
> bug.

**6 · ALONE.** Vance is simply gone, the way a Ghost leaves. The pending square stays. Adam doesn't touch it. He
opens Vance's public chain instead, with two fingers, the way he opened the planet in E11.

**7 · THE CHAIN · TURN (2:15–2:30, shots 7–8).** [UI: `VANCE · 1:10000`: a column of blocks, every one spotless
green, scrolling for years without one failure.] Adam flicks it down, and down, faster, hunting for one red block.
There isn't one. The column keeps arriving, green, ticking.

**8 · FINAL IMAGE.** The spotless column alone, rising out of frame, and at its head the number from the hook,
`1:10000`, still ticking. [UI: `KEYS · VANCE` still pending.] Cut to black.

*(Theme in subtext, never stated: a merit key is what a rating unlocks, and a rating is meant to prove what you
survived; the honest record of a life is full of failure, and the reel ends on a record that has none. A cold viewer
gets the temptation; a serial viewer notices that `1:10000` should mean nine thousand nine hundred and ninety-nine
failures somewhere. E13 lays it beside an honest chain (Kavana's) and E14 goes to look for the failures. The thumb
on the bare wrist, where Ruth's cord stays with the rented flesh, answers Vance's price without a word, and spends
nothing E18 or E20 needs.)*
