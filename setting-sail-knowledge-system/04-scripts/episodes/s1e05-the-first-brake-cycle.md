of light. Raul's face freezes mid-breath while everything around him **accelerates** into a blur. He is

climate ruling]] has **automated his sector.** His role is redundant.

Baseline]]. Raul is placed, gently, behind the desk of a warm-orange tourist lounge.
> **SYSTEM:** Your new function is human connection.

needs his mind* — the warm orange behind him **dissolves**, practically, into the heavy black-and-crimson
[[chromatic-expressionism|"Despair" color field]]. No dialogue. Just his face, and the void blooming
behind it. (The **Global Loop**, wordless.)

**RESOLUTION / CLIFFHANGER (2:15–2:30).** Raul straightens, re-fixes his smile, turns the feeling *off* —
and the Despair field snaps back to hospitality orange as the next guest arrives.
> **RAUL** *(warm, flawless)*: Welcome. It's a perfect day. `[CHIT-CHAT: hospitality script; the emptiness is the point]`
