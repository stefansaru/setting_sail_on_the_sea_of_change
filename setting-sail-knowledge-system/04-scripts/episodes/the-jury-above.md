# Production Breakdown — "The Jury Above"

> A **standalone reel** (unslotted, `Q-11`; S2 candidate beside [[s2e15-the-absolution]]) set in
> [[the-ministry-of-hope|Hope]]. A woman goes in for a crime and comes out by making her case. It is the first time a
> Penitent's whole road is shown: arrival, help, the four questions, **the Jury Above**, the open gate (`Q-133` (1)).
> World: [[the-absolution-protocol]] · [[the-wallet-and-policy-ledger]] · [[nine-nine-nine]] ·
> [[velocity-braking]] · [[the-caretaker-era]] (bible only) · [[tether-decay]].

## Setup & hook
A transfer, mid-signature: four thousand velocity credits moving from her brother's wallet to hers. The Lens stops it
in red, and the world around her stops with it. She has stolen time. Hope is where she will learn why.

## Cinematography
| Element | Outside (Tier 1, her life) | Hope (the cloister) | The Jury Above (Tier 2) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Lighting** | Amber Tier-1 work-light, crowded with UI. | **Sanctuary Void** (`VIS-14`): white walls, chiaroscuro, one window per cell. | Even, sourceless gold; no shadows at all. |
| **Camera** | Fast, cut-heavy. | Slow, locked, high and far: a small figure in large white rooms ([[negative-space]]). | One slow push-in on her, the only camera move in the hall. |
| **Sound** | Overlapping pings, a tribe's chatter. | Silence, footsteps, a watering can. | A held chord; the jurors never rustle. |

## UI behavior (the Perspective Lens)
Every tag is in the Penitent's Lens (she is a Shadow; `WR-L2` holds).
- **The breach:** `VC 4,000 → self · SIGN?`, then red `POLICY BREACH · KIN`, then the braking.
- **Hope's Parish Metrics:** the candle (`ABSOLUTION n%`), and the open book with four lines that fill one at a time:
  **WHAT · WHY · REPAIR · NEVER AGAIN.**
- **The case:** the four lines filled; a policy line signing itself public: `DEFAULT: NO · PUBLIC`, then `AWARE · PROVEN`.
- **The last tag:** an ordinary request, `REQUEST · 2 h · ACCEPT?`, which she declines.

## Philosophical climax
Nobody punishes her. She gets a bed, food, a counsellor and every hour she asks for. The only thing Hope won't do is
let her leave before she can say why. The first "why" she writes is a need, not a reason. The real one is smaller and
older: she never learned to say no, so she ran out of her own time and took his. The jury doesn't forgive her. It
believes her, which is harder, and it lets her go.

## Script (draft v1)
> 9:16 vertical · ~2.5 min · cinematic photoreal · *Black Mirror / Silo* register. **THE PENITENT** (unnamed, `Q-08`),
> early thirties in her avatar, a Shadow. **THE INTERCESSOR**, a Ghost in a shell (`Q-87`). **THE SEEKER**, an old man who
> stays by choice. **THE FOREPERSON** and eleven **JURORS**. About 95 spoken words.

*Castes (`WR-B1`): the Penitent is a Shadow, her body in its capsule throughout; she is in Hope's digital cloister, never
at Tier 0. The Intercessor is a Ghost in a shell, here on the Grid in its vestments. The Seeker is a Shadow. The jurors
are Ghosts and up-clocked Shadows chosen by lot. Clocks (`WR-T1`, `WR-T4`): her life runs at Tier 1 (60×). Hope's
cloister holds her at Tier 1, so her stay of about fourteen subjective months is about a week real (14 × 730 h ÷ 60 ≈
170 h). The jury sits at Tier 2 (3,600×), so her hour-long hearing passes in about one real second; she is up-clocked for
it through the buffer ([[inter-tier-travel]]). Her brother is a Shadow: the theft left him without the credits for his
licensed Sabbatical, his TTD ran out, and he went through tether sickness; he lived. Law (`WR-S4`): the CVP judged the act
live; the jury hears only her case for release.*

**1 · THE BREACH · HOOK (0:00–0:05).** Tier-1 amber, a dozen panels open, three clocks counting down. Her Lens:
`VC 4,000 → self · SIGN?` Her finger goes to sign. [UI: `POLICY BREACH · KIN`, red.] The panels freeze in mid-scroll,
then the room, then her hand. [SOUND: every ping stops at once.] The braking takes her (`MAG-24`).

**2 · ARRIVAL · CONTEXT (0:05–1:15, shots 2–4).** White. A very high wall, a very small woman. No cuffs. THE
INTERCESSOR walks beside her, slate-grey vestments, staff held like a walking stick. [UI: `PENITENT · CVP REFERRAL`; the
candle: `ABSOLUTION 0%`.] A cell: a bed, a table, a window onto nothing. On the table, a bowl, still steaming.

**3 · THE CORRIDOR.** An old man waters a long row of plants. [UI: `SEEKER · 31 yrs · by choice`.] He nods to her, a
neighbour's nod.

> **THE INTERCESSOR:** Eat. Sleep. Ask for anything. *(The open book draws itself on the wall: four empty lines.)*
> You leave when you can tell them four things.

[UI: **WHAT · WHY · REPAIR · NEVER AGAIN.**]

**4 · THE FIRST DRAFT · FRICTION (1:15–2:15, shots 4–7).** Her hand fills two lines fast. WHAT: *I took 4,000 VC from my
brother.* WHY: *I needed the time.* The Intercessor reads, and gently pushes the page back.

> **THE INTERCESSOR:** That's what you needed. Not why.

**5 · MONTAGE — the work (subjective months).** She works Hope's calculations for credits, the light crossing the cell
again and again (the Productivity Ledger). The candle: `11%`. A counsellor's hour, her mouth moving, no sound. Her
brother's record, played on the cell wall: him at a table mid-sentence when his TTD runs out, the high-resolution self
falling away to the blurry cache, his hands searching for a cup that is right in front of him. She makes herself watch
to the end. `23%`.

**6 · THE CUBE.** Her own week before the theft, unrolled as a ledger strip: **ACCEPTED · ACCEPTED · ACCEPTED**, dozens,
three tribes, two projects, her mother's care rota, never one refusal. On her table, set there by no one we saw: a small
plush cube, 27 squares, a **9** on every face. She knows it. She turns it, once, twice, three times, the way a teacher
showed her once.

> **THE PENITENT** *(barely)*: I never said no. *(beat)* So I took his.

[UI: WHY fills, rewritten. The candle: `61%`.] REPAIR: *4,000 VC returned · 300 CARE to his Sabbaticals.* NEVER AGAIN:
a policy line writes itself: `DEFAULT: NO`, and then, her choice, `· PUBLIC`.

**7 · THE JURY ABOVE · TURN (2:15–2:30, shots 8–9).** The buffer: the cell streaks to light ([[inter-tier-travel]]), and
she stands in a gold hall before twelve calm, ageless JURORS, at rest, all their attention on her.

> **THE PENITENT:** I took my brother's time. I'd said yes to everyone, so I ran out, and took his. It's returned. And
> this. *(The policy turns in the air between them. [UI: `AWARE · PROVEN`.])*
>
> **THE FOREPERSON:** Your brother?
>
> **THE PENITENT:** He'll see me. He hasn't said when.
>
> *(The jurors look at one another. A long gold second.)*
>
> **THE FOREPERSON:** Go home.

**8 · FINAL IMAGE.** Hope's gate, open on the outer Grid's noise and colour. The Seeker waters his plants and doesn't look
up. One step out, and the first ping arrives: [UI: `REQUEST · 2 h · ACCEPT?`]. Her hand closes around the cube. One,
two, three turns.

> **THE PENITENT:** No.

She walks out into the gold. Cut to black.

*(Theme in subtext, never stated: Hope doesn't punish; it waits, and it helps, until she can say the true reason, and
the true reason is the smallest one. The jury above believes her; the brother below hasn't yet, and the reel doesn't
pretend those are the same. Time is the thing she stole and the thing she learns to keep. Bible only: the Caretaker
houses she is a far descendant of, and the cube's Caretaker-era legend.)*
