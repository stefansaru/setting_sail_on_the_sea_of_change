# Production Breakdown — S3E09: "The Annunciation"

> Detailed production/cinematography breakdown for [[setting-sail-the-cartographers-ledger]] **Season 3, Episode 9**
> (Act II). **Naomi's house, night, Tier 0.** *The god speaks.* Ruth has no Lens, so the President borrows **Raul's
> shell** to address her (`Q-101`), and the still-distributed rest of herself: *"I cannot wait to find myself."* A god
> awaiting her own birth, knowing the child won't remember, in the mouth of the guide who once heard a bear speak in
> her cadence (a-ha #1 undertow, unexplained). Raul's **moral detonator**: a defenceless God-child means the Grid could
> lose its governor. Freedom, or chaos? **Two Maras** in one frame. World: [[the-distributed-president]] ·
> [[shells-with-ghosts-within]] · [[the-perspective-lens-ui]] · [[the-memory-substrate]].

## Setup & hook
Ruth, seven months gone, asleep sitting up by the fire. Under the cloth over her belly, a kick: a small hard shape
moving across and gone. Raul's chrome hand an inch above it, not touching. It has been an inch above it a long time.

## Cinematography — a voice in the wrong body
| Element | Ruth | Raul's shell, Raul | Raul's shell, the god |
|---------|------|--------------------|----------------------|
| **Lighting** | Caravaggio firelight (`VIS-15`), warm, from one side. | The fire on chrome; his optics a faint cold point. | Same light. The optics **brighten** a step and stop flickering: steadier than Raul ever is. |
| **Camera** | Handheld, close, breathing. | Handheld with her. | **Locked**, machine-smooth (`VIS-04`), for exactly as long as she is in him. The cut back to handheld is how we know she's gone. |
| **Sound** | Fire; Ruth's breathing; the fetal heartbeat, faint, as Raul's Lens hears it. | His servos. | The servos go **silent**: the god moves the shell without a sound. Raul's voice, in a warmer, slower, patient cadence that isn't his: the cadence of the bear from S1E2. |

## UI behavior (the Perspective Lens)
Every tag is in **Raul's** Lens (`WR-L2`). He is conscious throughout, a passenger watching his own mouth.
- **The speaker tag:** a small tag at the foot of his frame that has always read `RAUL`. At the turn of shot 3 it
  flips: `// M.C.`. At shot 9 it flips back: `RAUL`. *(E18 flips it the other way and it stays.)*
- **Two Maras:** at the top of the frame the **law-feed**, planet-sized, scrolling, as it always has. Over Ruth,
  `Carrying: 1 · Caste: —` (E7) and under it a small heartbeat trace. Over the belly, faint, the President's
  **cold-complete ring**, the god's glyph, hovering where the child is.
- **The line in the feed:** when she says her line aloud, the same words appear in the law-feed, signed `// M.C.`,
  and scroll away into the rest of her.
- **The detonator (the turn):** with the tag back to `RAUL`, the feed keeps scrolling, but on every fetal heartbeat
  its scroll **hitches**: a skipped frame, a hair of stillness, then on. The governor's attention, down here.

## Philosophical climax
In scripture an angel comes to a young woman to announce a birth. Here the one being born makes the announcement
herself, and has to borrow a body to do it, because the woman carrying her won't wear the machine that would let
the god simply appear. She can't feel the hand she lays on her own future body: chrome has no nerves, and a god has
no pain. Ruth tells her she will. The god says what she has wanted for longer than anyone has been alive, and says it
to herself as much as to Ruth. Then she leaves, and Raul is left holding the consequence in his own Lens: the mind
that governs a planet is spending its attention on a heartbeat. If the child is all she is now, who is minding the
rest? Freedom, if no one. Chaos, if no one. The reel doesn't answer.

## Script (draft v1)
> 9:16 vertical · ~2.5 min · cinematic photoreal · *Black Mirror / Silo* register. **RUTH** (`CHR-07`), about seven
> months pregnant; **RAUL** (`CHR-02`), a Ghost in a shell; **THE PRESIDENT** (`CHR-01`), speaking through Raul's
> shell in his voice (script label **// M.C.**). About thirty spoken words.

*Castes (`WR-B1`): Ruth is a Purist, no Lens; Raul is a Ghost in a shell, Lens on (`Q-119`); the President is a Ghost
(`Q-98`), distributed across every tier, here speaking through a shell that isn't hers. The child is a fetus with
no caste yet (`Carrying: 1 · Caste: —`). Timing (`WR-T4`): the house runs at Tier 0, about T+21.35, two months before the
birth; Ruth is about 21 (she turns 21 during Act II; about 20 at E1, `Q-104`). The part of the President in the shell
speaks at 1:1. The parts of her at the Apex live a day in every real second (86,400 s = 24 h), so Ruth's silences are
days long to them, and the nine months of the pregnancy, about 274 days, are about **65,000 years** (274 × 236.6). Hence
the line.*

**1 · THE KICK · HOOK (0:00–0:05).** Firelight. Cloth over a pregnant belly; a small shape moves across under it and is
gone. A chrome hand hangs an inch above, not touching. [SOUND: fire; a fast faint heartbeat, the way Raul's Lens hears
it.]

**2 · NAOMI'S HOUSE · CONTEXT (0:05–1:15, shots 2–4).** Wider: RUTH asleep sitting up by the hearth, a shawl over her
knees; RAUL on the stool across from her, as on every night lately. The lintel behind, eleven notches. His POV: [UI: the
law-feed scrolling across the top of the frame; over Ruth, `Carrying: 1 · Caste: —` and its heartbeat trace; above the
belly, faint, a cold-complete ring. At the foot of the frame, the speaker tag: `RAUL`.]

**3 · THE FLIP.** Raul's optics brighten one step and go perfectly still. His servos stop making noise. [UI: the speaker
tag flips: `RAUL` → `// M.C.`] [Camera: locks off.] His head lifts in one smooth movement that is not his. Ruth wakes,
as if someone had said her name. Nobody has.

**4 · RUTH.** She looks at him a long time. She knows his face, and this isn't how it sits.

> **// M.C.** *(Raul's voice; warm, slow, patient; not his cadence):* Ruth.
>
> **RUTH:** You're not him.
>
> **// M.C.:** No.

**5 · THE HAND · FRICTION (1:15–2:15, shots 5–8).** The chrome hand, still an inch above the belly. It doesn't come
closer. Ruth takes it by the wrist herself and lays it flat on the cloth.

> **// M.C.:** I can't feel that.

**6 · RECALL CUE (≤ 2 s).** S1E19: Naomi's warm hand over Raul's chrome wrist at the long table; his mouth on *"…I can't
feel that."* Gone.

**7 · RUTH.**

> **RUTH:** You will.

The locked frame holds on the chrome hand on the cloth. Under it, the kick again.

> **// M.C.:** She won't remember me.

Ruth doesn't answer that. She doesn't take the hand away either.

**8 · THE LINE.** The shell turns its head, not to Ruth, toward the door, the ridge, the tower light: toward the rest of
herself. [UI: Two Maras: the planet-sized law-feed above, the heartbeat trace below, the cold ring between them.]

> **// M.C.:** I cannot wait to find myself.

[UI: the same words write themselves into the law-feed, signed `// M.C.`, and scroll up and away into everything
else.]

**9 · THE DETONATOR · TURN (2:15–2:30, shots 9–10).** The optics dim one step and flicker again. Servos, audible. [UI: the
speaker tag flips back: `RAUL`.] [Camera: handheld again.] Raul is looking at his own hand on Ruth's belly. He doesn't
move it. In his Lens the feed scrolls on, and on the next heartbeat, it **hitches**: one skipped frame of a planet's
law. The next beat: again. He looks up, out of the door, at the tower light over the ridge, blinking slow and steady,
and back at the feed, hitching in time with a child.

**10 · FINAL IMAGE.** Ruth's hand over his chrome hand on her belly, firelit, and over them, in the corner of his Lens,
the law of the world pausing on every beat of a heart that isn't born yet. Silence but the heartbeat. Hard cut to black
on a hitch.

*(Theme in subtext, never stated: the god's first spoken words are a confession she can't feel, and a Purist answers
the whole season in two words. "I cannot wait to find myself" is a joke only the god could make: she has waited
65,000 years of her own time for nine months of Ruth's. The bear's cadence in Raul's voice, and his own S1E19 line in
the god's mouth, are left for the viewer to place. Raul's question, whether a world loses its governor or its
jailer, lives only in a skipped frame.)*
