# Production Breakdown — S4E10: "The Open Window"

> Production and cinematography breakdown for [[setting-sail-the-seed-ledger]] **Season 4, Episode 10** (mid-season,
> the end of Act II, Phase B: the window opens). **Hull Four, stasis bay C, firing day.** The routine of
> [[the-firing-protocol]] runs as it always has. The [[the-seed-fleet|scattering screen]] drops, the lock is
> counted, the bore fires, and in the same open second a gram-scale grain of natural rock, too small for any
> catalogue (`Q-66`), comes down the bore's line. Hull Four is struck head-on. This is S1E15's "night the Station
> is holed" (`Q-45`, `Q-115`; a-ha #5). Nobody says so on screen, and the viewer gets there alone. Kai **100%
> organic Shadow** ([[progressive-digitalization]]); glyph **whole → open → cracked**. Cuts straight in from
> E9b's flash.

## Setup & hook
Open on the bay board's bow feed, seen in Kai's Lens. The scattering screen **drops**: a held curtain of glittering
dust is drawn aside, and behind it the stars sharpen, one by one, as if a window has been wiped. At the frame's
edge, Kai's disc glyph does the same: the solid ring thins to an outline and the bore at its centre stands open.
Nobody in the bay looks up. It's firing day, and firing days are weather.

## Cinematography — weather, then the hit
| Element | Before the hit (the routine) | After the hit |
|---------|------------------------------|---------------|
| **Lighting** | [[chromatic-expressionism\|Caravaggio]] bay: ten capsules under frost glass, each lit from below by its amber chemistry line; one cold blue source, the bay board. | **Rothko Despair field**: the frame floods deep maroon to black, the emergency strip a single bruise of red, and it **holds** (`VIS-15`). |
| **Camera** | Locked, patient, eye-level with the capsules; the counting is shot in a slow push on Teo's mouth. | One violent jolt, then **dead still**. No handheld panic; the camera has been knocked flat with everything else and stays there. |
| **Sound** | The lines' faint pump, the board's soft tick, Teo counting under his breath. | The hull rings **once**, like a struck bell. Then nothing: no alarm and no score, only the pump on capsule 10 missing a beat. |

## UI behavior (the Perspective Lens)
Kai's Lens only (`WR-L2`; Kai is a Shadow, attending by proxy).
- **The glyph:** whole → **open** as the screen drops → **cracked** at the hit. The crack runs from the bore to the rim.
- **The lock:** a small counter on the board, `LOCK 00:00` counting up. It is the only number on screen until the hit.
- **A flick in the corner (Decimus):** the bay safeguard's rating tag `DECIMUS · 1:3` turns to `1:10` in the flash
  frame. Unexplained here: it is E9b's pass, landing in the same second as the hit (Validation, `WR-S2`).
- **Late lines:** after the hit the board keeps posting, in order: `TARGET · KILL CONFIRMED`, then the array's own
  automatic tag, `FWD · OBJECT · UNLISTED`. A status light blinks **once**. The tag rewrites itself:
  `FWD ARRAY · SENSOR FAULT`.

## Philosophical climax
To act you must expose yourself. The protocol was not broken. It was trusted, on a day it had always been right,
and the arithmetic that says *fire, don't steer* stays true after the hull is holed. The board proves it, absurdly:
the rock they aimed at is dust, confirmed, two minutes after it stopped mattering. Then comes the second failure,
the gentle one. The array says the honest thing first: *not in the catalogue*. Within one blink, which is an
afternoon of debate at the Apex, someone above chooses kinder words. Nobody below will ever see the first line
again. Kai sees it, for one second.

## Script (draft v1)
> 9:16 vertical · ~2.5 min · cinematic photoreal · *Black Mirror / Silo* register. **KAI** = the lead (`CHR-08`), a
> maintenance worker on Hull Four's rota; **TEO** (`CHR-09`) = Kai's bay-mate, the morning after his burst (E9).
> **DECIMUS** (`CHR-10`) appears only as a tag. Two lines of dialogue and a count. The hit is shown, never explained
> ([[fantasy-writing]] §10/§20).

*Castes (`WR-B1`): Kai and Teo are Shadows, bodies in deep stasis in bay C (capsules 10 and 09), minds attending the
bay at Tier 0 by leased proxy (`WR-B6`). The proxies are plain maintenance rigs. The other eight capsules hold Shadows
of the same rota group, asleep between watches. Decimus is a digital agent (`Q-71`). The High Clock (Ghosts, the Apex)
is felt only as the one blink of a status light. Clocks (`WR-T1`, `WR-T4`): Kai and Teo run at Tier 0, so the reel
runs on one clock, real = subjective. Decimus spends the same lock at Tier 2 (60 s real = 60 h inside, E9b). The
High Clock's blink is an afternoon: about 4 h at 86,400× is a sixth of a real second. Series clock: about **T+4.2**,
the day after Teo's burst ([[the-seed-fleet]] table). Window: the screen drops; a **60 s lock**; the bore fires; the
grain arrives in the same second. The target sits about 20 million km out, so its kill is confirmed by light about
**2 min 13 s** after the shot (67 s out, 67 s back). The hull is still 11 minutes short of the dust, so the margin
holds (`MAG-30`). Spends (`WR-E1`): firing costs watts, the bore's pulse off the reactor. Not steering saves fuel,
which is chemistry (`Q-70`). The grain's cost lands in the next reel. Bible only: a gram at a tenth of light speed
hits like about 107 tonnes of TNT.*

**1 · THE BOW FEED — the board, in Kai's Lens · HOOK (0:00–0:05).** The scattering screen **drops**: a glittering
curtain drawn aside, the stars behind it sharpening. [SOUND: a long, low magnetic sigh.] [UI: the disc glyph's ring
thins to an outline; the bore stands open.]

**2 · BAY C — WIDE, locked · CONTEXT (0:05–1:15, shots 2–4).** Ten capsules on their cradles under frost glass,
amber lines pulsing beneath them. Over the bay doors, the crew's worn stencil: **THE STATION**. KAI's proxy kneels at
capsule 10 with a line tester, working, not watching. TEO's proxy leans on capsule 09, slow, a little glazed.

> **KAI** *(not looking up):* You sleep?
>
> **TEO** *(a small, private smile):* Some.

**3 · THE BOARD — insert.** `LOCK 00:00` begins to count up. In the corner, small and steady: `DECIMUS · 1:3`, its
status light flickering faster than anything else in the room (E9b's sixty hours, seen from outside as one minute).

**4 · THE COUNT.** Teo counts the lock under his breath, the way every crew does (E1). Kai, still working, taps the
seconds on the glass of capsule 10 with one proxy finger, out of habit. Under the frost, a sleeping face we can't
make out. [Camera: a slow push on Teo's mouth.] *"…forty-one. Forty-two."*

**5 · THE FLASH · FRICTION (1:15–2:15, shots 5–7).** *"Fifty-nine."* On the board the bore **fires**, a white
needle down the open bore. In the same frame the corner tag flicks `1:3` → **`1:10`**. In the same frame the hull
**rings**, once, like a struck bell. Whiteout. [SOUND: cut to nothing.] [UI: the glyph **cracks**, bore to rim.]

**6 · THE ROTHKO FIELD.** The frame floods deep maroon to black and holds. The camera lies where it fell. Teo's
proxy is on the deck. Kai's proxy is on one knee, a hand flat on capsule 10's glass. Capsule 10's cradle has
**buckled**: a slow, small bend in the rail, nothing more. Its amber line goes red, then amber again. [SOUND: the
pump on capsule 10 misses a beat, then finds it.]

**7 · THE BOARD, LATE.** The board, cracked across one corner, keeps posting in order, as if nothing happened.
`TARGET · KILL CONFIRMED`. Two minutes and thirteen seconds after the shot, the rock they aimed at is dust.

> **TEO** *(from the floor, dazed, almost proud):* …We got it.

**8 · THE LINE · TURN (2:15–2:30, shots 8–9).** Under the kill, the array posts its own automatic tag:
`FWD · OBJECT · UNLISTED`. Kai reads it. A status light at the top of the board blinks **once**. The tag rewrites
itself, letter by letter: `FWD ARRAY · SENSOR FAULT`.

**9 · FINAL IMAGE.** Hold the Rothko field. Over the doors the stencil THE STATION flickers in the emergency red, and
one letter stays dark. Kai's proxy hand is still flat on the glass. Cut to black.

*(Theme in subtext, not stated: the shot worked and the hull is holed, both true, both on one board. Teo's private
smile is the night before (E9), and he will never connect it to the floor he is lying on. Kai saw one honest word
for one second, and the season's other wound is that it was replaced by a kind one. The serial viewer hears S1E15's
line under the bell; the cold viewer only needs the window.)*
