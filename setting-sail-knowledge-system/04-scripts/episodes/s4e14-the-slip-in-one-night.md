# Production Breakdown — S4E14: "The Slip in One Night"

> Production and cinematography breakdown for [[setting-sail-the-seed-ledger]] **Season 4, Episode 14** (Act III,
> Phase C). **Kai's bay, no surgeon.** Kai forces [[the-cyborg-slip|the Slip]] with the ship's own tools, cutting the
> body's claim on the mind line by line while the countdown reads hours. Decimus is the map and the second pair of
> hands. The ship's voice reads the hours to an emptying hull. The most physical episode of the series. **Body: 70%
> → 30%** ([[progressive-digitalization]], `WR-B3`): S1's twenty-episode descent, compressed into one night. Glyph
> **cracked**; the Lens flickers to **wireframe**.

## Setup & hook
Macro, under frost glass: the base of a sleeping skull, and hundreds of hair-fine glowing lines running into it. A
cutter's jaw closes on one. *Tick.* A bead of sealant. In the corner, `27:55:10`, and under it `Organic: 70%`. Then
the cutter moves to the next line, and the reel begins.

## Cinematography — a night of handwork
| Element | The work (macro) | The room and the hull |
|---------|------------------|-----------------------|
| **Lighting** | One clamped work-lamp, hard and white; the lines glow from inside. Caravaggio in miniature. | The bay's emergency red. Above, through the grille, the Flavin rows of the server deck, **fewer each time we look up**. |
| **Camera** | Locked macro on the cutter and the lines. Rack focus between the tool and the face under the glass. | Static wides, each one later in the night. The cuts between them skip hours. |
| **Sound** | *Tick* (cut) · soft hiss (sealant) · the pump on capsule 10, slowing very slightly over the reel. | The ship's voice reading hours. The hull groaning as the reactor winds down. Relay clicks from above, thinning, then gone. |

## UI behavior (the Perspective Lens)
Kai's Lens only (`WR-L2`).
- **The count** and **`Organic: 70%`**, then **`Organic: 30%`** at the turn. No intermediate percentages are shown.
  Between waypoints, the tether line at the frame's edge simply thins, cut by cut.
- **Wireframe:** at each cut, Kai's proxy hands **flicker to wireframe** in the Lens for a frame (S1E14's tell of
  how synthetic a self has become). By the end of the night the flicker lasts longer than the hands.
- **The handover:** each line Decimus brightens carries a tiny tag of what it holds, gone before it can be read
  except twice: `smell` and `balance`. Then `STACK DRAW ▲ · CHEMISTRY ▼`, every time.
- **The slip:** `Identity Key: unstable`, red, then `Identity Key: secure`.
- **The supplies:** `SEALANT · 3 left`.
- **The turn:** `Organic: 30%`, and under it, for the one line left, `Identity Key: in tissue · moves whole`.

## Philosophical climax
The Slip was meant to take a lifetime: parts swapped as they wear, until the last cell goes and you barely notice.
Here it's a night of handwork, with a faster clock at stake, done by a person to their own sleeping body. What
leaves isn't lost, because each function moves to the stacks before its line is cut. What goes is the body's
**claim**, its weight on the mind. So each cut is a small kind of lightness, and that is what's terrible about it.
Nobody tests this. No surgeon signs it. The proof of it is only that Kai is still here in the morning, and there is
no morning. When everything that can come in pieces has come, one thing is left. It can't be halved, and it's the
self.

## Script (draft v1)
> 9:16 vertical · ~2.5 min · cinematic photoreal · *Black Mirror / Silo* register. **KAI** = the lead (`CHR-08`);
> **DECIMUS** (`CHR-10`) = the map and the second pair of hands; **THE SHIP'S VOICE** = Hull Four's voice, reading the
> hours to the hull. Almost no dialogue. The work is the reel.

*Castes (`WR-B1`): Kai is a **cyborg by severance**, the stacks taking what the body lets go: 70% at the open, 30% at
the turn. The body lies in capsule 10, in deep stasis on its failing line. Kai's mind attends at Tier 0 through the
leased maintenance proxy (`WR-B6`). Decimus and the ship's voice are digital agents (`Q-71`). The minds leaving above
are Ghosts of the base, the last of the beam queue. Clocks (`WR-T1`, `WR-T4`): Tier 0 throughout, one clock. The reel
spans about **28 real hours** in cuts, `27:55:10` → `00:11:40`, about **T+4.2** (strike + 53 h to strike + 81 h less
twelve minutes). "One night" is the crew's word for it; a hull has no nights. For scale, S1's waypoints
70 → 30 took Raul's body years (S1E10 → S1E14). Kai's take one shift. Spends (`WR-E1`): every handover raises the
stacks' **watts** (they now run what the tissue ran) and lowers the line's **chemistry** draw (less tissue to feed).
Every cut spends one bead of the bay's medical sealant, which is **chemistry**. Decimus's watts come from the bay's
allocation, which it now holds at `1:10`. Who allows it: nobody is asked. A field severance needs no clock and no
ration; it runs at Tier 0, in real time, which is why it takes all night.*

**1 · MACRO — the lines · HOOK (0:00–0:05).** The cutter closes on one glowing line at the base of the sleeping skull.
*Tick.* Sealant hisses. [UI: `27:55:10` · `Organic: 70%`. The proxy's hand flickers to wireframe for one frame.]

**2 · THE RHYTHM · CONTEXT (0:05–1:15, shots 2–5).** A line brightens: Decimus's map. A tiny tag, gone before it can
be read. `STACK DRAW ▲ · CHEMISTRY ▼`.

> **DECIMUS:** Clear.

*Tick.* Sealant. The next line brightens.

> **DECIMUS:** Clear.

*Tick.* It becomes a rhythm, and the rhythm is the reel's heartbeat.

**3 · THE FACE.** Rack focus from the cutter to the face under the frost. For the first time we see Kai's body:
younger than the voice, plainer than the proxy, eyes closed, perfectly still. Kai looks at it for one beat between
lines, the way you'd look at a photograph of yourself you don't remember being taken.

**4 · WIDE — later.** The bay, static. Kai's proxy at capsule 10, small under the lamp. Through the ceiling grille,
the Flavin rows: fewer.

> **THE SHIP'S VOICE** *(warm, to the whole hull):* Twenty hours.

**5 · TWO LINES THAT SAY WHAT THEY ARE.** A line brightens and its tag stays long enough to read: `smell`. Kai stops.
Somewhere, a memory: nothing on screen but Kai's stillness. Then *tick*. Later, another: `balance`. *Tick.* The proxy
sways once, though it's a machine and has no balance to lose.

**6 · THE SLIP · FRICTION (1:15–2:15, shots 6–8).** The hull **groans**: a long shudder as the reactor drops a
stage. Kai's proxy hand jerks. A line parts that wasn't bright. [UI: red, `Identity Key: unstable`.] Everything
holds still: the camera, the proxy, the pump.

> **DECIMUS** *(no warmth now, only exactness):* Stop. Don't move.

Seconds. On the board Decimus's status light flickers very fast. Then: `Identity Key: secure`.

> **KAI** *(after a while):* Again.
>
> **DECIMUS:** Slower.

**7 · THE NIGHT, IN CUTS.** *Tick. Tick.* The count in cuts: `14:…` `09:…` `04:…`. The beads of sealant march across
the panel like stitches. The wireframe flicker on Kai's hands lasts longer each time. Above, the Flavin rows go
out until one is left, the row that runs Decimus and the part of Kai already on the stacks. *Click.* Then no more
clicks. [UI: `SEALANT · 3 left`.]

> **THE SHIP'S VOICE:** One hour. Thank you, everyone.

**8 · THE LAST SMALL ONE.** Decimus brightens a line. Kai cuts. Decimus brightens nothing. Kai waits for it, cutter
open. Nothing comes.

**9 · THE ONE LEFT · TURN (2:15–2:30, shots 9–10).** In the head panel, among the stitched-over cuts, one line is left:
thicker than the others, glowing, pulsing slowly, like a pulse in a wrist. [UI: **`Organic: 30%`**, and under it
`Identity Key: in tissue · moves whole`.]

> **DECIMUS:** That's all that comes in pieces.

**10 · FINAL IMAGE.** Kai's proxy sits down on the deck with its back against the bent cradle, the cutter in its
lap. Over its shoulder, the face under the frost. Bottom row: `00:11:40`, and the "arrive" square, half-lit. Cut to
black.

*(Theme in subtext, not stated: the Slip was a mercy because it was slow, and Kai doesn't get the slow version. What
leaves is the body's weight on the mind, not memory or function, and lightness turns out to be the frightening part.
The ship thanks everyone for leaving. Kai, the last person aboard who still has a body, is cutting it loose by hand,
and is thanked for nothing, because nobody asked.)*
