# Production Breakdown — S4E04: "The Ramp"

> Detailed production/cinematography breakdown for [[setting-sail-the-seed-ledger]] **Season 4, Episode 4**
> (Act I, Phase A). Hull Four's **synthesis bay**, Kai attending by proxy. The destination star is brighter, and the
> fleet has begun the **ramp** ([[the-stasis-economy]], [[capsule-birth]]): bodies grown for minds that crossed as
> Ghosts, stilled as soon as they are grown, to be woken at landfall. Kai meets a Ghost who comes every watch to watch
> other people get hands. She is Rafe's partner, unnamed here (a-ha #6). On the fleet-wide ramp board, one slot on Hull
> One carries no name, only **RESERVED** (a-ha #12 reminder, after S3E20's `Outbound · 4.4 ly`; `Q-112`). Nobody
> looks twice. One reveal: **the voyage will grow the bodies it promised.**

## Setup & hook
Open on a hand that has never been used: a grown human hand, curled, behind amber-lit glass, very still. A grey
machine hand rises into frame and stops a finger's width from the glass, the way you'd stop short of waking someone.
It is not Kai's machine.

## Cinematography — the nursery that isn't one
| Element | The synthesis bay | The ramp board |
|---------|-------------------|----------------|
| **Lighting** | **Caravaggio** (`VIS-15`): each capsule a warm amber pool in the dark, flesh glowing from inside the glass; the room's steel lost in black. | Flat, cool, administrative: white type on dark slate. |
| **Camera** | Slow, reverent dolly down the capsules at body height. Shallow focus: one capsule sharp at a time. | Locked-off wide, then a single slow pan along the columns that does **not** stop. |
| **Sound** | A warm fluid hush; a soft pump-pulse per capsule, slower than a heartbeat. | Silence, a faint electrical hum. |

**The capsules.** Never "pods" (`Q-69`). Most hold a shape too dim to read; a few hold a grown adult body, eyes shut,
already stilled. Show no growth in progress and no timescale; how fast a ramp body is made, and what that costs it,
is E18's line ([[the-quickening]], held).

## UI behavior (the Perspective Lens)
Kai's Lens only (`WR-L2`).
- **Top circles `[◯]` (Context):** the star, a single bright circle, a hair brighter than it was. Over each capsule,
  the Lens reads a status: `GROWING`, `STILLED`. And the two budgets from E2: the `CHEMISTRY` level drops one visible
  step as the reel opens, and still reads `nominal`. The ramp is where the chemistry was always going.
- **The board** is a physical slate in the bay, not UI; anyone can read it. Seven columns, `HULL ONE` to `HULL SEVEN`;
  each slot a colonist's name and a status, `GROWING` / `STILLED` / `QUEUED`. In the `HULL ONE` column, one slot has no
  name and no status, only `RESERVED`. The pan passes it at the same speed as everything else.
- **Bottom squares `[▢]` (Intention):** Kai's rota squares, `check` · `log`, and at the turn a new one, written by
  Kai: **`arrive`**.
- **The glyph:** the disc with its bore, **whole**.

## Philosophical climax
For two reels the body has been the expensive thing, the anchor, the ceiling. Here it is the prize. The many who
crossed as Ghosts will be given bodies at the end, and the woman at the glass is learning what she has been promised by
watching it be made for strangers. Kai, who has had a body the whole voyage, finds out that having one and having been
in one are not the same: Kai has never been inside that body either. The square Kai writes is the season's want, and
the word is chosen to be innocent. Somewhere on the board, a slot is waiting for someone who has not asked yet, and the
camera doesn't stop for it.

## Script (draft v1)
> 9:16 vertical · ~2.5 min · cinematic photoreal · *Black Mirror / Silo* register. **KAI** = the lead (`CHR-08`),
> attending by proxy; **THE PARTNER** = a woman of Hull Four, a Ghost in a maintenance shell, Rafe's partner, unnamed on
> screen (script label only; never named or tied to Rafe aloud). Dialogue sparse; the theme lands in subtext and in the
> [[the-perspective-lens-ui|UI]] (`WR-F4`).

*Castes (`WR-B1`): Kai is a Shadow, 100% organic (`WR-B3`), body stilled in capsule 10, at Tier 0 by leased proxy (`WR-B6`).
THE PARTNER is a Ghost in a shell: no body anywhere, a leased machine to stand in the bay (`Q-69`). The grown bodies in
the capsules are no one yet: ramp bodies are stilled until landfall (`Q-118`), so **nobody wakes into one in this reel
and no Woken appear** (`Q-124`). No Purists, no children. Timing (`WR-T4`, `WR-T6`): about **T+4.05**, about two real
months after E1; the fleet about 0.5 ly short of the destination, so its star has brightened by a few percent since E1,
enough for the Lens to read and no eye to notice. Kai and the partner are both at Tier 0, so one clock. Spends
(`WR-E1`): every growing body draws chemistry at the highest rate aboard; each stilled one drops to a trickle; the
capsules' warmth, the proxy and the shell draw watts. The reserved slot draws nothing until it is claimed.*

**1 · COLD OPEN — EXTREME CLOSE (9:16) · HOOK (0:00–0:05).** Behind amber glass, a grown human hand, curled, utterly
still. A grey machine hand rises into frame and stops a finger's width short of the glass. [SOUND: a soft pump-pulse,
slower than a heart.] [UI: `STILLED`.]

**2 · THE BAY — Caravaggio · CONTEXT (0:05–1:15, shots 2–3).** Pull back and dolly: capsules in two rows, amber pools in
the dark, most of them holding a dim shape, a few holding a whole adult body, eyes closed. Kai's proxy comes down the
row on rota: `check` · `log`. [UI: the star circle, a hair brighter. The `CHEMISTRY` level steps down once; `nominal`.]
At the end of the row, the other machine, the one from the hook, stands at a capsule with its hand still raised.

**3 · THE BOARD.** On the bay wall, the fleet's ramp board. [Camera: one slow pan along the seven columns, left to
right, never pausing.] Names and statuses: `GROWING`, `STILLED`, `QUEUED`, row after row. In the `HULL ONE` column, a
single slot with no name, reading only `RESERVED`, slides past at the same speed as the rest. Kai's eye passes over it
and on. Nobody in the bay looks twice.

**4 · THE GLASS · FRICTION (1:15–2:15, shots 4–5).** Kai stops beside THE PARTNER. In Kai's Lens the shell wears her
face: a woman in her thirties, ageless, watching the hand behind the glass with total attention.

> **KAI:** Which one's yours?
>
> **THE PARTNER:** None yet. I come to watch.
>
> **KAI:** Watch what?
>
> **THE PARTNER** *(a small smile, not taking her eyes off the hand):* Them getting hands.

A beat. She lowers her machine hand at last.

> **THE PARTNER:** When I wake into flesh, someone's waiting to find out what mine feels like. *(beat)* So am I.

She glances at Kai's proxy, then down, through the floor, toward the stasis deck.

> **THE PARTNER:** You've got one already.
>
> **KAI:** Downstairs. Asleep.
>
> **THE PARTNER:** Then you know.
>
> **KAI** *(after a moment):* I don't. Not yet.

**5 · INSERT — two machine hands.** Kai's grey hand and hers, side by side on the rail before the glass, both leased,
both cold. Behind the glass, the still, warm hand of no one yet.

**6 · THE SQUARE · TURN (2:15–2:30, shots 6–7).** She goes, unhurried, down the row. Kai stays. Kai looks at the
sleeping hand, then down at the proxy's own grey fingers. [UI: in the bottom row, beside `check` and `log`, a new
square draws itself, letter by letter, as Kai writes it: **`arrive`**.] It stays lit.

**7 · FINAL IMAGE.** Wide, raw glass, no UI: two rows of amber light in a dark bay, one grey machine standing among them.
On the wall behind, out of focus, the ramp board's seven columns. Cut to black.

*(Theme in subtext, not stated: the voyage's whole economy exists to pay for this room, and the people it is for are
learning about bodies by watching strangers' hands. Kai owns a body and has never been in it; she has none and has
someone waiting to hold her hand. "Arrive" is the season's want, written in innocence. The slot with no name is for the
serial viewer: the voyage is carrying a body for someone who hasn't asked yet, and the reel never says who.)*
