# Production Breakdown — S4E02: "Watts and Chemistry"

> Detailed production/cinematography breakdown for [[setting-sail-the-seed-ledger]] **Season 4, Episode 2**
> (Act I, Phase A). Hull Four's stasis bays, below. Kai's maintenance shift lays out
> [[the-stasis-economy]] in one walk down one row: ten bodies in deep stasis, each on a chemistry line, and above
> them the [[the-seed-fleet|server stacks]], which cost only light. One of the bodies is Kai's. Over the bay doors,
> worn into the steel, the crew's own name for their hull: **THE STATION** (a-ha #5 reminder, S1E15's word). One
> reveal: **aboard, every life is paid for in one of two currencies.**

## Setup & hook
Open on glass furred with frost. A hand wipes a clear stripe through it, and behind the glass is a sleeping face,
still as a photograph. The hand is a machine's: grey, jointed, careful. Pull back: the machine is Kai's proxy, and
the face is Kai's. The first time the audience sees the lead's face, it is asleep, and the lead is looking at it
from outside.

## Cinematography — two decks, two currencies
| Element | The stasis bays (chemistry) | The server stacks (watts) |
|---------|-----------------------------|---------------------------|
| **Lighting** | **Caravaggio** (`VIS-15`): a single warm source per capsule, flesh lit out of darkness, the rest falling to black. | **Flavin** (`VIS-15`): cold fluorescent tubes, clean colour, no shadow anywhere. |
| **Camera** | Slow lateral dolly down the row, capsule by capsule, at body height. | Static wides looking up the stack, small figures under tall racks. |
| **Sound** | The drip: one bead of fluid per capsule, ticking out of sync like ten small clocks; a low cooling hiss. | A dry, even fan-roar; nothing that ticks. |

**The deck order is the pyramid.** Shoot the bays and the stacks as one vertical: the bays at the bottom of the 9:16
frame, the stacks rising out of the top. The cheap people are above; the expensive ones are lying down.

## UI behavior (the Perspective Lens)
Kai's Lens only (`WR-L2`).
- **Top circles `[◯]` (Context): the two budgets, side by side.** Left, `WATTS`: a ring that refills as it drains,
  breathing. Right, `CHEMISTRY`: a level that only ever goes down, and under it, instead of a number, one word:
  `nominal`. Kai's own line, tapped, gives a number (`C-10 · 0.2 ml/h`); the hull's total never does. The base is
  told the kind version (a plant for [[the-sealed-manifest]], unexplained).
- **Bottom squares `[▢]` (Intention):** the rota, `check` · `log`, and at the end `WATCH END · 3`, counting down.
- **The glyph:** the disc with its bore, **whole**, small at the frame's edge.

## Philosophical climax
Kai is, abstractly, the expensive kind of person, and the reel makes it concrete. A Ghost on watch costs a slice of
light. A body costs chemistry carried from Earth, loaded once, never replaced until landfall. Kai has spent the whole
voyage outside that body, tending it by proxy like someone else's houseplant, and has never once been inside it. The
Ghost on the deck above says the honest thing lightly: his body is waiting at the far end; Kai's is right here. Then
the watch ends mid-gesture and Kai's mind pauses, and the only clock still running in the bay is the drip. A body is
the one thing aboard that costs something even while nobody is there.

## Script (draft v1)
> 9:16 vertical · ~2.5 min · cinematic photoreal · *Black Mirror / Silo* register. **KAI** = the lead (`CHR-08`),
> attending the bays by proxy; **THE STACK HAND** = a crewmate on watch on the server deck, a Ghost in a maintenance
> shell (script label only). Two lines each; the theme lands in subtext and in the
> [[the-perspective-lens-ui|UI]] (`WR-F4`).

*Castes (`WR-B1`): Kai is a Shadow, 100% organic (`WR-B3`): body in deep stasis in capsule 10, mind on the stacks, at Tier 0
in a leased proxy (`WR-B5`, `WR-B6`). THE STACK HAND is a Ghost in a shell: no body anywhere, leasing a machine one to
work the deck (`Q-69`: proxy for Shadows, shell for Ghosts). The nine other sleepers in the row are Shadows, unnamed.
No Purists, no children. Timing (`WR-T4`, `WR-T6`): about **T+3.95**; Kai and the stack hand are both at Tier 0, so one
clock, and the reel is one real stretch of a watch. Deep stasis pauses Kai's body's clock, so the face behind the
glass is the face that left Earth about 40 real years ago (launch at about T−36); at watch end Kai's mind pauses too,
and both of Kai's clocks stop while the drip goes on. Spends (`WR-E1`): the proxy and the shell draw watts; the stacks
draw watts; each capsule draws chemistry (and a little watts for cooling). At 0.2 ml an hour a stilled body has drunk about
70 litres of the hull's stock since launch: a trickle, and still more than a rack of minds will ever cost in chemistry,
which is nothing.*

**1 · COLD OPEN — EXTREME CLOSE (9:16) · HOOK (0:00–0:05).** Frost on glass. A grey jointed hand wipes one clear
stripe. Behind it, a sleeping face, perfectly still. [SOUND: one drip; a cooling hiss.] [UI: `C-10`.]

**2 · THE ROW — Caravaggio · CONTEXT (0:05–1:15, shots 2–3).** Pull back and dolly: ten capsules in a row in stasis bay C, each a pool of
warm light in the dark, each with a body in it and a clear line running into its arm. In each line a bead of fluid
gathers, falls, gathers. Ten drips, out of step. The grey proxy moves down the row, checking each line with two
fingers, the way a nurse checks a pulse. [UI: top circles: `WATTS` (a breathing ring) · `CHEMISTRY` (a level),
`nominal`. Bottom: `check` · `log`.]

**3 · THE STACKS — Flavin, the same vertical.** Tilt up through a grille ceiling: the server deck above, racks of cold
light going up out of frame. A second machine body moves among them, lighter, quicker: THE STACK HAND. In Kai's Lens
it wears a face, a young man's, ageless. [UI: Kai's gaze crosses a rack: the `WATTS` ring dips a hair and refills.
Back down to capsule 10: the `CHEMISTRY` level does not refill.]

**4 · THROUGH THE GRILLE · FRICTION (1:15–2:15, shots 4–5).** Kai back at capsule 10. Kai taps the line. [UI: `C-10 · 0.2
ml/h`.] From above, through the grille, the stack hand leans on a rack and looks down at the row.

> **THE STACK HAND** *(easy, not unkind):* Your row drinks more than my whole deck.
>
> **KAI** *(not looking up):* I'm planning to use mine.

A beat. The stack hand glances along the warm row, then at his own cold racks.

> **THE STACK HAND:** Mine's waiting at the other end.
>
> **KAI** *(the proxy's hand on the glass, over the sleeping face):* Mine's here.

He goes back to his racks. [Camera: hold the vertical, the Ghost small in the light above, the Shadow small in the dark
below, a steel grille between.]

**5 · INSERT — the hand and the face.** Kai's proxy wipes the frost again; it has already crept back. Kai looks at the
face. Raw glass: a machine looking at a sleeper. Kai's Lens: nothing changes. The Lens has no render for this; the face
in the bay is the one thing in the hull it does not dress.

**6 · WATCH END · TURN (2:15–2:30, shots 6–7).** [UI: bottom square: `WATCH END · 3`… `2`…] The proxy's hand lifts
toward the glass over the sleeper's chest… `1`. The Lens goes out. The proxy stops, hand an inch from the glass, and
does not move again. [SOUND: the fan-roar above; the ten drips.]

**7 · FINAL IMAGE.** Wide from the bay doors, raw glass, no UI: a row of sleepers, a grey machine frozen mid-reach over
one of them, the stacks humming overhead. The doors begin to slide shut. Above them, stencilled into the steel and
worn by forty years of hands: **THE STATION.** In the last sliver of light, capsule 10's line gathers a bead, and lets it
fall. Cut to black.

*(Theme in subtext, not stated: two currencies, and only one of them runs out. Kai's watch can end; Kai's mind can
pause; the body keeps spending. The touch that never lands is the season's promise in miniature: Kai signed on to
arrive in that body, and has never been inside it. The stencil is for the serial viewer: this is the hull a copy of
Raul woke on, and the night it is holed is coming.)*
