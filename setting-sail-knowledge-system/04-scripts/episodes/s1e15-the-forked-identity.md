# Script — S1E15: "The Forked Identity"

> [[setting-sail-tier-descent]] S1E15 · Act III (Phase C) · **"the Station," deep space** · POV:
> **Raul-Clone** · register: isolated cold blue → warm human firelight. 9:16 · ~2.5 min. A copy becomes a
> person.

**HOOK (0:00–0:05).** A Raul opens his eyes light-years from Earth, gasping — for a beat he doesn't know
*which* Raul he is. Cold cramped station; a red corruption-warning stutters across his Lens (cosmic-
radiation interference on the long beam).

**ACT I — CONTEXT (0:05–1:15).** Years of isolated deep-space work. Cut off from the Earth-self by
light-years of lag, **Raul-Clone diverges.** He survives a station accident that the original never lived;
he grieves crewmates the original never met; he **falls in love** with someone Earth-Raul will never know.

**ACT II — FRICTION (1:15–2:15).** Slowly the clone stops thinking of himself as a copy. His
[[the-perspective-lens-ui|bottom Intention squares]] fill with **entirely local intentions** — *stay,
build, love* — that **directly contradict** the Earth-Raul's programming. When the sync request finally
arrives from home, he hesitates: re-merging means dissolving the man he *became* out here.
> **RAUL-CLONE:** I'm not the file they beamed. I'm everything that happened *after.*

**RESOLUTION / CLIFFHANGER (2:15–2:30).** He looks at the partner beside him, then at the incoming
**Merge / Branch** prompt blinking on his Lens. He doesn't answer it.
> **PARTNER** *(quiet):* Rafe?
She calls him **Rafe** now; *Raul* was somebody else's name. He turns to her, away from the prompt. [UI: two glyphs — his and the distant
original — now drawn in **different shapes.**] Cut to black. *(The choice is E16.)*

*Canon: interstellar fork → branched personality ([[the-cosmic-clone-paradox]]); contradicting Intention
squares. The clone is bodiless, a Ghost by beam; Earth-Raul is 30% organic. Sets up the E16 collision.
Writers' room only: "the Station" is **Hull Four**, and the "station accident" is S4E10 (`Q-45`). Never state it in S1.*
