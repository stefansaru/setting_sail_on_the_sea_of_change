> The young Purist who was left behind and stayed anyway. When Adam climbs, she remains with his mother to
> the end, then agrees to carry **God** to be born and die. In scripture she's the loyal one who bears the line
> for bitter **Mara**.

## Identity
**Ruth** — from the Book of Ruth, the younger woman who refuses to leave Naomi: *"where you go I will go"*. Adam's
betrothed. A **Natural Purist** (`FAC-02`), which matters: Grid bodies can't carry a child ([[the-quickening]]).

## Arc
- **Season 2:** Adam leaves for the Grid and dilates away. She is widowed by a clock while he is still alive.
  She stays with Naomi, nursing her through the mortal months he skips. She sends the S2E10 message, *"She's dying.
  Come home."* (`Q-97`). She's at the bedside in S2E20.
- **Season 3:** about **20** at S3E1, Adam's age (`Q-104`). Asked to carry Mara's incarnation in a tribe that calls
  the Grid blasphemy. *Why a Purist?* Only the unrushed can carry. *Why her?* Because she is the one who stays. The
  child is a **true clone** of the god; Ruth only carries her (`Q-105`). About 21 at the birth (S3E10), she names the
  child **Naomi**. When the re-merge is put to the god, not the girl, she is the one who objects: *"She's seventeen."*
  (S3E18, `Q-100`, `Q-65`). About **38** when Mara dies (S3E20).
