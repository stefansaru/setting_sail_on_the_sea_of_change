> The young Purist who was left behind and stayed anyway. When Adam climbs, she remains with his mother to
> the end, then agrees to carry **God** to be born and die. In scripture she's the loyal one who bears the line
> for bitter **Mara**.

## Identity
**Ruth** — from the Book of Ruth, the younger woman who refuses to leave Naomi: *"where you go I will go"*. Adam's
betrothed. A **Natural Purist** (`FAC-02`), which matters: Grid bodies can't carry a child ([[the-quickening]]).

## Arc
- **Season 2:** Adam leaves for the Grid and dilates away. She is widowed by a clock while he is still alive.
  She stays with Naomi, nursing her through the mortal months he skips. She's at the bedside in S2E20.
- **Season 3:** asked to carry Mara's incarnation in a tribe that calls the Grid blasphemy. *Why a Purist?*
  Only the unrushed can carry. *Why her?* Because she is the one who stays. She names the child **Naomi**.

## Want · Fear · Lie · Truth
- **Want:** for someone, once, not to leave. **Fear:** that staying is only another way of being left.
- **Truth:** presence is the one thing no tier can buy.

## Relationships
- **Naomi** ([[naomi]]) — the mother she chose. **Adam** ([[adam]]) — her betrothed, who left and returned changed.
  **Mara** ([[mara]]) — the child she carries. **Raul** ([[raul]]) — the Ghost-guide among her people.
