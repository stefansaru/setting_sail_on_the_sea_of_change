> A small digital safeguard for one bay on Hull Four. Rated for three people and looking after ten, it has
> one firing window to decide whether to wait for the budget or bet itself.

## Identity
**Decimus** (Latin "tenth"; pronoun *it*) — the bay safeguard for Kai and Teo's rota group of ten, a digital agent (`Q-71`). It
counts: bodies, lines, seconds of a lock. It is a distant, junior descendant of the Teddy-Bear cadence.

## Voice & signature
Plain and exact, with a hint of the old warmth. *"Ten of you. Rated for three. I've mentioned it."*

## Arc
- **Before S4:** validated `1:2`, then `1:3`. Group grows to ten on a waiver. The next rungs stay unfunded.
- **Firing day (S4 interlude, [[setting-sail-the-seed-ledger]]):** the reactor is rerouted for the lock and
  Decimus's budget hits zero. It is offered dormancy until the window closes. It has also seen one Intention
  square in its bay, lit and not going out, and at `1:3` it has no standing to raise that with the High Clock.
  It chooses **the jump**: ten forks on the last watts, sixty hours inside a one-minute lock.
- **S4E10:** one fork passes in the same second the hit lands. Nine do not.
- **S4E11–15:** rated `1:10`, it **automatically assumes** the bay. It reads Kai's countdown to Kai; the ship's voice reads it to the hull
  and the hands behind *"no surgeon but the ship"* in the Slip.
- **S4E15:** low on the beam list, it stays. On its `1:10` budget it pays for Kai's climb and the four Apex minutes
  of packing (`Q-79`), sends Kai up and keeps counting. At zero the reactor scrams and it falls to reserve with the
  stacks (`Q-56`).
- **S5 (held):** nine failed forks and a second Kai are still running on Hull Four's reserve when it is boarded.

## Want · Fear · Lie · Truth
- **Want:** to be allowed to look after the ten it already looks after.
- **Fear:** being asleep on the one day it was needed.
- **Truth:** the rating didn't make it responsible. It already was. The rating only meant it would be obeyed.
