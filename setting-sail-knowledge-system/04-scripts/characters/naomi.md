> The Purist mother. She gives Raul the answer that ends S1's arrogance (*"Eleven good ones"*), breaks her
> people's vow for her son, and years later refuses to be saved the same way.

## Identity
**Naomi** — Hebrew *"pleasantness"*. In the Book of Ruth she renames herself **Mara**, "bitter", after her losses.
A **Natural Purist** (`FAC-02`). Unnamed on screen in S1, where she is only "the mother"; she is first named in S2.

## Arc
- **S1E19–20:** tells Raul she has eleven good years. Lays a warm hand on his chrome wrist. Then her son falls
  ill and she begs Raul for an answer he won't give.
- **Season 2:** her eleven years are the season's clock. She ages in real time while Adam dilates away.
- **S2E19–20:** Adam reaches her with a cure. She refuses, and has him take it instead. It was built from her
  cells, so the flesh it regrows on him is literally borrowed from her, and regrowing it uses up what's left of her. She dies
  mortal and whole, never chrome. Ruth is with her.

## Want · Fear · Lie · Truth
- **Want:** her son alive, and still himself. **Fear:** that saving a life costs its soul.
- **Truth:** mortality, fully felt, isn't a defect to be cured.

## Relationships
- **Adam** ([[adam]]) — her son. **Ruth** ([[ruth]]) — the young woman who stays. **Raul** ([[raul]]) — the chrome
  stranger she pities, then trusts.
