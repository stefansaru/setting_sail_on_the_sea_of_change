> The Purist mother. She gives Raul the answer that ends S1's arrogance (*"Eleven good ones"*), breaks her
> people's vow for her son, and years later refuses to be saved the same way.

## Identity
**Naomi** — Hebrew *"pleasantness"*. In the Book of Ruth she renames herself **Mara**, "bitter", after her losses.
A **Natural Purist** (`FAC-02`). Unnamed on screen in S1, where she is only "the mother"; she is first named in S2.

## Arc
- **S1E19–20:** tells Raul she has eleven good years. Lays a warm hand on his chrome wrist. Then her son falls
  ill and she begs Raul for an answer he won't give.
- **Season 2:** her eleven years are the season's clock. She ages in real time while Adam dilates away.
  - **E1:** a notch on her lintel for each year since the implants: ten, and room for one more (a-ha #3 plant).
  - **E4:** Adam's new Lens tags her `Cellular decay — advanced`; that scan is what he later designs the cure from
    (E16). She refuses to come up.
  - **E5:** at the threshold, as he breaks the vow *"One life, kept whole"* (`Q-74`): *"The Hurried don't have
    children. We do."*
  - **E10:** **Ruth** holds a handwritten sheet up to the village feed: *"She's dying. Come home."* (`Q-97`).
  - **E17:** from the Apex his Lens returns Raul's old reading over her, `11 yrs · elapsed` (a-ha #3).
- **S2E19–20:** Adam reaches her in person, with minutes left, after a descent of about 40 real minutes
  (`Q-93`). She refuses the cure, and has him take it instead. It was built from her cells, so the flesh it
  regrows on him is literally borrowed from her, and regrowing it uses up what's left of her. Only a willing,
  dying donor can give (`Q-53`), so only she can do this for him. On screen this is an image in E20, never a line
  (`Q-95`). She dies mortal and whole, never chrome. Ruth is with her.

## Want · Fear · Lie · Truth
- **Want:** her son alive, and still himself. **Fear:** that saving a life costs its soul.
- **Truth:** mortality, fully felt, isn't a defect to be cured.
