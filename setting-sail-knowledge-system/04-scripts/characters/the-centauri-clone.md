> Raul's **Branch fork** — the deep-space copy who diverged into his own person: survived what the original
> never did, loved whom the original never met, and refused to be re-absorbed. The road Raul didn't take.

## Identity
**The Centauri Clone** — a legal *second* person created when Raul data-beamed himself to Alpha Centauri and
chose **Branch** over Merge (S1E16). Identical past; entirely separate future. Begins at **30% organic**, like
his origin at the moment of the fork.

## Arc
- **S1E14–16:** beamed across the void while the original keeps running on Earth; over years of light-speed lag
  he **diverges** — survives a station accident the original never faced, grieves crewmates the original never
  met, **falls in love.** His own intentions fill in — *stay, build, love* — contradicting Earth-Raul's
  programming. When the sync request arrives, he refuses the merge: *"I'm not the file they beamed. I'm
  everything that happened after."* Earth-Raul lets him live (**Branch confirmed**, `Q-14`).
- **Held in the S2 reserve** for Season 3.
- **S4 (`Q-28`, author leans **yes**, 2026-10-12): the one at the top.** The [[the-seed-fleet|Seed Fleet]] *is* the
  Centauri expedition, in flight throughout S1–S3 (`Q-29`); he is a **High Clock** Ghost (`FAC-05`) who has flown the whole voyage at the Apex — the Ghost who receives
  [[kai|Kai]] after the Slip, and whose "better system" is now a *ship* ([[setting-sail-the-seed-ledger]]).
- **S3 (provisional `Q-22`): the rival-engineer.** He believes the fix for the world is a **better-built
  system** — the foil to Ghost-Raul's hard-won *"no system metabolizes grief."* Plausibly the only other mind
  who could counsel **Mara's** consciousness-into-DNA fork, having forked himself.

## Want · Fear · Lie · Truth
- **Want:** to keep the life he *became* out there. **Lie (tested in S3):** that a better architecture can
  heal what only mortality can. **Truth:** the same one Raul reaches — held in tension between the two selves.
