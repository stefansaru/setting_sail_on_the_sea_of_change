> Raul's **Branch fork**, who diverged into his own person: survived what the original never did, loved
> whom the original never met, and refused to be re-absorbed. The road Raul didn't take.

## Identity
**Rafe** — an old variant of Ralph, from the same root as Raul (*Radulf*, "wolf counsel"). Same name, drifted over
distance, which is what he is. His partner gives it to him, because "Raul" belongs to someone else. A legal
*second* person after Raul chose **Branch** (S1E16). Identical past; entirely separate future.

**He has no body.** A beam carries a mind, not flesh, so he arrives as a Ghost, years before Raul's death in E17.
He is the first Raul without a body, and he never felt the itch.

## Where he is (`Q-45`)
The beam's receiver is **the fleet**, already in flight to Centauri (`Q-28/29`). Light overtakes ships. He wakes on
**Hull Four**, whose crew call it **"the Station"**. Writers' room only until S4: the audience thinks it's a
Centauri outpost.

## Arc
- **S1E14–16:** beamed from Earth. Over years of lag he diverges: survives the night the Station is holed, grieves crewmates,
  **falls in love.** Refuses the merge: *"I'm not the file they beamed."* Raul lets him live.
- **S2 (touch):** a third glyph, far off and a different shape, at the edge of Raul's Lens in S2E18 (a-ha #14).
- **S3 (provisional `Q-22`):** the **E16 reply copy** (`Q-54`). The beam back after "Go home, Rafe" (≈ T+8.2) forks: one instance lands at the colony about T+12.6, after landfall, and merges with Rafe; the other stays on Earth, paused for about twelve years (T ≈ +8.2 → +20.5), the third instance, and knows nothing past the weeks after the strike. A Ghost, in a leased shell in the village.
  **Planted** S3E1 (`REPLY COPY · PAUSED · 12 yrs` in Raul's Lens); **woken** S3E2 by Raul for a dive that reads at
  1:1, where his *love* square greys: `Recipient: 4.4 ly` (a-ha #6 plant for S4E4/E17). **His turn** S3E6: asked
  whether a fork should be made, he says yes, packs the god's shard himself, and fails to make the memory fit. He works
  beside Raul as the rival-engineer. He believes the fix is a **better-built system**, the foil to Raul's *"no system
  metabolizes grief"* — shown, not said, in S3E12, where his better Helm drains their parents' ledger to `Charge: 0`
  and Raul keeps the ache. S3E16: `Slip: none` (a-ha #14 payoff); he takes the re-merge to the god, not the girl
  (`Q-65`). **S3E20:** S1E16's prompt returns, `MERGE ⟷ BRANCH`, for the beam to the colony, and this time he chooses for
  himself: **Branch**. He stays on Earth as his own person (`Q-99`, `WR-M1`).
- **S4 (told after S3, "meanwhile"):** the night the Station is holed was **the Open Window** (E10). He survived by beam-out and, as Hull Four's senior surviving Ghost, holds its seat on the **High Clock** by default (`Q-64`), full rank coming during the four-year jump
  (`FAC-05`), the Ghost who receives [[kai|Kai]]. His partner is the Ghost waiting for a body (E4). When refugees
  halt the ramp, he votes to keep them alive, against her (E17). **He pays** for Kai's Apex time in E18–19 and the
  watches to E20 (`Q-109`). He beamed out early, while the hull could still pause him, so no Rafe stays behind (`Q-55`).
- **Hull Cut:** dropped (`Q-113`).

## Want · Fear · Lie · Truth
- **Want:** to keep the life he *became* out there. **Lie (tested in S3):** that better architecture can heal
  what only mortality can. **Truth:** the same one Raul reaches, held between the two selves.
