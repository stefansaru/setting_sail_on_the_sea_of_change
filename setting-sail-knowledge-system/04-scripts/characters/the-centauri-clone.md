> Raul's **Branch fork**, who diverged into his own person: survived what the original never did, loved
> whom the original never met, and refused to be re-absorbed. The road Raul didn't take.

## Identity
**Rafe** — an old variant of Ralph, from the same root as Raul (*Radulf*, "wolf counsel"). Same name, drifted over
distance, which is what he is. His partner gives it to him, because "Raul" belongs to someone else. A legal
*second* person after Raul chose **Branch** (S1E16). Identical past; entirely separate future.

**He has no body.** A beam carries a mind, not flesh, so he arrives as a Ghost, years before Raul's death in E17.
He is the first Raul without a body, and he never felt the itch.

## Where he is (`Q-45`)
The beam's receiver is **the fleet**, already in flight to Centauri (`Q-28/29`). Light overtakes ships. He wakes on
**Hull Four**, whose crew call it **"the Station"**. Writers' room only until S4: the audience thinks it's a
Centauri outpost.

## Arc
- **S1E14–16:** beamed from Earth. Over years of lag he diverges: survives a "station accident", grieves crewmates,
  **falls in love.** Refuses the merge: *"I'm not the file they beamed."* Raul lets him live.
- **S4:** the "accident" was **the Open Window** (E10). He survived by beam-out and rose to the **High Clock**
  (`FAC-05`), the Ghost who receives [[kai|Kai]]. His partner is the Ghost waiting for a body (E4). When refugees
  halt the ramp, he votes to keep them alive, against her (E17). Note: if a dying hull can't pause (`Q-31`),
  a Rafe stays on Hull Four too.
- **S3 (provisional `Q-22`):** a **copy of him is beamed home**, a third instance, to work beside Raul as the
  rival-engineer. He believes the fix is a **better-built system**, the foil to Raul's *"no system metabolizes grief."*

## Want · Fear · Lie · Truth
- **Want:** to keep the life he *became* out there. **Lie (tested in S3):** that better architecture can heal
  what only mortality can. **Truth:** the same one Raul reaches, held between the two selves.
