> Raul's friend, who shows him that death can be gentle — a cyborg whose last living cell quietly gives out,
> who crosses into Ghost on *"a slight, passing itch"* and finds it doesn't hurt; it's just quieter. The
> rehearsal for Raul's own end.

## Identity
**Jun** — Japanese 順 "obedient", 純 "pure": the boy who grins at the open hand.  Raul's close friend; a body more **chrome than flesh** after decades of
ordinary repairs, a hand here, a lung there.

## Arc
- **S1E3a–b "He Started It" / "The Upper Level":** as a boy of about eight, fights Raul over the ball he once
  handed him, and is taken through the seam by Teddy. Enemies at the start, friends by the last shot. Jun is
  the one who grins at the open hand; Raul is the one who looks at it.
- **S1E7 "The Cyborg Slip":** mid-task, the **last organic cell** finally dies. No drama — a slight, passing
  itch on a synthetic wrist, scratched away, and a smile. A new **[[the-ghost-entity|Ghost]]** quietly logs
  onto the network. Jun is freed, weightless, painless — and Raul is shaken to watch a friend "die without
  dying." Directly foreshadows **Raul's own death** (E17).

## Voice & signature
Calm, even light, at the threshold — *"Shh. I think… it's now." / "Oh. Oh, Raul — it doesn't hurt. It's
just… quieter."*

## Want · Truth
- **Truth (he embodies):** the slip is not an ending to fear — it is *quieter*. The first time Raul sees that
  the descent might be mercy, not only loss.

## Relationships
- **Raul** ([[raul]]) — close friend and witness; Jun's slip is the gun that goes off in E17.

## Appears in
S1E3a–b, S1E7 (and as the haunting precedent under E17).
