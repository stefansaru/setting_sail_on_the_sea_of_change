> **The Grounded One** — the exact inverse of Raul. Born mortal and whole among the Purists, he climbs the
> tiers chasing gifts that each quietly subtract a piece of his mortality, and learns too late that the
> mortality he sold *was* the meaning.

## Identity
**Adam** — Hebrew *"man"*, tied to *adamah*, "earth": the creature of clay who eats the Grid's apple
and is expelled from the mortal garden. Pairs deliberately with **Raul**. Archetype: **"The Grounded One,"**
the inverse of S1's "Unsynced Mind" — he starts with everything Raul climbed *down* to reach (a mortal body,
an un-mediated mind, a grounded community) and gives it away rung by rung for gifts that feel like grace.

## Arc
- **Origin:** the Purist boy saved in [[setting-sail-tier-descent|S1E20]] by emergency cyborg implants — his
  digitalization begun against his people's vows. He is the child, about 8, who stares at Raul in S1E18; Raul
  doesn't scan him (`Q-81`). The implants need the Grid to live; the village can't service them, so he must climb.
- **Season 2 (lead) — the ascent (Tier 0 → 3), the trap of the climb.** First ascent for maintenance, and
  the pain stops — the first bite. Each rung offers a sweeter subtraction: no hunger, no fatigue, no fear;
  then no dreams; then risk-free pleasure; then the **Merging** (lose the singular "I"); then dilation enough
  to **outrun death.** His body rests in a **rented city capsule** while he climbs (`Q-91`). At S2E15 he takes
  **Vance's keys**, knowing they are a forger's: they buy his exit from Hope and his 2.4 Apex hours, and the price is
  the regrade of his last flesh, an Intent-Patch on his own squares paid down to E19 (`Q-61`, `Q-96`). He never takes
  the Validation. Racing to fund his mother **Naomi's** cure, he reaches the Apex — too late: mortal time
  ran out below while he dilated. He descends at ruinous cost, about 40 real minutes (`Q-93`), and reaches her a
  **near-0% cyborg, one cell from Ghost, not a Ghost** (`Q-57`). He switches his Lens off, barely (S2E19).
- **Season 3:** survives with **one borrowed mortal life**, flesh regrown by the cure Naomi refused, which consumed her
  cells; unrushed, it ages normally (`Q-57`). His caste is **cyborg**: mostly regrown flesh, with chrome seams where the
  S1E20 implants stayed, and his **Lens stays off** (`Q-98`). About **20** at S3E1, Ruth's age (`Q-104`); about 38 at
  the end. He becomes the **recognizer** of Mara's incarnation, the inverse pilgrim who alone knows what the god is
  looking for (S3E14). In S3E17 he tells her what she was: a **deliberate, costed breach** of the tribe's one vow
  (`Q-60`), and it **costs him his place in the tribe** (`Q-102`). He stays to witness her death from outside the fence.

## Body-clock & glyph
- **Body:** **90% → 60% → 30% → ~0%** organic, then borrowed flesh (a cyborg again, mostly flesh, in S3) — *inverted valence:* Raul's decay was **fate**; Adam's is
  **purchase** (he spends the mortality his people died to keep). [[progressive-digitalization]].
- **Glyph:** **absent** (a purist has no Lens) → **issued hollow** (convert-Merging) → **cold-complete**
  (Apex, eternal and empty) → **cracked: the Mortal Doughnut** (balance held one breath too late).

## Want · Fear · Lie · Truth
- **Want:** save his mother **Naomi.** **Fear:** become the hollow thing the Grid makes.
- **Lie:** that the gifts are grace. **Truth:** the mortality he threw away was the meaning — the tragic
  inverse of the [[the-macro-doughnut|Macro-Doughnut]], learned too late.
