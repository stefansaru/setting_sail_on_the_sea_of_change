> lifetimes apart. The human face of velocity-drift: closeness and distance, made the same thing.

## Identity
**Vesper** — the evening star. It is the same star as the morning star, but the two are never seen in one sky: two
people sharing a light on different clocks. Raul's partner, separated from him by the **[[frequency-tribes|Proximity Illusion]]**: she runs
**Tier 2** (1 second = 1 hour) while he runs Tier 1, then **Tier 0** after S1E10, their capsules bolted inches apart, their lived
time drifting lifetimes apart. Was "Maya" (renamed 2026-11-02).

## Caste
- **S1:** a **Shadow** at Tier 2, her body in the capsule beside Raul's.
- **Between S1 and S2:** her Quickened body fails (about ten real years is all a Grid body gets, `WR-B2`) and she
  **goes Ghost** (`Q-92`). She keeps running at Tier 2, in bursts, paused between them (`Q-121`).
- **S2E7:** a **Ghost**. Both capsules are empty, still tagged **VESPER** and **RAUL** (Raul's body died in S1E17).

## Arc
- **S1 (backstory):** the relationship the velocity tiers quietly dissolve — proximity without simultaneity.
  Never named. Two glimpses (`Q-88`): **S1E6**, a second capsule inches from Raul's on his body feed, untagged
  (plant); **S1E17**, the neighbouring capsule for one frame as his body fails (reminder).
- **S2E7 "The Proximity Illusion":** a Ghost in a Tier-2 [[frequency-tribes|Dunbar-150 tribe]], **adrift,
  lifetimes from the man she loved**; **Adam** crosses her path. On her old capsule feed the two capsules sit
  inches apart, both empty, tagged VESPER and RAUL (a-ha #15, payoff). Seduction: belonging; cost: it's an
  illusion of closeness.
- **S3:** the reunion with **Raul**, now a Ghost in a shell among the Purists at Tier 0: in S3E14 she arrives in a leased shell at the fire, tagged `VESPER · Tier 0` in his Lens, slowed to his clock, and says nothing (`Q-121`).

## Want · Fear · Lie · Truth
- **Want:** closeness. **Lie:** that being *near* is the same as being *with.* **Truth:** simultaneity, not
  proximity, is intimacy — the cost the tiers hide.
