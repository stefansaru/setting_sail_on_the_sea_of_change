## Identity
**Vesper** — the evening star. It is the same star as the morning star, but the two are never seen in one sky: two
people sharing a light on different clocks. Raul's partner, separated from him by the **[[frequency-tribes|Proximity Illusion]]**: she runs
**Tier 2** (1 second = 1 hour) while he runs **Tier 0**, their stasis pods bolted inches apart, their lived
time drifting lifetimes apart.

## Arc
- **S1 (backstory):** the relationship the velocity tiers quietly dissolve — proximity without simultaneity.
- **S2E7 "The Proximity Illusion":** reappears, **adrift, lifetimes from the man she loved**; **Adam** crosses
  her path inside a clock-based [[frequency-tribes|Dunbar-150 tribe]]. Seduction: belonging; cost: it's an
  illusion of closeness.
- **S3:** the reunion with **Raul** stays open.

## Want · Fear · Lie · Truth
- **Want:** closeness. **Lie:** that being *near* is the same as being *with.* **Truth:** simultaneity, not
  proximity, is intimacy — the cost the tiers hide.
