> The credible *"what if Raul is wrong?"* — a merit-holder, and a **Ghost**, who believes total optimization and strict
> accountability are **right**, and that mortality is a bug to be patched. He looks perfectly aligned. He
> isn't.

## Identity
**Vance** — a **merit-holder** who holds a cryptographic signature key to challenge and patch the
[[intention-in-context-law|Intention-in-Context Law]]. He prosecutes citizens by contesting their
**Proven-in-Use** record. Reserved from all of Season 1 by design.

**Caste: Ghost** (`Q-90`), in a leased shell when he must stand somewhere real. His collar reads `1:10000`: a claimed batch
of 10,000 with no failed forks filed anywhere, which is the forgery Adam sees in S2E14 (the empty row in the Data
Graveyards).

## Method
Runs **[[intent-patch|Intent-Patches]]** (`MAG-21`): he executes devastating systemic shifts while his
automated record stays spotless — gaming the **[[compliance-validation-protocol|CVP]]** ("Proven-in-Use").
The system reads him as flawless over a rotten act.

## Worldview (the opposition)
Total optimization + strict accountability are *correct*; **mortality is a bug.** A genuine, intelligent
counter to Raul's drift toward balance and forgetting — the argument the trilogy has to defeat on the merits,
not by caricature.

## Arc
- **Season 2 (antagonist):** the tempter who offers **Adam** the keys to save **Naomi** — for the last of his
  flesh (S2E12–13); his Intent-Patches game the CVP (E13); his forged batch shows as an empty graveyard row (E14);
  Adam takes the keys at E15, and they come with an Intent-Patch on Adam's own squares (`Q-61`, `Q-96`).
- **Season 3:** hunts the **root key** (`// M.C.`) — suddenly *loose in the world, inside a child*, once
  **Mara** incarnates and forgets she is God. He learns of it in E8. The oldest strata play only at 1:1, so even a
  Ghost reads them at Tier 0. He reaches the root chamber first (E15): the seat answers for everyone, so it asks a
  rating no one holds (`Requires 1 : all`), and his `1:10000` doesn't reach; the only other way in is the tissue key,
  living in a girl. In E18 he forces the question: let the key come home in a re-merge, or leave the seat empty for
  ever. His case, credibly right about something (`WR-S6`, `Q-103`): *"A world with no author is a world no one answers
  for."* He loses when she dies mortal and the key dies with her (E20).

## Want · Fear · Lie · Truth
- **Want:** to hold and wield the keys; to perfect the system. **Lie (his, weaponized):** the spotless chain
  over the rotten act. **The Truth he denies:** what cannot be optimized is what gives the rest meaning.
