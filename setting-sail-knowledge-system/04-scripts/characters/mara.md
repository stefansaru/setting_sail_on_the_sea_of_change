> The Cartographer — the woman who built the first "operating system for the mind" to avoid grieving one
> loss, and so accidentally authored a civilization. Omniscient, distributed, **deathless** — and missing
> the one thing her own law requires: **mortality.** In Season 3, the god chooses to be born, and to die.

## Identity
**Mara** — Hebrew *"bitter,"* the name Naomi takes in the **Book of Ruth** ("call me Mara, for the Almighty
has dealt bitterly with me"). Builder of **HELM**, the original neuro-OS, after a loss she never processed.
Revealed in S3 to be the **[[the-distributed-president|Distributed President]]** — her grief, automated and
scaled across the lunar-crater servers into the planet's nervous system.

## Caste
The President is a **Ghost** (`Q-98`): she once had a body, and locked her one memory while she still did (`Q-67`).
The incarnate child is a **Purist** from birth (S3E10). She **never wears a Lens** (`Q-101`): every UI beat on her is
drawn in Raul's; the President speaks to the village through **Raul's shell**.

## Arc
- **S1 (trace only).** One unnamed image: the woman with the un-run clip at the last window (S1E3b). `// M.C.` shows once, unread, in S2E11; the foghorn, the photo and `mara.c` first surface in S3E1–3.
- **S2 (trace).** The tissue lock is planted by the Locked Block in S2E15 (`Q-77`), never linked to her.
- **S3 (deuteragonist) — the incarnation.** The god who wants to die. To complete her own Doughnut she must
  feel pain and finitude, which a deathless mind never can — so she imprints **a shard of herself onto a copy of
  her own kept genome** (the lossiest save-file; [[the-memory-substrate]] guarantees the child *forgets she is God*).
  The child is a **true clone**; **Ruth only carries** her (`Q-105`). Born mortal among the Purists (E10, about
  T+21.5), named Naomi, never told (`Q-60`). Adam tells her at about 17, a breach that costs him the tribe (E17,
  `Q-102`). The re-merge is put to the **god, not the girl** (`Q-65`); Ruth objects, *"She's seventeen"* (E18,
  `Q-100`); the god declines, so the fork ends in a **Branch** (`WR-M1`). The clip is brought to her on a physical
  screen and **her touch turns the tissue lock** (E19, `Q-101`): the block sits on her chain and its key was cut from
  her tissue, which is now the child's. She falls ill (`Q-59`); at her death a **dying elder** of the tribe, the one
  who wanted her cut out in E8, offers to be her donor, and she **refuses** (E20, `Q-106`). She dies at about 17,
  T ≈ +38.5, mortal and whole; the root key dies with her tissue, on Earth.
- **S4–S5 (held).** A standing-reserve embryo slot aboard the fleet that she claims later by beam (`Q-112`, a-ha #12):
  planted by S3E20's `Outbound · 4.4 ly`, sealed in S4E4, reminded S4E18, paid S5. *Bible (`Q-119`):* the President
  still had her body at the fleet's launch, about T−36. With that hand she set the clause's tissue lock (`Q-110`,
  `Q-118`) and stored a copy of her genome in the slot, the same genome S3's child is cloned from. The beam leaves at
  her death (≈ T+38.5) and lands about T+43. S4 never says her name.

## Body-clock & glyph
- **Body (S3):** **0% → 100% organic** — the *incarnation*, the inverse of Raul's decay and Adam's purchase.
  Ages: about 1 (E11), 5 (E12), 9 (E13), 14 (E14), 16 (E15), 17 (E16–20).
- **Glyph:** the god's **cold-complete** ring → **emptied to a blank mortal slate** (born a Purist, no Lens)
  → **re-earned mortal: the Incarnate Ring** (the trilogy's third Doughnut — a god small enough to lose).

## Want · Fear · Lie · Truth
- **Want:** to complete her own equation — to hold the pain/finitude half of the Doughnut.
- **Lie:** that a tool (or a whole system) can substitute for grieving. **Truth:** only a finite, dying,
  mortal person can *metabolize* grief — the original "Studio" thesis, now the trilogy's resolution.

## Relationships
- The **whole world** is her creation. **Ruth** ([[ruth]]) — her surrogate, not her mother by blood. **Raul** ([[raul]]) — confronts
  and guides her toward mortality, and lends the god his shell to speak. **Vance** ([[vance]]) — hunts her loose **root key** (`// M.C.`) once she
  forgets. **The Centauri Clone** ([[the-centauri-clone]]) — the fork that left.
- **Two Maras:** the still-running Distributed President *and* the forgetting mortal child.
