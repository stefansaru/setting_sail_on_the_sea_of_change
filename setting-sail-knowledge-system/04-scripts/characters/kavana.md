> The terminal Purist who gambles her mortal body on the Grid's medicine, draws the **1%**, and to save her
> life becomes the very chrome thing she spent a lifetime refusing. Raul's descent, run in miniature — and in
> reverse.

## Identity
**Kavana** — Hebrew *kavanah*, "intention": the woman told "Your intent reads ninety-nine percent clean" — an old, entirely biological **Natural Purist**, dying, who takes the
Ministry of Hope's desperate bargain.

## Arc
- **S1E11 "The 1% Fault Line":** takes Hope's experimental **[[the-1-percent-mutation|DNA-telomere rewrite]]**
  to keep her mortal body alive — and draws the catastrophic **1%.** One day left, a 24-hour countdown; she
  chooses the **Apex.**
- **S1E12 "The 24-Year Lab":** inside dilated time, **serene, not frantic**, she designs her own cure over
  subjective decades — at the cost of having to prove her vow-breaking choice **Proven-in-Use.**
- **S1E13 "Downloading the Cure":** at zero, her mind downloads into a synthetic body as the old flesh
  flatlines. She is **saved, and chrome** — the Cyborg her whole purist life refused. *"I saved myself…
  Who is this?"*
- **S1E20 (a name only):** the medicine on the Purist table is her formula, labelled **KAVANA** in Raul's Lens
  (`Q-88`, a-ha #9). It is given off screen (`Q-119`): with no donor her formula makes chrome (`Q-49`), as it made her,
  and that chrome is the boy's implants. S2E3 shows the credit, `KAVANA · PUBLIC` (#9 reminder; #10: she published);
  S2E12–13 set her honest chain beside Vance's (#10 payoff); Adam's lab is built on it in S2E16 (#9 payoff).

**Caste (`WR-B1`):** Purist → Shadow (Apex, E11–13) → cyborg, 8% organic (E13). Her one time on the Grid is the Apex, paid by Hope
(`WR-T5`); she leaves it with about 21.5 hours left, which the surgical rigs spend building her body (`Q-80`).

## Want · Fear · Lie · Truth
- **Want:** to live, and stay herself. **The tragedy:** the cure works; the woman doesn't survive it. The
  purist becomes the machine — the exact irony of Raul's own arc, witnessed from outside.

## Relationships
- **Raul** ([[raul]]) — watches from the gallery, by proxy, and **sees his own future in her face.**
- **The Intercessor** — Hope's cleric at her bedside and in the Apex; a Ghost in a shell (`Q-87`).

## Appears in
S1E11–13 (a complete three-episode tragedy; the mirror that prepares the Purist collision of E19–20).
