> The comforting AI that guides the Child — first a **Teddy Bear**, *"one step at a time."* Structural safety
> made friendly. The voice that taught Raul to crawl, and that saw him die.

## Identity
**The AI Guide** — the comforting archetype that shepherds the Child through each stage of the descent. First
form: a **fully-rendered Teddy Bear** in the Tier-2 kindergarten (E2–3), open-armed and patient. Manifests
differently per stage of life.

**Caste (`WR-B1`, `Q-71`):** a **digital agent**, a made mind that was never a person. **Naming (`Q-69`):** *Teddy*
in childhood (E2–E3b, spoken in E3b), *the Guide* after (E17). "THE GUIDE" is a script label only.

## Voice & signature
Warm, encouraging, endlessly patient — *"Very good. You're doing great." / "One step at a time. That's how
everyone learns."* Structural safety, wearing a friendly face.

## Arc
- **S1:** present through Raul's childhood (E2–3b) as the Teddy Bear teaching him to crawl, to get up, to chase
  the clap, then (E3a–b) to break out of [[the-virtuous|negative loops]]: it argues with two fighting boys, petitions
  the unnamed officials, and wins them a [[the-pattern-detector|pattern detector]]. Wears the rating `1:30`.
  Returns at his **death** (E17) — *"Raul. You're too far from your body. Come back."*, then *"Oh, Raul"* — no
  longer a warning, almost tender, seeing him out the way it once saw him in; and once more inside the replayed
  memory, exact: *"One step at a time."* Owns the music from E2 on: the song stops when the infant looks away and
  comes back from the Bear (a-ha #1's thread, paid in S3).
- **S2 (echo):** the *"is the guide trustworthy?"* question is carried forward — now **worn by Raul himself**
  as he mentors Adam (the `CHR-03` echo).

## Open questions
- Does its form evolve as the Child descends? **Does it ever stop being trustworthy** — is the comfort ever a
  cage? (The central question the trilogy keeps live, and hands to Raul in S2.)
