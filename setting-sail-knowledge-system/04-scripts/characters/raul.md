> The single consciousness the whole trilogy is built around — **"the Unsynced Mind."** He descends the
> velocity tiers from a child in the capsule to a deathless Ghost, and in losing his entire body he learns
> the one thing the Grid can never optimize: how to hold joy and pain at once.

## Identity
**Raul** — from the Old Germanic/Norse *Radulfr*, "wolf counsel / wise advisor": the unsynced mind who must
*earn* the wisdom (and the merit) to update the world's ledger. Short, phonetically sharp, international for
vertical media. Stays warmly, recognizably human even when his shell is 0% organic. Archetype: **"The
Unsynced Mind" / the Child** — always starting at a disadvantage, mid-transition, sorting his own values
from hardcoded programming.

## Arc
- **Season 1 (lead) — the descent (Tier 3 → 0).** The child in the capsule (E1) → infant consciousness waking
  (E2) → child taught to chase the metric (E3, E3a–b, E4) → adult down-clocked out of his job (E5) → the **Shadow
  reveal** and the caste fault line (E6–9) → **the Cracking of the Sky** (E10: solar storm cooks his body,
  first amputation, he becomes a cyborg) → walks into Hope on his own, a voluntary Seeker (`Q-09`), and witnesses Kavana's tragedy and **forks himself** for deep space
  (E11–16) → **dies and becomes a Ghost**, snapping to the eidetic curse (E17) → steps onto the real surface
  among the Purists (E18–19) → **holds the mother's paradox** at the finale, his glyph at last solid (E20).
- **Season 2 (guide).** A 0%-organic **Ghost in a shell**, embedded with the Purists, mentoring **Adam** as
  the Teddy-Bear once mentored him — except Raul has *been* to the bottom and back. The held question: *is
  his counsel fully trustworthy?*
- **Season 3 (lead).** Confronts **Mara's** incarnation; counsels the god toward staying mortal; and the
  trust question is **answered** — he was right.

## Body-clock & glyph
- **Body (S1):** 100% organic (Act I) → **70%** (E10 amputation) → **30%** (E14 neural-synthetic upgrade) →
  **0% Ghost** (E17 death). See [[progressive-digitalization]].
- **Caste (`WR-B1`):** Shadow (E1–E9) → cyborg (E10, at Tier 0 from here, `Q-46`, save about four real days at Tier 1 in his Earth capsule to pack for the E14 beam, `Q-124`) → Ghost (E17) → Ghost in a shell
  (E18). At Tier 0 he attends real places by **leased proxy** (E7, E8, E11–13, E14, E17; `Q-86`); the proxy he dies
  in at E17 is the shell he walks out in at E18.
- **Body clock (real time):** about 20 real hours at E3a–b (he is 8 inside); 14 weeks at E6 under 34 lived years;
  about 6 months at E14 (T = 0); about 8.7 real years at E16 (T+8.2); about 9 at his death (T+8.5). See
  [[the-quickening]] and the T table in [[the-seed-fleet]].
- **The Lens (`Q-119`):** on from E2. In E19 he swipes it off, and the alignment setting with it. In E20 he turns
  the Lens back on, by his own hand, to read the vial for the mother (`KAVANA`); the alignment stays off for good.
  It stays on through S2 (the third glyph, S2E18) and S3 (every UI beat on Ruth and Mara). Adam mirrors him: he
  switches his own off at his mother's bedside in S2E19 and keeps it off.
- **Parallel Pedagogy (`Q-119`, `WLD-18`):** E3a–b are the nursery's last days, so Raul is still one child there. He
  forks into Alpha, Beta and Gamma on leaving it, just after E3b, at about 8; the detector won in E3b and the alignment, on by default, acting on it for the first time
  go into all three threads. E4 follows one thread, unlabelled. The Merging falls between E4 and E5 and issues the
  hollow glyph. The forks are never named in S1 (HELD to S2E9).
- **Glyph:** the inherited **hollow "Camel's Doughnut"** (graduation) → **fractured** (E10) → first
  un-forced **steadiness** (E16, the Branch) → **SOLID / earned** (E20). One evolving icon carries the arc.

## Want · Fear · Lie · Truth
- **Want:** to grow, to meet the world. **Fear:** shifts as the tiers harden.
- **Lie:** that optimization, speed, and safety are the point. **Truth:** the [[the-macro-doughnut|Arrow of
  Balance]] — you hold joy *and* pain; you don't collapse two truths into one.

## Voice & signature
Sparse, warm, increasingly quiet as he chromes over. His defining move is **refusing to optimize** — he
ends S1 not by solving the mother's paradox but by *staying inside it* with her.

## Appears in
All three seasons (lead in S1 & S3, guide in S2).
