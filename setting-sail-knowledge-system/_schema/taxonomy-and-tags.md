# Taxonomy & Tag Glossary

The controlled vocabulary for the codex. Keep it small and disciplined — tags are
for retrieval, not decoration. Propose a new tag only when an existing one cannot
carry the meaning, and add it here when you do.

## Page types
- **principle** — irreducible first principle. Lives in `principles/`.
- **concept** — a named idea/model/distinction. `concepts/`. Has a `tier`.
- **technique** — a concrete practice with steps. `techniques/`.
- **metaphor** — story / mascot / image. `metaphors/`.
- **influence** — a thinker or source. `influences/`.

## Concept tiers
- **major** — load-bearing; the worldview leans on it.
- **supporting** — refines, qualifies, or serves a major concept.

## Status ladder
- **seed** — title + a line; a placeholder a `[[link]]` pointed to.
- **draft** — all required sections present, rough.
- **stable** — reviewed, sourced, cross-linked.
- **synthesis** — **not stated in the book.** Analysis layered on top that resolves a
  logged [[contradictions|contradiction]] or fills a [[gaps|gap]], usually by adding a
  *missing dimension* (stage / time horizon / context / level of abstraction). Must carry
  a banner declaring it derived, and cite the source §§ of *both* poles it reconciles.
  Keeps the archival layer honest: the book's tension stays logged; the reconciliation is
  marked as ours.

## The four strata (architectural tags)
Every page is tagged with the stratum of the worldview spine it belongs to.
See `01-codex/maps/worldview-spine.md`.

- **stratum/inheritance** — what we are given/programmed by (evolution, society, the subconscious, inherited emotion).
- **stratum/liberation** — seeing the programming and gaining distance (observing, diffusing, debiasing, decluttering).
- **stratum/authorship** — creating your own values, goals, finances, use of time & energy.
- **stratum/meaning** — orienting the whole toward purpose and contribution.

## The Nietzschean arc (developmental tags)
- **arc/camel** — inherited burden, conformity, "thou shalt."
- **arc/lion** — rebellion, negation, saying no.
- **arc/child** — creation, play, self-authored values.
