> Chase happiness and it flees; pursue meaning and contentment follows. Lasting fulfillment comes not from what you consume but from what you contribute and the mission you serve.

## The mode of thinking
Happiness is a fickle, adapting set-point; meaning is durable. The corpus repeatedly points
away from pleasure/achievement-as-destination and toward purpose, contribution, and process.
Meaning is *created*, not found — and it's bigger than any single goal.

## Contradictions / open tensions
- Money's role resolved at [[the-money-happiness-curve]]: money buys freedom from suffering, not meaning.
- **Failure mode:** "find your one grand Mission" pressure; meaning also lives in daily relationships and the [[the-journey-is-the-destination|journey]].

## How to apply it
1. Ask of a pursuit: *does this nourish (meaning) or just fill (pleasure/status)?*
2. Tie goals to a mission and values; savor the process, not only the milestone.
3. Shift effort from consumption to contribution where you can.
