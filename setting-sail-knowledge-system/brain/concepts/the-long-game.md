> Trade smaller-sooner for larger-later. Be patient with what needs time, build redundancy so no single failure ruins you, and remember the clock is finite — so start now.

## The mode of thinking
Two forces pull against each other: we over-discount the future ([[hyperbolic-discounting]]),
yet our time is finite ([[memento-mori]]). The resolution is *patient urgency* — act now, but
play for compounding payoffs: plant the seed and leave it in the ground, diversify so setbacks
aren't fatal, and judge by process, not lucky outcomes.

## Contradictions / open tensions
- Patience vs. quitting: persist with compounding that's working; cut genuine dead-ends ([[sunk-cost-fallacy]]).
- **Failure mode:** "long game" as procrastination ("someday") — finitude ([[memento-mori]]) is the counterweight.

## How to apply it
1. Make the future vivid; use commitment devices (automatic savings, deadlines).
2. Optimize for compounding (habits, skills, relationships, investments), not quick wins.
3. Build redundancy so one failure can't end the game; then keep showing up.
