> Emotions are signals and editable representations, not commands. You can process the past, soften a memory's charge, and choose your state — be the thermostat, not the thermometer.

## The mode of thinking
You are not at the mercy of your feelings. Much fear is old memory replaying; much suffering
is resistance to pain. With the observer stance plus concrete tools, you can metabolize
emotional baggage, reduce a memory's intensity, and deliberately set your state — turning
emotion from a driver into a dashboard.

## How to apply it
1. When a feeling spikes, ask: *is this about now, or a memory replaying?*
2. Soften a painful memory (dim it, distance the sound) and re-title it with its lesson.
3. Pre-build state anchors / playlists; choose your temperature before the day sets it.
