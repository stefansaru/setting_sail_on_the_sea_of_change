> Power lives in your response, not your circumstances. Sort the world into what you control and what you don't — and invest only in the former.

## The mode of thinking
The single most central idea in the corpus so far: between what happens to you and what you
do about it lies a gap, and in that gap is your freedom. You can't choose the wind, the
economy, other people, or the past — but you choose your interpretation, your response, and
your direction. Mastery is relocating effort from the uncontrollable to the controllable.

## Contradictions / open tensions
- The deep tension with **acceptance** is resolved at [[acceptance-and-change]]: you accept
  what's outside control *and* change your response — different objects, not a contradiction.
- **Failure mode:** over-extended into hyper-responsibility / self-blame for genuine misfortune,
  luck, or injustice. Agency is not omnipotence (see [[swimmers-body-illusion]]).

## How to apply it
1. When distressed, ask: *what here is up to me, and what isn't?*
2. Spend zero energy on the "not me" column; act on the "me" column (response, attitude, next step).
3. Convert uncontrollable wishes into controllable objectives ([[wishlist-to-objectives]]).
