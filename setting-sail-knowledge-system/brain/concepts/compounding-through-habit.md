> Big outcomes are the compounding of tiny, repeated actions. Don't rely on motivation — design systems so the right action is easy, then show up consistently until it's automatic.

## The mode of thinking
You don't rise to your goals; you fall to your systems. Identity and life are built by small
actions repeated — installed into the automatic mind through repetition. The leverage points
are *starting small*, *designing the environment*, and *consistency over intensity*.

## Contradictions / open tensions
- Tension with [[consistency-beats-restarting]] vs. knowing when to quit ([[sunk-cost-fallacy]]):
  persist with slow-but-working systems; abandon genuinely failing ones.
- **Failure mode:** "tiny habits" as an excuse to avoid a needed big, discontinuous change.

## How to apply it
1. Shrink the habit to a 2-minute version; attach it to an existing routine (stacking).
2. Design the environment: make the good cue obvious, the bad one hard.
3. Optimize for showing up daily, not for intensity; let it compound.
