> Thinking in face value (nominal) instead of purchasing power (real) — making you feel richer or poorer than you are.

## What it is
Ignoring inflation when judging money. "Real wealth is measured in what your money can do,
not what it says on paper."

## How it misleads
- **Wages:** a 5% raise with 5% inflation = no real gain.
- **Investing:** a 3% return with 2% inflation = only 1% real.
- **Savings:** low-interest "safe" accounts quietly losing to inflation ([[zero-risk-bias]]).
- **Real estate:** a house that "doubled" over 20 years while costs of living also doubled.

## How to counter it
- Check **real** value, not just nominal numbers.
- Adjust for inflation on raises, savings, and investments.
- Ask: *"What can this money actually buy today?"*
