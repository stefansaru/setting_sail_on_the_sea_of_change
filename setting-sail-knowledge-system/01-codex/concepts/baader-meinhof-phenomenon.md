> Notice something for the first time, then suddenly see it everywhere — nothing changed except your awareness.

## What it is
The frequency illusion: heightened attention masquerading as a real increase in frequency.
"What you focus on feels bigger, even if the world hasn't changed."

## How it misleads
- **Products:** learn about a car model, then "see it on every street."
- **Language:** a new word that suddenly "pops up everywhere."
- **Social issues:** once you notice a topic, it appears in every headline and post.
- **Law/politics:** repeating terms so they feel more frequent and important.

## How to counter it
- Remember: "noticing more doesn't mean something is happening more."
- Check real data before assuming frequency changed.
- Be aware of how repetition shapes perception.
