> Judging how likely something is by how easily examples come to mind — so vivid, dramatic events feel far more common than they are.

## What it is
Estimating probability from memorability. "What grabs attention isn't what matters most."

## How it misleads
- **Travel:** fearing flying after a crash on the news, while driving (riskier) feels safe.
- **Health:** worrying about shark attacks/rare diseases while ignoring heart disease and smoking.
- **Investing:** piling into a stock because of media hype, not statistics.
- **Law:** one shocking story outweighing dry but decisive data.

## How to counter it
- Ask: *"Is this risk memorable, or actually common?"*
- Seek **hard data** over what sticks in memory.
- Remember dramatic ≠ likely.
