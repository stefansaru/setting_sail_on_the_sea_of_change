> Resisting something *just because* you feel your freedom is being limited — even when the request makes sense.

## What it is
Push-back triggered by perceived control, not by the merits. "Reactance isn't about the
request — it's about freedom." (The shadow side of the [[lion-phase]]'s "no.")

## How it misleads
- **Marketing:** "act now or miss out forever" backfires — people walk away to prove they're free.
- **Parenting:** "be home by 9" → staying out later to resist the rule.
- **Public health:** refusing measures because they feel forced, not on the evidence.
- **Workplace:** micromanaged employees underperforming to push back.

## How to counter it
- Frame **choices**, not commands — people respond to options.
- Explain the *why* behind a rule, not just the rule ([[explain-dont-force]]).
- Notice when your resistance is about control, not the actual issue.
