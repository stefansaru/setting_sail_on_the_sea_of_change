> Believing people notice you far more than they do — the spotlight is mostly in your head.

## What it is
Overestimating how much others observe and judge you. The Cornell "Barry Manilow T-shirt"
study: students wearing an embarrassing shirt thought ~half would notice; only ~20% did.

## How it misleads
- Spill coffee on your shirt and feel everyone's staring (they aren't, or forget fast).
- A stumble in a presentation feels huge to you, barely registers to the audience.
- Fearing judgment on a post most people just scroll past.

## How to counter it
- Remind yourself: "people aren't watching as closely as you think" — they're busy with their own "coffee stains."
- Take the risk — speak up, share, wear what you like.
- "Stop shrinking your dreams to fit imagined criticism."
