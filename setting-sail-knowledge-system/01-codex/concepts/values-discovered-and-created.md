> **Synthesis page — not in the book.** The book says values are *listened for* (discovered) and also *created* (child phase). Both are true at different **stages** and **levels of abstraction**: you discover values as signals; you create values as commitments.

## The polarity (C1)
- **Pole A — Discovered:** "the answer comes from your inner voice"; a value is "a quiet whisper… that resonates deeply" ([[inner-voice]], [[defining-your-own-values]]).
- **Pole B — Created:** "the child *creates* new values" unlike the camel (accepts) or lion (rejects) ([[child-phase]], [[meaning-is-created-not-found]]).

## The missing dimensions: stage + level of abstraction
Three stages, operating at two levels:
1. **Discovery (signals — bottom-up).** You *notice* what resonates: what angers you, inspires you, feels meaningful. You don't manufacture these; you observe them. (This is the [[inner-voice]].)
2. **Selection.** Many values resonate, and you can't serve them all equally. You *choose* which to honor — the first authored act.
3. **Creation (hierarchy — top-down).** You build an *ordering*: e.g. `family > career > money`, or `truth > comfort > popularity`. **The hierarchy is the part you author.** Two people can discover near-identical signals and construct completely different lives from how they rank them.

So discovery supplies the raw *data* (which values exist in you); creation supplies the
*structure* (how they're prioritized into a livable code).

## The synthesis
> **Values are discovered as signals, and created as commitments.**

What you care about is found; what you *do* about competing cares is authored. This also
sharpens [[defining-your-own-values]]: "strip away external expectation and listen"
(discovery) *and* "write them as your present-tense code of conduct" (creation/hierarchy).

## Why it matters
It dissolves a tension that otherwise makes the book's value-theory feel inconsistent, and
it gives a clearer practice: **listen** for resonance, then **rank** into a hierarchy you
own. The ranking — not the raw feelings — is where self-authorship actually happens.

## Opposing ideas & tensions
- **Discovered signals can be distorted** by conditioning, trauma, or bias (cf.
  [[inner-voice]] / [[cognitive-bias]]); "what resonates" isn't automatically wise — the
  selection/creation stages must include scrutiny, not just feeling.
- Hierarchies aren't fixed; they get re-authored as you grow ([[values-alignment-in-relationships]] notes values shift over time).

## Practical examples
- Discovery: you notice unfairness genuinely angers you (a justice signal).
- Creation: you decide justice ranks above comfort but below your family's safety — that ordering is yours, and it shapes very different choices than someone who ranks them oppositely.

## Common mistakes
- Treating raw resonance as a finished value (skipping selection/hierarchy).
- Believing values are *purely* invented (ignoring the given signals) or *purely* found (ignoring the authored ranking).
