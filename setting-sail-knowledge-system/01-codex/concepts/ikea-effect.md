> Overvaluing something simply because you helped create it — effort breeds attachment, even when the result isn't great.

## What it is
Inflated valuation of self-made things. "True judgment separates the work you put in from
the worth it actually has."

## How it misleads
- **DIY:** loving the wobbly furniture you assembled.
- **Cooking:** your homemade meal tasting better to you than to guests.
- **Workplace:** overrating your own ideas because you invested time in them.
- **Law:** clinging to arguments you built even as the case weakens.

## How to counter it
- Ask: *"Am I valuing this because it's good, or because I made it?"*
- Seek outside feedback — others see more clearly.
- Enjoy the pride of creation, but check if the outcome stands on its own.
