> Bad events and emotions hit harder than good ones — a single harsh comment can outweigh ten kind words.

## What it is
Asymmetric weighting of negative over positive. (Partly an evolutionary [[vestigial-mind-wirings|wiring]] — missing a threat once cost more than missing a treat.) "Balance comes from
noticing the good with equal weight."

## How it misleads
- **News:** disasters and scandals dominate because bad news grabs attention.
- **Relationships:** one harsh comment lingers longer than ten compliments.
- **Workplace:** fixating on a single criticism in a glowing review.
- **Law:** shocking negative details remembered more vividly than neutral facts.

## How to counter it
- Count positives as **deliberately** as negatives.
- Remind yourself one bad event doesn't erase the good.
- Limit constant negative-news exposure to protect perspective.
