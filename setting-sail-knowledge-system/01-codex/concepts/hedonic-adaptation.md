> Big changes — good or bad — move your happiness only for a while; you return to your baseline. Happiness lives in how you live, not in things.

## What it is
The drift back to a happiness set-point after highs and lows. "Happiness doesn't live in
things. It lives in how you live."

## How it misleads
- **Money:** a raise or new car thrills briefly, then fades to normal.
- **Relationships:** the thrill of new romance settles as routine takes over.
- **Adversity:** people often recover emotionally even after serious setbacks.
- **Law:** assuming large damages will permanently "make someone whole."

## How to counter it
- Don't chase endless upgrades — the high fades back.
- Invest in experiences, growth, and relationships ([[the-will-to-meaning]]) over possessions.
- "Happiness is more about perspective than possessions."
