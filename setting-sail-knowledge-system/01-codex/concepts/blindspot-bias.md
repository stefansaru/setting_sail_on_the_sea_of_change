> Spotting bias in everyone but yourself — assuming you're the objective one in the room.

## What it is
The meta-bias: failing to see your own biases while readily seeing others'. It's why merely
*knowing* the bias list doesn't debias you (see [[cognitive-bias]] tensions).

## How it misleads
- **Workplace:** a manager sure their own calls are fair while flagging colleagues' favoritism.
- **Academia:** a researcher decrying peers' bias while ignoring flaws in their own methods.
- **Social:** noticing a friend's snap judgments, missing your own.
- **Law:** judges/lawyers believing themselves perfectly neutral.

## How to counter it
- Accept that *everyone, including you*, is biased.
- Ask for feedback from people who see things differently.
- Check your own reasoning as critically as you check others'.

- [[becoming-the-observer]] — watching your *own* mind, not just others'.
