> Judging a person by the group they belong to instead of who they actually are — easy, lazy, and often wrong.

## What it is
Assuming an individual's traits from a category. "Groups don't define people — individuals
do." (Roots partly in the ancient in-group/out-group [[vestigial-mind-wirings|wiring]].)

## How it misleads
- **Workplace:** young = "tech-savvy," older = "slow to adapt."
- **Education:** lowered expectations by background, gender, or ethnicity — shaping outcomes.
- **Healthcare:** assuming some groups "tolerate pain better" or "exaggerate symptoms."
- **Media/law:** narrow group portrayals; juries swayed by category instead of case facts.

## How to counter it
- Look for evidence about the **person**, not the category.
- Ask: *"What do I actually know, and what am I assuming?"*
- See the individual in front of you, not the label.
