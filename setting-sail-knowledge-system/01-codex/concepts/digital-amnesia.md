> Forgetting information because you know you can look it up — your brain outsources memory to the internet.

## What it is
Reduced recall from reliance on devices. "The sharpest mind is one that remembers enough
without reaching for the phone."

## How it misleads / manifests
- **Education:** "I can Google it later" → weaker deeper understanding.
- **Workplace:** relying on search for details instead of learning them (risky if access fails).
- **Personal:** birthdays, numbers, addresses vanishing because apps store them.
- **Navigation:** losing the ability to read maps because GPS handles it.

## How to counter it
- Balance convenience with practice — memorize facts you use often.
- Use tech as **support**, not a substitute for all memory.
- Train recall: write, repeat, review without devices.
