> Thinking more people agree with you than really do — your views feel "normal," but often they aren't.

## What it is
Overestimating how widely your opinions are shared. "Silence is not the same as agreement."

## How it misleads
- **Politics:** a politician mistakes cheering supporters for the majority.
- **Business:** a manager reads no complaints as support for a new rule.
- **Social:** "everyone loves this playlist" — half the party didn't, but no one spoke up.
- **Law/debate:** "everyone knows this" usually means "a few people who agree with me."

## How to counter it
- Check assumptions — **ask, don't guess**.
- Seek out different opinions, even uncomfortable ones.
- Remember silence ≠ agreement.
