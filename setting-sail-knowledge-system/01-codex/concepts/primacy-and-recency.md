> You remember the first thing (primacy) and the last thing (recency) best — the middle gets lost.

## What it is
A memory bias toward the beginning and end of a sequence. "What sticks isn't what's actually
the most important."

## How it misleads
- **Interviews:** first impression (primacy) + closing moments (recency) outweigh the middle.
- **Presentations:** people recall the opening and closing more than the detailed charts.
- **Advertising:** key slogans placed at start and end.
- **Law:** lawyers put their strongest points first and last.

## How to use / counter it
- When **speaking**: place your most important points at the start and end.
- When **listening**: pay extra attention to the middle; it's easy to forget.
- Review key info afterward to reinforce what fades.
