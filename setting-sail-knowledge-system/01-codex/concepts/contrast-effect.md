> Your judgment of something shifts depending on what it's next to — the same item looks better or worse by comparison.

## What it is
Relative, not absolute, judgment. "Real clarity means seeing the thing itself, not just its
neighbors."

## How it misleads
- **Shopping:** a $100 shirt looks dear alone, a bargain next to a $300 one.
- **Appearance:** someone seems more/less attractive depending on who's beside them.
- **Salaries:** a demand seems high or low depending on the comparison candidate.
- **Law:** showing weak evidence first to make the strong evidence look stronger.

## How to counter it
- Judge things on their **own merits**, not just in context.
- Ask: *"Would this seem the same without the comparison?"*
- Beware deliberate setups (decoys) designed to shift your view.
