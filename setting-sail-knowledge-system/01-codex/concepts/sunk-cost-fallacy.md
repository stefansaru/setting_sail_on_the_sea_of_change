> Throwing more time, money, or effort into a losing cause *because* you've already invested — even though past costs are gone either way.

## What it is
Letting irrecoverable past investment dictate future choices. "Past costs are gone. Only
future costs and benefits matter."

## How it misleads
- **Business:** funding a failing project because "we can't waste what we've spent."
- **Personal:** staying in an unhappy relationship over "five years already."
- **Career/education:** sticking with a degree/job you no longer want because of years invested.
- **Law/debate:** pursuing a weak case to justify resources already spent.

## How to counter it
- Ask: *"If I started today, knowing what I know now, would I choose this again?"*
- Remember past costs are gone regardless of what you do next.
- "Don't confuse persistence with wisdom — sometimes quitting is the smart move."
