> Blaming someone's character instead of their situation — "they're lazy/rude" — without asking what else might be going on.

## What it is
Over-attributing others' behavior to personality and under-weighting circumstance. "It makes
us harsh with others and gentle with ourselves."

## How it misleads
- **Workplace:** a missed deadline → "disorganized" (maybe it was an emergency).
- **Driving:** someone cuts you off → "reckless" (maybe rushing to a hospital).
- **Social:** a quiet friend → "unfriendly" (maybe just tired).
- **Law:** painting defendants as "bad people," ignoring context.

## How to counter it
- Pause before judging character: *"What else could explain this?"*
- Give weight to context — situations shape behavior more than we think.
- Extend others the benefit of the doubt you give yourself ([[self-compassion]]).
