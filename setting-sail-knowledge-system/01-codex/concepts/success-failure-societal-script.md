> From birth you're handed an unspoken script — be successful, don't fail, be "good" — that your System 1 absorbs from what gets clapped for and what gets side-eyed. But you can direct your story, not just act it.

## Definition
"From the start, you're handed a script: be successful, don't fail, and above all, be
'good.' This isn't said out loud — but it's there, in the way people react, in what gets
attention, in what gets ignored." [[system-1-and-system-2|System 1]] "picks up the
patterns": struggle is respected (pain "makes a better story than steady progress"),
sacrifice is admired more than self-care, success earns a "well done" plus "a quiet
expectation to keep doing more." The book lists the 3am consequences (§5.15): guilt for
success, fear of judgment, shame in asking for help, anxiety about failure, unhealthy
perfectionism, inferiority complex, fear of vulnerability, people-pleasing. The exit:
"you don't just act in your story — you direct it... your worth isn't tied to applause."

## Why it matters
It connects the cognitive machinery (System 1 pattern-absorption) to the
self-authorship arc: the script is how the [[camel-phase]] gets installed and how
[[internalizing-the-exterior|inherited values]] take hold. Naming the script — and the
specific anxieties it breeds — is the first step to [[becoming-the-observer|observing]]
and rewriting it.

## Opposing ideas & tensions
- **Some of the script is prosocial and worth keeping** — valuing kindness, effort, and
  responsibility isn't all toxic. The task is auditing, not blanket rejection.
- **"Direct your own story" can underweight real constraints** (economic, social) that
  limit how freely one can defy the script.

## Practical examples
- From the book: "fall, get back up, and you're praised; move forward without falling, and no one says much."
- Beyond the book: feeling guilty for taking a well-earned rest because the script equates worth with hustle.

## Common mistakes
- Mistaking the absorbed script for your own values.
- Tying self-worth to applause, hustle, or struggle.
- Not pausing System 2 long enough to even notice the script running.
