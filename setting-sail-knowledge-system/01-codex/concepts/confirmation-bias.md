> Seeking and trusting information that confirms what you already believe, while ignoring what contradicts it.

## What it is
A pull toward evidence that fits your existing view. In [[the-makeup-artist]] (Ch.4 §4.19):
the artist "sticks firmly to her belief — 'my skill is worth 800 rand' — and ignores that
flexibility might win more clients."

## How it misleads
- **Beliefs:** reading only sources that agree with you.
- **Business:** noticing data that supports your plan, discounting warnings.
- **Relationships:** collecting evidence for a suspicion you already hold.

## How to counter it
- Actively seek **disconfirming** evidence — argue the other side.
- Ask: *"What would change my mind, and have I looked for it?"*
- Invite critics ([[blindspot-bias]] reminds you that you have blind spots).

## Related concepts
- [[backfire-effect]] — confirmation bias hardened against contrary proof.
- [[status-quo-bias]] — both keep you anchored to the familiar/already-held.
