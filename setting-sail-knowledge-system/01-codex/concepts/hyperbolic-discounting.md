> Choosing a smaller reward now over a bigger reward later — the present feels far more real than the future. Procrastination is this bias in action.

## What it is
Steep discounting of future value. "Hyperbolic discounting tilts life toward the short-term."

## How it misleads
- **Money:** preferring $50 today over $100 in a year.
- **Health:** junk food's instant pleasure beating slow-building diet benefits.
- **Work/study:** procrastination — resting now over finishing early.
- **Environment:** convenience (plastic, disposables) over far-off sustainability.

## How to counter it
- Remind yourself of the bigger **future** gain; make it vivid (visualize it).
- Use **commitment devices** — deadlines, automatic savings, accountability.
- Shrink the first step ([[two-minute-rule]]) so the present cost feels tiny.
