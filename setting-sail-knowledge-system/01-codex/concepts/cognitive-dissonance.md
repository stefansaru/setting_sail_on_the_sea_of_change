> The discomfort of holding clashing beliefs — or acting against your values. To ease it, we usually change our *story*, not our behavior.

## What it is
Tension between belief and action (or two beliefs). "We'll go far to avoid the discomfort —
often patching the gap with excuses instead of closing it with honesty."

## How it misleads
- **Everyday:** "I value health" + daily junk food → "I'll start next week."
- **Money:** "I'm responsible" + overspending → "it was on sale, so I saved."
- **Relationships:** wanting honesty while hiding parts of yourself.
- **Politics:** disliking corruption but excusing it — "at least they get things done."

## How to counter it
- Notice when your story doesn't match your reality.
- **Either change the action or admit the conflict** — don't paper over it.
- "Close the gap with honesty," not rationalization.
