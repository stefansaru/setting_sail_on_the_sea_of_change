> Shown clear evidence against a belief, people sometimes cling to it *harder* — because identity and emotion outweigh facts.

## What it is
Contradictory evidence strengthening rather than softening a belief. "Truth isn't just
about facts — it's about how people feel when they hear them."

## How it misleads
- **Politics:** proof a favored politician lied → "the media is biased," trust deepens.
- **Health:** safety studies on vaccines read as part of a "cover-up."
- **History:** uncomfortable facts about one's country → defending the old story harder.
- **Law:** strong counter-evidence making committed jurors feel attacked, not persuaded.

## How to counter it
- Don't flood people with facts — start with **empathy and respect**.
- Ask questions instead of lecturing.
- Recognize identity/emotion often matter more than data ([[explain-dont-force]]).
