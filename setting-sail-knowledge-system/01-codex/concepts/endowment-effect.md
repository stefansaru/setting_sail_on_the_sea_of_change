> Valuing something more just because you own it — possession inflates worth in your eyes.

## What it is
Ownership raising perceived value. "Clarity comes from judging as if you were an outsider."

## How it misleads
- **Consumer:** buy a mug for $5, then refuse to sell it for $15.
- **Real estate:** asking far above market because of memories/emotions.
- **Business:** overvaluing owned assets in a merger.
- **Personal:** keeping clothes, gadgets, "junk" because "it's mine."

## How to counter it
- Ask: *"If I didn't own this, how much would I pay for it?"*
- Compare to market value, not personal value.
- Remember ownership ≠ increased worth.
