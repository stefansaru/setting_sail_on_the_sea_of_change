> Beginners overestimate their skill; experts underestimate theirs. Confidence is loudest where competence is thinnest.

## What it is
A mismatch between confidence and competence: "the less you know, the easier it is to
believe you know enough."

## How it misleads
- **Learning:** a chess novice sure they can beat veterans, not yet seeing the depth.
- **Workplace:** the inexperienced pushing for promotion while a skilled colleague doubts themselves.
- **Students:** light studiers feeling ready; top students worrying because they grasp the complexity.
- **Social media/law:** shallow confidence sounding convincing to non-experts.

## How to counter it
- "Confidence isn't the same as competence."
- Seek feedback; measure skills against real standards.
- "If you feel like an imposter, you may actually know more than you think."
