> Too many options make us less satisfied, not more — abundance brings pressure, doubt, and regret.

## What it is
Overchoice reducing satisfaction. "Freedom can backfire. Fewer options often bring more
peace."

## How it misleads
- **Shopping:** a stand with 6 jams sells more than one with 30 (overwhelm).
- **Streaming:** endless scrolling → paralysis; more browsing than watching.
- **Careers:** hundreds of paths making commitment harder, fearing a missed better option.
- **Law:** too many arguments leaving a jury confused, not persuaded.

## How to counter it
- Set clear **criteria** before choosing.
- Limit options to a manageable number.
- Accept that no choice is perfect — and that's okay.
