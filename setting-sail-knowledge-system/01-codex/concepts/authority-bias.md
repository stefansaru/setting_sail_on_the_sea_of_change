> Trusting an opinion just because it comes from an "expert" or someone in power — without checking the actual evidence.

## What it is
Letting status stand in for proof. "Authority bias makes power sound like proof."

## How it misleads
- **Advertising:** actors dressed as dentists recommending toothpaste.
- **Finance:** following a TV "expert" whose advice doesn't fit your situation.
- **Workplace:** agreeing with a manager's poor decision because challenging feels risky.
- **Healthcare/law:** accepting treatments or trusting "expert witnesses" by title alone.

## How to counter it
- Respect authority, but don't follow blindly.
- Ask for **reasons and evidence**, not names and titles.
- Remember: "experts can be wrong, and authority can be misused."
