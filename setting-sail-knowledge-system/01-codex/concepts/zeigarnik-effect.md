> Unfinished or interrupted tasks nag at the mind more than completed ones — an open loop stays "alive" until closed.

## What it is
Better recall of incomplete tasks. "Completing tasks, even small ones, clears the mental
clutter."

## How it misleads / manifests
- **Workplace:** an interrupted report keeps intruding on your thoughts.
- **Studying:** material from incomplete sessions recalled more vividly.
- **Everyday:** you forget finished chores but keep remembering the unpaid bill.
- **Law:** leaving a jury with an unresolved question keeps them thinking about it.

## How to use / counter it
- Use **lists** — writing tasks down reduces the mental tension.
- Finish small tasks quickly to free mental space (pairs with the [[two-minute-rule]]).
- Notice how much energy open loops consume.
