> When a group wants harmony so badly it stops questioning ideas — the need to agree beats the need to be right.

## What it is
Consensus without critical thinking. "Silence looks like agreement, but it's really fear of
standing out."

## How it misleads
- **Politics:** weak policies pushed through because no one challenges the leader.
- **Business:** bad products launched because no one wanted to "kill the vibe."
- **Social:** risky plans unopposed; everyone later admits private doubts.
- **Law/debate:** juries caving to one dominant voice instead of weighing evidence.

## How to counter it
- Ask: *"What are we missing? What's the other side?"*
- Appoint a **devil's advocate** to challenge on purpose.
- "Welcome disagreement as a tool, not a threat."
