> Assuming that more effort means more value — confusing hours and exhaustion with quality and impact.

## What it is
The belief that the longer/harder something was, the better it must be. (The book covers it
twice — §7.10 and §7.23.) "Busy is noise. Effective is music."

## How it misleads
- **Art:** an identical painting rated more beautiful when said to take 26 hours vs. 4.
- **Workplace:** bragging about 12-hour days and back-to-back meetings as if exhaustion proved worth.
- **School:** a 20-page essay assumed better than a tighter 10-page one.
- **Personal:** valuing a handmade gift purely for the effort.

## How to counter it
- Measure work by **results**, not time: "did this make an impact, or just make me tired?"
- Stop treating exhaustion as proof of success.
- Celebrate impact, not busyness.
