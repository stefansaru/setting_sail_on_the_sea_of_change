> A private inner place no one else can enter — a cabin, an island, a spaceship — that you visit to recharge, and also use as a rehearsal room: "imagination is practice for reality."

## Definition
"There's a place inside you that no one else can enter... your temple of the mind. You
don't need to build it — it's already there." A vividly imagined safe place (the author's
is "a cabin on top of a quiet mountain"; yours might be a tropical island, a spaceship, a
castle armory). It serves two purposes: a **reset button** to recharge, and a **rehearsal
space** to design and pre-live what you want ("want a new home? go to your temple and
design it... imagination is practice for reality").

## Why it works
A richly detailed mental sanctuary reliably shifts your state (a deliberate, place-based
[[anchoring|anchor]]) and gives the [[becoming-the-observer|observer]] somewhere to stand,
apart from the day's noise. Mental rehearsal in vivid detail primes the brain for real
action — "to remind your brain what matters."

## Steps
1. **Choose your place** — somewhere that "feels safe... like home." A cabin, island, spaceship, cave — anything.
2. **Build it in detail** — sights, sounds, textures, temperature; engage all senses (the wind in the pines, hot tea, soft light).
3. **Visit to recharge** — when a day drains you, go there; sit, walk, breathe; let "time slow."
4. **Use it to rehearse** — design a goal or future scene in real detail (your future home, a coming conversation).
5. **Return and revise often** — "rebuild it if you want. Make it better, stranger, cozier, bigger. No rules — just comfort and clarity."

## When to use it
- To reset after stress or overwhelm.
- To mentally rehearse a goal or hard situation before facing it.

## Common mistakes
- Using it only as escape rather than also as rehearsal/clarity.
- Keeping it vague — the power is in vivid, multisensory detail.
