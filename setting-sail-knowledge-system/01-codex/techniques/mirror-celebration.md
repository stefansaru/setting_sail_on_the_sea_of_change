> When you do something great, look in the mirror, smile, give yourself a pat on the back, and a small treat — then carry on. That's it.

## Definition
A tiny self-validation ritual for acknowledging your own wins instead of waiting for
others to. From the friend's boss: "when you do something great, go look in the mirror.
Smile. Give yourself a pat on the back, and treat yourself to your favorite chocolate.
And that's it."

## Why it works
It "fills your own feel-good tank" — building an internal source of recognition so your
sense of worth doesn't depend on scarce external praise (the heart of
[[be-your-own-biggest-fan]]). The physical act (mirror, smile, treat) makes the
acknowledgment concrete and repeatable, banking the esteem you've
[[self-esteem-is-earned|earned]].

## Steps
1. After doing something you're proud of — large or small — pause.
2. Catch your reflection in a mirror.
3. Smile; give yourself a wink, a nod, or a "mirror high-five."
4. Acknowledge specifically what you did well.
5. Give yourself a small treat (a favorite chocolate, a moment).
6. Then move on — "and that's it." No need for an audience.

## When to use it
- After any win, especially in roles where external recognition is rare.
- Whenever you catch yourself waiting for someone else to notice your effort.

## Common mistakes
- Reserving it only for big achievements (do it for small ones too).
- Skipping it because it feels silly — the small ritual is the point.
- Waiting for the "plane to arrive at the train station" (external validation that may never come).
