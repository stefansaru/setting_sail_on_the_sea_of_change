> A short set of reflection questions to check whether you're filling your life with what nourishes — or just what's flashy and convenient.

## Definition
The "checkout of reflection" from [[the-existential-supermarket]]: a periodic audit of
whether your pursuits are nourishing (meaning) or merely filling (pleasure/power), plus
prompts for locating your purpose and flow.

## Why it works
It applies the [[theme/observer-stance|observer stance]] to your life direction —
making the difference between *busy* and *meaningful* explicit. Asking "am I living with
purpose, or just keeping busy?" interrupts autopilot and surfaces drift before years
pass.

## Steps / questions
1. **The checkout questions** (do periodically):
   - Have I filled my cart with what truly satisfies, or been distracted by "bright lights and flashy deals"?
   - Am I pursuing what truly matters — or getting lost in convenience?
   - Am I living with purpose — or just keeping busy?
2. **Find your flow** (purpose clue): *What activities make me lose track of time because I enjoy them so much?*
3. **Find your purpose moments:** *When have I felt a deep sense of purpose, and why?*
4. **Map it:** run the answers through [[ikigai]] (love / skill / need / pay) or [[pure-model]] (purpose / understanding / responsibility / enjoyment).

## When to use it
- Periodically (quarterly, birthdays, year's end) as a life check-in.
- When the [[the-existential-void|existential void]] grows loud.

## Common mistakes
- Answering aspirationally rather than honestly.
- Auditing once and never revisiting (purpose shifts over time).
- Confusing "lose track of time" doom-scrolling (pleasure) with genuine flow (meaning).
