> A seven-step micro-retreat to quiet the world and hear your own voice — no monastery required.

## Definition
A concrete, repeatable routine for [[solitude-for-self-discovery]]. It needs only a
quiet spot and a little time, and ends by carrying one insight back into your day.

## Why it works
It removes external input long enough for the [[inner-voice]] to become audible, and
structures reflection so it produces something usable rather than drifting into
rumination.

## Steps
1. **Choose your space** — a quiet spot where you won't be disturbed (room, park bench, walk in nature).
2. **Disconnect** — phone on silent or left behind; remove distractions so attention turns inward.
3. **Breathe** — sit comfortably, close your eyes, slow breaths, let the body settle.
4. **Listen** — notice thoughts without judgment; don't push them away, just observe.
5. **Ask gently** — pose simple questions: *What matters most to me right now? What do I need less of? What do I need more of?*
6. **Write it down** — jot anything that feels true; keep it short and honest (reveals patterns over time).
7. **Return slowly** — ease back into your day carrying one insight, however small.

## When to use it
- During the [[lion-phase]]'s reflective pause ("be alone for a while").
- Whenever values feel cloudy or a decision won't settle.

## Common mistakes
- Skipping step 2 (real disconnection) and letting screens fill the silence.
- Forcing answers instead of listening for them.
- Never returning with a concrete takeaway (step 7).
