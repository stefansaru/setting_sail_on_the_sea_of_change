> Three steps turn "how much do I need?" into a single target number you can work toward, one coin at a time.

## Definition
A simple calculation of the invested net worth whose returns cover your annual cost of
living — the point at which you no longer *have* to work for money.

## Why it works
It replaces an open-ended anxiety with a finite, directional goal. Knowing the number
gives "direction" and makes progress measurable.

## Steps
1. **Calculate your yearly cost of living** — monthly expenses × 12 (from your [[cashflow-sheet]]). *Example: 2500/month × 12 = 30,000/year.*
2. **Choose a realistic annual return rate** — e.g. 5% or 6% after adjusting for inflation.
3. **Apply the formula:** `target = yearly cost ÷ return rate`. *Example: 30,000 ÷ 0.06 = 500,000.*

That target is the net worth that can generate enough passive income to cover your life
without working.

## When to use it
- After you have positive cashflow, a backup fund, and some investments — to set a long-range target.
- Whenever your expenses or goals change (recompute).

## Common mistakes
- Treating the number as exact — use a conservative return rate and a safety margin.
- Forgetting it's freedom *to* pursue your mission, not necessarily to stop working.
- Picking an optimistic return rate that overstates how close you are.
