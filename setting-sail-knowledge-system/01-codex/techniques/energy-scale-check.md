> Rate your energy 1–10 and aim to keep it in the 4–8 band — neither drained empty nor unsustainably overflowing.

## Definition
A self-monitoring practice from the [[will-to-receive-and-will-to-bestow]] vessel
model. Treat your energy as a scale from 1 to 10; the healthy, sustainable band is
**4 to 8**. Dipping below 2 — or swinging unpredictably between 2 and 9 — signals
imbalance, "either from exhaustion or from an unchecked pursuit of external validation."

## Why it works
It gives a quick, quantifiable read on whether you're giving from abundance or from
depletion ([[giving-from-abundance-vs-sacrifice]]). A number makes an otherwise vague
state ("I feel off") actionable: below 4 → receive/recharge before giving; above 8 and
overflowing → ease off before you run dry.

## Steps
1. A few times a day (or when you feel "off"), rate your current energy 1–10.
2. **If below ~4:** stop giving; deliberately receive — rest, ask for help, do something restorative ([[fill-your-own-cup-first]]).
3. **If in 4–8:** you're in the sustainable band; give from this surplus.
4. **If pushing 9–10 by overflowing into others:** pull back; even generous giving must be refilled.
5. **Watch for wild swings** (2↔9) — a sign of instability or validation-chasing, not health.

## When to use it
- As a daily check-in, and before deciding whether to take on more for others.
- When you notice irritability or resentment creeping in (often a low-energy tell).

## Common mistakes
- Only noticing energy once you've crashed to a 1–2.
- Treating a brief 9 as the goal; the target is a stable mid-band.
- Ignoring the signal and giving anyway, then resenting it.
