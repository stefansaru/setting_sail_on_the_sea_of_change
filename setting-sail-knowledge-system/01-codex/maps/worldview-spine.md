# The Worldview Spine

> The hidden architecture beneath the book. The chapters are the *delivery order*;
> this is the *logic*. New pages attach here, not to chapter numbers.

## The master metaphor

> **Life is a sea of change. You cannot command the wind, but you set the sail.**
> *You're not the wind — you're the sailor.*

Everything in the book is downstream of this single claim: agency lives in your
*response*, not your *circumstances*. See [[you-are-the-sailor-not-the-wind]].

## The developmental engine: camel → lion → child

The book's spine is Nietzsche's [[three-metamorphoses]]. Every major move maps to a
stage of becoming yourself:

```
   CAMEL  ───────────▶   LION   ───────────▶   CHILD   ───────────▶  (ÜBERMENSCH)
  inherit values        reject values         create values         master your fate
   "thou shalt"          "I will not"          "I will"               self-affirmation
   conformity            rebellion             play & creation        meaning
        │                    │                     │
   camel-phase          lion-phase            child-phase
   camel-phase-crisis   art-of-saying-no      defining-your-own-values
   thou-shalt-dragon    negation-is-not-      sparks-of-creation
                          creation             world-as-playground
```

The hinge is [[negation-is-not-creation]]: rebellion frees you but cannot fill the
space — you must move from "no" to "I will."

## The four strata

Beneath the chapters, every concept belongs to one of four strata of a single
project — *moving from a life you were handed to a life you author and orient toward
meaning.* (These are the `stratum/*` tags.)

| Stratum | The question it answers | Nietzsche stage | Feeder chapters |
|---------|------------------------|-----------------|-----------------|
| **1. Inheritance** | What was I given / programmed by? | Camel | 2 (evolution), 5 (subconscious), 7 (bias) |
| **2. Liberation** | How do I see and loosen the programming? | Lion | 6 (emotions), 7 (bias), parts of 5 & 8 |
| **3. Authorship** | What do I choose to build? | Child | 3 (values), 4 (esteem, money), 8 (time, energy) |
| **4. Meaning** | What is it all *for*? | Übermensch | 9 (Frankl, ikigai), 10 (intention, contribution) |

### Where Chapter 3 sits
Chapter 3 is the **keystone**: it contains the developmental engine itself, and it
spans strata 1→4 in miniature (camel = inheritance, lion = liberation, child =
authorship, übermensch = meaning). That is why it was chosen as the exemplar — it
touches every part of the architecture.

## First principles (the irreducible layer)

These are the load-bearing truths the whole structure rests on. Chapter 3 supplies
four; others arrive with later chapters.

- [[you-are-the-sailor-not-the-wind]] — agency is in the response.
- [[define-values-before-setting-course]] — values precede goals.
- [[negation-is-not-creation]] — freedom *from* is not freedom *to*.
- [[courage-is-acting-despite-fear]] — act while afraid.
- *(forward)* mood-follows-action · discipline-over-motivation · the-journey-is-the-destination · pain-is-not-suffering · give-from-abundance.

## How to use this map
- When adding a page, tag its `stratum/*` and (if relevant) `arc/*`, and check it
  connects to the engine above.
- If a new idea doesn't fit any stratum, that's a signal — either the architecture
  needs extending, or you've found a genuine [[gaps|gap]].
