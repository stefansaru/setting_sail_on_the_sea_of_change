# Bias Catalog — The Illusion of Accuracy (Chapter 7)

> Index of every cognitive bias, effect, and logical fallacy in the book, grouped by
> the kind of error. Each links to its atomic page. Framing: [[cognitive-bias]] ·
> [[the-book-and-pen-test]] · [[knowns-and-unknowns]].
>
> **Master counter-move:** awareness + the [[becoming-the-observer|observer stance]] —
> "notice the trap, pause, and choose better."

## 1. Crowd & conformity (we follow others)
- [[bandwagon-effect]] — popularity feels like proof.
- [[groupthink]] — harmony over correctness.
- [[false-consensus]] — overestimating who agrees with you.
- [[bystander-effect]] — no one acts because everyone assumes someone else will.
- [[authority-bias]] — trusting titles over evidence.
- [[stereotypical-bias]] — judging by group, not individual.

## 2. Self & perception (how we see ourselves/others)
- [[spotlight-effect]] — overestimating how much others notice you.
- [[fundamental-attribution-error]] — blaming character over situation.
- [[blindspot-bias]] — seeing bias in others, not yourself.
- [[dunning-kruger-effect]] — confidence ≠ competence.
- [[barnum-forer-effect]] — vague statements feel personal.

## 3. Decisions, effort & value
- [[effort-heuristic]] — busy ≠ better.
- [[ikea-effect]] — overvaluing what you built.
- [[endowment-effect]] — overvaluing what you own.
- [[sunk-cost-fallacy]] — throwing good money after bad.
- [[outcome-bias]] — judging by results, not process.
- [[paradox-of-choice]] — too many options reduce satisfaction.
- [[contrast-effect]] — judging by comparison, not merit.
- [[anchoring-bias]] — the first number drags the rest *(Ch.4)*.

## 4. Risk, probability & statistics
- [[zero-risk-bias]] — eliminating a small risk over reducing a big one.
- [[planning-fallacy]] — underestimating time and cost.
- [[availability-bias]] — judging risk by what's memorable.
- [[base-rate-neglect]] — ignoring the underlying odds.
- [[regression-to-the-mean]] — extremes drift back to average.
- [[survivorship-bias]] — only counting the winners.
- [[swimmers-body-illusion]] — mistaking selection for cause.
- [[illusion-of-control]] — feeling power over chance.
- [[money-illusion]] — nominal vs. real value.
- [[hyperbolic-discounting]] — smaller-sooner over larger-later.
- [[loss-aversion-bias]] — losses loom larger than gains *(Ch.4)*.

## 5. Memory & attention
- [[mandela-effect]] — false shared memories.
- [[primacy-and-recency]] — remembering first and last best.
- [[zeigarnik-effect]] — unfinished tasks nag.
- [[digital-amnesia]] — outsourcing memory to devices.
- [[baader-meinhof-phenomenon]] — noticing something everywhere.

## 6. Belief defense & emotion
- [[cognitive-dissonance]] — clashing belief/action discomfort.
- [[reactance]] — resisting when freedom feels threatened.
- [[backfire-effect]] — doubling down against evidence.
- [[confirmation-bias]] — seeking what confirms you *(Ch.4)*.
- [[status-quo-bias]] — preferring the familiar *(Ch.4)*.
- [[negativity-bias]] — bad outweighs good.
- [[hedonic-adaptation]] — happiness returns to baseline.

## Reasoning tools (not biases, but listed here)
- [[occams-razor]] — prefer the simplest sufficient explanation.

## Logical fallacies (from Ch.4 §4.19, the makeup-artist analysis)
- [[false-dilemma]] — only two options presented when more exist.
- [[appeal-to-emotion]] — feelings in place of reasons.
