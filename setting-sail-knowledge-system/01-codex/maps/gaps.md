# Gaps & Missing Concepts

> Ideas the worldview *implies or needs* but does not (yet) supply, plus places the
> book leans on something unexamined. These are the highest-value seeds for new
> content and for strengthening the system. (Chapter-3 scope; expand on each ingest.)

## G1 — No decision rule for "repair vs. leave" *(RESOLVED)*
The book said both "build brick by brick / repair" and "let go when values diverge"
(see [[contradictions]] C2) but offered **no criteria**. **Resolved by
[[friction-vs-divergence]]:** repair *friction* (same destination, different route);
release *divergence* (different destinations); first check for *realignment* (a temporary,
co-evolvable divergence).

## G2 — How to *cultivate* the sparks of creation
[[sparks-of-creation]] lists ~19 qualities of the child phase but treats them as a
*description of arrival*, not a *path*. Missing: practices that actually develop
playfulness, forgiveness, curiosity. → *Candidate techniques.*

## G3 — Relapse and non-linearity
The [[three-metamorphoses]] is presented as a forward progression, but the book never
addresses **regression** (child → camel under stress) or holding different stages in
different life domains at once. A "metamorphosis is not one-way" page would
strengthen the model.

## G4 — The inner voice can be wrong *(RESOLVED — Ch.7)*
[[inner-voice]] is trusted as truth, but Ch.7's [[cognitive-bias|biases]] show intuition is
systematically misleading. **Resolution:** trust the inner voice for *values/resonance*
("what matters to me?"); route *factual/probabilistic* judgments through System 2 and the
[[bias-catalog]] ([[the-book-and-pen-test]], [[becoming-the-observer]]). The whisper answers
"what do I value?", not "what is statistically true?" A dedicated synthesis page would still help.

## G5 — "What comes after the child" is under-built
The book admits Nietzsche's scheme ends at the child, then gestures at the
[[ubermensch]] as a further stage — but this extension is thin and partly the
author's own. Either develop it rigorously or frame it explicitly as speculation.

## G6 — Power, privilege, and luck
[[you-are-the-sailor-not-the-wind]] under-weights how much "wind" (circumstance,
luck, structural advantage) shapes outcomes. A counter-balancing page on the limits
of self-authorship would make the principle honest and more durable.

## G7 — Gendered/dated framing in the relationship material
The "sparkle" quote and "keep options open" advice ([[fulfilling-life-beyond-the-relationship]],
[[partner-filtering-protocol]]) carry a specific (pickup-influenced, gendered)
framing. The underlying principles are broader; a de-gendered restatement would age better.

## G8 — Mental-health reframes are not treatment
The "deep rest" and "trauma response" framings of depression
([[depression-vs-low-self-esteem]], [[the-stage-and-the-roles]]) are humane and
de-stigmatizing, but the book gives **no guidance to seek professional help** and no
warning signs for when self-reframing is insufficient. A "when to get help" page would
make this responsible. *High priority for the legacy layer.*

## G9 — The financial module assumes income slack
The whole money sequence ([[positive-cashflow]] → [[financial-independence-number]])
assumes you earn more than you spend and can cut or earn more at will. It barely
addresses the harder, more common problem: **insufficient income / poverty**, where
budgeting alone can't close the gap. → *Candidate page on earning-side strategy and structural constraints.*

## G10 — "Beliefs shape results" underplays structure
[[limiting-beliefs-about-money]] and the broader [[you-are-the-sailor-not-the-wind]]
ethic can slide into a bootstrap myth that treats wealth as purely a mindset problem,
under-weighting wages, debt, discrimination, and luck (cf. [[gaps|G6]]). A
counter-balancing page on the limits of mindset would keep the system honest.

## G11 — Unattributed borrowings
"Will to receive / will to bestow" ([[will-to-receive-and-will-to-bestow]]) echoes
Kabbalistic language; [[rocky-the-stoic-dog]] descends from Zeno/Chrysippus's
cart-and-dog. The book presents them without lineage. Not errors, but worth noting for
scholarly integrity and for the influences map.
