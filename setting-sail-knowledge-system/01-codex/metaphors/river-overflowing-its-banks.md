> Giving from abundance is a river overflowing its banks — nourishing everything around it without losing its essence.

## The image
"Giving from abundance is like a river that overflows its banks, nourishing the land
around it without losing its essence." When you are "filled to the brim with
self-care, understanding, and happiness," giving becomes "effortless and boundless" —
"like sunlight dappling through the leaves," done "not from obligation, but from the
sheer delight of sharing your fullest self."

## What it teaches
The positive half of [[giving-from-abundance-vs-sacrifice]]: when the source is full,
generosity costs nothing and the giver is undiminished. Contrast with its paired image,
[[tree-bending-under-fruit]] (giving from sacrifice).

## Why it sticks
A river that gives freely yet "loses no essence" captures the paradox of abundant
generosity — you can pour out and remain whole — in a single natural image.
