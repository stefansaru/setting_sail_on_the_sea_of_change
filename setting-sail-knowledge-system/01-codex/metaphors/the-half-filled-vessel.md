> Your self is a glass of energy: empty and endlessly thirsty (pure taking), or overflowing and soon dry (pure giving). The wise state is gracefully half-full.

## The image
"Imagine your body — your physical vessel — as a glass filled with energy, much like
water." We begin empty, driven to receive (children "soaking up water"). A "hollow
vessel... remains thirsty when the desire to receive is insatiable." The opposite
extreme overflows, giving until it "runs dry." The ideal: "neither perpetually empty
nor endlessly overflowing but gracefully half-filled most of the time" — energy held
between 4 and 8 on a 1–10 scale.

## What it teaches
The core image for [[will-to-receive-and-will-to-bestow]]: emotional health as a
*balance point*, not an extreme. It justifies both receiving (refilling) and giving
(without draining), and supplies the measurable [[energy-scale-check|4–8 target]].

## Why it sticks
A glass of water is universal and the "half-full" phrasing slyly inverts the
optimist/pessimist cliché into a literal prescription: half-full is not a mood, it's
the goal.
