> Talking to a psychologist is like inviting a stranger into your home: you live there, but haven't opened every door. They help you notice rooms you ignored — and turn on lights in places you didn't know were dark.

## The image
"Talking to a psychologist is like inviting a stranger into your home. You know the space —
you live there. But you haven't opened every door. The psychologist helps you notice rooms
you ignored. Buttons you haven't pressed yet. They might even help you turn on the light in
places you didn't know were dark. What could take you years — or a lifetime — to figure out
alone, a good therapist might show you in just a few sessions."

## What it teaches
The image for [[combining-self-help-with-therapy]]: an outside guide sees what your
familiarity hides. It dignifies seeking help (you own the house; they just help you explore
it) and connects to [[the-light-switch]] (awareness) and [[the-temple-of-the-mind]] (your
inner space).

## Why it sticks
"Rooms you've never opened" in your own home is an intimate, slightly eerie image that makes
the value of an outside perspective immediately tangible — and removes the shame from
needing one.
