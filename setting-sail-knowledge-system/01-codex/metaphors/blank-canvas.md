> Life hands you a blank canvas and reveals you're the artist — no instruction manual, no pre-written script; every choice is a brushstroke.

## The image
"It's like being handed a blank canvas and realizing — you're the artist. There's no
instruction manual, no pre-written script." Meaning is painted on, not found: "each
moment paints a mark so bright, each choice a brushstroke, bold or light." The colors
come "in kindness, growth, and love... in hands we help, in dreams we chase."

## What it teaches
The central image for [[meaning-is-created-not-found]]: a life's meaning is authored,
not received. It echoes the [[child-phase]]'s creative self-authorship (Ch.3) and casts
the absence of a script as freedom rather than lack.

## Why it sticks
The artist-and-canvas image reframes the anxiety of "no instructions" into the
privilege of "you get to paint it" — a hopeful inversion that's easy to picture and
recall.
