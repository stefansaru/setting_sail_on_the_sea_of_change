> A control room of screens, dials, and sliders where you sit in the director's chair and edit your memories — a video-editing suite (or recording studio) for System 2.

## The image
To do memory work, you "step into the operations room of System 2, where conscious thought
and deliberate action take place." You sit "surrounded by screens, dials, and controls —
each representing access to the vast archive of your experiences." For visual memories it's
an editing suite with brightness/contrast/saturation sliders; for [[diffusing-audible-memories|audio]]
it becomes "a recording studio... a soundboard, filled with controls, dials, and
waveforms." You're "the director," "the producer" — in command.

## What it teaches
The staging metaphor for [[memory-as-editable-film]] and [[becoming-the-observer]]: it
puts you in the seat of conscious control (System 2), framing a memory as a manageable file
on a console rather than an overwhelming flood. It's the editing-bay cousin of
[[the-mind-cinema]] (Ch.5).

## Why it sticks
A control room full of dials makes the abstract act of "reframing a memory" feel concrete
and *operable* — you have knobs to turn, not just feelings to endure.
