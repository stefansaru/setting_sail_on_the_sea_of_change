> Every light casts a shadow, and every shadow holds a spark of light — good and bad each carry a drop of the other, so balance, not either-or, is the goal.

## The image
"Think of the yin-yang symbol. Every light casts a shadow. And every shadow has a spark
of light somewhere." Each side "holds a drop of the other. It's not just black or just
white." Pursue only good and "you'll soon run into resistance"; choose only the dark and
"you'll begin to notice beauty... crave kindness... seek connection." Carl Jung: "the
brighter the light, the darker the shadow."

## What it teaches
The image behind [[awareness-of-both]] and the critique of [[toxic-positivity]]: joy and
pain are entangled, so chasing pure positivity (or sinking into pure negativity) fights
reality. The mature move is to "see the gray" and hold both.

## Why it sticks
The yin-yang is one of the most recognizable symbols on earth; the "drop of the other in
each side" detail gives the abstract point about balance a precise, memorable anchor.
