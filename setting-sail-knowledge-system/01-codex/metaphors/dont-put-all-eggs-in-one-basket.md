> Put all your eggs in one basket and a single drop cracks them all — concentration is fragility; spread your risk.

## The image
"In your financial life, putting all your eggs in one basket is a very risky strategy.
If the basket were to suddenly drop, then your eggs would crack." Applied to investing:
"don't keep all your eggs in one basket — spread your investments across multiple areas
(stocks, bonds, real estate, etc.)."

## What it teaches
The intuitive case for [[diversify-for-resilience]] and [[outcome-independence]]:
concentration means one failure is total; diversification localizes shocks. It applies
beyond money — to income, identity, and sources of meaning.

## Why it sticks
A proverb everyone already knows, given a concrete, slightly wince-inducing image (the
dropped basket of cracked eggs) that makes the cost of concentration visceral.
