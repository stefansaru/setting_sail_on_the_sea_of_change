> The subconscious is an enormous warehouse storing every experience; the conscious mind is just a tiny sticky note for what matters right now.

## The image
"Think of [the subconscious] as an enormous warehouse, storing every experience, memory,
and pattern you've accumulated over a lifetime." By contrast, "the conscious mind is the
little sticky note you use to remind yourself what's most relevant right now." The
warehouse runs the background (heartbeat, faces, emotions); the sticky note handles the
small, urgent foreground.

## What it teaches
The scale image behind [[the-subconscious-mind]]: it dramatizes *why* willpower (a sticky
note) struggles against ingrained patterns (a whole warehouse), and why change requires
reprogramming the warehouse, not just rewriting the note.

## Why it sticks
The size contrast — vast warehouse vs. tiny sticky note — instantly conveys the imbalance
between conscious and subconscious, making "willpower isn't enough" obvious.
