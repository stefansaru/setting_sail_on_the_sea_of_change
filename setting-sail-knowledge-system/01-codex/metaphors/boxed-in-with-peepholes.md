> Each of us is a box with small peepholes — our senses, beliefs, and past experiences — through which we glimpse a different slice of the same reality.

## The image
"Imagine yourself as a box — one with small openings that allow you to perceive the world
outside. These openings represent your senses, beliefs, and past experiences." Everyone
else is also a box with their own openings: "some wider or narrower... one person might
focus on details you overlook, while another perceives patterns you hardly notice." So
people "witness the same event but interpret it in entirely different ways." An old
monastery's inscription captures it: **"1000 monks, 1000 religions."**

## What it teaches
The vivid image behind [[the-map-is-not-the-territory]]: perception is partial and
constructed, so differing views often reflect different *peepholes*, not different facts.
It motivates curiosity and humility — and [[explain-dont-force]] (others may simply see a
different slice).

## Why it sticks
The box-with-peepholes is a simple, slightly claustrophobic picture that makes the
abstract point about limited perception physical: you literally can't see the whole room.
