> "Freedom isn't a dream. It's a number." — financial freedom reframed from a vague longing into a concrete target you approach one coin at a time.

## The image
The book closes its money module by collapsing an abstraction into arithmetic:
"freedom isn't a dream. it's a number. and you can work toward it, one coin at a time."
The number is your [[financial-independence-number]] — the invested net worth whose
returns cover your cost of living.

## What it teaches
That "freedom" — usually felt as a hazy, romantic wish — can be *operationalized*. Once
it's a number, it stops being a daydream and becomes a plan with milestones. Carries
[[financial-independence-number]] and motivates [[calculate-your-fi-number]].

## Why it sticks
The blunt reframe ("not a dream — a number") is quotable and slightly provocative,
turning an emotional concept into something you can put on a spreadsheet and chip away
at.
