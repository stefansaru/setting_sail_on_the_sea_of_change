> Where the lion saw a battlefield, the child sees a playground — the same world, reframed from threat to possibility.

## The image
As Cammy transforms from lion to child, she "begins to see the world not as a
battlefield but as a playground, vast and ripe with possibilities." The
[[thou-shalt-dragon]] that was an enemy becomes "a magnificent playground waiting to
be explored." The ferocious roar "softens into sparks of laughter."

## What it teaches
The decisive shift of the [[child-phase]] is not a change in circumstances but a
change in *frame*. The same reality that the [[lion-phase]] experienced as combat is
experienced by the child as creative play. It encodes [[sparks-of-creation]] —
playfulness, curiosity, affirmation — and resolves the lion's exhausting battles.

## Why it sticks
"Battlefield vs. playground" is a crisp, portable contrast that captures an entire
psychological maturation in two words — easy to recall and apply to any situation.
