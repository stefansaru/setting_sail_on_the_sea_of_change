> Stumbling in a dark room, you keep hitting furniture. The obstacles don't vanish when you turn on the light — but now you can see them, navigate them, and move forward.

## The image
"Picture yourself in a pitch-dark room, stumbling into furniture — frustrating, right? But
the moment you turn on the light, everything becomes clear. The obstacles didn't disappear,
but now you can see them, navigate them, and move forward." Paired with: "light must come
from inside. You cannot ask the darkness to leave; you must turn on the light" (Sogyal
Rinpoche).

## What it teaches
The image for awareness as the precondition for change — central to [[pain-vs-suffering]]
(stop cursing the dark; flip the switch and act) and [[becoming-the-observer]]. Insight
doesn't remove problems; it lets you *navigate* them. Connects to
[[awakening-the-point-in-the-heart]] (inner light).

## Why it sticks
Everyone has groped through a dark room; the instant relief of the light switch makes
"awareness changes everything" viscerally obvious.
