> Carrying unprocessed emotions is like dragging an overstuffed suitcase — or stacking boxes labeled "do not open" in an attic and throwing a blanket over the clutter.

## The image
"Carrying emotional baggage is like dragging an overstuffed suitcase. Every experience
can add an item — some bright and joyful, others heavy with past pain." Unresolved
emotions get "stored away, avoided rather than addressed": the mind becomes "an attic
filled with boxes labeled 'do not open.'" We cover the discomfort with distractions —
"like throwing a blanket over clutter and pretending the room is clean."

## What it teaches
The concrete image for [[emotional-baggage]]: avoidance doesn't empty the suitcase; it
just postpones the weight. Facing emotions is like "decluttering years' worth of
forgotten storage" — overwhelming but necessary, and easier with support.

## Why it sticks
The overstuffed suitcase and the blanket-over-clutter are everyday, slightly comic
images that make an invisible burden tangible — you can *picture* dragging it.
