> An ancient mirror at a hidden oasis that gently tells Cammy her burdens are too heavy — and that she carries them only because she keeps choosing to.

## The image
At an oasis "not marked on any map," Cammy finds an ancient magical mirror that
whispers truths rather than showing reflections: *"the burdens you carry, my child,
are too heavy,"* and *"these burdens are yours only because you keep choosing to
accept them."* It asks how much others really care about her when they pile their
burdens on her. Later, the same voice — now from a *shattered* mirror — names her
transformation: *"you're the leader of your own revolution."* Then it breaks into
pieces and is gone, just when she wants more advice.

## What it teaches
The mirror personifies the [[inner-voice]] — the quiet inner truth that surfaces at
the [[camel-phase-crisis]]. Its breaking dramatizes a key lesson: external guidance
is temporary; eventually you must define values yourself, without the oracle.

## Why it sticks
A talking mirror that reveals *inner* rather than *outer* truth is a vivid inversion
of the familiar "mirror" trope. Its disappearance — right when more guidance is
craved — gives emotional weight to the idea that self-authorship can't be outsourced.
