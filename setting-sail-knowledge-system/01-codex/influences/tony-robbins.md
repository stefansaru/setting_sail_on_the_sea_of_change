> The performance coach whose *Unlimited Power* supplies the book's "7 beliefs of high performers" and its conviction that success starts with mindset.

## Who
Tony Robbins (b. 1960), American coach and author of *Unlimited Power* and *Awaken the Giant
Within*, a major popularizer of NLP-influenced peak-performance psychology.

## What the book borrows
- **The 7 convictions of high performers** ([[winners-do-things-differently]]): everything
  serves us; no failure, only results; take responsibility; you needn't understand everything
  to use it; people are your greatest resource; work is play; lasting success requires commitment.
- **"Nothing has power over me other than that which I give it through my conscious thoughts"**
  (used in [[memory-as-editable-film]], Ch.6).
- The framing that **success starts with mindset** — how you see challenges shapes actions and results.

## How it is used (and bent)
- The book presents Robbins' convictions as practical operating beliefs, illustrated with
  everyday examples — motivational heuristics rather than tested claims.
- Some convictions are double-edged: "there is no failure, only results" aids resilience but
  can deny real, costly failures; the codex flags this on [[winners-do-things-differently]].
- Robbins' NLP lineage links him to the techniques in Ch.6 ([[nlp-bandler-grinder]]).
