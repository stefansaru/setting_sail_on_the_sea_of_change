> The author of *The 7 Habits of Highly Effective People*, source of "start with the end in mind" and the values-before-goals ethic.

## Who
Stephen R. Covey (1932–2012), American educator and author of *The 7 Habits of Highly
Effective People* (1989).

## What the book borrows
- **"Start with the end in mind"** (Habit 2) → [[start-with-the-end-in-mind]]: everything
  is "created twice," first mentally, then physically.
- **The personal mission statement** as "a compass, not a map" → [[write-your-eulogy-and-mission]].
- **Values before goals**: "goals can be achieved and still feel empty if they don't
  align with your core values" — reinforcing [[define-values-before-setting-course]] (Ch.3).
- **Act with purpose, not habit; reflect and recalibrate** — periodic course-correction.
- The "write your eulogy" exercise as the truest compass-north.
- Covey's "be proactive" (Habit 1) surfaces elsewhere as [[be-proactive]] *(forward link — Ch.8)*.

## How it is used (and bent)
- The book lifts Habit 2 fairly directly and pairs it with its own values-first thesis,
  treating Covey as a kindred source rather than reinterpreting him heavily.
- It threads Covey through its synthesis: mission statement ↔ [[ikigai]]/[[pure-model]],
  recalibration ↔ [[intention-in-context]].
