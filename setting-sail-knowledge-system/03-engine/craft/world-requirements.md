# World Requirements — what must be true of *Intention in Context*

> The testable contract for the **world**, as [[writing-requirements]] is for the **craft**. The
> [[canon-registry]] says *what has been established*; this page says *what must always hold*, and how to check
> it. Every season, blueprint and script is tested against it. Words are defined in the [[glossary]].
>
> **Precedence:** a dated author ruling → this page → the registry → world pages → scripts. A conflict is a
> finding, never something to smooth over. To change a requirement, add a dated note and update the affected
> seasons in the same pass.
>
> **IDs:** `WR-*`. **Type:** `G` gate, pass or fail. `S` scored 1–5. Each gate says how to verify it.

---

## Part 1 — Characteristics: what this world is
These never change. If an idea needs one of them broken, it belongs in a different story.

1. **A world where experience and reality have come apart.** People live on a simulated Grid while bodies wait
   elsewhere. The drama is in the gap.
2. **Time is the central resource.** Who gets how much subjective time is class, power and mercy.
3. **Every gift has a cost, and the cost is physical.** Watts, chemistry, flesh, memory, years.
4. **Systems, not villains, cause most harm.** An antagonist always has an argument that could be right.
5. **Kindness and control are hard to tell apart.** The comfort may be a cage, and the show doesn't settle it.
6. **Nothing is solved by a better system.** Grief, mortality and meaning must be lived through.
7. **The goal is balance, not victory:** holding joy and pain at once, the Doughnut.
8. **The world is larger than the frame.** The bible knows everything; the audience earns it.

## Part 2 — Requirements

### Time (`WR-T`)
| ID | T | Requirement | How to verify |
|----|---|-------------|---------------|
| WR-T1 | G | **Tier rates are exact:** Tier 0 = 1×, Tier 1 = 60×, Tier 2 = 3,600×, Tier 3 (Apex) = 86,400×. Nothing is faster. | Recompute every stated duration. Key values: 1 real day at Apex ≈ 237 years; 24 Apex years ≈ 2.4 real hours; 0.5 s at Apex = 12 hours. |
| WR-T2 | G | **High speed feels normal,** calm and unhurried from the inside. | No frantic high-tier time. |
| WR-T3 | G | **Light-speed is a hard limit.** No live talk across interstellar distance. Beams and news take real years. | Any Earth↔fleet exchange has a stated lag or is a beamed copy. |
| WR-T4 | G | **Real and subjective time are both accounted for** in anything that spans time, using the chronology in [[the-quickening]]. | State both clocks for each jump. |
| WR-T5 | G | **Apex time is rationed,** by priority on Earth and by reactor budget aboard. | Any Apex use names who pays or who allowed it. |
| WR-T6 | G | **The fleet keeps two clocks.** Deep stasis pauses a body's clock; minds pause between watches. Nobody runs the whole voyage at speed. | Place each fleet event on the table in [[the-seed-fleet]]. |

### Bodies and castes (`WR-B`)
| ID | T | Requirement | How to verify |
|----|---|-------------|---------------|
| WR-B1 | G | **Every person has one caste:** Purist (body, no Grid), Shadow (body in capsule, mind on Grid), cyborg (partly replaced), Ghost (no body), Ghost in a shell (Ghost leasing a machine body), Woken (a Ghost woken into a ramp body, which is Quickened like every Grid body). Made minds that were never people, like Teddy and Decimus, are **digital agents** (`Q-71`). | Each character's caste is stated and valid for that point. |
| WR-B2 | G | **Grid bodies are Quickened:** fast-grown, short-lived, worn, barren. Purist bodies are not. | No Grid body lives past about ten real years unaided, not counting time paused in deep stasis; no Grid body conceives or carries. |
| WR-B3 | G | **Body clocks follow their waypoints:** Raul 100→70→30→0; Adam 90→60→30→~0, then borrowed flesh; Mara 0→100; Kai 100→70→30→0 in one night. | Compare each episode's stated percent. |
| WR-B4 | G | **Memory follows substrate:** in-grid recall is perfect; a brain forgets; Ghost recall is perfect and permanent. | Forgetting happens only through a brain. |
| WR-B5 | G | **Where you are decides your limit:** Earth capsules can hold any tier; fleet stasis holds Tier 0–1, with rare credit to Tier 2; the fleet's Apex is Ghosts only. | Check each fleet Shadow's tier. |
| WR-B6 | G | **The body has a pull:** a Shadow can't leave its node indefinitely; at Tier 0 a Shadow attends by telepresence or a leased proxy, never in person. One exception: a licensed Sabbatical, brief, costly and supervised (`Q-72`). | Check each Shadow at a real location. |
| WR-B7 | G | **A beam carries a mind, not flesh.** Anyone beamed arrives without a body. | Check each beam's arrival. |
| WR-B8 | G | **Regrown flesh has a donor.** Naomi's cure rebuilds flesh from a donor's cells and consumes the donor, who must be willing and already dying; a body can't donate to itself. With no donor it can only make chrome. | Anyone regrown names a willing, dying donor, and that donor is gone. |

### Minds and identity (`WR-M`)
| ID | T | Requirement | How to verify |
|----|---|-------------|---------------|
| WR-M1 | G | **Forks are real people after divergence.** Each fork ends in a Merge or a Branch. | Each fork's outcome is stated. |
| WR-M2 | G | **Minors can't fork by choice.** The only child fork is the system's Parallel Pedagogy, ending in the Merging. One signed exception: Mara, a shard of an adult god (`Q-65`). | No minor branches voluntarily. |
| WR-M3 | G | **Short hops pause; long hops fork.** A dying hull can pause a mind until arrival is confirmed, while it has minutes left; only its last beams fork. | Check each transfer. |
| WR-M4 | G | **A Ghost's Identity Key is singular and fragile.** | Key loss or severance has its stated cost. |
| WR-M5 | G | **Packing a mind takes time.** Any mind can be beamed given time. Short of time, it must pack at the Apex; aboard the fleet, only a Ghost gets there. | Each beam has enough real time, or an Apex pack. |

## Part 3 — Things and concepts
The full list, with definitions, is the [[glossary]]. Every entry belongs to exactly one kind:

| Kind | What it is | Must always state |
|------|-----------|-------------------|
| **Person** | A named character | caste, season, name meaning |
| **Caste** | A way of existing | body, limits |
| **Group** | Faction or institution | what it wants, its argument |
| **Place** | Physical or simulated location | real or simulated, tier |
| **Technology** | A device or system | **cost and limit** (`WORLD-01/02`) |
| **Process** | Something that happens to people | trigger, cost |
| **Rule** | A law of the world or of society | who enforces it |
| **Interface** | Something seen in the Lens | what it reports |
| **Slang** | Words characters use | who says it, the plain meaning |
| **Motif** | A recurring image or line | what it carries |

Run each season against **all gates in Part 2** plus its own test below. A blueprint is tested on what it claims.
A season passes when every gate is PASS or N/A, with evidence.
