# Glossary — every word of *Intention in Context*

> The one place that says what a word means, who may say it, and when it may reach the screen. It implements
> **`WR-N1..N5`** in [[world-requirements]]: every proper name, coined term and recurring expression is listed here
> (`N1`), one thing has one word and each variant is named (`N2`), a claimed name meaning is true or marked
> *meaning unverified* (`N3`), near-collisions are flagged (`N4`), and bible words stay in the bible (`N5`).
>
> **Precedence:** a dated author ruling → [[world-requirements]] → [[canon-registry]] → world pages → scripts. Where
> a source disagrees with the requirements, the definition here follows the requirements and the conflict is logged,
> never smoothed. **Undefined** means the sources don't say. Don't invent an answer; raise it.
>
> **Reading "On screen from":** an episode code is the first time the word or thing reaches the audience; codes past S1
> come from blueprints and move with them. **HELD** marks a concept in a season's Reserve Vault ([[revelation-cadence]]
> §6), and it may not appear earlier except as that vault allows. *bible only* never appears on screen. *writers' room
> until Sn* may not be stated before that season. *reel* is a standalone not yet given an episode slot.
>
> **Kinds** follow [[world-requirements]] Part 3, and each entry has exactly one. Episode titles live in the episode
> guides, and only the terms inside them are listed here. Dated files in `audit-reports/` keep old names on purpose.
