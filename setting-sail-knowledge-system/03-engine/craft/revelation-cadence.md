# Revelation Cadence — rationing the world across a season

> **How much of the world the *audience* gets, and when.** The world bible ([world/](../world/README.md),
> [[canon-registry]]) holds the *whole* iceberg; this guide governs the **tip that shows per episode** so
> *Intention in Context* unfolds like its touchstones — ***Black Mirror* × *Star Trek* × *Rick and
> Morty*** ([[market-research]] §1) — instead of front-loading a lore dump. It extends
> [[fantasy-writing]] §14 (incluing) and §31 (POV gates mystery) from the *single short* up to the
> *whole season*, and it is the source of the **REVEAL** requirement layer in [[writing-requirements]],
> run as perspective **P9** in [[audit-script]].
>
> One line: **the writer knows everything; the audience earns it.**

---

## 1. The discipline — the Bible⇄Audience firewall
The [[canon-registry]] + the [world bible](../world/README.md) are **omniscient by design** — every mechanic,
faction, and future beat written down so the world stays consistent ([[market-research]]: this audience
*dissects*; softness gets "savaged in the comments"). But **knowing a thing is not the same as showing
it.** The firewall: the bible is the writers' room; each episode is a keyhole. A fact may be *load-bearing*
(it keeps the physics honest behind the scenes) without ever being *spoken on screen*. Most of the world
should stay felt-but-unseen — that very mass is what the audience calls an "airtight global framework"
([[market-research]] §2): *the larger machine humming in the background.* **Withholding is worldbuilding.**

## 2. Three cadence models (our touchstones, used deliberately)
We borrow a different rationing trick from each anchor franchise:

- **Black Mirror — one clean idea per episode.** Each 2.5-min reel literalizes **one** new world-concept,
  turned until it bites. Resist stacking. If an episode needs two new mechanics to work, it's two episodes.
  → the **per-episode reveal budget** (§4).
- **Star Trek — episodic surface, serialized undertow.** Every episode stands alone for a cold viewer
  ([[writing-requirements]] `ANTH-04`) while a slow arc compounds underneath ([[setting-sail-tier-descent]]'s
  descent). The lore breakdowns ([[distribution-strategy]] §2, the 16:9 deep-dives) are our "tech manuals" —
  where the *dissecting* viewer is rewarded with the depth the episode withheld.
- **Rick and Morty — throwaway density + canon hoarding.** Drop world-detail fast and *unexplained*
  (a slang term, a UI readout, a glimpsed place) and **trust the audience to bank it.** Never stop the
  story to gloss it. The unexplained throwaway is a deposit the wiki/breakdowns cash later (§6).

## 3. The four reveal valves — *how* a fact is allowed on screen
A world-fact may only reach the audience through one of these. **A lecture is never a valve**
(this is `WORLD-05` / `THEME-02` enforced across the *arc*, not just the scene):

1. **Diegetic UI** — the [[the-perspective-lens-ui|Perspective Lens]] *is* the gauge: a new readout,
   a single word, a prompt. (E6 *Tether*; E10 the *Pain* circle; E17 *Organic 0% · Ghost: ACTIVE*;
   E16 *MERGE ⟷ REMAIN SEPARATE*.) The viewer learns the world *alongside Raul* — the show's core device.
2. **Lived experience** — the mechanic happens *to* a character, with a cost. (E5 the down-clock & job
   loss; E7 the "slight itch"; E13 waking up chrome.) The rule is felt before it's named.
3. **The wonder beat** — pure scale, almost no words, one per episode where it earns it
   (`CINE-05`). (E9 the lunar servers + Mirror Constellation: the body of the Grid, shown not explained.)
4. **Idiom & slang** — castes and customs surface as overheard language, never a glossary. (E8 *"shell
   with a ghost within," "Vapor," "Glacial"* — [[frequency-tribes]] slang dropped Rick-&-Morty-fast.)

## 4. The per-episode reveal budget
Default ceiling, deviate only when an episode *is* the reveal (e.g. a mid-season climax):

> **≤ 1 *major* new world-concept + ≤ 2 *minor* details per episode.**

- **Major** = a new load-bearing system or caste the arc will reuse (the Shadow tether; the Cyborg Slip;
  velocity tiers; the Memory-Substrate curse). One per reel.
- **Minor** = a detail that colors the major without opening a new system (a UI word, one slang term,
  a place glimpsed in a wonder beat).
- **Payoff over setup.** Mid/late episodes should spend more on **paying off** planted guns than on
  planting new ones; an episode that introduces three brand-new systems is over budget (see Finding F-1).

## 5. Foreshadow ≠ spoiler
Both plant the future; only one *answers* it early.

| | Plants a **question** (good) | Answers it **early** (spoiler) |
|---|---|---|
| **Effect** | the viewer leans in, banks a curiosity | the turn is spent before it's earned |
| **Examples (do)** | E4 billboard glitches to raw code → *what's underneath?* (paid E10); E9 *"Solar weather advisory — elevated"* telemetry (paid E10); E6 Ghosts pass with **pity** → *what do they know?* (paid E7/E17) | — |
| **Guard (don't)** | — | E7 shows the Cyborg Slip happen *to Jun*: keep the **mechanism** known but Raul's **personal** slip and its **timing** unspoiled, so E17 still lands. Foreshadow the *what*, never the *when-to-Raul*. |

Rule of thumb: a plant is safe if it raises a question the audience can't yet answer. The moment a scene
lets them *complete* a future episode's turn, it's a leak.

### Permanent mysteries — protect from *ever* over-explaining
Some gaps stay gaps even past the finale ([[fantasy-writing]] §17 — the reader co-builds what you leave
blank). Do not bottom these out, in S1 or later:
- **~~The Distributed President's true nature~~** — moved 2026-11-02: **held until S3**, which reveals her as Mara. In S1–S2 she stays a villain-less systemic weather.
- **The AI Guide** ([[canon-registry|`CHR-03`]]) — is the Teddy-Bear/Guide *ever* untrustworthy? Keep it open.
- **What lies "under" the render** beyond raw code — don't reveal a final substrate; the descent is the point.
- **E20's actual choice** — the finale cuts *before* the Purist mother chooses; the *holding* was the point.
  Never shoot it clean. *(Amended 2026-11-02: the result may be known, as S2 requires, and the moment may appear
  only through a lossy memory.)*

## 7. How it's audited
- **REVEAL layer** in [[writing-requirements]] (`REVEAL-01..06`) — gates the budget (§4), the valve (§3),
  the spoiler guard (§5), and the Reserve Vault (§6); scores the season's drip-balance and mystery economy.
- **Perspective P9** in [[audit-script]] — runs on a full-season (`TARGET=ANTHOLOGY`) pass, or on a single
  episode *in sequence* (it needs the arc to judge cadence; skip with a note for a context-free short).
- **The Revelation Ledger** (below) is the evidence base: keep it current as episodes draft, and read it
  as a *column* — each act should drip steadily, no act front-loaded or starved (`REVEAL-05`).
