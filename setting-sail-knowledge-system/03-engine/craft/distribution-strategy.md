# Production Ops — Distribution Strategy (The Barbell)

> The execution of the `Q-13` decision: a **YouTube–TikTok Barbell** — TikTok funds immediate production
> overhead (Creator Rewards) while routing traffic to YouTube to build permanent, monetizable community
> equity. Every 2.5-min episode does **double duty without re-editing the visual assets.** Companion to
> [[market-research]] / [[ai-production-toolkit]].
>
> ⚠️ **Freshness:** payout rules, length thresholds, and program names (Creator Rewards, YPP) are
> early-2026 facts that change fast — **re-verify before launch.**

## 1. The TikTok engine — maximizing Creator Rewards
- **Beat the 60-second retention trap.** Most creators lose viewers by 15 s; the episodes' immediate
  high-concept hooks (Raul calmly facing a hull breach inside a Tier-3 dilation window) capture early
  watch-time.
- **Payout optimization.** Creator Rewards needs **>60 s**; at **150 s** the episodes sit in the premium
  tier, letting the algorithm place ads and lift RPM.
- **The in-universe CTA.** Never "Link in bio!" Instead, end on a clean diegetic
  [[the-perspective-lens-ui|system notification]]: `[ Full Memory Ledger Decrypted at
  YouTube.com/IntentionInContext ]` — the funnel never breaks immersion.

## 2. The YouTube sanctuary — the value funnel
Upload the 9:16 files as **regular (mid-form) videos, not Shorts** (which cap at 60 s) — this unlocks the
full Partner Program, search indexing, and community features.
- **Playlists (the binge factor).** Sequence into episodic playlists (*Season 1: The Tier Descent*); the
  algorithm rewards playlist watch-time and auto-recommends the next episode.
- **The SEO trapdoor.** The audience searches "existential sci-fi," "AI governance," "simulation
  paradoxes" — keyword-rich, conceptually dense descriptions pull passive Google traffic for years.
- **Long-form lore breakdowns.** Every 3–4 episodes, release a **16:9, ~10-min** breakdown analyzing the
  sociology of the [[frequency-tribes|Dunbar-150 Tribes]], the [[intent-patch|CVP hacks]], or the
  [[chromatic-expressionism|Rothko art-history]] choices. *These are the wiki turned into video* — and
  they command high CPMs from tech/hardware sponsors.

## 3. Production-ready runtime map (the 2.5-min episode template)
A **default pacing matrix** every script should hit (deviate only when the story demands — *use a beat
because it earns it*, [[scifi-cinematography]] §2):

| Time | Beat | Chromatic style | Algorithmic goal |
|------|------|-----------------|------------------|
| **0:00–0:05** | **The disorienting hook** — crisis / system paradox | [[chromatic-expressionism|Caravaggio]] tenebrism or a violent color shift | stop the swipe on the TikTok FYP |
| **0:05–1:15** | **Act I — Context:** Raul navigates the rules | [[chromatic-expressionism|Albers]] structural lines (terminals, architecture) | cross the **60-s monetizable threshold** |
| **1:15–2:15** | **Act II — Friction:** the ethical trade-off peaks | [[chromatic-expressionism|Rothko]] bleeding color fields, built into the set | deep psychological engagement + watch-time |
| **2:15–2:30** | **Resolution & cliffhanger:** the ledger logs the choice | [[chromatic-expressionism|Turrell]] neon void + clean text overlay | shares, profile clicks, YT subscriptions |

This is also a **craft control**: the four beats map the [[the-doughnut-principle|Doughnut → Macro-Doughnut]]
turn onto the algorithm's retention curve — and four of the five chromatic palettes appear in every
episode by design.
