## 2. Fixes (≈80 edits)
- **Clock (`WR-T4`):** E1–E5 re-stamped to S2E1 ≈ T+19.25, death ≈ T+19.5 (`Q-127`).
- **Geography:** the village is one ridge from the airlock (E1, E2, E19; `Q-128`). The feed camera sits on the post
  across the yard, so the notches are in its frame.
- **Threads:** Ruth's cord paid in E19; "Hurried" reminded in E19; the TTD bar near empty in E17; the forks planted
  by one frame in E8.
- **Repetition:** "Are you lost?" kept for E19 only; candle hooks replaced; the chains compared once (E13); E17
  begins the descent so it differs from E16.
- **Tells removed:** E8, E10, E11, E13 shots rewritten as images; E12 dialogue cut from ~64 to ~40 words.
- **Small facts:** glyph hollow seven reels, not ten; 35 years, not forty; thousandths, not hundredths; E16's window
  three hours ten minutes; E15's "Will your mother?"; the chrome seam on the left ribs.
- **Glossary (`WR-N1`, `WR-N2`):** 28 new rows (UI strings, the village feed, sector nine, SYSTEM lines, "Did I pass?",
  "Are you lost?"), eight rows amended; `EXIT · APEX · 2.4 h` and "TTD bar" unified.
