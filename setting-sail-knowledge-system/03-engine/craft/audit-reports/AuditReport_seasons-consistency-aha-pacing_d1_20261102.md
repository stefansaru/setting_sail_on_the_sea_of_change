## 5. Pacing rules for a 150-second reel
**Budget.** 150 s at about 2.5 words a second is a hard ceiling of 375 words, but this show lives on images.
Target **≤ 90 spoken words** (about 36 s), ≤ 120 for a flagship. S1 mostly sits well under this.

**One reel's beats**
| Time | Job |
|------|-----|
| 0:00–0:05 | The hook works cold. No recap. |
| 0:05–0:30 | **Place the plant in the open,** in a calm moment, doing a second job so it doesn't look like a clue. |
| 0:30–1:45 | Pressure. A viewer should form a wrong assumption here. |
| **1:45–2:00** | **Recall cue:** one quick image of the earlier plant, ≤ 2 s. Weeks may have passed since they saw it. |
| **2:05–2:20** | **The turn. One image or one UI line, never a sentence that explains.** |
| 2:20–2:30 | **Silence.** Hold, then black. The viewer does the sum. Don't resolve it. |

**A-ha rules**
1. **One per reel.** A new fact or a payoff can be the turn, not both, unless the reel *is* the reveal.
2. **Two layers.** A cold viewer gets a complete surface turn; a serial viewer gets the second meaning.
3. **Three touches for anything long-range:** plant, reminder, payoff. Long-range means across seasons.
4. **Minimum gap of three reels** between plant and payoff. Sooner reads as setup.
5. **Never two big a-has in adjacent reels.** Follow each with a reel of people, not plot.
6. **The viewer finishes the sentence.** If a character says the realization, you've spent it.
7. **Rotate the ending.** Nearly every S1 reel closes on a UI change and "cut to black", which sells mood more
   often than reversal. Aim for a true reversal in at least one reel of every three.
8. **Final frame echoes the first** where possible, so the loop on TikTok rewards a rewatch.

**Across a 20-reel season**
| Size | How many | Where |
|------|----------|-------|
| Small (same act) | About 1 per reel | Reel endings |
| Medium (same season) | 4 | E5, E10, E15, E20 |
| Large (cross-season) | 1–2 per season | Mid-season, finale, or first reel |
