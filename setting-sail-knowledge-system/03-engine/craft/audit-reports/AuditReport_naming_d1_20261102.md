# Audit Report — Naming (d1, 2026-11-02)

> **Tests:** Does the name carry meaning the story uses? Can it be told apart at caption speed? Is the claimed
> meaning actually true? Names were changed only when one of these failed badly or a better name added story.

## Changed
| Was | Now | Why |
|-----|-----|-----|
| **Maya** | **Vesper** | *Mara/Maya* is one letter apart, both are women, and both matter in S2–S3. Maya's meaning ("illusion") was excellent, but the collision is worse at caption speed. Vesper is the evening star, which is the same star as the morning star, never seen in one sky at once: two people, one light, different clocks. |
| **The S1–S2 mother, "Ruth"** | **Naomi** | Decided under `Q-44`. Unnamed on screen in S1, so no script changed. |
| **The Centauri Clone** | **Rafe** | He is no longer at Centauri, and "clone" denies what E15 insists on: he's a person. Rafe is an old form of Ralph, the same root as Raul (*Radulf*): one name, drifted over distance. His partner gives it to him. "The Centauri Clone" stays as the file slug and a descriptive label. |
| **The rushed-body process** *(new)* | **The Quickening** | *Quick* means both alive and fast. Grid slang: the Quick. Purist slang: the Hurried. |
| **The incarnate child** *(`Q-23`, unset)* | **Naomi** | In the Book of Ruth, Naomi ("pleasant") says *call me Mara* ("bitter"). Here Mara comes back and Ruth names her Naomi. The verse runs backward. |
| **Alpha Centauri mining station** | **"the Station"** | Crew nickname for Hull Four. It hides the S4 reveal in plain sight. |
| **The One-in-N Rule** | **The Validation** *(title; slug kept)* | Your word for it, and a better one. |

## Corrected meaning
| Name | Problem | Fix |
|------|---------|-----|
| **Kai** | Canon called it a homophone of "key". It isn't: Kai rhymes with "sky". | Keep the name. It means "sea" in Hawaiian and Japanese, and in Greek *kai* means **"and"**, written the same. That suits the character who must hold *I escaped* **and** *my body is dust*. Claim corrected. |

## Accepted risks
- **Raul / Ruth / Rafe** all start with R. Raul and Rafe share a root on purpose. Raul and Ruth share scenes, so
  give Ruth the first line in any scene they open together.
- **Vance / Vesper:** they never share a scene, and sound nothing alike.
- **Naomi twice:** deliberate. Call the child "the child" until she's old enough to need the name.
- **Hull Cut** keeps its one Ruth, by design.

## Canon status
`Q-08`, `Q-16`, `Q-23`, `Q-27`, `Q-34` closed. Dated audit reports keep the old names as historical record.
