# Audit Report — Season 1 Revelation Cadence (information rationing)

- **Date:** 2026-06-05 · **Target:** all 20 drafted episode scripts (`scripts/s1e01…s1e20`), read as a
  sequence. **Layer:** the new **REVEAL** set ([[writing-requirements]] `REVEAL-01..06`), perspective
  **P9** of [[audit-script]]. **Guide:** [[revelation-cadence]] (the Ledger + Reserve Vault).
- **Question (author brief):** is world-information revealed to the audience *gradually* — a manageable,
  *Black Mirror / Star Trek / Rick-and-Morty* cadence — **without spoilers**, not all at once, with **some
  detail left hidden**, only the most relevant beats surfaced and made spectacular?
- **Verdict:** **PASS** (one P2 OWNER + three P3 watch-items; rest confirmations). The season is
  cadence-disciplined: **one major reveal per reel**, delivered through a valve (never a lecture), no
  premature payoffs, and **~half the bible deliberately withheld.** The one real defect is **E12**
  (over-budget, unpaid); E5 and E11 sit *at* the ceiling (hold), E6 is the lone exposition-register risk.
- **Two passes:** (1) season-level cadence + Reserve Vault; (2) a per-episode gate matrix (below), each
  episode's actual text checked against `REVEAL-01..04`. The per-episode pass added F-5/F-6.

## What's working (confirmations — the model to keep)
- **One clean idea per reel (`REVEAL-01`).** Each episode literalizes a single major concept and turns it
  (Black-Mirror discipline). Only the mid-season climax (E10) and the keystone (E17) spend big, *because
  they are the reveal.* No reel stacks multiple new systems except E5/E11/E12 (above) — and only E12
  goes *over* (E5/E11 hold because their loads converge on one lived event).
- **Reveals arrive via valves, never lectures (`REVEAL-03`).** Diegetic UI (*Tether*, *Pain* circle,
  *Organic 0%*, *MERGE ⟷ REMAIN SEPARATE*), lived cost (the down-clock, the itch, waking chrome), wonder
  beats (E9), and overheard slang (E8 *Vapor/Glacial*). `WORLD-05`/`THEME-02` hold across the whole arc.
- **Plant-then-pay, never pre-pay (`REVEAL-02`).** E4 glitch→E10 storm; E9 telemetry→E10; E6 Ghost-pity→
  E7/E17; E19 hand-image→E20. Every plant raises a question the audience *can't yet answer.*
- **Steady drip, no front-load (`REVEAL-05` = 5).** Act I drips the *frame*, II the *self*, III the
  *paradoxes*, IV the *descent home*; payoffs land where guns were planted (see Ledger column reading).
- **The world feels larger than it shows (`REVEAL-06` = 5).** ~Half the bible is reserved — governance,
  the Law/antagonist engine, vice/commerce, the Tribes/Maya, TTD, Data Graveyards, Hope's carceral half.
  That off-screen mass is exactly the "airtight global framework" this audience rewards ([[market-research]] §2),
  and four permanent mysteries are protected from ever bottoming out.
