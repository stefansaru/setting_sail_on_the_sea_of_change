## 2. Fixes (≈70 edits)
- **The strike minute:** Kai at capsule 10 in bay C; Teo paused until the 09:40:00 drop, then woken for the watch; the
  bore fires at the close of the lock's 59th second ("Fifty-nine. Six—"); `LOCK 00/60` everywhere.
- **Physics:** kill confirmation about 1 min 50 s (E1 and E10 agree); E20 moved to about T+8.0, a week before the brake
  burn (braking 1.94 years); the disc resolved by array composite.
- **Clock gaps:** E11's Tier-1 triage lift explains the brake; E16's beam waits unopened in Hull One's queue.
- **Threads:** Decimus's nine forks reminded on the E15 board; E7's crowd costed in watts.
- **Wording:** Kai's pronoun slips removed; "shield" retired for "screen"; counting and frozen-figure repetitions
  thinned; E4 opens on the ramp board; E17's turn less literal.
- **Glossary:** about 30 new rows (UI strings, Bay C, re-still, voyage stamps, lines), reactor from S4E11, the motif
  row corrected; the fleet table's E20 row updated.
