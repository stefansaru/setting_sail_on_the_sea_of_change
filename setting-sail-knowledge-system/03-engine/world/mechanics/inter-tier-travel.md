> Because each tier runs on a different processing clock, a mind can't hop instantly between them
> — it must be accelerated or braked through a buffer until its frequency phase-matches the target.

## Concept
Moving up or down the [[velocity-tiers]] is a *clock-synchronization* problem, not a teleport. A
consciousness enters a **Data Buffer State** in which its processing frequency is systematically
accelerated or decelerated until it perfectly **phase-matches** the destination tier's cycle.

## Rules / mechanics
- **No instant hops** between non-adjacent clocks (e.g. Tier 1 → Tier 3): the frequency must be
  ramped across the gap.
- The buffer **brakes** a mind coming down from a fast tier and **spins up** a mind going to a
  faster one, until clocks align.
- Transition time scales with the size of the clock gap (a design lever for tension).

## In the world / visual language
On screen this reads as **extreme motion blur, or the character freezing entirely** while the
universe around them radically accelerates into streaks of light — or drops into a glacial
standstill. (A natural home for the dolly-zoom / time-dilation moves in
[[cinematography-techniques]]; record the convention in `VIS-04`.)

## Story role
A built-in suspense valve: the buffer is a vulnerable, in-between moment — you are neither here
nor there, and time is doing something violent to you.

## Open questions
- Can a transition be *interrupted* mid-buffer, and what happens to the mind if so? Undecided.
