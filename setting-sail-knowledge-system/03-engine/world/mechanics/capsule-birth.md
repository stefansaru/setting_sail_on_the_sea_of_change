> How a citizen comes into being: an **IVF embryo** is placed into a real-world **capsule** and wired into
> the Grid. The child is **physically born in the real world** — the capsule placement *is* the birth — but
> **begins and experiences life entirely inside the simulation.** Birth is split from biography.

## Concept
In this world, **conception and birth are real-world, physical, clinical events; *life* — experience,
learning, identity — begins in the simulation.** To be *born* is to be placed in the capsule and connected;
to *live* is to wake in the Tier-3 sim. There is no "alive but unborn" limbo: the capsule is the cradle, and
crossing into it is the birth. (This is the rule the S1E1 dialogue turns on — *"And our baby… it's born?" /
"Yes. Inside the capsule. Safe, warm, learning."*)

## Rules / mechanics
- **Conception (lab / IVF).** An embryo is created by **in-vitro fertilization** — a selected egg + sperm
  fertilized in the lab.
- **Birth (real world).** The fetus is placed into a real-world clinical **capsule** (the same class of
  stasis bay the parents occupy, `FCT-009`) and **connected to the [[the-shadow-grid|Grid]]**. *This
  placement is the birth* — a physical, real-world act (`FCT-012`).
- **First life (in-sim).** The newborn's mind boots inside the simulation at the most dilated tier
  ([[velocity-tiers|Tier 3]]) — *"safe, warm, learning"* (`FCT-010`) — guided from the first moment by the AI
  Guide / Teddy Bear ([[the-perspective-lens-ui]], `FCT-011`). The body rests in the capsule; the self grows
  on the Grid.
- **Quickened.** The capsule forces the body's growth to keep up with the mind, and the cost is real
  ([[the-quickening]]). Grid bodies are barren, so every embryo starts from stored or Purist cells.
- **Consequence — born a Shadow.** The child is a **[[the-shadow-entity|Shadow]]** from birth (body in
  stasis, mind on the Grid), and the body-clock ([[progressive-digitalization]]) starts at **100% organic.**
- **The exception — the Purists.** The [[setting-sail-tier-descent|Natural Purists]] (`FAC-02`) reject this
  entirely: their children are born **mortal, un-capsuled, outside the Grid** — the contrast the series ends
  on (and the origin of **Adam**).

## In the world / look
The cold, real **capsule bay** (`FCT-009`) against the warm, golden **Tier-3 preserve** — the dichotomy that
opens the whole series in **S1E1**: parents walking a flawless simulated meadow while a real, newly-born
fetus sleeps wired in a capsule a world away. The first instance of the Grid's defining gap between the real body
and the lived simulation (the seed of [[the-ageless-default|substrate dysmorphia]]).

## Story role
The series' first image of its central trade: a life that is **materially safe and experientially perfect**,
bought at the cost of **never beginning in the real world it was born into.** It sets up the **Merging**
([[parallel-pedagogy]]) — multi-threaded childhood and ledger-fusion — and the whole descent: **Raul is *born*
a Shadow** (S1E1–2) and spends the season learning what the capsule cost.
