> There are no police and no retroactive trials. Equilibrium is kept by a real-time automated verifier —
> the **CVP** — and your defense isn't a lawyer, it's your **ledger.** If your past intentions
> consistently produced balance, you are **"Proven-in-Use,"** and prosecution dies before it starts.

## Concept
An automated infrastructure node run by the [[the-distributed-president]] that continuously tracks the
**public segments** of every citizen's [[the-personal-blockchain]] and validates behaviour against the
[[intention-in-context-law|Intention-in-Context law database]] — the civic, real-time counterpart to
Hope's punitive [[the-absolution-protocol]].

## Rules / mechanics
- **Real-time intent verification.** The CVP matches a citizen's live actions and recorded
  **intentions-in-contexts** against the global legal-principle database.
- **Algorithmic safety alignment.** For a consequential act (an emergency medical download, a localized
  asset relocation), the CVP weighs **background environmental metrics** *plus* the user's active
  **Intention squares `[▢]`** to confirm it aligns with safety parameters — judging *intent in context*,
  not the bare act.
- **The "Proven-in-Use" defense.** If a citizen's history has **consistently produced systemic balance
  without network disruption**, the CVP mints an **un-falsifiable cryptographic compliance token.**
  Challenged by a human merit-holder (e.g. **Vance**, `CHR-04`), the citizen doesn't hire a lawyer —
  they **submit their CVP ledger.** Mathematically absolute proof of past alignment **shuts down the
  prosecution** before it can touch their network-priority status.

## In the world
Justice is **pre-emptive and data-driven**: you are what your ledger has provably done. *("Proven-in-
use" is a real safety-engineering doctrine — components trusted by demonstrated operating history;
here it's promoted to constitutional law.)*

## Story role
The **conflict engine** for the meritocracy: a high-merit challenger (**Vance**, `CHR-04`) versus a
citizen's Proven-in-Use record. The series' diagram literally pits *"Raul's historical sins"* against
the law database — so **Vance can come for Adam** (S2E12–15; his S3 stake in Raul is still open, `Q-10`), and Raul's defense is whether his intentions have, in
fact, aligned. (Helps answer audit S-09: an opposing-worldview antagonist.)

## Open questions
- *(Answered)* **Can the CVP be gamed?** Yes — the [[intent-patch]] exploit (`MAG-21`) spoofs the
  Intention Squares to broadcast false "balance." Looking aligned ≠ being aligned — the season's drama.
