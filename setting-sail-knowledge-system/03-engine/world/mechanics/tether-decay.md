> The Grid never sleeps — there is **no REM, no dreaming** in the simulation, only unceasing logical
> execution. A [[the-ghost-entity|Ghost]] handles infinite wakefulness fine. A tethered
> [[the-shadow-entity|Shadow]] cannot: the biological brain in the capsule **still needs to dream**, and if it
> can't, it will **tear itself off the machine.** Sleep becomes a survival countdown.

## Concept
Digital consciousness is a continuous data stream — **dreamless.** But a Shadow's physical brain-cache
([[the-memory-substrate]]) in its stasis capsule still needs organic maintenance: the subconscious dream
state purges neural waste and reorganizes synapses. Starved of it, the brain **degrades, then
hallucinates independently** of the sim — a phase-mismatch between the digital **identity-key** and the
physical cache that **decays the network link.** This is the mechanism beneath
[[the-shadow-entity|Tether Sickness]]: the felt panic *is* tether decay.

## Rules / mechanics — the TTD telemetry (CVP-tracked)
The [[compliance-validation-protocol|CVP]] tracks two metrics per citizen:
- **Tether Decay Rate (λ_td)** — the real-time velocity at which the biological link is desynchronizing
  from the ledger.
- **Time to Decay (TTD)** — the countdown until the brain-cache suffers **irreversible structural
  collapse**, after which the tether severs and the citizen must **die or transition instantly into a
  [[the-ghost-entity|Ghost]].**

$$ TTD = \frac{\Psi_{\text{organic}}}{\lambda_{td}\,\cdot\,V_{\text{tier}}} $$

- **Ψ_organic** = remaining organic brain resilience · **V_tier** = the velocity multiplier (60× / 3,600×
  / 86,400×).
- **The Tier-3 emergency.** At Tier 3 a citizen burns a *subjective year* of wakefulness in ~6 real
  minutes, so TTD can fall **100% → 1% in a single real-world afternoon** — high speed is a biological
  gamble.
- **The body-clock interlock ([[progressive-digitalization]]).** As Ψ_organic drops with each
  cyborg-ization, **TTD shortens** — the more synthetic Raul becomes, the *worse* his dream-hunger and
  the more often he must reset, a tightening noose that helps make his **E17 Ghost-transition feel
  inevitable.** (A 0%-organic Ghost has *no* tether → no TTD: freedom, bought by losing the body that
  dreamed.)

```
[◯] Substrate: Tier 2 (Hour Velocity) · Wakefulness: 4 subjective months
[▢] λ_td: 4.12% / real-hour
[!] TIME TO DECAY: 02:14:09 — INITIATE MANDATORY SUB-VELOCITY SABBATICAL NOW
```

## The Sub-Velocity Sabbatical (the Dream Vacation)
To avoid catastrophic failure, citizens budget [[velocity-tiers|velocity credits]] for mandatory
**Sabbaticals**: down-clock to **1:1**, disconnect from the [[frequency-tribes|tribe]], push the mind
fully back down the antenna into the living tissue — and **live as a pure mortal**: close your eyes,
have a warm, messy biological sleep, and **dream.** *The paradox:* to the high-tier elite the physical
world is a primitive, low-res **vacation resort** where they're forced to slow down, disconnect, and
endure the vulnerability of **human forgetting** ([[the-memory-substrate]]) just to keep their tethers
alive. (As a *leisure* ritual this becomes the **Glacial Vacation** — see [[recreation-culture]].)

## Story role
A structural survival clock that drives immediate pacing tension (the **[[the-dream-hunger|Dream
Hunger]]** reel); the quantification of Tether Sickness; and the rising pressure that makes Raul's E17
death/transition land as *earned*, not arbitrary.
