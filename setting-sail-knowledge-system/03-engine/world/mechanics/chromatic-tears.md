> Inside the high-velocity tiers, data *is* architecture and pure color field — so a virus is not
> corrupted code you read, it's an aesthetic wound you **see**: jagged, hyper-saturated shards that
> tear across a serene scene and accelerate the victim toward death.

## Concept
On the [[the-shadow-grid]], a citizen running a high [[velocity-tiers|velocity tier]] experiences raw
data as inhabited space rendered through their [[the-perspective-lens-ui|Perspective Lens UI]]. Malware
therefore can't hide in unreadable code — it manifests as a **violent corruption of the architecture
and color itself**: the **Chromatic Tear.** It is the dark twin of the world's [[chromatic-expressionism]]
visual grammar, the same palette logic turned into a weapon.

## Rules / mechanics — costs, limits, consequences
- **Manifestation.** A Tear appears as jagged, hyper-saturated, **flickering artifact shards** that
  pierce clean, symmetrical **Albers** nested squares or rip apart a serene **Turrell** neon void
  (the calm palettes of `VIS-15`). It is corruption made visible — beauty desynchronizing.
- **The attack vector — UI desync.** Tears actively **bleed across the frame** and corrupt the
  victim's Perspective Lens, forcing the **Intention squares `[▢]`** to flicker rapidly. This
  desynchronizes the victim's local processing clock against their tier.
- **The lethal payload.** The clock desync artificially **accelerates the victim's
  [[tether-decay|Tether Decay Rate (TTD)]]** — burning down the survival countdown — while the visual
  chaos drives the mind toward panic. The damage is to the *substrate timing*, not just the image.
- **Limit / tell.** Because it corrupts the *rendering*, a Tear can't fully disguise itself as a clean
  scene — the flicker and saturation are its signature, which is exactly what the
  [[compliance-validation-protocol|CVP]] flags before a [[velocity-braking|Velocity-Brake]].

## In the world / look
A Tear is the worst thing you can witness inside a tier that is otherwise rendered as sublime calm: a
[[chromatic-expressionism|Turrell]] infinity void suddenly knifed by screaming neon shards, the floor's
nested squares stuttering. The serenity of the palette is the point — the horror is watching order
*tear*. See [[chromatic-expressionism]] / [[the-perspective-lens-ui]].

## Story role
Makes a cyber-attack **cinematic and embodied** rather than abstract — a virus you can shoot in 9:16,
with a body-clock stakes meter ([[tether-decay|TTD]]) ticking down. A Tear loosed in a high tier is a
ticking-clock set piece: panic plus accelerating decay, resolved (or not) by a [[velocity-braking|
Velocity-Brake]]. Vectors trace back to the [[the-ghost-brokers|Ghost Brokers]] who deploy them.

## Open questions
- Can a Tear be *authored* (a targeted assassination weapon keyed to one victim's Lens), or is it only
  broadcast contagion? Sets whether it's a murder tool or a plague.
