> Dying Purists take Hope's DNA rewrite to keep a mortal body alive (Kavana, S1E11) — a 99%-safe
> procedure whose 1% failures are fatal within 24 hours, and whose only escape is the Apex.

## Concept
A **terminal Purist**, who rejects both the [[the-shadow-grid|simulation]] and the [[the-cyborg-slip|cyborg path]], may take Hope's last-resort **DNA correction**: a rewrite of the genetic code and telomeres that buys a mortal body more years, never agelessness (the vow, "One life, kept whole", `Q-74`).

## Rules / mechanics
- **99% success / 1% catastrophe.** The treatment is highly stable, but the 1% failure manifests
  as spectacular, fatal genetic collapse — an absolute real-world limit of **24 hours to live**.
- **The Apex Escape Loop.** A 1% victim can apply for an emergency transfer to a **Tier 3 (Day
  Velocity)** capsule ([[velocity-tiers]]). Hope's credit buys about **2.4 outside hours** of it: a subjective **24-year
  lifetime** inside — long enough to run complex biochemical models and **invent a customized
  cure**, then execute it in the physical world via automated surgical rigs.
- **The cost of surviving:** they return to the outside as a **heavily modified cyborg body**,
  keeping only a few original parts — the very fate the purist path tried to avoid.
- **If the Loop fails** (no cure found in time, or a catastrophic collapse), the body dies and the
  mind is force-uploaded as a [[the-ghost-entity|Ghost]] — the failure outcome catalogued by
  [[the-medical-sanctuary]]. The cyborg and ghost results are the **two branches of the same 24-hour
  window.**

## In the world
The risk drives the **Cultural Chain-Check** custom (see [[the-personal-blockchain]]): peers press
the few who opted out of Full Disclosure to re-open their ledgers (`Q-124`) before a high-risk DNA rewrite — *"you're tracking a 99%
success rate today; sure you don't want your chain public, just in case?"*

## Story role
A clean ticking-clock engine (24 hours / 24 years) with a bitter irony at the end: the purist
who survives becomes the cyborg they refused to be. Strong material for a single-protagonist short.
