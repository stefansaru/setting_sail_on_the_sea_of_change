> Minds live years in hours, so capsules force the body to keep up. An adult can be grown in weeks. Nobody
> gets that for free: a Quickened body is short-lived, barren and thin, and the Grid calls this normal.

## Concept
To *quicken* means both to come alive and to speed up. The capsule does both. Because a mind at speed outruns
its flesh, the capsule drives the body's growth so that a thirty-year-old mind doesn't sit in an infant. Raul's
body is an adult at **14 weeks**. Everyone on the Grid is like this. Almost nobody looks.

Slang: the Grid says *Quick*, as in "the quick and the dead". The Purists say **the Hurried**.

## Rules / mechanics
- **Rate.** Childhood runs in days and adulthood arrives in about three months ([[velocity-tiers]]).
- **Chemistry, not time.** Growth is paid for in nutrients and telomere length, not years.

### The side-effects of rushing
1. **Short flesh.** Growth spends the body's lifespan up front. A Quickened body rarely lasts ten real years.
   *"Eleven good ones"* (S1E19) is more than Raul's flesh will ever get.
2. **Constant wear.** Organs fail young, so replacing them is routine. This is why almost everyone drifts
   toward chrome: [[progressive-digitalization]] isn't a choice, it's maintenance. Telomere damage is also why
   [[the-medical-sanctuary|Hope's DNA therapy]] has a market.
3. **The hollow cache.** The brain is the Shadow's fallback memory ([[the-shadow-entity]]), and a Quickened brain
   has barely been used. When a tether fails, a mind with decades on its ledger drops into a brain that has
   lived weeks. That's what makes Tether Sickness so terrifying: not lost memory, but almost nothing to land on.
4. **Growing pains.** While the body races, stress signals spike with no cause on the Grid. Children feel a
   dread they can't place. It also trips the [[the-pattern-detector|detector's]] stress trigger, so
   some "loops" a child is warned about are only their bones.
5. **Barrenness.** A Quickened body can't conceive or carry. Every Grid child starts from a stored egg and
   sperm ([[capsule-birth]]). **Only the unrushed can carry a child**, which is why a god who wants to be born
   needs a Purist (S3).

## In the world
Host age is on every Shadow's vitals, in a menu no one opens. Asking someone theirs is rude. In the Purist
village a chrome stranger is told, kindly, that he looks tired for a child.

**Aboard the fleet** the ramp ([[the-stasis-economy]]) uses the same process, so a body "woken into flesh" is
Quickened too. The promise of arrival is about a decade long. **That is in the sealed manifest** (S4E18).
Deep stasis pauses a body's clock (`Q-52`), so the decade starts on waking.

## Story role
It answers why people accept the cyborg path, and it sharpens the series' thesis. The Grid gives you centuries
of experience in a body that barely gets to be alive. Purists get one slow life, and they can still make more.
