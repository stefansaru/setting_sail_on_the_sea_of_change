> An automated system analyzes the connections between people and warns you, in your Lens, that a negative
> loop is emerging. Its companion setting goes further: it moves your hand.

## Concept
The first half is a **warning**: pattern analysis across a person's relationships, drawn into the
[[the-perspective-lens-ui|Perspective Lens]] as an amber thread between two figures, tagged **LOOP FORMING**.
The second half is the **automated course-of-action setting**: children's Lenses can fill an Intention square
on their behalf and carry it out, aligning the act with [[the-virtuous|the Virtuous]].

## Rules / mechanics
- **Always-on is expensive.** Continuous analysis of a person's connections costs real compute. *"We can't do
  this for everyone all the time."* Teddy asks for it as a special measure.
- **The on-demand trigger.** The cheap version wakes only when stress is high. Officials call it a **cortisol
  trigger**, but real cortisol peaks 20–30 real minutes after a stressor, about 20 subjective hours at Tier 1.
  So the trigger actually reads a fast **stress marker over the [[the-shadow-entity|tether]]**. "Cortisol" is
  just what people call it.
- **The Ghost gap.** No body means no tether and no trigger. A [[the-ghost-entity|Ghost]] gets the detector
  only by paying for always-on, and few do. **The minds with the most power have the fewest alarms.**
- **Alignment by age.** On by default for minors. Adults may switch it off; almost none do, because it feels
  like being good.
- **What it can't catch.** It reads *loops*, not ends. A sincere, values-aligned act that happens to be
  catastrophic trips nothing (S4E9, Teo).
- **Failure modes.** A solar storm can knock it out (E10). An [[intent-patch]] can spoof the squares it reads.

## In the world
Parents like it. Teachers rely on it. Most adults stop noticing the thread and only register that their
quarrels end quickly. Nobody discusses the moments their hand opened by itself.

## Story role
The cleanest image the series has for its thesis. *Experience is the intention you project onto a context*,
so what happens to intention when the system projects it for you? Teddy is sincere and the system works, and
that is what makes it unsettling. Raul looking at his own open hand (end of [[s1e03b-the-upper-level]]) is the
image the rest of the arc answers. In E19 he switches the Lens off; in E20 he leaves the choice to the mother.

Keep `CHR-03`'s trust question **open** ([[revelation-cadence]] §6): never let an episode conclude that the
setting is simply a cage or simply a gift.
