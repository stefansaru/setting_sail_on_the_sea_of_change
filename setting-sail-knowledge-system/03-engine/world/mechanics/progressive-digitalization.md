> A countdown made of flesh: across the season the protagonist's physical body decays from organic to
> synthetic — an **Organic-to-Synthetic Ratio** ticking down in their stasis capsule that *physically
> mirrors* the psychological descent. "Becoming digital" turned into a tragic, irreversible body fact.

## Concept
While the [[setting-sail-tier-descent|series]] descends the [[velocity-tiers]] *psychologically*, the
protagonist's body descends *biologically* — losing organic mass act by act until nothing human
remains. The ratio is an implicit clock; the more optimized/safe/fast they become, the less alive.

## Rules / mechanics — the four thresholds
| Act | State | Catalyst | Physical change | UI / avatar tell |
|-----|-------|----------|-----------------|------------------|
| **I (Ep 1–5)** | **100% Organic — Shadow** | — | Wholly biological body in an amniotic stasis pod. | Bright, clean "Utopia" UI; warm, fully-human avatar. |
| **II (Ep 6–10)** | **70% Organic — Cyborg** | **Ep 10:** the [[the-shadow-grid-cooling|solar storm]] surge backfires down the biological antenna/[[the-shadow-entity|tether]], cooking the nervous system → organ failure. | Automated pods amputate damaged limbs; spine + respiratory system replaced with cybernetics. | A cold **diagnostic widget** permanently attaches to the 9:16 edge (battery, hydraulic pressure). |
| **III (Ep 11–15)** | **30% Organic — Cyborg** | **Ep 14–15:** [[the-cosmic-clone-paradox|data-beaming/forking]] bandwidth exceeds the organic brain → a voluntary **Neural-Synthetic Upgrade**. | Face, skull, sensory organs → synthetic shielding; only heart, lungs, and core emotional brain remain. | Avatar **glitches to metallic wireframe** under high stress. |
| **IV (Ep 16–20)** | **0% Organic — Ghost** | **Ep 17:** [[the-shadow-entity|Tether Sickness]] triggers systemic rejection; the last cells collapse — the **"slight, passing itch"** ([[the-cyborg-slip]]) — and they become a [[the-ghost-entity|Ghost]]. | No biological body remains. | To walk the surface (Ep 19) they download into a **Cyborg Shell** ([[shells-with-ghosts-within]]). |

## In the world / look
The decay is legible on screen: the diagnostic widget, the wireframe glitches, and finally the
chrome-and-carbon-fiber [[shells-with-ghosts-within|shell]] — set against the warm, mortal flesh of
the [[setting-sail-tier-descent|Purists]] in the Act-IV collision.

## Story role
The season's **tragic momentum**: in chasing optimization, speed, and safety up/through the tiers, the
protagonist systematically trades away the one thing that gave context meaning — **linear, fragile,
mortal life**. By Ep 20 they are an immortal mind in a cold casing, looking into a warm harbor they can
never truly return to. The body-clock is the silent counter to the [[the-macro-doughnut|Arrow of
Balance]] they must still reach.

**The memory curse (with [[the-memory-substrate]]).** Replacing organic brain with quantum processors
doesn't just synthesize the body — it **destroys the only tissue that could forget.** So the
protagonist descends *toward* the warm, forgetting, mortal Purist world while losing the very capacity
to forget. In Ep 19 the Purist mother's memory of the chrome-hand-over-flesh moment will fade as she
ages and dies; the 0%-organic protagonist is cursed to recall its exact warmth and micro-pressure
**flawlessly, forever.** The body-clock and the memory-clock are the same tragedy.
