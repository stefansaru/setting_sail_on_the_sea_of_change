> The fatal flaw in a meritocracy that judges **intent**: you can **fake the intent.** A black-market
> patch makes a malicious act broadcast a flawless signature of *"Balance"* — and the automated
> [[compliance-validation-protocol|CVP]] blesses it.

## Concept
Because the [[compliance-validation-protocol|CVP]] measures *intent-in-context* rather than the raw
physical act ([[intention-in-context-law]]), the system can be spoofed at the **intent layer.** Citizens
under extreme optimization anxiety buy **Intent Patches** to do it.

## Rules / mechanics
- **The supply.** Patches are manufactured and sold on the black market by the [[the-clock-brokers|Clock Brokers]] (the desperate end) and wielded with surgical polish by merit-holders like Vance (the elite end).
- **The hack.** Before an unauthorized transaction or malicious data-copy, the user runs a black-market
  script that hooks their [[the-perspective-lens-ui|Perspective Lens UI]] and forces the bottom
  **Intention Squares `[▢]`** to broadcast a mathematically flawless signature of **"Structural Balance"
  / "Sublime Contemplation"** to the watching system.
- **The blindspot.** The CVP reads the **false intent mask**, logs the action as completely harmonious,
  and mints an **un-falsifiable [[compliance-validation-protocol|"Proven-in-Use"]] token** — clean record,
  rotten act.

## In the world
The gap between **looking aligned and being aligned** is now a purchasable product. The perfect-merit
society has a perfect-merit underworld.

## Story role
**Vance's weapon (`CHR-04`).** An antagonist can execute devastating systemic shifts while holding a
spotless automated record of innocence — the system's own logic shields him. (This resolves the CVP
page's open "can it be gamed?" — *yes, here's how.*) It also re-stakes the whole thesis: the world
trusts the *signal* of intention; the show insists on the *substance* — the [[the-macro-doughnut]] can't
be spoofed, only the metric can.
