> The time-dilation hierarchy: digitized minds run at strictly tiered clock speeds, so a second
> outside can be a minute, an hour, or a whole day of lived experience inside.

## Concept
Humanity does not experience existence at a uniform speed. While the physical universe runs at
**Natural Time (1:1)**, minds on the [[the-shadow-grid]] are organized across a strict
meritocratic ladder of processing speeds. Higher tiers buy more *subjective lifetime* per
outside second — the world's central resource and status marker.

## Rules / mechanics
| Tier | Name | Rate (outside → inside) | Purpose / inhabitants |
|------|------|--------------------------|-----------------------|
| **0** | Real-Time Velocity | 1s = 1s | Pure biological humans, [[the-cyborg-slip|progressive cyborgs]], heavy space-mining machinery, maintenance sub-routines |
| **1** | Minute Velocity | 1s = 1 min (60×) | Civic administration, standard education, baseline digital life |
| **2** | Hour Velocity | 1s = 1 hr (3,600×) | High-level engineering, systemic problem-solving, advanced R&D |
| **3** | Day Velocity / **The Apex** | 1s = 1 day (86,400×) | Crisis management, evolutionary design, premium generational incubation |

- **The Apex math:** one real hour = about 10 subjective years, and a real day = about **237 years**. A subjective
  **24-year lifetime** takes **2.4 real hours**, and Apex time is rationed (`Q-42`).
- **Allocation is meritocratic** (per [[the-shadow-grid]]): your **baseline** tier follows capability +
  society's processing priority, not free choice. Any caste ([[the-shadow-entity|Shadow]] or
  [[the-ghost-entity|Ghost]]) can reach any tier **while connected** — the gate is priority, not biology. *(On Earth. Fleet stasis
  can't hold fast clocks: [[the-stasis-economy]], `Q-46`.)* Bodies keep up through [[the-quickening]].
- **Velocity credits** (resolves audit S-05) are **temporary processing allocations** earned as
  incentives — compress an urgent project into a fraction of a second — **not** purchases of a
  permanent tier/class.
- Moving between tiers is non-trivial — see [[inter-tier-travel]].

## The subjective clock — high speed feels *normal*
Crucially, **a high tier does not feel rushed.** Because a mind's cognitive cycle *matches* its
processing frequency, time inside any tier feels **completely normal and serene** — no white noise, no
urgency, no panic. A Tier-3 entity processes complex legal/philosophical updates "with the unhurried
serenity of a person reading a book on a quiet afternoon."
- **Algorithmic Compression (Tier-3 language).** At Tier 3, spoken language has evolved away: tribes
  flash dense **symbolic data-packets** across their interface lenses, each packet *expanding instantly*
  into a rich, leisurely landscape of meaning. To an outside viewer, **an entire afternoon of Tier-3
  debate looks like a single microscopic blink** of a status light.
- **Outrunning death.** The cinematic payoff: a real-world catastrophe can unfold in a brutal fraction
  of a second while, inside, the mind has **hours of calm to react.** A 0.5-second hull-breach death at
  Tier 0 = **12 subjective hours** of stillness at Tier 3 — long enough to audit your ledger, patch the
  [[compliance-validation-protocol|CVP]], and calmly sever your [[the-ghost-entity|Identity-Key into a
  Ghost]] before the body is even crushed. The Grid isn't just *faster* — it's a **sanctuary you can
  hide inside from your own death.**

## In the world
Tier is rank, and a [[frequency-tribes|Frequency Tribe]] is the ~150 minds who share your clock. The
Apex is the pinnacle of simulated reality and the engine of the [[the-1-percent-mutation|Apex Escape
Loop]] (2.4 outside hours → 24 subjective years to invent a cure).

## Story role
