> Mind-altering substances are a **regulated health-optimization choice**, not a moral failing. Whether
> a high is *flesh or code* changes everything — including whether you'll **remember it.**

## Concept
The neuro-chemical branch of [[standardized-vices]]. Two substrates, two completely different drugs:
**licensed physical substances** (organic chemistry) and **Disruptive Algorithms** (the digital
equivalent, run as software exploits on the Grid).

## Rules / mechanics
- **The Bio-Fitness License (physical).** To consume real psychedelics / stimulants / depressants
  (LSD, psilocybin, cannabis…), a citizen holds a **periodically-renewing license**. The system screens
  their real-time psychological metrics + public [[the-personal-blockchain|ledger]] for **mental
  resilience** and freedom from **addictive looping patterns**.
- **Controlled Contexts (the physical high).** Use is authorized only in designated spaces — psychedelic
  music festivals, isolated wilderness reserves. You clear morning biometrics, then take a **safe,
  profoundly un-optimized organic trip** that writes only to your **lossy biological cache** — i.e.
  you'll **half-remember it, humanly** ([[the-memory-substrate]]).
- **Disruptive Algorithms (the digital high).** For digital citizens, drugs are *simulated* — coded as
  active **software exploits** that bypass the standard sensory firewalls and rewrite the
  [[the-perspective-lens-ui|Perspective Lens UI]] in ways a physical brain can't process: **shatter
  geometry**, force reality into [[chromatic-expressionism|Turrell color voids]], or **accelerate
  subjective [[velocity-tiers|time-dilation]]** inside a single room. Fully synthetic
  [[the-ghost-entity|Ghosts]] rely **exclusively** on these, chosen by taste and **computing
  tolerance.** *Risk:* firewall failure → permanent **ledger fragmentation.**

## In the world
The choice of high *is* the show's thesis in miniature: the organic trip is mortal, messy, and
**forgotten**; the digital trip is precise, controllable, and **kept forever in 4K.** Some chase the
forgetting; some can't stop keeping.

## Story role
Vivid set-pieces (a Turrell-void overdose; a festival organic trip Raul can't fully recall later); a
clean addiction/“looping” jeopardy; and a quiet thematic mirror of [[the-memory-substrate]].
