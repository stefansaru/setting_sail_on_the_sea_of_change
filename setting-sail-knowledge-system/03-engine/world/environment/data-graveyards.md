> Billions of lifetimes written to the cloud means the cloud **rots.** The dead who didn't disclose are
> dumped into frozen archival slums where the simulation itself develops **"Data Dementia."**

## Concept
The decay layer of the [[the-shadow-grid|Grid]]. When a citizen is deleted or their
[[the-personal-blockchain|Identity-Key]] is purged and they **didn't opt into Full Disclosure**, their
encrypted private ledger becomes locked dead weight.

## Rules / mechanics
- **The Archive Slums.** The [[the-distributed-president|AI President]] clears the clutter by dumping
  these un-indexed, locked ledgers into **low-priority archival nodes** deep inside the frozen
  [[the-shadow-grid-cooling|lunar polar craters]] — a potter's field for the undisclosed dead.
- **Bit-Rot ("Data Dementia").** An obsolete, un-maintained sub-sector **loses its pristine
  [[chromatic-expressionism|Turrell]] smoothness**: textures fragment into **pixelated compression
  artifacts**, audio echoes with **lossy packet delay**, and abandoned AI sub-routines drift the halls
  like **broken, looping static.** A corrupted, low-res Ghost Town.

## In the world / look
The one place the optimized world is allowed to look *broken* — a deliberate visual counter to the
clean tiers. (The decayed-Turrell render is its signature; pair with [[lighting]].)

## Story role
Where lost data hides and the disowned dead drift; a refuge for someone who needs to be *un-indexed*, or
a horror locale (the looping ghost sub-routines). A natural place to bury — or unearth — a secret.

## Open questions
- Can a still-living mind **hide** in the slums to escape the [[compliance-validation-protocol|CVP]]'s
  gaze (going off-index deliberately)?
