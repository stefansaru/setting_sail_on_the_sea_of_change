> Billions of lifetimes written to the cloud means the cloud **rots.** Sealed ledgers of the dead and the failed
> forks of every Validation are dumped into frozen archival slums where the simulation itself develops **"Data
> Dementia."**

## Concept
The decay layer of the [[the-shadow-grid|Grid]]. Full Disclosure is the norm (`WR-S5`): at death a citizen's
Read-Keys are broadcast and the Identity Key is wiped. Whatever was **sealed** instead (under a sovereign lock)
can't be broadcast or read, so it becomes locked dead weight. The failed forks of every batch Validation join it.

## Rules / mechanics
- **The Archive Slums.** The [[the-distributed-president|Distributed President]] clears the clutter by dumping
  these sealed ledgers into **low-priority archival nodes** deep inside the frozen
  [[the-shadow-grid-cooling|lunar polar craters]] — a potter's field for the undisclosed dead.
- **Un-indexed.** Nothing here is catalogued for search. What is or isn't filed shows only to someone who walks
  the rows, which is how a missing batch can stay hidden (S2E14).
- **Bit-Rot ("Data Dementia").** An obsolete, un-maintained sub-sector **loses its pristine
  [[chromatic-expressionism|Turrell]] smoothness**: textures fragment into **pixelated compression
  artifacts**, audio echoes with **lossy packet delay**, and abandoned AI sub-routines drift the halls
  like **broken, looping static.** A corrupted, low-res dead town.
- **Storage rot, not forgetting.** `WR-B4` holds: a running, maintained mind keeps perfect recall, and a living
  Ghost's recall is permanent. What rots here is storage nobody maintains. The minds kept here are not
  run; each wakes for a frame when the archive is swept, and loops that frame.
- **Failed forks (`Q-37`).** Branches that failed the Validation come here, filed under the name of whoever
  claimed the batch. Failing means permanent damage, not necessarily death; forks damaged past repair are shut
  down, not filed. A batch of `1:N` should leave about N−1 rows under its claimant's name.
- **Aboard the fleet (`WR-S3`).** There are no lunar craters in flight; a hull's failed forks wait in its own
  stacks, and a dying hull's stacks become a local graveyard (Decimus's nine on Hull Four, S5).

## In the world / look
The one place the optimized world is allowed to look *broken* — a deliberate visual counter to the
clean tiers. (The decayed-Turrell render is its signature; pair with [[lighting]].) Seen at Tier 2 in S2E14.

## Story role
Where lost data hides and the disowned dead drift; a refuge for someone who needs to be *un-indexed*, or
a horror locale (the looping ghost sub-routines). A natural place to bury — or unearth — a secret.
- **S2E14:** Adam walks the rows of failed forks under merit-holders' names. **Vance**, a Ghost, claims a batch of
  10,000 (`Q-90`); under his name nothing is filed, here or anywhere. The forgery is the empty row.

## Open questions
- Can a still-living mind **hide** in the slums to escape the [[compliance-validation-protocol|CVP]]'s
  gaze (going off-index deliberately)?
- Who pays the watts to keep a filed fork waking for its frame, and can anyone bring one back?
