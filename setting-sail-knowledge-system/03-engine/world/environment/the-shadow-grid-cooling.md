> The heat of running humanity's minds at up to 86,400× is dumped into cosmic and subterranean
> geography — frozen lunar servers fed by a fragile constellation of mirrors.

## Concept
Because [[velocity-tiers|tier-dilation]] generates enormous heat and the brain is only an antenna
(see [[the-shadow-grid]]), the network's thermal load is managed entirely by **where** the servers
sit — in the coldest, darkest places available.

## Rules / mechanics (the places)
- **`PLC-03` The Lunar Cold Traps.** Supercomputing servers are anchored deep inside the Moon's
  **Permanently Shadowed Regions (PSRs)** — polar craters like **Shackleton** — where, shielded from
  all sunlight, temperatures hold at a constant **~40 K (−233 °C)** for passive cooling. *(Resolves
  audit S-12: the lunar far side is not permanently dark; PSR polar craters are.)*
- **`PLC-04` The Mirror Constellation.** A delicate, shifting constellation of **Mirror Satellites**
  orbits the sunlit rim, capturing raw solar energy and **bouncing precision beams** down into the
  shadowed crater onto the PSR collectors — powering the servers without exposing them to direct heat. *Failure
  mode:* a misalignment of even a fraction of a degree (cosmic debris) sends a beam of concentrated
  solar energy **slicing across deep space**, causing temporary **localized blackouts**.
- **`PLC-05` Terrestrial Redundancies.** Backup ground nodes are anchored deep beneath **polar ice
  sheets** or inside **high-pressure oceanic trenches**.
- **The Solar Flare Contingency.** If a severe solar storm compromises a lunar component, digital
  citizens are **not deleted** — their running states are re-routed at lightspeed to an underground
  terrestrial vault.

## In the world / look
A strong visual register: frozen black-side datacenters, a glinting lattice of orbital mirrors, a
stray light-blade crossing the dark. Earthbound vaults under ice and ocean. (Record the look in
`VIS-*`; pairs with [[setting-design]] scale + [[lighting]].)

## Story role
Built-in jeopardy and scale: a single mis-aimed mirror or a solar storm threatens millions of
minds at once — disaster that is environmental, not villainous. The re-route contingency is also a
mercy/hope beat.

## Open questions
- How autonomous is mirror re-alignment — can it be sabotaged, or only fail by accident? (Sets
  whether blackouts are threat or hazard.)
