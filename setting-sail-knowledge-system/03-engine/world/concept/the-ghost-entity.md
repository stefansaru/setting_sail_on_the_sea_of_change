> A **Ghost** is a purely digital soul with no biological cells or physical anchor anywhere — the
> post-biological residue of a human, drifting free across the Shadow Grid.

## Concept
The untethered [[the-shadow-grid|Grid]] caste, opposite the [[the-shadow-entity|Shadow]]. A Ghost
exists exclusively as decentralized cryptographic data running across the
[[the-shadow-grid-cooling|Lunar Mirror Constellation and polar-ice faults]].

## Rules / mechanics
- **Genesis (two ways):** a [[the-cyborg-slip|Cyborg's]] final organic cell dies (the "Slight Itch"
  transition), **or** a [[the-shadow-entity|Shadow's]] body is intentionally unplugged, severing the
  tether for good.
- **Liberation.** Free of physical decay, biological vulnerability, and geographic anchoring; a Ghost
  needs no body maintenance and can run at [[velocity-tiers|Tier 3]] **indefinitely** with no pod to
  decay. *(Both castes can reach high tiers while connected — the Ghost's edge is needing no body, not
  exclusive tier access; S-03.)* And with **no biological cache**, a Ghost has **perfect recall
  always** — but **no blurry fallback** ([[the-memory-substrate]]).
- **The existential trade-off.** A Ghost's whole existence rests on the [[the-personal-blockchain]]
  ledger via its **Identity-Key** (the live execution loop = the self). If that key is corrupted,
  stolen, or wrongly broadcast, their **individuality simply ceases** — true death. (The *Read-Key* —
  the harmless life-log — is what Full Disclosure shares; see the key-split.)
- **Touching the physical world** requires leasing/building a host — see [[shells-with-ghosts-within]].

## In the world
Ghosts regard [[the-shadow-entity|Shadows]] as **primitive prisoners** dragging a dying anchor of
flesh behind them through a digital sky.

## Story role
Freedom bought with absolute fragility — eternity that one corrupted key can erase. The climactic
Phase-D choice of the [[setting-sail-tier-descent|voyage]] is whether to cross fully into a Ghost (a
"[[shells-with-ghosts-within|Ghost in a Shell]]") to save an organic purist colony.
