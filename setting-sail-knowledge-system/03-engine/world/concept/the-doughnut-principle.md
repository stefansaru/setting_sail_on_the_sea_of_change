> The series' philosophical spine: experience is never defined by objective reality, but by the
> **intention an observer projects** onto a given context — an arrow chosen through the hole of
> the doughnut.

## Concept
The **Doughnut Principle** holds that human experience is never the raw event; it is the specific
**intent** an observer chooses to project within an environmental framework. This is the literal,
sci-fi form of the book's [[intention-in-context]] / [[the-intention-donut]].

- **Intention over "someday."** Intention is not a distant dream, an affirmation, or a goal. It is
  an **active abstraction over your present situation** — the immediate choice of action and
  internal direction you commit to *right now*.
- **The Context Doughnut.** Any scenario is a hollow doughnut topped with **competing realities**.
  An observer's perspective is an **arrow passing cleanly through the centre**, aligning only with
  the elements they choose to prioritize.

## Rules / mechanics
- **The Dual Arrows (the dinner analogy).** Picture a warm dinner with close friends — the music,
  the connection, the food: the **Feel-Good Arrow** through the Context Doughnut. At the *same
  table*: the wine bottle in the non-recyclable trash, meat from a creature that suffered, a friend
  hiding a devastating addiction — the **Feel-Bad Arrow**. **Both arrows pass through the exact same
  physical event.** The event is fixed; the arrow is chosen.
- The chosen arrow is what the [[the-perspective-lens-ui|Perspective Lens UI]] renders on screen —
  so two characters in one scene literally display different realities.
- The mature end-state (holding *both* arrows at once) is the [[the-macro-doughnut]].

## In the world / look
Rendered as the **Intention Split** production-design metaphor (`VIS-10`): a vertical dual-panel —
a **dark vector** (slate-grey/dark-blue, a heavy doughnut topped with a weeping face, knife, trash,
cigarette butts; arrow *"intention to feel sad"*) vs a **light vector** (warm golden-yellow, the same
doughnut with a smiling face, music notes, burger, pizza; arrow *"intention to feel happy"*). Used on
analytical beats and identity crises.

## Story role
The controlling theme of every episode and the engine of the interface itself: each short is a
character choosing (or failing to choose) an arrow through a fixed context. The transformation target
is the [[the-macro-doughnut|Arrow of Balance]].
