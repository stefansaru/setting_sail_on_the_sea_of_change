> A mind that never sleeps and never dreams ([[tether-decay]]) still has to come down somehow. So "fun"
> in the Grid is engineered for one job: **cognitive decompression and identity stabilization** —
> recreation as maintenance for a self that can't switch off.

## Concept
Because citizens are permanently awake inside the network and don't naturally dream, leisure isn't
escapism — it's **upkeep**. Recreation takes three canonical forms: a digital release valve (Turrell
Raves), a slow-time indulgence (Historical Parsing), and a physical detox (the Glacial Vacation), plus
[[intimate-commerce|Ghost Intimacy]] as its own licensed branch.

## Rules / mechanics — the forms of fun
- **Turrell Raves (the social shift).** Inside a [[frequency-tribes|Dunbar-150 tribe]], entities load
  *synchronized* [[disruptive-algorithms|Disruptive Algorithms]] that temporarily dissolve the
  [[the-perspective-lens-ui|Perspective Lens UI]]. The room becomes a borderless, vibrating color field —
  shifting intense violets and amber yellows — and the group rides a wave of **synthetic euphoria.** The
  point of the *shared, dosed* form is safety: a rave is tuned to release without risking the **permanent
  ledger fragmentation** that an unlicensed solo overdose courts. (Listening to music is replaced by
  *becoming* the light.)
- **Historical Parsing (the Living Archive).** A high-tier citizen downloads deep-history files onto
  their [[the-personal-blockchain]] and, running at [[velocity-tiers|accelerated speed]], subjectively
  spends *an unhurried summer* inside a high-resolution sim of ancient Earth oceans or classical
  architecture — all within a single real-world lunch hour. Leisure as **time arbitrage.** (And, per
  [[the-memory-substrate]], it's kept in eidetic 4K — unlike a mortal vacation, it never fades.)
- **The Glacial Vacation (physical detox).** The bodily counterpart: a citizen spends the [[velocity-
  tiers|velocity credits]] for a [[tether-decay|Sub-Velocity Sabbatical]] and travels to a **physical
  surface sanctuary** — protected wilderness zones, the **lunar polar-crater resorts** — to buy the
  *luxury of slowness.* Turn off the antenna, stand on un-optimized organic dirt, and let the biological
  cache do the one thing the Grid forbids: **forget** ([[the-memory-substrate]]).
- **[[intimate-commerce|Ghost Intimacy]].** Casual romance and sex inside risk-free digital simulation
  zones — *"what happens in the digital realm remains in the digital realm."* (Full mechanics on its own
  page.)

## In the world / look
A rave is a [[chromatic-expressionism|Turrell]] void breathing violet→amber over a tribe of still
bodies; Historical Parsing is a citizen gone glassy-eyed for ten seconds who returns having *lived* a
season; the Glacial Vacation is the rare warm, low-res, hand-held image in a sharp world — the elite
slumming it in mortality. See [[chromatic-expressionism]].

## Story role
Lets a 2.5-min episode breathe with lived-in texture and contrast Raul's high-speed interior against
the slow, sometimes painful physical world. Fun is never *just* fun here — every form quietly serves the
survival clock or the identity it's stabilizing, which keeps even the leisure beats thematic.

**Deployment.** The **Glacial Vacation** is dramatized in [[the-first-supper]]; **Turrell Raves** and
**Historical Parsing** have no S1/S2 home and are **deferred to the S3 reserve** ([[revelation-cadence]]
§6) — surface only if a slot opens.
