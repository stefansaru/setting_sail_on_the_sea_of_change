> The deepest moral friction in [[the-ministry-of-hope|Hope]]: an emergency hospital for the very
> [[the-1-percent-mutation|Natural Purists]] who reject all modification — funded by using the
> terminally desperate as the state's first human trials.

## Concept
Hope's physical wings double as an emergency medical refuge. When old, injured, or critically ill
**Natural Purists** need interventions their primitive surface villages can't provide, they are
brought here — and a facility built on supercomputing tech becomes a delicate, highly-regulated
**sanctuary state** for people who reject digital augmentation.

## Rules / mechanics
- **The Puritan Confinement.** A Purist patient is *technically free* yet **entirely confined by
  absolute physical dependency** on the facility's life-support frameworks.
- **The Bio-Tech Trade-off.** To fund itself and hold meritocratic authority, Hope is the state's
  **primary testing ground** for volatile cutting-edge medicine. The bargain offered to a terminal
  patient with zero traditional options: access to **un-vetted, experimental treatments** — making them
  the first human trials for radical radiation therapies, **neural-synthetic grafts**, and aggressive
  **DNA-telomere rewrites** ([[the-1-percent-mutation]]).
- **The two outcomes:**
  - **99% success** → the Purist is *saved but heavily augmented*, leaving as a **Cyborg** who must
    navigate the psychological shift into a mechanical shell ([[progressive-digitalization]]).
  - **1% failure** → the biological body is destroyed; the mind is **forcibly scraped and uploaded** as
    a new, disembodied [[the-ghost-entity|Ghost]]. *(For a [[the-1-percent-mutation|DNA-telomere
    rewrite]] specifically, the 1% failure first opens a 24-hour window: the Apex Escape Loop can still
    salvage it into a **cyborg** — the Ghost outcome is when that loop also fails.)*

## In the world / look
The bitter irony staged visually: a Purist who spent a life refusing modification wheeled out as chrome
— or erased into the Grid they feared. The clinical wing under the [[the-ministry-of-hope|Sanctuary
Void]] light (`VIS-14`).

## Story role
**This is where the Ep 11–13 1%-mutation arc happens** — the "24-Year Lab" and the cure are a Hope
trial. The grey line (saving a life by violating the patient's deepest values) is a complete moral
premise for a mid-season episode, and the engine that *makes cyborgs and ghosts out of purists*.
