> **The foundational law of mind in this universe** (canon amendment resolving the 2026-06-04 audit).
> Memory fidelity is anchored to the **physical substrate** running the mind: perfect and eidetic on
> the Grid, lossy and forgetting in living tissue. Memory stops being passive storage and becomes the
> **currency of human suffering and digital transcendence.**

## The architecture
The brain is **not** a backup drive. It is a **lossy, organic, high-latency biological cache** beside a
real-time quantum antenna. The absolute, immutable, eidetic record of a life lives **only** on the
network ledger — the [[the-personal-blockchain]].

```
        THE MASTER BLOCKCHAIN LEDGER
   (Grid network: immutable · eidetic · "4K")
                  ^
                  |  quantum-tether sync
                  v
        BIOLOGICAL BRAIN SUBSTRATE
   (physical cache: lossy · associative · forgetting)
```

## The substrate rules
- **In-grid (pure digital mind):** running inside the [[the-shadow-grid|Grid]] nodes, the mind has
  **perfect, high-granularity, eidetic recall** — data written cleanly to the blockchain. Amnesia,
  emotional fading, and natural decay are **mathematically impossible: the mind cannot forget.**
- **Out-of-grid (living tissue / Tier 0):** running on biological tissue cut off from network sync, the
  mind defaults to **organic fallibility** — associative leaps, distorted timelines, natural
  forgetting. *Biology decays, therefore biology forgets.*

> ### The Unifying Law of Fidelity
> **Memory granularity ∝ (active digital code) / (disconnected organic tissue).**
> You forget only to the exact extent that you are running on **biological tissue severed from the
> Grid.** A pure digital mind → perfect recall; a pure biological [[the-1-percent-mutation|purist]] →
> complete human forgetting; every cyborg or [[the-shadow-entity|Shadow]] sits on a shifting spectrum
> set by **remaining organic brain mass × real-time network proximity.**

## The Lossy Outside Principle
When an in-grid citizen acts in the physical world (a cyborg body or proxy antenna), the experiences
they accumulate are written to the ledger **only as accurately as their biological cache registered
them.** So the Grid's permanent record of a citizen's *mortal hours* is inherently **blurry,
impressionistic, dreamlike** when reviewed from the digital core — time spent outside looks like an
organic dream.

## What this resolves (2026-06-04 stress-test audit)
- **S-01** — the brain was never a full backup; it is a lossy rolling cache. On desync /
  [[the-shadow-entity|Tether Sickness]] a Shadow doesn't lose their mind — they **fall back to the
  blurry biological version** of recent events.
- **S-02** — a Tier-3 (86,400×) mind draws memory from the **ledger**, not down the antenna; the 1:1
  body rests **inert in its capsule** (a cellular heartbeat) while the digital mind races. No mismatch.
- **S-03** — Shadows can freely reach any tier **while connected**; the limit is network priority /
  merit ([[velocity-tiers]]), not the skull.
- **S-04** — *birth:* when a child's mind is generated in an incubator (Ep 2), a Tier-0 cloning vat
  **grows a matching biological body** in parallel; mind educated in-grid, body matured outside →
  **born a true tethered Shadow.** *(Amended 2026-11-04, `Q-126`: the body is the IVF embryo grown in the capsule, rushed to keep up, not a vat clone; [[capsule-birth]], `FCT-012`.)*
- **S-05** — *velocity credits* are temporary processing allocations (compress a project into a
  fraction of a second), **not** purchases of a permanent tier/class.
- **S-10 / S-12** — closed at their home pages: the Identity-Key / Read-Key split
  ([[the-personal-blockchain]]) and the lunar PSR cooling ([[the-shadow-grid-cooling]]).

## Dramatic contexts unlocked
- **The cost of the descent.** As the protagonist descends the tiers, perfect clarity erodes into
  messy, associative human memory; by the Purist village they taste a memory *slipping away* for the
  first time.
- **The digital curse of [[the-ministry-of-hope|Hope]].** Under the [[the-absolution-protocol]] a
  Penitent **cannot let time heal the wound** — denied the ability to forget, they relive the crime in
  4K. You can't run from a shadow hardcoded into the ledger; you must **hold** the pain
  ([[the-macro-doughnut]]).
- **The tragic irony with [[progressive-digitalization]].** The protagonist moves *toward* the warm,
  forgetting, mortal world while replacing the very organic brain that could forget — arriving (Ep 19,
  0% organic) **incapable of forgetting.** The Purist mother's memory of the chrome-hand-over-flesh
  moment will fade as she ages and dies; the protagonist is cursed to recall its exact warmth,
  temperature, and micro-pressure **flawlessly, forever.**
