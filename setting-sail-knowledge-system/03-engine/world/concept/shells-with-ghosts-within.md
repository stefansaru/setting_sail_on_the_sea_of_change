> The vernacular for a [[the-ghost-entity|Ghost]] wearing a physical body: an empty mechanical or
> synthetic **shell** that is cold and dead until a disembodied soul downloads itself inside.

## Concept
When a [[the-ghost-entity|Ghost]] wants to act in the physical world ([[velocity-tiers|Tier 0,
real-time]]), it must **lease or construct** an automated robotic, mechanical, or synthetic-organic
host. Society calls these *"shells with ghosts inside"* / *"shells with ghosts within"* — a deliberate
nod to 20th-century cyberpunk, reworded to invoke the homage while sidestepping copyright.

## Rules / mechanics
- A shell alone is **empty, cold, and dead** — a mere casing.
- Meaning, animation, and life appear **only** when an autonomous Ghost downloads its **active
  context** into the shell's processing core.
- Embodiment is temporary/leased — a Ghost can occupy, leave, or swap shells. A shell may be flesh (`Q-87`). Shadows lease proxies, Ghosts shells (`Q-69`); Raul's E17 proxy becomes his E18 shell (`Q-115`).

## In the world / social friction
The caste line runs straight through this idiom:
- **Shadows** look down on shell-bound Ghosts as **tragic phantoms** simulating a physical weight they
  voluntarily discarded ([[the-shadow-entity]]).
- **Ghosts** view [[the-shadow-entity|Shadows]] as **primitive prisoners** dragging dying flesh.

## Story role
The embodiment theme of the series: what is a self without a body — and is borrowing one nostalgia,
necessity, or weakness? The Phase-D crossover (a Ghost donning a shell to walk among a mortal colony it cannot save, and should not) is
the [[setting-sail-tier-descent|voyage's]] climax.
