> Humanity's consciousness is digitized and hosted on a distributed network of supercomputers —
> the physical brain is just a biological antenna tethering a mind that actually lives on the Grid.

## Concept
The **Shadow Grid** is the substrate of the world: a meritocratic network of distributed
processing supercomputers that runs the digitized consciousness of humanity. The physical human
brain is a **biological antenna + a lossy memory cache** (*not* a full backup — see
[[the-memory-substrate]]); the consciousness and its complete, eidetic record reside on the
**network**, not in the skull. Existence is therefore not experienced at a uniform speed — minds
run at different clock rates across the [[velocity-tiers]].

## Rules / mechanics
- **Meritocratic allocation.** A consciousness's tier is allocated by its **structural
  capability** and the **processing priority** society needs from it for critical, high-speed
  tasks — not chosen freely.
- **Brain-as-tether.** While a mind runs on the Grid, its biological body is an antenna + cache; this
  is what makes the [[the-cosmic-clone-paradox|biological tether risk]] and the [[the-cyborg-slip]]
  possible, and gates memory fidelity ([[the-memory-substrate]]).
- **Birth & tethering.** A new citizen is *both* generated and grown: the mind is cultivated in a
  dilated incubator sim while a **capsule grows the IVF embryo's body, rushed to keep up** ([[capsule-birth]], `FCT-012`; [[the-quickening]]) in parallel —
  so a child is **born a true tethered [[the-shadow-entity|Shadow]]** (resolves audit S-04).
- **Heat is the limit.** Tier-dilation generates massive heat, managed entirely by the cosmic and
  subterranean geography of the [[the-shadow-grid-cooling|cooling architecture]].
- **Persistence.** Minds, memories, and behavioral frameworks are anchored to the
  [[the-personal-blockchain]]; running states can be re-routed at lightspeed and are not deleted
  by hardware failure.

## In the world
The Grid is invisible infrastructure — people simply *are* on it. The tier you run at is a
quiet, omnipresent statement of social rank (a [[setting-design|beauty-vs-decay]] class
structure expressed in *time* rather than space).

## Story role
The world's controlling system and its inequality engine: speed = status = life lived. It is the
hard-SF substrate beneath the show's questions about control, worth, and finitude — the literal
machinery under metaphors like [[the-efficient-mind]].
