> A **Shadow** is a digital mind still anchored to a living biological body in stasis — a digital
> existence *cast by* an organic object, the way a shadow is cast by a thing in the light.

## Concept
One of the two **cognitive castes** of the [[the-shadow-grid]] (the other is the [[the-ghost-entity]]).
A Shadow's consciousness runs on the Grid while its **living body is preserved in a stasis capsule**
on Earth or in orbital bays. They are an extension of a biological organism moving through a virtual
medium — hence the name.

## Rules / mechanics
- **Brain as a lossy cache (not a backup).** The organic brain is an **antenna + a low-fidelity
  rolling cache** ([[the-memory-substrate]]); the full eidetic record lives on the Grid ledger. On
  desync, a Shadow doesn't lose their mind — they **fall back to the blurry biological version** of
  recent events (the redundancy [[the-ghost-entity|Ghosts]] lack is *this fuzzy fallback*, not a full copy).
- **The pull of the body.** The biological container needs maintenance, nutrient cycling, and waste
  management; a Shadow **cannot stray from its home node indefinitely**. *(Tier access itself is
  unbound while connected — the limit is network priority/merit, not the skull; resolves S-03.)*
- **Tether Sickness.** When network proximity decays (the tether "stretches" = connection strength
  drops), the mind **drops to lossy biological memory** mid-thought — the high-granularity self falls
  away and only the blurry human version remains: a visceral, hallucinatory panic. *(The felt experience
  of **tether decay**, quantified as the TTD countdown — [[tether-decay]] `MAG-22`. There is no dreaming
  in-grid, so the brain-cache must be rested via Sabbaticals or it collapses.)*

## In the world
Shadows hold full legal rights and a measure of cultural seniority, but limited mobility. They tend to
**look down on [[shells-with-ghosts-within|shell-bound Ghosts]]** as tragic phantoms miming a physical
weight they discarded.

**Attending Tier 0.** Because a Shadow's mind lives on the Grid while its body sleeps in a pod, it reaches
the physical surface only by **down-clocked telepresence** (perceiving real-time through surface sensors)
or by **leasing a proxy shell** for actual contact — never by walking out in person. So a Shadow at a
Tier-0 scene is "there" the way you're on a call: present, but a world away from their own warm flesh.
(Resolves the E7/E8/E11/E13 presence question.)

## Story role
The protagonist archetype's likely starting caste: *a young Shadow struggling to differentiate their
own values from the network's hardcoded cultural programming* (Phase A/B of the
[[setting-sail-tier-descent|voyage]]). Tether Sickness is a ready hazard.
