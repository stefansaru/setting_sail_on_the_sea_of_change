> Earth is governed not by ego, parties, or lobbying but by a single non-biological executive — **The
> Distributed President** — that derives law directly from the **real-time physical status of the
> planet**.

## Concept
Politics is fully decentralized. The governance loop runs:
**Planetary Status Engine** (real-time bio-metrics, climate vectors, resource data) → **The Distributed
President** (an AI that dynamically queries the [[intention-in-context-law|Intention-in-Context law
database]]) → **The Core Blockchain Ledger** (merit-based human rule updates and dynamic principles).

## Rules / mechanics — Dynamic Environmental Legislation
- Law is **not** written from ideological trends; social goals are **derived from the planet's measured
  state.**
- **The feedback interface:** thousands of automated sensors track regional climate, soil toxicity,
  sea-level shift, and localized heat anomalies.
- **The systemic pivot:** when degradation threatens a region, the AI cross-references the ecosystem
  against the legal core and **automatically enacts a targeted regional law** to fight that specific
  vector — e.g. *freezing processing output in adjacent [[the-shadow-grid|Grid]] nodes* or *forcing
  immediate terraforming protocols.*

## In the world / look
A climate threat surfaces on the [[the-perspective-lens-ui|UI]] as a **Context circle `[◯]`** at the
top of the frame — the first beat of the "Global Loop" (sensor → law → displacement → feeling).

## Story role
The systemic backdrop and a wordless engine of personal upheaval: the planet shifts, the President
re-legislates, and an ordinary citizen's life is reorganized without a villain. The world *accepts the
uncontrollable* (climate, the [[you-are-the-sailor-not-the-wind|Sea of Change]]) and only adjusts the
response — the book's thesis as a constitution.

**Held to S3 (`WR-R5`):** the President is a Ghost, Mara (`Q-01`, `Q-98`). She locked her memory in tissue while embodied (`Q-67`) and stored her genome on Hull One before launch (`Q-119`), and her remainder rides `Outbound` after S3E20 (`Q-125`).
