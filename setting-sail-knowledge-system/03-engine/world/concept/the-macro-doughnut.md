> The maturity goal: step back far enough to see the overarching doughnut that holds joy *and* pain
> at once — the Arrow of Balance, not the toxic positivity of a curated feed.

## Concept
Beyond the competing [[the-doughnut-principle|Feel-Good / Feel-Bad arrows]] lies the **Macro-
Doughnut**: the overarching frame that **holds space for both joy and pain simultaneously**. Its
arrow is the **Arrow of Balance**. Reaching it is true psychological maturity — and the explicit
goal of the *evolutionary child* inside the simulation (see [[child-phase]] /
[[camel-lion-child-trilogy]]).

## Rules / mechanics
- It **accepts the shadow cast by the light** without collapsing into the **toxic positivity** of
  society's curated, dopamine-driven digital highlights (cf. [[social-media-iceberg]]).
- It is a *higher tier* of awareness — narratively aligned with the meritocratic ascent of the
  [[velocity-tiers]] (childish reactivity → balanced, high-tier awareness).
- Where the single arrows *select* a reality, the balance arrow *contains* both.
- **The Two Doughnuts — hollow vs solid (`Q-12`).** The UI glyph has two states. The **hollow / outline**
  Macro-Doughnut is the **"Camel's Doughnut"**: a system-issued *factory default* handed to every
  graduate at [[parallel-pedagogy|the Merging]] — the *shape* of balance, **inherited and untested**
  ([[societal-values]] Phase A). The **solid / filled** Macro-Doughnut is the **earned** one — authored,
  tested, load-bearing (Phase D). Raul's whole arc is one glyph: **hollow → fractured (the descent) →
  solid (Ep 20).** Adam's S2E20 glyph cracks to the Mortal Doughnut instead (`Q-19`).

## In the world / look
Rendered as the **Unified Equilibrium** production-design metaphor (`VIS-11`): a rustic, ochre stone
doughnut balanced dead-centre in a neutral-yellow frame, pierced left-to-right by one bold arrow
*"intention to feel balance"*; resting on its top curve, two opposing planetary counterweights — a
radiant smiling **sun** (left) and a dark, craggy, weeping **storm-cloud** (right). Reserved for
breakthroughs and high-tier philosophical alignment.

## Story role
The destination of the character arc — the visual/▶thematic payoff of an episode's turn: the moment
a character stops reacting and holds both truths. The single, stable, centred composition is the
opposite of the unstable [[the-doughnut-principle|Intention Split]].
