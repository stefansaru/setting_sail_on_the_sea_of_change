> Every society draws its line at what disgusts it. In the Grid the line moved: it's no longer about sex
> or violence (both [[standardized-vices|licensed]]) but about **synchronization, self-regulation, and
> transparency.** To be uncouth here is to be *out of phase, leaky, or hidden.*

## Concept
With no shared timeline, manners reorganized around the physics and accountability of the substrate.
Three taboos define polite society — each the inverse of a civic virtue (sync, composure, disclosure).

## Rules / mechanics — the three taboos
- **The "Glacial" Stutter (tier mismatching).** It is deeply uncouth to interact with someone outside
  your active [[velocity-tiers|velocity tier]] **without an automated buffering interface**
  ([[inter-tier-travel|the Data Buffer State]]). Conversing raw across speeds inflicts an agonizing
  communicative **"lag"** — chaotic noise on both ends. Forcing it on someone is like shouting gibberish
  in their face on purpose.
- **Organic Leakage.** For digital-first citizens, raw biological anomalies — sneezing, coughing, an
  un-optimized emotional breakdown — read as **intense disgust**: a failure of self-regulation and a
  breach of baseline civic aesthetics. (The flip side of the [[the-memory-substrate|messy-but-human]]
  warmth the Purists prize — one culture's authenticity is another's filth.)
- **Masked Intent.** Operating in public with **grey or unverified Intention Squares `[▢]`**
  ([[the-perspective-lens-ui]]) is a severe taboo. Hiding your active motivations from the network draws
  immediate suspicion — *akin to walking a city wearing a ski mask.* (This is the social pressure that
  makes the [[intent-patch|Intent-Patch]] valuable: it doesn't go *grey* — it broadcasts a flawless
  *false* signal, passing the taboo while breaking the law.)

## In the world / look
The Glacial Stutter is shown as a dropped-frame, pitch-bent smear when two mismatched Lenses touch;
Organic Leakage is the whole room subtly recoiling from a single human cough; Masked Intent is the
quiet, total social freeze around a figure whose bottom-bar squares read grey. Etiquette rendered in UI.

## Story role
Cheap, legible friction for a vertical episode — a protagonist can commit a *faux pas* the audience
instantly reads as shameful, no exposition needed. The taboos also load the dice morally: the
[[setting-sail-tier-descent|Natural Purists]] are *defined* by violating Organic Leakage (they leak on
purpose), and Masked Intent makes secrecy itself feel criminal — useful pressure on Raul and cover for
Vance.

**Deployment.** **Organic Leakage** is dramatized in [[the-substrate-paradox]] / [[the-first-supper]];
the **Glacial Stutter** and **Masked Intent** have no S1/S2 home and are **deferred to the S3 reserve**
([[revelation-cadence]] §6) — Masked Intent is a natural fit for a future Vance scene.
