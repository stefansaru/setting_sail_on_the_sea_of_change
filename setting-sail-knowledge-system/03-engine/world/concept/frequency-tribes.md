> When minds run at wildly different speeds, biological family and lifelong romance break down. The new
> social unit is the **Frequency Tribe**: ~150 minds sharing a processing speed — and the cruelest
> casualty is two people *physically touching* who are **a lifetime apart.**

## Concept
Dunbar's number (~150 stable relationships) has crystallized into formal **Frequency Tribes**. A tribe
is defined not by genes or geography but by a **shared processing frequency** — ~150 minds running at
the same [[velocity-tiers|tier]], forming deep, insular monocultures with one localized, real-time
context. Drift to a different tier and you drift, permanently, into a different tribe.

## Rules / mechanics
- **The tribal bond** is *clock-based*, not blood-based. You belong to whoever shares your *now*.
- **Parent–child detachment.** Children are generated in incubator nodes ([[the-shadow-grid]]) and
  advance by independent merit — so a child can **out-clock their parents in a single real-world week**,
  out-aging them by subjective decades and migrating into a different tribe. Lineage de-centers.
- **The slang vernacular** (caste shorthand, for punchy 2.5-min dialogue):
  - **Glacials / Lag-Brains** — high-tier Ghosts' insult for biological Purists / Tier-0 workers
    (crawling at 1:1 = frozen in ice).
  - **Smoke / Spooks** — surface Cyborgs' & Purists' slur for disembodied [[the-ghost-entity|Ghosts]]
    (skin discarded, just drifting data).

## The Proximity Illusion (story role)
The signature tragedy: **Raul (`CHR-02`) and Vesper (`CHR-05`)** lie in stasis capsules *bolted inches apart*
— *"our biological capsules are right next to each other"* — but the monitors tell the truth. Raul's
reads **Tier 0 (1:1)**; Vesper's pulses ice-blue at **Tier 2 (1s = 1hr)**. While Raul lives one outside
day, Vesper lives **~10 subjective years** in her high-speed tribe. To Vesper, Raul is a frozen statue she
left an eternity ago; to Raul, Vesper is a blur too fast to hold. *Physically touching, subjectively
separated by a lifetime.* (Shot in [[chromatic-expressionism|Caravaggio]] chiaroscuro + heavy
[[negative-space]] — slotted S2E7 (`Q-11`), recast for Adam (`Q-76`). By then both capsules stand empty, tagged VESPER and RAUL (`Q-92`); her capsule is planted in S1E6/E17 (`Q-88`) and the reunion is spent in S3E14 (`Q-121`).)
