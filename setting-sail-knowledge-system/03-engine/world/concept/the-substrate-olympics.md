> The Games survived — but you can't put a 3,600×-mind and a mortal in the same race. So the Olympics
> split by **substrate physics** into separate brackets. And the cruel joke is that the fastest minds in
> existence are the *worst* athletes the moment you drop them into flesh.

## Concept
A single shared contest is impossible without a shared timeline, so the Games were deconstructed into
two divisions defined by processing physics: the **Cognitive League** (Tier-3 Grid speed — data routing,
logic, kinematic problem-solving) and the **Mortal Baseline** (Tier-0 real-time — biological tissue
only). Sport became a public referendum on what a "body" even is.

## Rules / mechanics — the Tier-Reality Paradox
The defining law of the Games: a champion of a [[velocity-tiers|Tier 1 (60×)]] or **Tier 2 (3,600×)**
digital athletic tribe will **fail catastrophically** if they download into a biological body to compete
in the **Tier 0 (1:1)** Mortal Baseline. The *advantage inverts.*
- **The Neurological Desync.** Inside the Grid the champion's mind runs up to 60× faster than the
  physical world. Downloaded into flesh, consciousness perceives the race in **hyper-slow motion** — they
  see the finish line and map a perfect kinematic trajectory.
- **The Biological Failure.** But motor nerves, lactic-acid build-up, muscle-fibre twitch speed and raw
  gravity run at an **un-alterable, sluggish organic pace.** The mind commands a stride; the chemical
  signal arrives late through the meat. The hyper-accelerated mind **over-anticipates** the body's
  momentum → spatial disorientation, mistimed strides, **structural muscle tears.** *They are mentally
  too fast for the physics of flesh.*
- **Why no buffer saves them.** A lawful [[inter-tier-travel|Data Buffer State]] phase-matches two
  *digital* clocks; it can't speed up biochemistry. The body has no higher gear — which is exactly why
  the brackets must stay separate, and why a [[progressive-digitalization|more-synthetic]] athlete is
  even *worse* at the Mortal Baseline, not better.

## In the world / look
The Cognitive League is rendered as serene, impossible geometry — minds solving a course like light
through crystal. The Mortal Baseline is sweat, grunt, and [[chromatic-expressionism|Caravaggio]] strain
— and the money image is the **desync disaster**: a celebrated Tier-2 champion collapsing mid-stride in
real-time, mind screaming *faster* while the flesh tears, the crowd silent. See [[chromatic-expressionism]].

## Story role
A self-contained episode engine and a clean dramatization of the show's spine: the most "optimized" mind
humbled by the body it abandoned — mortality as a skill you *lose.* It hands the
[[setting-sail-tier-descent|Natural Purists]] their proudest argument (only *they* can run the Mortal
Baseline) and gives Raul's descent a competitive stage where being slower is, for once, the gift.
