> When automation and the [[the-shadow-grid|Grid]] make traditional jobs redundant, no one is cast into
> poverty. An empathy-driven safety net offers two doors: **serve** (the Hospitality Baseline) or
> **withdraw** (the Ministry of Hope sanctuary).

## Concept
Advanced AI sub-routines and high-speed Grid processing render most cognitive and physical jobs
obsolete. Society handles human deployment through a strictly-enforced safety net rather than
unemployment.

## Rules / mechanics
- **The Hospitality Baseline.** On mass layoffs / technological displacement, citizens are
  **automatically transitioned** into the default public framework: the **Tourism & Human-Experience
  Economy.** *Philosophy:* machines can process data and mine asteroids flawlessly but **cannot simulate
  genuine human connection, cultural storytelling, or organic hospitality.** *Roles:* well-funded posts
  in cultural sanctuaries, eco-resorts, organic restaurants, and interactive historical hotels —
  cultivating real, digital-noise-free human interaction. The organic restaurants here are the
  **physical foil to digital [[flavor-scripts]]** — valued precisely because the meal *can't* be
  perfectly logged on the ledger.
- **Voluntary Cloistering.** Those who reject the outward, social nature of hospitality can instead
  **check into [[the-ministry-of-hope|Hope]]** of their own free will (the institution's *Seekers*).
  They trade outward labor for inward reflection — compiling personal context ledgers, quiet
  craftsmanship, or volunteering — using isolation to **recalibrate their internal compass.**

## In the world
Displacement is reframed as choice: face outward into service, or inward into the cloister. Either way,
the question becomes *what is a human for, once work is optional?* ([[ikigai]],
[[meaning-over-happiness]]).

## Story role
The engine of the **Ep 5 "First Brake Cycle"** beat — the protagonist's first job reassignment — and a
recurring source of quiet displacement drama across the season (the "Global Loop": a climate ruling
automates a sector → a worker is reassigned → the [[chromatic-expressionism|Despair filter]] projects
their wordless terror).

## Open questions
- Is the Hospitality Baseline genuinely dignified, or a gilded sedation — and does the show take a side?

## Origin
The safety net descends from the Caretaker Initiative ([[the-caretaker-era]], `TML-10`), which gave people work
"AI couldn't take, yet": sorting, recycling, care. Here the "yet" has run out. What is left is work only people can
do, paid in the **Care** coin ([[the-wallet-and-policy-ledger]]).
