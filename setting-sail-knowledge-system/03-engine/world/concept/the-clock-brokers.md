> The Grid's criminals aren't greedy — they're **drowning.** Clock Brokers illegally overclock their
> avatars into high-velocity tribes to steal the processing time they need just to keep breathing. They
> don't scam for wealth; they scam to **survive one more cycle.**

## Concept
The high-tech crook of the [[the-shadow-grid]], living at **Tier 0 real-time** in the underclass.
Clock Brokers are physically desperate, hollowed-out people who pay catastrophic biological costs to
run crimes inside the higher [[velocity-tiers|velocity tiers]]. They are the human face of the world's
crime tier — the opposite of [[intent-patch|Vance]]'s polished high-merit corruption: the same system's
floor, where survival itself is illegal.

## Rules / mechanics — how they live and what it costs
- **The overclock.** They illegally overclock their avatars to operate inside high-velocity tribes
  ([[frequency-tribes]]) and **steal velocity credits** — the processing currency of the
  [[the-post-labor-economy]] — to keep their own nodes running.
- **The body-price ([[tether-decay|Tether Sickness]]).** Because they skip the mandatory **Sub-Velocity
  Sabbaticals** to stay online, their physical bodies pay catastrophically: emaciation, constant muscle
  tremors, and dangerously low **TTD timers**. They are dying in the flesh to keep the avatar alive.
- **The trade.** They harvest velocity credits from high-tier citizens and **manufacture black-market
  [[intent-patch|Intent-Patches]]** to mask systemic crimes from the watching
  [[the-distributed-president|AI President]]. They are also the typical vector behind a
  [[chromatic-tears|Chromatic Tear]].
- **Motive (the limit on sympathy/villainy).** Goals are rarely material — they steal to *survive*,
  which makes them tragic; but the theft accelerates *other* citizens' decay, which keeps them
  dangerous. Both at once.

## In the world / look
They nest in **un-zoned underground capsule bays** and makeshift surface outposts, ringed by cooling
vents and illegal antenna arrays — the squalid cousins of the [[data-graveyards]]. The image is pure
[[chromatic-expressionism|Caravaggio]] tenebrism: a single source over a tremoring, hollow-cheeked body
wired into a contraband capsule, eyes flickering at a tier no one around them can see. See [[setting-design]].

## Story role
Gives the world a **criminal underclass with a beating heart** — desperation, not malice — and a ground-
level vantage on the same accountability machine the elites game from above. A Clock Broker is the
natural target of a [[velocity-braking|Velocity-Brake]] and a natural inmate for
[[the-ministry-of-hope|Hope]] — and the gaunt, un-synced smuggler who locks their ledger in the
[[the-encrypted-block-paradox|Encrypted Block Paradox]] is almost always one of them. Raul can meet the
system's cost written on a starving body.

## Open questions
