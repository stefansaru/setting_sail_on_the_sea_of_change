> The world doesn't *outlaw* vice — it **licenses and standardizes** it. The [[the-distributed-president]]
> runs on pragmatic **harm reduction**: strip the criminal underground by absorbing intimacy commerce
> and neuro-chemical alteration into regulated civic frameworks. The moral burden moves from
> puritanical taboo to **structural accountability + cryptographic validation.**

## Concept
There is no taboo, only **accountability**. Ethics becomes a *verifiable data standard*: a vice is fine
so long as it is licensed, transparent on the [[the-personal-blockchain|ledger]], and provably harmless
in context. Two domains are regulated this way — **[[intimate-commerce]]** and **neuro-chemical
alteration ([[disruptive-algorithms]])** — and enforcement is the real-time
[[compliance-validation-protocol]], not police.

## Rules / mechanics — vice by substrate
Like everything in this world, a vice is classified by the **substrate** it runs on; each has its own
risk-management regime and its own [[chromatic-expressionism|chromatic register]]:

| Substrate | Execution | Risk management | Visual register |
|-----------|-----------|-----------------|-----------------|
| **Physical / organic** | cellular ingestion · licensed physical intimacy | biosecurity checkups; blockchain **client liability** for transmission | **Caravaggio** chiaroscuro — warm shadow, natural light, raw skin texture |
| **Cyborg / synthetic** | mechanical-fluid replacement · shell leasing | automated hardware diagnostics; cooling monitoring | **Flavin** — intersecting neon bars on brushed metal |
| **Digital / grid** | **Disruptive Algorithms** run on the ledger core | code-integrity checks; firewall vs **ledger fragmentation** | **Turrell** — physical lines dissolve into borderless color voids |

## In the world
Vice is a *prestated business*: licensed, lucrative, and logged. The same act carries totally different
risk (and a totally different look) depending on whether you do it in **flesh, chrome, or code** — and,
per [[the-memory-substrate]], whether you'll **forget it** (organic) or **keep it forever** (digital).

## Story role
The world's moral skeleton: no shame, only proof. It also stages the show's thesis in a single night
out — a mortal, forgettable organic high vs an eidetic digital one. Part of the [[societal-values]]
transformation (worth/meaning over taboo).
