> Sex work is a fully legalized, licensed, blockchain-transparent enterprise — its risk and its rules
> set entirely by the **substrate** of the bodies involved.

## Concept
A regulated branch of [[standardized-vices]]: a lucrative, prestated business under strict occupational
licensing and absolute ledger transparency. Risk management changes completely with the substrate.

## Rules / mechanics
- **Biosecurity Protocol (organic).** Organic workers — including **Natural Purists** who trade inside
  specialized surface sanctuaries — undergo rigorous, mandatory periodic medical/cellular checkups.
- **Client Liability Laws.** If a client with a biological tether (a [[the-shadow-entity|Shadow]])
  infects an organic worker with a pathogen due to a **faulty stasis-capsule diagnostic**, the client is
  held **cryptographically and legally liable** for the worker's full medical-restoration cost (a
  [[compliance-validation-protocol|CVP]]-enforced, ledger-signed debt).
- **Cyborg / synthetic alternatives.** Mechanical hosts and robotic workers are **exempt from
  biosecurity** — interfaces are systematically sanitized, wear tracked on a hardware-maintenance
  schedule.
- **Ghost Intimacy (digital).** Inside the sim tiers, intimacy is purely data — digital avatars, **zero
  physical-pathology risk.** Cultural idiom: *"What happens in the digital realm remains in the digital
  realm."* A risk-free, highly lucrative slice of the experiential economy.

## In the world / look
Per [[standardized-vices]]: organic = [[chromatic-expressionism|Caravaggio]] warmth and skin; cyborg =
Flavin neon-on-metal; digital = Turrell void. And per [[the-memory-substrate]], an organic encounter is
**forgotten** like anything human; a Ghost encounter is **kept in eidetic 4K** — a quiet, real reason
some choose flesh and some choose code.

## Story role
Harm-reduction and accountability without shame; the **purist-in-a-sanctuary** corner is a sharp
moral-grey (the un-augmented selling the one thing the digital can only simulate). A natural locale for
a single-encounter character study.

## Open questions

  of discretion the eidetic Grid can't offer?
