> Inside the Grid, physical appearance is a fully **decoupled data variable**: everyone is rendered
> beautiful, healthy, and forever twenty-something. The instant a mind steps outside the simulation, the
> illusion shatters into raw mortal meat. The gap between your ageless avatar and your decaying body is the
> universe's quiet horror — **substrate dysmorphia**.

## Concept
In the simulation tiers, looks are not a fact of biology but a **render setting**. To keep minds
psychologically stable inside a digital environment — and to prevent rejection of the illusion — the
[[the-distributed-president|AI President]] enforces a standardized visual baseline for any human-facing
interaction. The result is a world that has abolished the *visual* markers of class, illness, and age,
while the bodies those minds left behind keep aging and failing in their stasis capsules. The half of the
**Aesthetic Dichotomy** about *bodies* (its sibling, [[asymmetric-perspective]], covers *spaces*).

## Rules / mechanics

```
       [ INTRA-GRID INTERACTION MATRIX ]
                       |
       +---------------+---------------+
       |                               |
       v                               v
 [ SYSTEM-TO-SYSTEM ]         [ SYSTEM-TO-HUMAN ]
 - Raw digital protocols      - "Golden Baseline" avatar
 - No human interface         - Late 20s / early 30s
 - Algorithmic shorthand      - Flawless physical symmetry
```

- **The "Golden Baseline" avatar.** When biological minds congregate in-grid — even at Tier 0 (real-time)
  speed — the rendering engine defaults every human form to its **late twenties / early thirties**: peak
  health, flawless symmetry, no cellular decay, no baldness, no scars, no metabolic tells. Perpetual youth
  as a system default.
- **Re-skinning on contact.** Digital-only entities and autonomous [[the-ghost-entity|Ghosts]] talk to
  each other in raw, hyper-efficient protocols and floating data arrays — no human aesthetic at all. But
  the *millisecond* a biological [[the-shadow-entity|Shadow]] or human mind enters their node, they
  dynamically **re-skin into the ageless human default** to prevent cognitive shock to the biological user.
- **The Organic Retaliation (outside the Grid).** The moment a citizen takes a *Sub-Velocity Sabbatical*
  ([[tether-decay]]) and slides out of the capsule, the illusion is gone. There are **no default skins** in
  the physical world: bodies bear genetic defects, asymmetry, illness, and the marks of
  [[progressive-digitalization|progressive degradation]] — cyborgs rust around their interface brackets;
  Purists wear the deep weathered wrinkles of real biological aging. The shift is absolute: from clean,
  vector-perfect human shapes to the raw, visceral texture of mortal meat.

## In the world / look (`VIS-16`)
**The Aesthetic Dichotomy** is a hard render-rule: the **Grid** = vector-perfect, ageless, symmetrical,
clean light; the **Outside** = [[lighting|Caravaggio]] chiaroscuro on mortal-meat texture — rust, scars,
wrinkles, sweat, tremor. The cut *between* them is the show's most reliable gut-punch (see the reel
[[the-mirror-asymmetry]]). Pairs with the chromatic master-palettes (`VIS-15`) and the rendering-style
canon (`VIS-13`): the imperfection is achieved **photoreally**, never as a "gritty filter."

## Story role
The tragic irony of the whole series, made literal on the body: in-grid, humanity achieves an absolute
ageless utopia — and pays for it by living a lie they can never fully inhabit, since the flesh keeps the
real score in the capsule. **Substrate dysmorphia** is the wound — loving a face that the body underneath has
already outlived. It is the [[progressive-digitalization|body-clock]] seen from the outside in.
