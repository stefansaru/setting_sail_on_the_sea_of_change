> A loose formation of hulls crossing deep space toward a habitable system — each one a Shadow Grid in a
> can, with a reactor, a stasis deck, a forward shield, a bore laser, and a beam link to its sisters.

## Concept
Interstellar colonization in this world is not a ship of sleepers but a **fleet of servers**. Each hull
carries a few hundred minds and only a handful of bodies ([[the-stasis-economy]]). The fleet flies as
**several hulls at a safe spacing**, so that no single strike, debris plume, or reactor failure can take
more than one. The hulls are numbered, not named (`Q-32`): **Hull One** is the lead and the High Clock's
seat; **Hull Four** is the one the story breaks.

## Rules / mechanics
- **The deck order is the pyramid.** Bottom: the **stasis bays** (bodies, chemistry lines, the lifeboat
  docks). Middle: the **synthesis bay** ([[capsule-birth]] at the far end of the voyage). Top: the **server
  stacks** running the [[velocity-tiers]] — cooled by the void, like the [[the-shadow-grid-cooling|lunar
  dark-side servers]]. Clock speed costs watts, so the reactor's budget *is* the ceiling of the Apex aboard.
- **The forward shield** is a **deployable scattering screen** — a magnetically-held curtain of dust and
  plasma held ahead of the bow that turns micrometeorites to vapour before they reach the hull. It is
  *not* a plate. It scatters light as well as rock, which is why it must **drop** to fire
  ([[the-firing-protocol]]).
- **The bore laser** sits on the hull's centreline and fires straight ahead through the screen's gap. Seen
  from dead ahead, the fleet's shield is a **disc with a dark bore**: the series' doughnut-and-arrow motif
  (`VIS-12`) as engineering.
- **The fleet sees as one.** A shared sensor array across all hulls keeps the **catalogue**: every object
  out to the horizon, tracked and listed. An object the catalogue does not hold is, in sensor slang,
  **Unlisted** (`FAC-06`). In the whole voyage, nothing has been.
- **Minds move by beam; bodies move by lifeboat.** Hull-to-hull hops are short, so a mind travels the safe
  interplanetary way ([[the-cosmic-clone-paradox]]: pause here, wake there — one continuous self). *(Whether
  a dying hull's transmit leaves a copy running is `Q-31`.)* Bodies can only cross in **lifeboats**: small,
  slow, chemistry-hungry craft with a handful of bays each.
- **Spacing is a cost.** Safe distance means a sister hull is minutes away by beam and **hours** by lifeboat.
  Steering the whole formation (to deflect around debris) burns fuel on every hull at once — the arithmetic
  behind [[the-firing-protocol]].

## In the world
Cold, dark, procedural. The Flavin-neon of the server decks (`VIS-15`), the Caravaggio tenebrism of the
stasis and lifeboat bays, the Turrell void of the Apex. The base-tier colonists experience the fleet as a
**slow, orderly town**; the High Clock experiences it as a **ship** it is flying. The firing days are the
fleet's weather.

## Story role
The hull makes every world rule *heavy*: a tier costs watts, a body costs mass, a shield costs sight.
**Failure mode:** the open window — the one second in which the screen is down and the catalogue is wrong
([[setting-sail-the-seed-ledger|S4E10]]).
