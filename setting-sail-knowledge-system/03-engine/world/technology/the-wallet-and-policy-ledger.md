> Money is several coins, one for each kind of thing you can give. Every coin sits in a wallet on your own ledger.
> A payment is signed only when you can show you understand what it will do. Since that is hard, people live by
> standing policies they have committed to, and every transaction is checked against them.

## Concept
An extension of the [[the-personal-blockchain]] (`MAG-11`). Each person, in the flesh or digital, has:
1. **A wallet** on their ledger, holding several **coin types**. Everything is digital, including for Purists' rare
   dealings with the Grid, which go through a cyborg or a Ghost.
2. **A policy ledger:** the standing rules they have committed to, each one signed. Some people keep it all public,
   some all private, most a mix.

## The coins
Different kinds of transaction use different coins. A coin is never converted into another at a posted rate. Trades
between coins exist, but each one is a transaction of its own and has to be signed.

| Coin | On screen | Buys | Why it is separate |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Velocity credits** | `VC` | Clock speed and processing time: tier hours, Apex time, compute ([[velocity-tiers]]). | Time on the Grid is made by power, and power is rationed. Already canon (`WLD-04`). Apex credit is VC drawn at Tier 3. |
| **Matter** | `MAT` | Physical things and energy at Tier 0: food, materials, a body's upkeep, a shell lease. | Atoms can't be rendered; they have to be mined, grown or recycled. |
| **Care** | `CARE` | Hours of a person's real attention: hospitality, attendance, tending, teaching, a supervised Sabbatical. | The work no machine was allowed to take. It descends from the Caretaker-era volunteer hours ([[the-caretaker-era]], [[the-post-labor-economy]]). |

## Signing (awareness, proven)
- **A transaction is signed only when the signer declares they understand its implications and can prove it.**
  - The declaration goes in the Intention squares.
  - The proof is checked by the [[compliance-validation-protocol|CVP]] against the signer's ledger: has this mind read,
    and shown in its own past acts, what this transaction will do?
  - A good signature carries the stamp `AWARE · PROVEN`.
- **This is hard on purpose.** Proving awareness for every payment would be unbearably slow, and for a large or
  unusual one it can take days of Lens questions. That is the old Caretaker rule: a choice counts only if you
  understood it.

## The policy ledger
- **Standing policies.** Because signing is hard, people commit in advance to policies. Examples:
  - "never sell Apex time";
  - "care hours only to kin";
  - "nothing over 100 `MAT` without a night's wait";
  - `DEFAULT: NO` (the grown-up form of [[nine-nine-nine]]).
- **Automatic checking.** A transaction inside your policies is signed quickly, because you proved awareness once,
  when you signed the policy. A transaction that breaks a policy is stopped with `POLICY BREACH` and needs a full
  awareness proof, or a change to the policy. Changing a policy is itself a signed act.
- **Public, private, mixed.**
  - Public policies let others trust you without asking; they are part of what makes someone Proven-in-Use.
  - Private policies are known only to you and the CVP.
  - Most people show some and keep the rest. The Full Disclosure Clause opens them all at death, the same as the
    Read-Key (`MAG-11`, `WR-S5`).
- **Policies are not law.** Law is the [[intention-in-context-law]], and it is the same for everyone. A policy is a
  person's own rule, stricter than law. Not to be confused with a **Policy Update** (`MAG-25`): a lesson from a
  Penitent's ledger shipped into the law itself.

## In the world
- **On the Lens:** the wallet shows as a small three-line tally, `VC · MAT · CARE`. A signature resolves as
  `AWARE · PROVEN`, or `POLICY ✓ n/n` when it passes standing policies.
- **Group policies:** a tribe or a vow can be a shared set of policies everyone signs. The Purist vow, "One life, kept
  whole" (`Q-74`), is one, held on paper and in memory.
- **Where people get caught:** someone signs a narrow policy when young, finds it costs them later, and has to prove
  awareness every time they step outside it.

## On screen
- **S2E17:** Adam's consent ledger. Each rung he signs resolves `AWARE · PROVEN`; the Apex credit counter that empties
  is his `VC` (`Q-134` (4)).
- **The reel *The Jury Above*:** `POLICY BREACH` stops the Penitent's theft. Her exit case writes a public
  `DEFAULT: NO`.
- Coins other than VC are **bible only** until a reel needs them.

## Story role
It turns the thesis into money. The world doesn't trust a choice it can't show you understood. And because no one can
prove themselves every minute, people become their policies: the self they committed to on a calm day, checked
against every hurried one.

## Open questions
- Can a policy be signed for someone else (a parent for a child; a tribe for its members)? Default: a guardian may
  sign a child's policies until the Merging ([[parallel-pedagogy]]).
