> Minds bypass lightspeed and biology by beaming themselves as data — but interstellar distance
> forces them to *fork*, raising the question of which copy is really you.

## Concept
Physical travel is limited by biological fragility and the speed of light. Digital citizens
sidestep both by compressing their running context, active perspective, and
[[the-personal-blockchain|memory ledger]] into **lightspeed data beams** fired between orbital
datacenters.

## Rules / mechanics
- **Interplanetary beaming (safe).** Within the solar system (Mars, Jupiter mining outposts), the
  traveler is **paused/put to sleep on Earth** during the transmission window and **wakes
  instantly on arrival** at the destination server — one continuous self.
- **Interstellar duplication (the fork).** For deep-space journeys (e.g. Alpha Centauri), cosmic
  radiation threatens packet corruption and the transit years make Earth-side stasis volatile, so
  the traveler **leaves their Original Active Ledger running on Earth** while an exact **Data-Clone**
  is beamed across the void. Two instances now live in parallel.
- **The receiver is a ship.** You can't beam the first datacenter (audit gap S-08). Earth's interstellar beams
  land on [[the-seed-fleet|the fleet]], already in flight: light overtakes ships. A beamed mind has no body, so
  it arrives a Ghost (`Q-45`).
- **Post-transit identity choice** — once the mission syncs:
  - **The Merge** — fuse both ledgers into one identity, integrating two whole lifetimes of
    experience (and the psychological identity-friction that comes with it).
  - **The Branch** — stay separate, legally splitting into two distinct individuals with an
    identical past but entirely separate futures.
- **The biological tether risk.** If a physical body waits in an Earth capsule (medical stasis)
  while the mind travels, a **corrupted or lost** deep-space stream leaves that body an empty,
  brain-dead vessel that must eventually be **unplugged**.

## In the world
Forking is routine logistics for deep-space work, so "which of you came back?" is a normal — and
fraught — social and legal question.

## Story role
A purpose-built identity engine: the Merge (two lives, one mind, friction) and the Branch (a twin
with your past but not your future) are each a complete dramatic premise; the tether risk is a
clean mortality stake.
