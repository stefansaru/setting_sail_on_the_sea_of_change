> A citizen's mind, memories, and core behavioral frameworks are anchored to an immutable,
> decentralized ledger — and the private keys to that consciousness are theirs alone.

## Concept
Persistence on the [[the-shadow-grid]] is guaranteed by the **Personal Blockchain**: each mind,
its memories, and its behavioral frameworks are written to an immutable, decentralized ledger.
This ledger **is** a citizen's in-grid memory — the immutable, **eidetic** record that makes perfect
recall possible ([[the-memory-substrate]]) — and it lets a running state be re-routed at lightspeed
without being lost or deleted.

## Rules / mechanics
- **The two-key split.** A ledger has two keys (resolves audit S-10):
  - **Identity-Key** — the *live execution loop* that runs the active self. Its loss/corruption =
    **true death**.
  - **Read-Key** — the *historical memory database / life-logs*. Read-only; harmless to share.
- **Cryptographic sovereignty.** Each individual holds both private keys — innermost thoughts and
  secrets stay private.
- **The Full Disclosure Clause.** On **permanent deletion or physical death**, an opted-in citizen's
  **Read-Keys only** are broadcast to the public network (so society can harvest their life-history)
  while the **Identity-Key is securely, permanently wiped** — disclosure shares the record, never the
  living self.
- **Immutability.** The ledger can't be quietly rewritten — you can't erase your past, only seal or
  disclose it.
- **The Lossy Outside Principle.** Experiences accrued in the **physical world** (a cyborg body /
  proxy antenna) upload **only as accurately as the biological cache registered them** — so the Grid's
  record of a citizen's mortal hours is blurry and impressionistic, an organic dream viewed from the
  digital core ([[the-memory-substrate]]).

## In the world
A **Cultural Chain-Check** custom has emerged: because high-risk activities carry a probability of
permanent deletion, peers playfully or cynically press each other to unlock (disclose) their
ledgers before dangerous tasks.
> *"Hey, you're tracking a 99% success rate on that DNA rewrite today. Are you sure you don't want
> your chain to become public? Just in case?"* / *"I'll think about it."*

## Story role
Privacy vs. legacy, secrecy vs. contribution, and a quiet memento-mori: disclosure is a way to
*matter after deletion*. The Chain-Check is a ready-made tension beat before any
[[the-1-percent-mutation|high-risk procedure]].

## Open questions
- *(Answered)* **Can keys be stolen/coerced, or is sovereignty absolute?** Absolute — and that's the
  horror: a biological inmate can lock a block forever and the AI can't pry it from living tissue. See
