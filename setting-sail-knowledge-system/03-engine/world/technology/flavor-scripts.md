> receptors, and a restaurant is an *environmental event*: order a Cabernet and the whole room turns the
> color of the wine.

## Concept
Digital gastronomy on the [[the-shadow-grid]]. For a citizen with no physical mouth in-grid, eating is
data: **Flavor Scripts** — software packets that recursively simulate taste directly into the sensory
layer. It's the culinary counterpart to a [[disruptive-algorithms|Disruptive Algorithm]], and the foil
to the physical [[the-post-labor-economy|Hospitality Economy]].

## Rules / mechanics
- **Execution, not ingestion.** An entity routes a recursive algorithm (e.g. a *"Vintage Cabernet"*
  script) into its taste receptors — no chewing, no calories, no waste.
- **The restaurant as environmental event.** Dining is multi-sensory by design: as the script runs, the
  virtual room's lighting **morphs to mirror the chemical weight of the flavor** — a Cabernet floods the
  space with a rich, warm [[chromatic-expressionism|Caravaggio gold]]; lighter scripts lift toward Rothko
  golds. The [[the-perspective-lens-ui|Perspective Lens]] composes the room around the taste.
- **Perfect & repeatable (the cost).** Like everything digital, a Flavor Script is flawless, infinitely
  re-runnable, and **recorded in eidetic 4K** on the ledger ([[the-memory-substrate]]) — which is exactly
  what makes it feel *less real* than a meal you can only half-remember. At [[velocity-tiers|high tiers]]
  a citizen can savor a subjective feast in seconds.

## In the world / look
A Flavor-Script bistro is a chromatic instrument: each course re-paints the room, so a tasting menu is
experienced as a sequence of color-states a diner walks through. Contrast the [[the-post-labor-economy|
physical resort]] — real ingredients, a human chef, warm imperfect light — where the value is precisely
that the experience **can't** be flawlessly logged.

## Story role
The fork in the show's thesis at the dinner table: the digital meal is perfect, controllable, and kept
forever; the organic meal is imperfect, mortal, and **forgotten** — and the second is the luxury. A
high-tier citizen's **first night at a real Hospitality restaurant** (sensory shock at actual, lossy,
human-cooked food) is a ready-made character beat — the optimized palate undone by an imperfect tomato.

## Open questions
- Can a Flavor Script be *corrupted* — a malicious or addictive taste-loop (gastronomic
  [[disruptive-algorithms|Disruptive Algorithm]]), or a [[chromatic-tears|Chromatic Tear]] in the dining
  room? Sets whether food is also an attack surface.
