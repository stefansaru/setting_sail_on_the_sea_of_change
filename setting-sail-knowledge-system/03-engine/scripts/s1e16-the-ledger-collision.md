# Script — S1E16: "The Ledger Collision"

> [[setting-sail-tier-descent]] S1E16 · Act IV (Phase C→D) · **the Synchronization Node** · Raul **30%
> organic** · POV: Raul (Earth) · register: neutral void + flashing selection matrix. 9:16 · ~2.5 min.
> Which version of your life is the *true* one?

**HOOK (0:00–0:05).** Two identical Rauls materialize **facing each other** in a featureless virtual
staging room. Same face, different eyes — one homebound, one star-worn. A blunt prompt blinks between
them: *MERGE ⟷ REMAIN SEPARATE.*

**ACT I — CONTEXT (0:05–1:15).** The rules are cold ([[the-cosmic-clone-paradox]]): **Merge**, and two
whole lifetimes fuse into one mind — gaining the clone's years at the cost of a violent identity crisis.
**Branch**, and they split into two legal people forever, an identical past, separate futures. Earth-Raul
*wants* the clone's life — the love, the grief, the growth he never got.

**ACT II — FRICTION (1:15–2:15).** The old Raul would have **optimized** — absorbed the clone, taken the
data, maximized himself. But this Raul has spent a fractured season learning that you don't have to
collapse two truths into one to hold them.
> **RAUL-CLONE:** If you merge me, the man I became out there — *he* dies. The one she loves.
> **RAUL (EARTH), quiet:** …Then stay him. *(beat)* I don't need to *win* you. I just needed to know you're
> real.
He declines the merge. He lets the other live.

**RESOLUTION / CLIFFHANGER (2:15–2:30).** The matrix resolves: **BRANCH — confirmed.** The clone fades
back toward Centauri, free, alive, separate. Earth-Raul stands alone in the node — and his fractured
glyph **steadies a fraction**, not solid, but no longer only flickering: the first time he held two truths
without forcing one. [UI: two glyphs, both intact, drifting apart.] Cut to black.

*Canon: **Branch** chosen (Earth-Raul releases the clone — proto-balance, seeds S2). `Q-14`: confirm
Merge-vs-Branch. Raul 30% organic; the glyph's first un-forced steadiness (toward the solid Ep 20).*
