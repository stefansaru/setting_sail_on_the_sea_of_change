# Script — S1E20: "The Macro-Doughnut Equilibrium" *(SEASON FINALE)*

> [[setting-sail-tier-descent]] S1E20 · Act IV · **Phase D** · **the intersection of flesh & ledger** ·
> Raul **0% organic** (Ghost in shell) · POV: Raul · register: warm Caravaggio → the **Macro-Doughnut**.
> 9:16 · ~2.5 min. The hollow glyph, finally filled.

**HOOK (0:00–0:05).** A child's ragged cough cuts the village quiet. A **Purist mother** (`Q-08`) cradles
her fevered son — a curable illness their un-augmented world cannot treat. Time, real and mortal, is
running out.

**ACT I — CONTEXT (0:05–1:15).** The mother faces the unthinkable: break the vow of her entire people and
let the Grid's medicine into her child — saving his life by **starting his digitalization** — or hold her
purity and let him die mortal and whole. She turns, in anguish, to the one person there who has lived
*both* sides: **Raul**, chrome and eidetic, who gave up everything she's about to risk.

**ACT II — FRICTION (1:15–2:15).** She begs him for the answer. The old Raul — the optimizer, the
metric-chaser — would have one. This Raul doesn't, and that's the point.
> **MOTHER:** You've *been* the machine. Tell me it's worth it. Tell me he'll still be *him.*
> **RAUL:** …I can't. He'll gain everything and lose the thing that made it matter. *(beat)* And he'll
> live. Both are true. *(he takes her hand — warm flesh over cold chrome, the E19 image returned)* I can't
> choose it for you. I can only stay while you do.
He does not resolve the paradox. He **holds** it — and her — inside it.

**RESOLUTION / FINALE (2:15–2:30).** The mother chooses (we cut before we see which — the choice was never
the point; the *holding* was). And in Raul's Lens, the flickering circles and squares stop fighting and
**fuse**: the **hollow "Camel's Doughnut" from his graduation, at last filled SOLID** — sun and storm-cloud
counterweighted, *"intention to feel balance,"* earned. He is whole — not because the pain is gone, but
because he can finally carry it. Hard cut to black on the solid ring.

*Canon (`Q-12`): the glyph **hollow→fractured→SOLID** completes here; the E19 hand-image returns; Phase D /
[[the-macro-doughnut]] earned. Raul 0% organic. Theme is *held*, never stated. Season close.*
