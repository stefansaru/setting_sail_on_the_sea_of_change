# Episode Blueprint — "The Split Mirror" (The Eve of Merging)

> A **standalone / origin reel** for [[setting-sail-tier-descent]] (Raul's `CHR-02` graduation; slot
> `Q-11`), dramatizing [[parallel-pedagogy|The Merging]]. 9:16 vertical · ~2.5 min · cinematic photoreal.
> A bravura showcase of the [[chromatic-expressionism|five-artist palette]]: three lives in three
> registers inside one **Josef Albers** structural frame, fusing into one.

## The visual setup & hook
The vertical screen splits into **three equal horizontal bands** — a rigid **Albers** geometric frame.
All three faces are teenage Raul; all are 16; **they have never met.**
- **Top band — [[chromatic-expressionism|Turrell]] cold blue void.** **Raul-Alpha** stares into walls of
  scrolling variables and graphs (Analytical thread).
- **Middle band — [[chromatic-expressionism|Caravaggio]] warm chiaroscuro landscape.** **Raul-Beta**,
  outdoors, dirt-stained, crying silently as a biological purist creature dies (Intuitive thread).
- **Bottom band — [[chromatic-expressionism|Flavin]] harsh neon-pink lightbar.** **Raul-Gamma** in a
  metal training cage, breathing hard, mechanical fingers clenching as he calibrates a heavy shell
  (Kinesthetic thread).

## The friction
Identical faces; entirely different souls behind the eyes. A synchronized notice blinks across all three
Lenses at once: *[ Graduation Protocol Initiated · Automated Merging in T-minus 60s ]*. A soft voiceover
layers their thoughts into a rhythmic, chanting chorus:

> **RAUL-ALPHA** *(top):* I know the exact mechanics of the sky…
> **RAUL-BETA** *(middle):* …but I don't know why the dark hurts this much.
> **RAUL-GAMMA** *(bottom):* …I just know how to swing the steel.

## The climax & visual fusion
The horizontal dividers **vibrate and dissolve** into bleeding fields of amber and violet; the frame
lines collapse inward. A rapid **push-in** drives the three faces toward one central focal point. The
countdown hits zero — a high-frequency data tone strikes.

The split-screen **snaps** into a single, high-contrast 9:16 portrait. **Raul opens his eyes.** The
fragmented expressions are gone — intense, calm, *cognitive* clarity (he is **whole, remembering
everything at once**). His top circles and bottom squares settle into the **hollow outline** of the
[[the-macro-doughnut|Macro-Doughnut]] — the system's factory-issued *shape* of balance, the
[[parallel-pedagogy|"Camel's Doughnut"]]. He draws one deep, integrated breath. *He is Raul now — whole
in mind, handed a balance he has not yet earned.* (The hollow glyph is the quiet tell: this is the high
before the fall, not the resolution.)

## Notes / continuity
- **The Merge tech** = the same fusion as [[the-cosmic-clone-paradox]] / [[parallel-pedagogy]] — everyone
  meets it once, here.
- **The Two Doughnuts (`Q-12`, settled):** the Merging confers **cognition, not equilibrium**. The
  graduation glyph is the **hollow "Camel's Doughnut"** (inherited Phase-A balance, [[parallel-pedagogy]]);
  the descent **fractures** it; Ep 20 is the **same glyph filled solid** (earned). The UI carries the
  whole arc: **hollow → fractured → solid.** Frame this as the high before the fall.
- **Body-clock note:** at 16 / graduation Raul is **~100% organic** ([[progressive-digitalization]] Act
  I). The "partially synthetic hands" beat should read as **Gamma's training-shell residue / a flash**,
  not a permanent cyborg state — his real cyborg-ization starts at Ep 10. Adjust the final image so it
  doesn't pre-empt the body-clock.
- **Audit hooks:** `STORY-07`, `CINE-09` (Albers symmetry), `VIS-15` (all five palettes), `SCENE-05`.
