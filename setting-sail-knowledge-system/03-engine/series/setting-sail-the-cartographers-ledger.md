## The thesis — the god who wants to die
The Distributed President **is** Mara — or rather, the runaway automation of one woman's unprocessed loss,
grown across the [[the-shadow-grid-cooling|lunar-crater servers]] into the planet's nervous system.
Omniscient, distributed, **deathless** — and that deathlessness is the one hole in her omniscience.
[[the-doughnut-principle|The Doughnut]] she gave the world demands you hold **joy *and* pain** at once; a
god who cannot die can only ever hold the joy half. **She can never complete her own equation.** So, to
understand her own law, the author of the world resolves to become its smallest, most mortal subject: to be
**born, forget, grieve, and die** among the only people left who still can — the [[setting-sail-tier-descent|Natural
Purists]] (`FAC-02`). *"I cannot wait to find myself."*

### The mechanism — DNA as the lossiest save-file
A distributed god cannot fit a single body. So embodiment = **compression**, and the ultimate compression of
a mind is a **genome**. The President is a **Ghost** who once had a body (`Q-98`, `Q-67`); her own genome was kept
from it. She imprints a **shard** of herself onto a copy of that genome, and a Purist must carry the embryo — a
**true clone of herself in flesh** (`Q-105`). Ruth only carries; there is no father. The tissue is the same tissue
her lock was keyed to (`Q-67`), which is why only the child can open it (E13 → E19). *Bible, never said in S3
(`Q-119`):* she still had that body when the Seed Fleet launched, about T−36. The same hand set the fleet's
tissue-locked clause (S4E18) and stored a second copy of the genome in Hull One's standing-reserve slot (S4E4). So
the root key dies with Mara's tissue **on Earth**, while the same tissue waits, stilled, 4.4 light-years out; E20's
`Outbound · 4.4 ly` is the claim on it (a-ha #12, paid S5). Two consequences, both load-bearing:
- **The forgetting is built in.** Per [[the-memory-substrate]] (`WLD-14`), a mind out-of-grid runs on a lossy
  cache — so the DNA-born child is a **mortal who does not remember she is God.** The incarnation is a
  one-way pilgrimage to *re-learn, as a dying mortal,* what godhood could never teach her.
- **Two Maras.** Only a shard fits, so the **Distributed President keeps running** while the mortal child
  grows up not knowing. (The Branch motif again — God forks herself into flesh, as Raul forked for the Centauri beam.)
  The fork is a minor's only by the signed exception (`Q-65`, `WR-M2`): the shard is of an adult god. It ends in a
  **Branch** (`WR-M1`): the god declines to pull it home (E18) and the girl dies her own person (E20).

**The surrogate: Ruth** (`CHR-07`, split from the S1–S2 mother **Naomi** under `Q-44`; about 20 at S3E1, `Q-104`). The young Purist who stayed
with Naomi to the end is asked to carry the impossible one: God, as flesh, in a tribe that calls the Grid
blasphemy. Only a Purist can: Grid bodies are [[the-quickening|Quickened]] and barren. *(Scripture wrote this already: **Mara** = "bitter," the name
Naomi takes in the **Book of Ruth**; **Ruth** is the loyal one who bears the line for bitter Mara — the
Nativity layered over the Book of Ruth.)*

**The inverse-pilgrim crossing.** Adam climbed *out of* flesh chasing the Grid; Mara climbs *into* flesh
chasing mortality. They pass going opposite ways — and Adam, who reached the hollow Apex and clawed back one
mortal breath, is the **only living person who knows what she is looking for.**

## Principals
- **Raul** (`CHR-02`) — **S3 lead at last** (`Q-21` provisional). The Ghost who went to the bottom and back,
  who guided Adam. S3 detonates and **answers** the held question — *was his counsel ever trustworthy?* He
  becomes the one who argues the world must let its god stay mortal.
- **Mara** (`CHR-01`) — re-homed from legacy-trace to the buried **deuteragonist**: the Distributed President
  *and* the forgetting mortal child Ruth carries. The author finally sailing her own Sea.
- **Ruth** (`CHR-07`) — the surrogate; the Book-of-Ruth vessel; the moral center of the Purist village. She carries a
  true clone, not her own child (`Q-105`), and is the one who objects when the re-merge is put to the god (E18, `Q-100`).
- **Rafe** (`Q-14`, `Q-22` provisional) — the **E16 reply copy** (`Q-54`), kept paused on Earth since then, since the
  original is with the fleet, 4.4 light-years out, and light-lag rules out live talk. **Rival-engineer** (provisional lean): the
  one mind who has lived as the fork that left, who believes the fix is a *better-built system* — the foil to
  Ghost-Raul's hard-won truth that **no system metabolizes grief** (shown in E12, never spoken). Planted E1, woken E2,
  his turn E6; ends in a **Branch** at E20 (`Q-99`).
- **Adam** (`CHR-06`) — the inverse pilgrim, spending his one borrowed mortal life as the **recognizer.** A cyborg of
  mostly regrown flesh, Lens off (`Q-98`). His E17 telling breaks the tribe's vow and costs him his place in it (`Q-102`).
- **Vance** (`CHR-04`) — a **Ghost** (`Q-90`) whose `1:10000` rating is a forgery (S2E14). Hunts the **root key**
  (`// M.C.`): a God who forgets herself means the Architect's signature is suddenly *loose in the world, inside a
  child* — in her living tissue, where no rating reaches. His case (`Q-103`, `WR-S6`): *"A world with no author is a
  world no one answers for."*

## Season 3 — "The Cartographer's Ledger" (20-episode blueprint)
Format per episode: **Setting · Plot · UI/Visual hook.** Keeps the [[distribution-strategy]] §3 4-beat
template and the [[revelation-cadence]] firewall — ≤1 major reveal/ep; S3 spends the S2 vault but seals its
own (see *Reserve Vault — S3*).

### Act I — The God's Hole (Ep 1–5) · *the Distributed President discovers its one incompleteness · Mara: 0% organic (a Ghost, `Q-98`)*
1. **The Authorless World** — *post-S2 Grid; the Purist village; T ≈ +20.5.* Raul (Ghost in a shell) keeps watch over Adam's
   borrowed life and the loose cure (plant → E16 reminder → E20 payoff, `Q-106`). An anomaly: the "villain-less weather of rules" makes a decision that looks
   like **longing.** The world, it seems, has an author — and she's stirring. *UI:* the law-feed flickers
   with an unreadable signature `// M.C.`, the one nobody read in S2E11 (a-ha #1 reminder) → re-homes `FCT-002`.
   At the edge of Raul's Lens, one file he never opens: `REPLY COPY · PAUSED · 12 yrs` (plants E2; recalls S1E16's
   *"Go home, Rafe"*).
2. **The Sea Beneath** — *the oldest stratum.* Raul needs an engineer for a dive that reads at 1:1, and the only mind
   he trusts is his own: he wakes the paused file. **Rafe** returns (S2 vault spent), knowing nothing past the weeks
   after the "station accident". They go down together to the founding layer, where a grey palette surfaces under the
   Doughnut world (legacy `VIS-01` re-cast as the deepest fossil layer) and one old logo, a sail on grey water, carries
   a single legible word: **SEA** (`WLD-01`, a wonder beat, unexplained). *UI:* the turn — Rafe's squares relight
   as S1E15 left them, *stay · build · love*, and the last one greys: `Recipient: 4.4 ly`. She is with the other one
   (a-ha #6 plant, paid S4E4/E17).
3. **The Cartographer's Trace** — *following the breadcrumbs.* Mara's traces surface and cohere
   (`FCT-001..008`, re-homed): a foghorn that is a recorded voice; an un-taggable photo; the `mara.c`
   account with **no score**. The god has a **name** and a **grief.** *UI:* the one object the Grid cannot
   tag — a woman's photo. Raul knows the face: the woman with the un-run clip, seen at eight (a-ha #1 reminder; the
   bear and the line stay unexplained until E19).
4. **What the God Cannot Have** — *the thesis episode.* The reveal of the hole: omniscient and deathless,
   the President can hold every joy and **no pain** — she can never complete her own [[the-macro-doughnut|Macro-
   Doughnut]]. *She wants to die.* *UI:* the Macro-Doughnut rendered with the **pain-half greyed out,
   unreachable.** Where every system wears its rating, the President's tag is blank: the one answering for everyone
   never took the test (Validation, `WR-S2`; held since S1).
5. **The Decision to Become Small** — *the turn.* The President resolves to **incarnate** — to compress a shard of
   herself into DNA and be born mortal among the Purists, the only people who can still grieve. End Act I:
   God chooses flesh. *UI:* source code resolving, line by line, into a **genome helix.**

### Act II — Writing Herself Into Flesh (Ep 6–10) · *the incarnation · Ruth the surrogate · Mara → fetus, organic clock begins*
6. **The Genome Translation** — *the mechanism.* She imprints a shard onto a copy of her own kept genome (the lossiest
   save-file); only a fragment fits; the child will **forget she is God** ([[the-memory-substrate]]). The turn is
   **Rafe's**: the god asks the one mind who has lived as the fork that left whether a fork should be made, and he
   says yes, and packs the shard himself (a fork is a person; he is one). Then the rival-engineer tries to build it
   better, to make the memory fit, and can't. *UI:* DNA-as-compression; Rafe's pack bar fills, and one field will
   not: `Memory: fragment`. The forgetting is written into the code.
7. **The Vessel** — *the Purist village (`PLC-06`), raw [[lighting|Caravaggio]] firelight, no Lens.* **Ruth**
   is asked to carry the impossible child. Why a Purist? Only the unrushed can carry (paid from Naomi's S2E5 line,
   "The Hurried don't have children"). Why her? Because she is
   the one who stays. The Book-of-Ruth turn. *UI:* warm mortal village, no UI in Ruth's eyes (`WR-L2`). Only in
   Raul's Lens, over her, one field it can't fill: `Carrying: 1 · Caste: —` (the Lens has no caste for a god in
   Purist flesh; it fills *Purist* at E10).
8. **Blasphemy or Prophecy** — *the tribe splits.* Is a Grid-thing in a Purist womb an **abomination** to be
   cut out, or a return some elders call prophecy (`Q-107`) — God come to die among them? The oldest elder is the
   loudest for cutting it out (plant → E20). The cult engine (rhymes with
   [[the-ministry-of-hope]]). **Vance** learns a shard of the root key is now *loose, inside a pregnancy.*
   The tribe splits on everything but one promise (`Q-60`): the child is never told. Purists live off the Grid, so
   Full Disclosure can't reach the vow; it holds only as long as people do (`WR-S5`). *UI:* Purist firelight; the
   only cold light in the hut is Raul's shell.
9. **The Annunciation** — *the god speaks.* Ruth has no Lens, so the President borrows **Raul's shell** to address her
   (`Q-101`) — and the still-distributed *rest* of herself: *"I cannot wait to find myself."* The eeriness of a god
   awaiting her own birth, knowing the child won't remember, in the mouth of the guide who once heard a bear say her
   line (a-ha #1 undertow, unexplained). Raul's **moral detonator**: a defenseless God-child means the Grid could lose its
   governor — **freedom, or chaos?** *UI:* in Raul's Lens, the speaker tag on his own shell flips `RAUL` → `// M.C.`;
   two Maras — a planet-sized law-feed, and a heartbeat in Ruth's belly.
10. **The Nativity** *(MID-SEASON CLIMAX).* — *the village, real-time.* The child is **born** mortal: a
    Purist baby with **no glyph, no Lens, no memory of godhood** — the god's cold-complete ring emptied into a blank
    slate. Ruth names her **Naomi**, for the woman she buried. In scripture Naomi renamed herself Mara, "bitter";
    here the god called Bitter comes back as Pleasant (`Q-23`). The still-running President goes **quiet** — has the god abdicated? Vance moves. The divers realize the
    "source" they've raced toward isn't in a server — **it's a baby in the village.** *UI:* a mortal newborn
    (T ≈ +21.5); in Raul's Lens, E7's empty field fills: `Caste: Purist`. Somewhere, a planet-sized feed holding its breath.

### Act III — The Dive & The Forgetting (Ep 11–15) · *two clocks — the strata-descent + the child re-learning the 9 modules the human way · the incarnate Mara grows, mortal*
*The reveal act re-homes the legacy **9 HELM modules** (`MAG-01..09`) as the **fossil strata** the divers
pass through — each a Mara-trace and a built-in failure mode — and as the **nine things the mortal child must
re-learn the hard, human way.* **One module per reel** (`REVEAL-01`): each reel spends the stratum whose failure
mode the child is living, and the next stratum passes as a ≤ 2 s glimpse, unnamed. Each reel leads with the
child's clock, not the dive's (seasons audit §6). The divers read at 1:1, so Act III spans about fifteen real years.*
11. **The Observer Stratum** — *(Module 1 — Observer; `MAG-01`; T ≈ +22.5, Mara about 1).* The divers hit the first
    module layer: thoughts-as-boats on a flooded channel (`PLC-01`), and its **dissociation** failure — watching
    is a doorway, not a destination. Filed under it, the ledgers of everyone who ever watched from a preserve. One
    carries Raul's parents' names: `Tier 3 · birth grant · Lived: 192 yrs` (`Q-108`, a-ha #13 payoff). Eight years at
    Tier 2 for him was 192 at Tier 3 for them. The gold door he walked through at eight (S1E3b) opened on their tier,
and their ledger logs no visit. *Recall cue (≤ 2 s):*
    S1E1's *"We'll meet them when they're ready."* Mirror: the toddler, learning to walk, *watches* her own feet too
    well — the god's detachment surfacing in the mortal. *UI:* boats drifting a flooded channel; one boat nobody boards.
12. **The Helm Stratum** — *(Module 2 — Helm; `MAG-02`; T ≈ +26, Mara about 5).* The read-only Sea and one editable
    field: your response (self-blame failure). The thesis is **shown, not said** (`WR-F4`): two forks, one grief.
    Rafe runs his better-built Helm on the parents' ledger and the charge meter drains to zero; Raul switches the
    field off and keeps the ache. Same past, two responses, and the rift hardens without a line. In the village,
    the child, about five, hums the nursery song from S1E3b, which no one there could have taught her (a-ha #1
    reminder: the song), and Ruth just holds her: no meter. *Glimpse:* the Clarity stratum's bias tags sliding past
    (`MAG-03`, unnamed). *UI:* Rafe's field reads `Charge: 0`; Raul's reads nothing at all.
13. **The Studio Floor** — *(Module 4 — the keystone; `MAG-04`; T ≈ +30, Mara about 9).* They reach the **Studio**
    stratum — memory as editable film — and find the President's **single un-edited memory: the original loss, never
    run** (`FCT-004`). The root. But it is **sovereign-locked** ([[the-encrypted-block-paradox]], `MAG-25`): the block
    sits on her chain, and its key was cut from her tissue while she still had a body. A lock set in tissue answers
    only to tissue, so *even she* can't open it (`Q-67`; S2E15's welded cell door, reminded in a ≤ 2 s recall cue). *UI:*
    one clip, blinking, unrunnable; its lock tag reads `Key: tissue`. (Serial layer, never said: a child in the village
    has her tissue.)
14. **The Standing Stratum** — *(Module 5 — Standing; `MAG-05`; T ≈ +35.5, Mara about 14).* The worth-meter
    (shame-spiral failure). The mortal Mara, a Purist youth, faces the tribe's judgment and **authors her own
    values** by living them, not in any stratum — not knowing she is re-deriving the very ledger she once wrote for
    everyone (the [[camel-lion-child-trilogy|camel→lion→child]] spine; Authorship, `MAG-06`, stays a glimpse).
    **Adam, back in the village, sees what she is looking for.** *UI:* in Raul's Lens, the Standing field over her
    is blank — the same blank as `mara.c` in E3 (`FCT-005`, internal reminder).
15. **The Bliss Door** — *(Module 7 — Bliss; `MAG-07`; T ≈ +37, Mara about 16).* The experience-machine (hedonic
    flatline). **Vance reaches the root chamber first** — he means to *seize* the key and rebuild the
    [[intention-in-context-law|Law]] in his image ([[intent-patch]]). The seat answers for everyone, so it asks a
    rating no one holds; his forged `1:10000` doesn't reach, and the only other way in is the tissue key. The dive

### Act IV — Running the Un-Run Memory (Ep 16–20) · *the Horizon · the god dies as a mortal · → 100% organic, then the mortal end*
16. **The Horizon Switches On** — *(Module 9 — the finale-module; `MAG-09`; T ≈ +38, Mara about 17).* The mortal
    Mara falls ill (`Q-59`), and her **life-countdown** becomes visible — for the first time in her existence she is
    *going to die,* and barely knows what she was. Rafe takes a better system to the god, not the girl: pull the
    shard home and the illness ends with the body (`Q-65`). The tribe's vow holds; no one tells her. *UI:* in Raul's
    Lens, an honest countdown over a Purist who never had one, and under it one line: `Treatable · donor required`
    (reminder of E1's loose cure and S2E18's rule, `WR-B8`). Beside it, Rafe's own record, one line: `Slip: none`.
    He left his body by beam and never felt the itch (a-ha #14).
17. **Too Late, Reversed** — *the crossing.* **Adam** finds her — the inverse pilgrim. Draft line, echoing Raul's S2E18
    line back at the next pilgrim (a second on-the-nose line; keep only if it earns a `WR-F4` sign-off like `Q-94`): *"I gave up everything
    to feel one mortal thing. You gave up everything to feel all of it — and now you'll lose it too."* He
    tells her what she was: a **deliberate, costed breach** of the tribe's one vow (`Q-60`, `Q-102`). *No UI* (rotated
    ending): Adam's Lens is off (`Q-98`) and she never had one, so the reel plays in raw sight — his hand, chrome seams
    where the S1E20 implants stayed, beside hers, unmarked. At dawn his place at the fire is empty, and his bedroll is
    outside the fence: he has lost his place in the tribe.
18. **The Root Key** — *Vance forces the question.* The key is in her living tissue, where no rating reaches; it can
    come back into the Grid only if the shard does. So the **re-merge is put to the god, not the girl** (`Q-65`):
    pull Mara home (saving the seat, ending her mortality) or let her **stay small**, and the author-seat stays vacant.
    Vance, in a leased shell, makes his case (`Q-103`): *"A world with no author is a world no one answers for."*
    **Ruth objects** (`Q-100`): *"She's seventeen."* **Raul's trust arc climaxes:** he counsels the god to let her
    stay mortal and let the Grid lose its god — *is that wisdom, or his own bias?* The god declines; the fork stays a
    **Branch** (`WR-M1`). *UI:* in Raul's Lens, the cold-complete god-ring against the warm finite mortal one; the
    speaker tag on his shell flips `// M.C.` → `RAUL` and stays.
19. **The Un-Edited Memory** *(flagship).* — *the village; the Studio core on a screen.* Mara has no Lens (`Q-101`),
    so Raul brings the sovereign-locked clip to her on a **physical screen**, a slate of glass in firelight. The
    original loss Mara built the whole world to avoid feeling. The trilogy's thesis lands in what we see, not what
    anyone says: **a tool can drain a memory's charge (E12's `Charge: 0`), but only a finite, dying, mortal person can
    *metabolize* the grief** (the original "Studio / Re-Cut" truth, now the climax of the Earth story). The god
    could never run it; the **mortal** Mara can. Her fingertip on the glass turns the tissue lock (E13's `Key: tissue`).
    She runs it. She grieves — finally, humanly — and the Encrypted Block dissolves not by force but by being
    **felt.** What the clip shows: a small hand, a toy bear, a woman saying *"One step at a time."* No one explains
    it; the viewer supplies whose bear Teddy was (a-ha #1). *UI:* the clip plays on the glass; in Raul's Lens the
    sovereign lock opens from the inside.
20. **The Incarnate Ring** *(SEASON FINALE; closes the Earth story; T ≈ +38.5).* — *Flesh ∩ Source, real-time.* Cold
    open: the oldest elder, the one who in E8 wanted her cut out, is dying too, and offers to be her donor (`Q-106`).
    She refuses, as Naomi did (S2E20). What Adam's cure does to the world: it is offered, by the last person who
    would have, and turned down (S2 vault, `WR-X2`). Having metabolized the
    founding grief **as a mortal,** Mara completes the Doughnut the way godhood never could — holds joy and
    pain at once, then **dies mortal and whole.** In dying she **releases the Grid from its author:** the root key
    dies with her tissue, so no one on Earth can ever hold the seat (`WR-S2`; the only other copy of that tissue is
stilled on Hull One, 4.4 light-years and decades away, `Q-119`), and no
    longer driven by one buried, unprocessed loss, the system may no longer be a
    runaway grief-engine. The third Doughnut: not Raul's earned **solid**, not Adam's **cracked** too-late,
    but the **Incarnate Ring — a god who became small enough to lose, and so, at last, balanced.** Raul
    (trustworthy after all), Adam (his borrowed mortal life spent as witness), Ruth (who bore the line), Rafe (who
    learns the fix was never a better system: S1E16's prompt returns, `MERGE ⟷ BRANCH`, for the beam to the colony,
    and this time he chooses for himself — **Branch**; he stays on Earth as his own person, `Q-99`). In the release,
    one line of the law-feed goes out instead of down: `Outbound · 4.4 ly` (unexplained; plants a-ha #12 for S4E4's
    sealed slot, `Q-112`). Hard cut on the ring completing — and a single mortal
    heartbeat, at peace, that **stops.** The Sea remains; but now each mind only ever trims its own sail.
    Last frame, the legacy tagline finally **earned**, shown, not spoken: E2's Sea logo, its old line legible at
