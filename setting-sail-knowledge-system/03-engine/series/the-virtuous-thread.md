# Thread Map — The Virtuous, across the seasons

> *(Status: **DRAFT, 2026-11-01; partly placed 2026-11-03 under `Q-84`; S3 rows re-placed 2026-11-03 under `Q-98..Q-108`.** S1 rows are proposals: no S1 script has
> been changed for this map. In S2–S4, rows marked **✅ placed** now sit in the season blueprint's episode text; rows
> marked *not placed* stay proposals, with the reason. The a-ha list these beats share is in
> `AuditReport_seasons-consistency-aha-pacing_d1_20261102` §4.)*
>
> **One question ties all of it together: *who may answer for other people, and what proves it?*** A nursery
> fight asks it at the scale of two. The rule asks it at the scale of N. The god asks it at the scale of everyone.

Sources: [[the-virtuous]] · [[the-one-in-n-rule]] · [[the-pattern-detector]].

## New concepts vs the reveal budget
≤1 major reveal per reel still holds. S1 gets **two** (the loop in E3a, the detector in E3b). The word `1:N`
stays unexplained in S1. The rule itself is first *spoken* in E10 as one line, and fully paid in S2E12–14
(now placed: named at E12, kept apart from the CVP at E13, turned at E14).
