## Narrative arc — one hull, four voyages
A child is born near the top of a pyramid he does not know is a ship. He descends it, rung by rung, into the
slow and expensive dark where bodies live, and is on the one hull that is struck when the pyramid is tricked
into opening its own shield. In the dying hull he is burned in the hit, refuses to leave the slow people behind, spends his priority to guide a worker through the Slip in one night, and stays to die with the hull. On the
sister hull a Wake-Deck boy climbs the tiers for a cure that costs him his mortality; a Ghost reads the sealed
manifest and finds the fleet's mind is a woman who built the whole pyramid to avoid a grief, and has quietly
put herself first in the queue of bodies to be grown. The planet comes into view through the bore of the
shield. The hull they left is still transmitting, in Raul's voice: his last log, recorded before the scram.

**The Doughnut, four times, in one frame:** Raul's (hold the world and the weight), Adam's (hold the gift and
the subtraction), Mara's (hold the joy and the grief), Kai's (hold *I escaped* and *my body is dust*).

### Body clocks
- **Raul:** 100% → **70%** (the hit, E10) → **30%** (field surgery, E13) → dies at 30%, in the flesh (E15, `Q-78`):
  at zero the reactor scrams, his implants die with it, and he dies whole, with no Ghost left behind (`Q-113`).
- **Kai:** 100% → 0% in **E13–15**, the Slip in one night, with Raul as guide.
- **Adam:** 100% Wake-Deck boy → implants (E11) → climbing (E16–17) → the choice (E20).
- **Mara:** distributed → **compressed to DNA** (E18–19) → **the ramp's first embryo, carried by Ruth** (E20) — 0% to 100%.

### The glyph
One icon for all four: the **shield disc with its bore** (`VIS-12`). Whole (E1–3) → open (E4; again E10) → cracked (the hit) → re-drawn solid (E19), with the planet inside it (E20).

## "The Hull Ledger" — 20-episode blueprint
Format per episode: **Setting · Plot · UI/Visual hook.** 4-beat template; one major reveal per reel
([[revelation-cadence]]). The aliens are never shown.

### Act I — The Pyramid (Ep 1–5) · *the descent · Raul 100% · Phase A: the serene dark*
1. **The Meadow in the Hull** — *Hull One, Tier 2.* Raul is born into a capsule ([[capsule-birth]]), about a year before the ramp starts (T ≈ +3) and
   wakes in a meadow that is a sim running at 3,600×, the fleet's birth grant and the fastest clock a body aboard
   is ever allowed (`Q-85`, `WR-B5`). His parents' bodies rest in bays below. Teddy, a bear, keeps him company. *UI:* the disc glyph, whole; nobody yet calls it a shield.
2. **Watts and Chemistry** — *the teaching sim.* Raul is taught the pyramid as a kindness: fast minds cost
   light, slow bodies cost chemistry ([[the-stasis-economy]]); the fleet carries its people as minds so it can
   arrive. "Arrive" is the first Intention square he is given. *UI:* the two budgets as two circles.
3. **The Wake Deck** — *Tier 0, Hull Four.* Raul's first descent, on a school trip by proxy (`WR-B6`): the few who live awake in
   bodies, in real time, doing the work hands must do. **Ruth** and her boy **Adam**. Raul's Lens tags them
   *inefficient*; Ruth tags him *asleep*. *UI:* Caravaggio tenebrism — raw sight, no Lens, for one shot.
4. **Firing Day** — *Hull Four, forward bay.* The protocol as weather ([[the-firing-protocol]]): the screen
   drops, the long lock, the bore. **Kai** and **Teo** on the maintenance rota. Raul, grown, is **down-clocked
   for a deep-space assignment** — demoted to Hull Four's base; his stilled body crosses by lifeboat (`WR-E3`). His
   descent is no longer a trip. *UI:* the
   disc opens for the first time; the audience learns it is a window.
5. **The High Clock** — *the Apex, glimpsed.* Raul's fault report goes up a tier and he sees the council that
   runs everything — **Vance** among them, and a chair that is always empty, kept for the founder, its Apex
   ration drawn every watch and never spent (plants the chair: E15 reminds, E18 pays). An
   afternoon of theirs is his blink ([[the-sealed-manifest]]). **End Act I.**

### Act II — The Lure (Ep 6–10) · *Phase B: the window opens*
6. **The Supernova** — *every hull.* A burst with a nova's shape from the star the catalogue expected to die.
   The base gathers; the Wake Deck watches with real eyes. Teo weeps. *UI:* every circle fills with light.
7. **Help** — *analysis deck.* Not a star: an artificial satellite beaming in all directions
   ([[the-lure-beacon]]). **"help."** Then **"we're just like you, please help us,"** in every Earth language.
   Raul, who speaks three, translates for the Wake Deck. *UI:* the circles become mouths.
8. **Heading, Speed, Acceleration** — *split-frame.* The ask. The High Clock reads the reassurance as the
   tell and orders silence, unexplained. Vance's vote: *fire on the debris ahead; ignore the voice.* A lock is
   scheduled. Vesper, Raul's partner, a Ghost two tiers up, is already a week past the argument he is still having
   (a week at Tier 2 is under three real minutes).
   *UI:* a closed square from above.
9. **You Can Be Our Hero** — *Hull Four, after lights-out.* Teo finds layer four and sends the burst. Raul
   half-sees the console glow and thinks nothing of it. *UI:* one square lights **"help"** and stays lit.
10. **The Open Window** *(mid-season)* — *Hull Four.* Screen down, lock held, bore fired — and something not listed comes
    through the window: a grain of natural rock, hitting like about 107 tonnes of TNT (`Q-66`). The strike cooks the base deck: **Raul's body is burned** (first amputation,
    70%; he wakes in it on a surgical rig in the medical bay), Adam is crushed under a bulkhead, the hull is dying. *UI:* the disc cracks; Rothko Despair; cut.

### Act III — The Countdown (Ep 11–15) · *Raul 70% → 30%, then death · Kai 100% → 0% · Phase C: the storm in the hull*
11. **Eighty-One Hours** — *Hull Four, dark.* The hull's voice reads the number; at zero the reactor scrams and the
    stacks fall to reserve (`Q-56`). Kai is down-clocked to Tier 0, so every real hour counts. Adam is saved by **emergency
    implants** from the medical bay — Ruth breaks the Wake Deck's vow to let it happen; the implants will
    need the stacks to live. The beam-out list is drawn by priority. *UI:* the countdown in the squares.
12. **The Lifeboats** — *bay doors.* Bodies out: Ruth, Adam, Teo. Kai's bay is cut off; Kai's body cannot go, and
    never leaves that bay. Raul, a cyborg now, is **offered a lifeboat seat** — ranked, wanted upstairs. *UI:* "arrive" dims for two
    people and brightens for the rest.
13. **Priority Denied** — *the Data Buffer.* Kai asks for a faster clock and is refused. Raul has a seat on the last
    boat, looks at the one person left at the base, and **refuses to leave.** His first un-optimized act.
    The surgical rigs take his cortex to **30%**. *UI:* the vertical ruler; two figures pinned to its base.
14. **The Slip in One Night** — *the medical bay / Kai's bay, split frame.* Kai's body stays where it is. Raul, on his
    rig in the medical bay, works the bay's maintenance arms through his implants and talks Kai through the
    [[the-cyborg-slip|Slip]], the way the Teddy Bear once talked him through everything — except he has *been* to
    the bottom. Kai: 70%, 30%. *UI:* two Lenses flickering to wireframe in one frame.
15. **A Slight Itch** — *the Identity Key.* Minutes left. Kai severs the Key — *"A slight itch. Nothing
    more."* Packing at Tier 1 would take four real days; Kai has minutes. **Raul spends his priority** on Kai's climb
    and four Apex minutes, most of a year inside, to pack the self (`Q-113`); the last half-second is twelve hours.
    His last words to Kai are about the chair he saw in E5: *"Whoever that chair is for can't finish the equation
    from up there."* He stays at Tier 0 in what's left of his flesh and holds the beam (`Q-78`). The
    beam fires. **The last beam off a dying hull cannot pause:** a Kai keeps running in Hull Four's failing servers
    ([[the-cosmic-clone-paradox]] — the Branch, with no star between; never said on screen). *UI:* Turrell void; the cracked disc
    does not stop ticking. At zero the reactor scrams; Raul's implants die with it, and he dies whole, mortal, at
    real time. His last log is already recorded. **End Act III.**

### Act IV — The Cartographer's Manifest (Ep 16–20) · *Phase D: the brightening star*
16. **Hull One** — *arrival.* One Ghost wakes fast, at Tier 2, near the High Clock. Below, the lifeboats dock in slow
    motion and the ramp halts: every refugee is a colonist not grown. On the array, Hull Four keeps pace, dark. Adam's implants fail at Tier 0 — he
    makes his **first ascent** for maintenance and the pain *stops*. The first bite. *UI:* "arrive," for
    other people.
17. **The Spare Margin** — *synthesis bay / the Apex.* The High Clock cuts the colonist count and tells the
    base the ramp is "paused." **Vance** offers Adam a permanent up-clock (Tier 2, his body stilled in a bay) and a guaranteed chemistry line for
    Ruth — for the rest of his flesh. Adam, pain-free for the first time in his life, says he'll think about
    it. *UI:* two circles, two truths, one budget.
18. **The Cartographer** — *the Apex, the empty chair.* The only Apex time nobody is using is the founder's ration,
    drawn every watch since E5 and never spent, and **Vance**, who hates waste, seats the refugee Ghost on it. That
    pays for Kai's Apex time in E18–19 and the watches to E20. Reading [[the-sealed-manifest]] on it, Kai finds whose
    chair it is: **Mara** — the fleet's mind, the hull's voice, the author of the pyramid — a woman
    who built a world where nobody has to grieve because she could not. And one more line: **the first body
    the ramp grows is hers.**  Kai hears Raul's last
    words again, and now knows who they were about. *UI:* Albers archways; the base's tiny frame at the centre.
19. **Sensor Fault** — *the shared array, on the chair's ration.* Hull Four's last log at Apex speed: a grain of
    natural rock, and its timestamp sits under the burst logged leaving Hull Four in Teo's hand. The log never says
    *because* (`Q-111`). The High Clock buried the pairing under *sensor fault* to spare Teo the base. Kai keeps it. Mara, compressing herself to DNA, keeps everything — for a few more years. *UI:* the disc
    re-drawn solid, as a Ghost's.
20. **Visual Range** — *forward bay, Hull One, about four real years later; Kai has stood short watches on the
    chair's ration.* The planet resolves through the bore of the shield. In the
    synthesis bay the ramp's first embryo is placed in **Ruth** to carry — the god, to be born a colonist, remembering
    nothing. Adam, watching his mother take it on, **declines Vance** and chooses to stay slow enough to be
    present when she dies. Kai, who could wake into flesh only by taking someone's slot, watches the people who will. Far
    astern, past the catalogue's edge, where a dead hull can't be, **Hull Four is still transmitting — in Raul's
    voice, his last log, on a reserve that should have died years ago** (`Q-56`, `Q-113`). Cut to black.

## What the Hull Cut gains and loses
- **Gains:** one location, one clock, one budget — every rule has mass from episode 1. The four Doughnuts
  land in one frame. The Cracking of the Sky and the Open Window become the same event, which they always
  wanted to be. Mara's incarnation through the *ramp* is cleaner than through Earth's Purists: the god who
  wants to die is born as the colony's first citizen. No data-beam to a star is needed — the Branch is
  simply what a dying hull does.
- **Loses:** the long, slow seduction of Adam's climb (S2's whole engine, here three episodes); Raul's
  twenty-episode descent (here five); Earth, the Purist village, the Ministry of Hope, the Clock Brokers,
  the Data Graveyards — the world's *width*. The Hull Cut is a deep story, not a wide one.
- **The 40-episode version** restores most of the width: **Season A** = Acts I–II at twenty episodes
  (Raul's descent in full, the Wake Deck as the Purists, the lure over five episodes, the hit at E20);
  **Season B** = Acts III–IV at twenty (the countdown and Slip in ten, Adam's climb and Mara's manifest in
  ten, visual range at E40).
