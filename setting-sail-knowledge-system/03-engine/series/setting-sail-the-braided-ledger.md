# Series — *Intention in Context* · **ALT CUT: "The Braided Ledger"** *(S1–S4 intercut, 32 episodes)*

> *(File slug `setting-sail-the-braided-ledger`. **An alternate cut, not a replacement.** The four canonical
> seasons stay as they are, and so does the [[setting-sail-the-hull-ledger|Hull Cut]]. Display title
> provisional, `Q-41`. **Status: DRAFT, 2026-11-01.** No canon rows beyond those in [[the-virtuous-thread]].)*

> **The thesis.** The Hull Cut merges by removing Earth. This one keeps Earth *and* the fleet and cuts between
> them, as canon already allows: the fleet is in flight throughout S1–S3 (`Q-29`). Raul's and Kai's stories
> run side by side and rhyme: **a boy who is told he may not lead, and a safeguard told it may.** Adam and
> Mara carry the second half. Everyone answers one question: *who may answer for other people, and what
> proves it?* **32 episodes × 2.5 min**, 4 Acts of 8, against 84 today (22 + 20 + 20 + 22 with E9a–b).

## Structure
- **A strand:** Raul on Earth. **B strand:** Kai, Teo and Decimus on Hull Four. **C strand:** Adam, then Mara.
- **Rule of the cut:** each switch of strand answers or echoes the episode before it. Earth's news reaches
  the fleet years late, so strands rhyme by theme, not by clock.

## What it gains and loses
- **Gains:** one question and one rule, paying off from Ep 4 to Ep 32. Earth keeps its width. Tension is
  carried across strands: a Raul cliffhanger can hang over a Kai episode. Ep 12 and 15 become twin catastrophes.
- **Loses:** Adam's slow seduction (five reels instead of 20) is the biggest cost, and S3's nine HELM strata
  collapse into one reel. Several rows here fold more into 2.5 minutes than it can hold (Ep 24, 25, 31).
- **A 44-episode version** is more honest about that: give Ep 24, 25, 29, 30 and 31 three reels each, and
  double Ep 16 and 17. It keeps the structure and would be my recommendation if this cut is adopted.
