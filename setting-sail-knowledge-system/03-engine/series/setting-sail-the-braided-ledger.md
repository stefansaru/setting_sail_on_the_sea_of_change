# Series — *Intention in Context* · **ALT CUT: "The Braided Ledger"** *(S1–S4 intercut, 33 episodes)*

> *(File slug `setting-sail-the-braided-ledger`. **An alternate cut, not a replacement.** The four canonical
> seasons stay as they are, and so does the [[setting-sail-the-hull-ledger|Hull Cut]]. A non-canon drawer (`Q-41`, 2026-11-04). **Status: DRAFT, 2026-11-01; `Q-114` applied 2026-11-03** (S1E15 moved to a cold open,
> everything after it renumbered; Ep 7 and 8 of the old order swapped). No canon rows beyond those in [[the-virtuous-thread]].)*

> **The thesis.** The Hull Cut merges by removing Earth. This one keeps Earth *and* the fleet and cuts between
> them, as canon already allows: the fleet is in flight through S1, landed before S2 (`Q-29`, `Q-52`). Raul's and Kai's stories
> run side by side and rhyme: **a boy who is told he may not lead, and a safeguard told it may.** Adam and
> Mara carry the second half. Everyone answers one question: *who may answer for other people, and what
> proves it?* **33 episodes × 2.5 min**, a cold open and 4 Acts of 8, against 84 today (22 + 20 + 20 + 22 with E9a–b).

## Structure
- **A strand:** Raul on Earth. **B strand:** Kai, Teo and Decimus on Hull Four. **C strand:** Adam, then Mara.
- **Rule of the cut:** each switch of strand answers or echoes the episode before it. Earth's news reaches
  the fleet years late, so strands rhyme by theme, not by clock.
- **The one flash-forward:** Ep 1 is S1E15, taken out of Raul's order (`Q-114`), so that every S4 reminder of
  a-has #4, #5 and #6 lands after its plant.

## What it gains and loses
- **Gains:** one question and one rule, paying off from Ep 5 to Ep 33. Earth keeps its width. Tension is
  carried across strands: a Raul cliffhanger can hang over a Kai episode. Ep 13 and 16 become twin catastrophes.
  The cold open turns S1E15's quiet "night the Station is holed" into a clock the viewer carries through Act II. Ep 32
  lands a-ha #12's reminder harder than canon can, because S4's clause and S3's lock sit three reels apart.
- **Loses:** Adam's slow seduction (five reels instead of 20) is the biggest cost, and S3's nine HELM strata
  collapse into one reel. Several rows here fold more into 2.5 minutes than it can hold (Ep 25, 26, 32). Ep 1
  asks a cold viewer to meet Raul as a copy before meeting him as a child.
- **A 45-episode version** is more honest about that: give Ep 25, 26, 30, 31 and 32 three reels each, and
  double Ep 17 and 18. It keeps the structure and would be my recommendation if this cut is adopted.

Each folded reel must be re-scripted and still hold ≤1 major reveal ([[revelation-cadence]]). Folding breaks
the S1/S2/S3 Reserve Vaults as written: they would need one combined vault, rationed across the braid.

**Requirements set aside:** `WR-X1` (the seasons are intercut, not released in order; Earth lag is kept), `WR-X2` and
`WR-R1` as written (one combined vault). `REVEAL-01` stays in force but no folded row meets it until re-scripted.
`WR-X4` holds inside the braid: each of #4, #5, #6 and #12 is planted before its reminder.
