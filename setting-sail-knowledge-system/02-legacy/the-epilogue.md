# The Epilogue — Why This Book Exists

> This page preserves the book's epilogue (Ch.10 §10.12), which states the entire
> purpose this knowledge system is built to serve. It is the single most important
> page for the **legacy** pillar — the author's own words on *why*.

## What the epilogue says
The author wrote the book — and these ideas — primarily **for his daughter**, "just in
case something happens" and he doesn't get the chance to say them in person:

> "i've written down some things that i consider important, especially for my
> daughter—just in case something happens and i don't get the chance to say these
> things when she's old enough to understand. accidents happen. cancer happens. life
> doesn't wait for the right moment. if i'm not around, i want her to still hear my
> voice in these pages."

> "my dearest daughter, when you were just a few years old, your dad wrote this book
> for you... i wanted to leave you something meaningful. something that speaks to you,
> even if i'm not around someday. and if you're reading this now—then it worked."

- Signed: **— jini solutions**
- Feedback contact given in the book: **everything.lowercase@gmail.com**
- A personal detail links back to the body: the **palmaris longus** muscle "is present
  on your father's hands and absent on your mother's" (see [[vestigial-structures]],
  Ch.2 — the book's "see page 44").

## Why it matters to this system
This epilogue is the mission statement of the whole repository. The four pillars exist
*because of* this: the codex preserves the ideas precisely, and the **legacy layer**
exists so his daughter can "still hear his voice" — exactly what
[[02-legacy/voice.md]] and the [[letters]] are for. When in doubt about tone or
priority in `02-legacy/`, return to this page.

## How to use it
- Treat this as the north star for the legacy layer (cf. [[start-with-the-end-in-mind]]: write the eulogy / hold the end in mind — the author did exactly this).
- Letters in `letters/` are the author speaking to his daughter; this epilogue is the original instance of that voice.
- The book's own closing tool, [[intention-in-context]], frames the epilogue: the *context* is a father's mortality; the *intention* is to be present for her anyway.
