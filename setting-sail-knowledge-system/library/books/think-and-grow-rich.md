> A burning, definite desire — held in mind with faith, impressed on the subconscious by repetition, organized into a plan, and pursued with relentless persistence and a "Master Mind" of allies — turns thought into wealth. *"Thoughts are things."*

## The main idea
Hill claims to distill a "Secret" gathered over 20 years from ~500 wealthy people (commissioned
by Andrew Carnegie). The thesis: success starts in the mind — a **definite** aim charged with
**burning desire**, repeated until the **subconscious** accepts it, then executed through
planning, **decision**, and **persistence**, amplified by a **Master Mind** group. For
self-help in 1937 this was foundational; it seeded a century of motivation literature. It is
also where the corpus first meets **magical thinking** worth flagging (see Connections).

## Key concepts (the 13 "steps", condensed)
- **Definiteness of purpose / Burning Desire** — a specific aim, not a wish. ≈ [[define-values-before-setting-course]], [[self-authorship]], [[start-with-the-end-in-mind]].
- **Faith + Autosuggestion** — repeat and *feel* the aim until the subconscious believes it. ≈ [[atomic-habits]] (identity), [[anchoring]], [[memory-as-editable-film]] — *minus* the metaphysics.
- **Specialized knowledge & Imagination** — acquire what the aim needs; combine ideas. ≈ continuous learning.
- **Organized Planning** — turn desire into concrete steps. ≈ [[bridge-the-gap]], [[setting-smart-goals]].
- **Decision** — successful people decide fast, change slowly; indecision is the seed of fear. ≈ [[change-begins-with-a-decision]].
- **Persistence** — the trait that outlasts failure. ≈ [[consistency-beats-restarting]], [[the-long-game]], [[every-master-was-once-a-disaster]].
- **The Master Mind** — a coordinated group of allied minds. ≈ relationships, [[generous-tit-for-tat]], mentorship.
- **The Subconscious / The Brain / The Sixth Sense** — reprogram the subconscious; "infinite intelligence" and "invisible counselors." ≈ [[the-subconscious-mind]] — *but* the sixth sense slides into mysticism.
- **Fear is a state of mind** — the "six ghosts of fear" (poverty, criticism, ill-health, lost love, old age, death). ≈ [[most-fears-are-memories]], [[emotional-mastery]].
- **Responsibility** — "the responsibility is yours." ≈ [[taking-responsibility]], [[agency-over-circumstance]].

## Techniques
- **The six steps to riches** — write the *exact amount* you want, what you'll *give in return*, a *deadline*, a *plan*; start at once; read the statement aloud **twice daily**, seeing and feeling yourself already in possession.
- **Build a Master Mind** — surround yourself with allies whose knowledge/energy you blend with yours.

## Gold nuggets

## Connections (the compounding layer)
- **Agrees with** [[self-authorship]] / [[define-values-before-setting-course]] — definiteness of purpose is Hill's whole foundation.
- **Agrees with** [[the-long-game]] & [[compounding-through-habit]] — persistence + daily autosuggestion = compounding by repetition.
- **Agrees with** [[change-begins-with-a-decision]] and [[taking-responsibility]] / [[agency-over-circumstance]] (responsibility, fear as a chosen state).
- **Autosuggestion ≈ identity habits + anchoring** — strip the metaphysics and it's [[atomic-habits]] + [[anchoring]] + [[memory-as-editable-film]].
- **⚠ Tension with [[clear-thinking-under-bias]]** — "thoughts become things," the "sixth sense," and "invisible counselors" are **magical thinking**; and the method itself is built on **survivorship bias** (it studies only the rich winners — see [[survivorship-bias]]). *Logged as a contradiction.*
- **⚠ Tension with [[agency-over-circumstance]] / [[you-are-the-sailor-not-the-wind]]** — Hill implies *near-total* control of outcomes via thought; Setting Sail says control your *response*, not the wind/luck. *A polarity about how much is controllable.*
- **⚠ Mild tension with [[meaning-over-happiness]] / [[the-money-happiness-curve]]** — riches as the central aim (Hill broadens "riches" to spiritual/mental only at the very end).

## Verdict / who should read it
A foundational motivation classic — superb on **clarity of purpose, decision, persistence, and
autosuggestion-as-identity**. But it's built on survivorship bias and repeatedly slides into
law-of-attraction mysticism. **Keep the psychology (definite aim, repetition, persistence,
mastermind); discard the metaphysics.** Best for someone who needs ignition and commitment.
